Design decisions taken at planning time (read the migrations and tests as they are now, 2026-11-12):

1. STORIES UPDATE is guarded by a BEFORE UPDATE trigger, not by restrictive policies. A policy cannot limit columns, and a USING-filtered UPDATE matches zero rows silently. `app.feed_like_count()` and the story comment counter function are not security definer: they run `update public.stories set like_count = like_count + 1 ...` as the member who liked or commented, on a story that member did not write. A restrictive UPDATE policy narrowed to the author would turn every such counter update into a silent zero-row update and the counters would drift. This is the exact reason `app.feed_posts_member_update_guard` (migration 20261020123631, rewritten as an allow-list by 20261111144205) is a trigger; the new `app.stories_member_update_guard` copies its shape (security invoker, `set search_path = ''`, qualified names, returns NEW at once unless the role claim is `member`, SQLSTATE 42501 with a fixed message that names no row data).
2. STORIES DELETE is a restrictive policy `stories_member_delete_gate` with `using (app.tenant_role() <> 'member')`, the shape of `communities_member_delete_gate`. No endpoint hard-deletes a story (the service soft-deletes with an UPDATE, so the guard above covers it), a USING-filtered DELETE matches zero rows, and a null role claim denies (fail closed).
3. HIGHLIGHT and ITEM tables use restrictive per-command policies (INSERT with check, UPDATE using plus with check, DELETE using), because their rule is purely about the row: the highlight's community. A member claim passes only where `exists (select 1 from public.communities c where c.id = story_highlights.community_id and c.tenant_id = app.tenant_id() and c.kind = 'member' and c.created_by_user_id = app.user_id())`; any non-member claim passes as today; a null role denies. INSERT gates also carry `(select app.social_enabled())` for the member branch, like every insert gate of this phase (MPUB-04 says "reading social from SQL"); UPDATE and DELETE gates carry NO social term, exactly assumption A11 of the Phase 10 research for `communities_member_update_gate` (the service already stops those writes while social is off, and a social term on a delete would only stop a creator from taking down their own highlight).
4. THE ITEM INSERT GATE ALSO PINS THE STORY'S AUTHOR for a member claim: `exists (select 1 from public.stories s where s.id = story_highlight_items.story_id and s.tenant_id = app.tenant_id() and s.author_user_id = app.user_id())`. This is the database mirror of `curatableStory` (T-10-34, T-10-35, D-716): without it a creator could save ANOTHER member's followers-only story into their own community's highlight, and branch 3 of `stories_audience_gate` would then make it readable by the whole community, which is the audience widening WR-03 names. Items are never updated by the service, so a member UPDATE of an item is refused outright (staff only), which also stops a re-point of an existing item to another highlight or story.
5. THE REVIEW TEXT SAYS "deleted_at/deleted_by" but `stories` has no `deleted_by` column (observed in `packages/modules/stories/db/schema.ts`); the soft delete the service writes is `set deleted_at = now()` and nothing else. The guard's allow-list for the author is therefore: the two counters, plus `deleted_at` as the single transition from null to a value.
6. Migration shape: ONE file, two halves, the precedent of `20260925191156_story_highlights.sql`. Half 1 is GENERATED by `pnpm db:generate --name=member_content_write_gates` from the TS policy declarations (the six highlight and item gates, `stories_member_delete_gate`, and the changed `with check` of `feed_posts_social_insert_gate`), which is what keeps the TS schema, the snapshot and the folder honest. Half 2 is HAND-WRITTEN (functions and triggers are not modeled by Drizzle): the guard function plus its trigger, and `create or replace function app.media_asset_hidden`. Neither half calls anything the other defines, so statement order inside the file is free.

Not in scope (do not add): IN-04 (a staff UPDATE of `communities` can flip `kind`), the member-photo id-capability residual, the per-instance limiters (IN-05), any social term on UPDATE or DELETE gates (A11), any status or deleted-community term on the new gates (the service already answers the archived and removed cases before writing), and the UI findings WR-01, WR-02 and WR-06 (already fixed by commits b219a0b0, 700af570 and ffa1426a). Do NOT edit `10-VERIFICATION.md` (the orchestrator re-runs the verifier). No push, no deploy, no hosted project, no `db:two --reset --bare`.
