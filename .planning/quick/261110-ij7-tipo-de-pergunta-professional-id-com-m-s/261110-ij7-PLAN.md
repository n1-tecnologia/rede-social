## FORMAT SPEC (planner decisions, binding for all three tasks)

Where the format is stored: `limits.format` in the existing `limits` jsonb of the question (a lowercase key from `PROFESSIONAL_ID_FORMATS`). The `profile_form_questions_limits_chk` only requires a jsonb object, so no column and no extra CHECK is needed. The database does NOT check the format value; the contract and the service do (the DB change is the type CHECK only).

What is stored as the answer: the canonical masked text, uppercase, exactly as the mask displays it, in the existing `profile_form_answers.value` jsonb as a JSON string (at most 25 characters, far under the 4096-byte CHECK).

Formats, in this order (this is the order of `PROFESSIONAL_ID_FORMATS`, the builder list and the catalog):

| key | canonical text | rules (format only, never a registry lookup) | example (must pass its own judge) |
|---|---|---|---|
| cro | CRO-UF NUMERO | UF is one of the 27 real codes; number is 1 to 9 digits, leading zeros kept | CRO-SP 123456 |
| crm | CRM-UF NUMERO | same rules as cro | CRM-SP 123456 |
| crbm | CRBM-R NUMERO | region R is ONE digit 1 to 9 (CRBM has five regional councils; one permissive digit); number 1 to 9 digits | CRBM-1 12345 |
| crf | CRF-UF NUMERO | same rules as cro | CRF-SP 123456 |
| coren | COREN-UF NUMERO | same rules as cro | COREN-SP 123456 |
| crefito | CREFITO-R/NUMERO-F | region R is 1 or 2 digits, value 1 to 19, no leading zero (17 regions exist; 19 is the largest a one-digit-then-greedy typing rule can address); number 1 to 9 digits; the suffix is the fixed literal -F | CREFITO-3/123456-F |
| crbio | 000000/00-A | exactly 6 digits, "/", exactly 2 digits, "-", exactly one letter A to Z | 003566/01-D |
| cnpj | AA.AAA.AAA/AAAA-DD | ONE format: 12 characters from 0-9 and A-Z, then 2 digits; the traditional CNPJ is the all-digit case; the two check digits must match (rule below); 14 identical characters are refused | 12.345.678/0001-95 |

IMPORTANT, the CNPJ example Igor wrote, 12.345.678/0001-90, has WRONG check digits (the correct ones are 95, verified). The example constant uses 12.345.678/0001-95 so the placeholder is not itself refused; a test pins that every example passes its own judge. Report this in the SUMMARY.

The 27 UF codes: AC AL AP AM BA CE DF ES GO MA MT MS MG PA PB PR PE PI RJ RN RS RO RR SC SP SE TO.

CNPJ check digits: take the first 12 characters; the value of a character is its ASCII code minus 48 (digits 0 to 9, letters A=17 to Z=42). First digit: weights 5,4,3,2,9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2 over the 12 values; remainder of the weighted sum by 11; digit = 0 when the remainder is below 2, else 11 minus the remainder. Second digit: same method over the 12 characters plus the first digit with weights 6,5,4,3,2,9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2. Verified vectors (computed with this exact method): base 112223330001 gives 81 (11.222.333/0001-81); 123456780001 gives 95 (12.345.678/0001-95); 12ABC34501DE gives 35 (12.ABC.345/01DE-35); AB1CD2EF3GH4 gives 90 (AB.1CD.2EF/3GH4-90); A0B1C2D3E4F5 gives 08 (A0.B1C.2D3/E4F5-08); 000000000001 gives 91 (00.000.000/0001-91, the real Banco do Brasil CNPJ, valid); 112223330002 gives 62.

The engine (all in `packages/contracts/src/professional-id.ts`, pure, no Node imports, linear time, no backtracking regex):

1. Significant text: from the raw string keep only ASCII letters and digits (filter BEFORE upper-casing so a character like the German sharp s can never become letters), upper-case them. For crefito also keep the non-alphanumeric characters, because a typed "/" ends the region. Only the first 64 UTF-16 units of the raw are read.
2. Council tag: when the significant text starts with the council letters (CRO, CRM, CRF, COREN, CRBM, CREFITO, CRBIO) the tag is removed. crbio strips CRBIO; cnpj strips nothing. A UF can never be confused with a tag because a tag is 3 or more letters and a UF is exactly 2.
3. Layout into slots, dropping any character that does not fit its slot (a digit where a letter is due, a letter where a digit is due, anything beyond capacity). UF councils: 2 letters then up to 9 digits (a digit before the UF is complete is dropped). crbm: one digit then up to 9 digits. crbio: 6 digits, 2 digits, 1 letter. cnpj: 2, 3, 3, 4 characters of 0-9A-Z then 2 digits. crefito: when the text contains a "/", the region is the digits before it (at most 2 are placed) and the number is the digits after it (at most 9); without a "/", the region closes automatically after one digit that is not 1 or after two digits, and a first digit 1 stays open so 12 can be typed (a region of just 1 is closed by typing "/"). ONE trailing F that follows the number digits is the suffix, not data.
4. Literals are written lazily: a separator or the council tag is written only just before the next placed character (the mask never shows a dangling separator), with two exceptions for crefito: the suffix "-F" is written once the number has one digit, and a "/" the member typed right after the region is kept even before any number digit.
5. `layoutProfessionalId(format, raw)` returns the text, `consumed` (placed characters), `total` (significant characters after the tag and the suffix are removed) and `ends` (for each placed character, the index in the text just after it; the web uses it to keep the caret). `formatProfessionalId` is its `text`.
6. `canonicalizeProfessionalId(format, raw)` returns the canonical text or `null`: null when the raw is longer than 64 units, when `consumed` is below `total` (something would have been dropped), when a slot is incomplete (UF councils need 2 letters and at least 1 digit; crbio needs all 9 characters; crbm and crefito need region and number; cnpj needs all 14), or when a semantic rule fails (UF not real, region out of range, CNPJ check digits wrong or 14 identical characters). The result is idempotent: formatting the canonical text returns itself.
7. `isProfessionalIdText(text)` is true when `canonicalizeProfessionalId(f, text) === text` for at least one format; it feeds the loose `answerValueSchema` variant.

Layout vectors (format, raw gives text; the verified outputs of a throwaway prototype): cro S gives CRO-S; cro sp gives CRO-SP; cro SP123456 gives CRO-SP 123456; cro "CRO-SP 123456" gives itself; cro "cro sp 12" gives CRO-SP 12; cro 1SP12 gives CRO-SP 12 with consumed 4 of total 5; cro SP1234567890 gives CRO-SP 123456789 with consumed 11 of total 12; cro 123 gives the empty text; crm "crm-sp 123456" gives CRM-SP 123456; crbm 1 gives CRBM-1; crbm 112345 gives CRBM-1 12345; crefito 3 gives CREFITO-3; crefito 3123456 gives CREFITO-3/123456-F; crefito 1 gives CREFITO-1; crefito 12 gives CREFITO-12; crefito 123 gives CREFITO-12/3-F; crefito "1/" gives CREFITO-1/; crefito "1/23" gives CREFITO-1/23-F; crefito "CREFITO-3/123456-F" gives itself; crefito "CREFITO-20/1-F" gives CREFITO-20/1-F (the judge then refuses region 20); crefito "CREFITO-123/4" gives CREFITO-12/4-F with consumed 3 of total 4; crbio 003566 gives 003566; crbio 00356601D gives 003566/01-D; crbio "CRBio 003566/01-D" gives 003566/01-D; cnpj 12 gives 12; cnpj 12ABC gives 12.ABC; cnpj 12345678000195 gives 12.345.678/0001-95; cnpj 1234567800019A gives 12.345.678/0001-9 with consumed 13 of total 14.

Caret map vectors (`ends`): cro SP12 has text CRO-SP 12 and ends 5,6,8,9; crefito 3123 has text CREFITO-3/123-F and ends 9,11,12,13; cnpj 12ABC has text 12.ABC and ends 1,2,4,5,6.

Judge vectors, accept: cro "cro-sp 123456" and SP123456 give CRO-SP 123456; crm, crf, coren each accept their example and one second case (COREN-RJ 12); crbm "1 12345" gives CRBM-1 12345; crefito 3123456 and "CREFITO-17/123-F" accepted, "CREFITO-3/123456" (no suffix) gives CREFITO-3/123456-F; crbio "00356601d" gives 003566/01-D; cnpj accepts all five valid vectors above (as masked text, as 14 raw characters, and lower-case such as 12.abc.345/01de-35 giving the upper-case canonical), plus 00.000.000/0001-91.
