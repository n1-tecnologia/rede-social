# Phase 05.3 Plan 05: Reels Pager Summary

**`ReelsPager` is a props-only index pager. It pages on dominant-axis swipes, arrow keys, a locked wheel and desktop buttons, all through one `go()` that calls `onActivate` before `onIndexChange` inside the gesture. Its tap surface is `DoubleTapHeart` with new opt-in `onSingleTap` and `tapSlopPx` props, so a single tap pauses, a double tap only likes and a swipe is neither. The plan also ships `ticksWindow`, `ReelsStage` and `ReelPlaybackError` in the `@rede-social/module-reels/ui` barrel.**

## Accomplishments

- **`DoubleTapHeart` (D-124, UI-D-86).**
  - `onSingleTap` fires once when the 300 ms window closes. A second tap cancels it, so a double tap never also pauses.
  - With `tapSlopPx`, a pointerup farther than N px from its pointerdown is no tap. It resets the window and cancels any pending single tap.
  - A caller that passes neither prop (the feed's `PostMedia`) keeps its old path: no pointerdown listener and no timer. A test pins `vi.getTimerCount() === 0` for that path.
  - The docblock records why the props are opt-in and that no ancestor may use pointer capture.
- **`ticksWindow`.** A pure function that returns a 7-wide window centred on the current reel, shifted inward at either end. An edge tick is marked faint when items lie beyond it, loaded or behind `hasMore`.
- **`ReelsStage`.** A `<section aria-label>` with `data-theme="dark"`, `fixed inset-0 z-40 bg-black md:left-60` and `touch-action: none`. Its docblock records the z-order against the BottomNav, `BottomSheet` and toasts.
- **`ReelPlaybackError`.** A sized, centred block: `CircleAlert` 24, the message, and a 44 px retry pill.
- **`ReelsPager`.**
  - **Structure.** An index stack at `top: k*100%` moved by `translateY(calc(-i*100% + drag))`, with the 0.42 s settle. The slide is instant under reduced motion and for two frames after `instantKey` changes.
  - **Mounting.** Only pages within ±1 of the current one mount their media and overlay. Neighbour pages are `inert` and `aria-hidden`, and each page carries the prototype veil.
  - **Gestures.** The dominant axis decides at 60 px, and only a vertical drag follows the finger (rubber band). `pointercancel` resets without navigating, and the pager never uses pointer capture.
  - **Tap surface.** Each mounted page's media is wrapped in `DoubleTapHeart`; only the current page gets the handlers.
  - **Other controls.**
    - The overlay and top slots sit in layers that stop pointer propagation.
    - The badge, spinner and ticks are siblings of the tap surface.
    - A polite live region reads the filled position label.
    - An sr-only pause control is the first focusable element in the current page.
  - **Desktop and input.**
    - At `md` the pages sit in a 9:16 column, with ↑/↓ `IconButton`s 24 px outside its right edge.
    - ↑/↓, Space and M are handled on the window, with the sheet, field and focused-control exclusions.
    - A native non-passive wheel listener moves one video per 50 px and then locks for 600 ms.

## Decisions Made

- **The tap wrapper is rendered for every mounted page, not only the current one.** Handlers are passed only on the current page. Swapping a plain div for the wrapper when a page became current would remount the neighbour's pre-mounted video and cause the black flash that UI-D-92 rules out.
- **A travelled pointer also cancels the pending single tap before it.** "Resets the window" is read as forgetting the previous tap entirely. Otherwise a tap followed within 300 ms by a swipe would pause the video the swipe landed on.
- **Overlays and the pause control follow the mount radius.** Pages outside ±1 render nothing at all, not just no media, so an off-screen rail never builds its like engine.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Held arrow keys do not repeat navigation**
- **Found during:** Task 2.
- **Issue:** A held ↓ auto-repeats about 30 times a second. Each repeat would be a navigation and a token mint, which is the flooding that T-05.3-13 mitigates.
- **Fix:** Keydown events with `event.repeat` still `preventDefault` but move nothing. Ctrl, Meta and Alt chords are ignored, so browser shortcuts such as Cmd+M are not hijacked.
- **Files modified:** `packages/modules/reels/ui/ReelsPager.tsx`
- **Committed in:** 136bd71

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] The wheel leaves natively scrollable elements alone**
- **Found during:** Task 2.
- **Issue:** A blanket `preventDefault` on the stage's wheel would stop the expanded caption from scrolling. The caption is `max-h-[40vh] overflow-y-auto` per UI-D-88, and plan 06 builds it.
- **Fix:** A wheel event whose target sits inside an element that can still scroll in that direction is left to scroll natively. Line and page `deltaMode` values are also converted to pixels.
- **Files modified:** `packages/modules/reels/ui/ReelsPager.tsx`
- **Committed in:** 136bd71

**3. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] A mouse drag that leaves the stack is cancelled**
- **Found during:** Task 2.
- **Issue:** Without pointer capture, a mouse released outside the stack never reports its pointerup, which would leave the drag stuck.
- **Fix:** A mouse `pointerleave` during a drag resets it, like a cancel. Touch pointers are implicitly captured by the browser and are unaffected.
- **Committed in:** 136bd71

**4. [Interpretation] An extra test file, `reels-stage.test.tsx`**
- **Issue:** The plan's `<behavior>` tested only `DoubleTapHeart` and `ticksWindow` in Task 1.
- **Change:** `ReelsStage` and `ReelPlaybackError` got three cases of their own in RED, covering the landmark, the dark scope, the classes and "never full-cover".
- **Committed in:** 243f96f

**5. [Interpretation] Docblock wording changed so the acceptance grep counts are exact**
- **Issue:** Prose in two docblocks inflated the AC4 counts: "absolute inset-0" in `ReelPlaybackError` and a second `data-theme` in `ReelsStage`.
- **Fix:** The prose was reworded. The code is unchanged.
- **Committed in:** 3a27e19

**6. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Biome's `useAriaPropsSupportedByRole` and a test typing error**
- **Issue:**
  - A conditional `role` next to an unconditional `aria-labelledby` failed lint.
  - A `getAllByRole(...)[0]` failed `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`.
- **Fix:**
  - `role`, `id` and `aria-labelledby` now move together in one conditional spread (`panelProps`).
  - The test casts the element.
  - Both changes are in the GREEN commit, and the test file's cases are unchanged.
- **Committed in:** 136bd71
