<objective>
The phase's tracer: Reels exists end to end at the API, without a single screen yet.

A member of a tenant with `reels` and `feed` on opens their bootstrap and finds a Reels tab entry
(order 30, film icon, media chrome), and the feed answers `GET /v1/feed?media=video` with exactly
the ready video posts Início would show them. That one path crosses every back-end layer the phase
builds on: the `tenant_modules_key_chk` widening and its backfill, the kernel manifest's two new
fields (`requires`, `chrome`), the new `@tria/module-reels` package, the registry's `requires`
composition, and feed's one video predicate. Because the backfill only touches rows on a database
that already has tenants, it is applied to the developer's LIVE local database after a data-only
backup, which is the only place it can be proven (a fresh reset seeds after migrating).

The UI layers are deliberately not in the tracer: the prototype-less Reels surfaces wait on the D-33
review (plan 04), exactly as 05.2's tracer stayed at the API.

Purpose: prove the module boundary (no module-to-module edge, feed owns the query) and the flag
composition on the agent's best early-context tokens.
Output: the module key, migration, `@tria/module-reels` manifest package, `effectiveKeys`, the
`media` filter, the reset consent, pgTAP 130, and every API pin updated for the seventh key.

Budget note: the projection exceeds 100k on purpose. The tracer is one vertical slice (migration,
kernel, module, registry, feed, test) and cannot be thinned without shipping the key without its
backfill proof or the filter without its test. The estimate calibrator has no samples (project
memory), so the figure is uncalibrated.

Task-size note: Task 1 lists 22 paths, but nine are copied scaffold or generated files
(`package.json`, `turbo.json`, `tsconfig.json`, `vitest.config.ts`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`, the migration
and its journal and snapshot) and two are one-line dependency entries; the hand-written surface is
about ten files, each edit small. Splitting it would ship the key without the filter the tracer
exists to prove.
</objective>

   E2e specs create their own video posts (plan 09), so the pinned feed total of 33 cannot move.
