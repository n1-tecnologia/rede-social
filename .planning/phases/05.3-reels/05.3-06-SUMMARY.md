# Phase 05.3 Plan 06: Lanes, Rail and Caption Summary

**The three over-video pieces around the pager, plus the like button's over-media tone:**
- `ReelsLanes` is an ARIA tablist that disappears below two lanes.
- `ReelRail` draws avatar, host-built like, comment and share, with reserved `h-5` count slots drawn through `compactCount`.
- `ReelCaption` clamps the linkified caption by rendered lines, shows a measured "… mais", and expands into a 40vh box over a veil without pausing the video.
- `LikeButton` gains an opt-in `tone="overMedia"`; the default tone is byte-identical.

## Accomplishments

- **`ReelsLanes` (UI-D-84, D-117, D-118, D-120).**
  - With fewer than two lanes it returns `null`.
  - Otherwise it renders `absolute left-16 right-16 z-[3]` at `top-[calc(var(--safe-top)+8px)] md:top-4`, a scroller with a hidden scrollbar and `overscroll-x-contain`, and a centred `mx-auto flex w-max gap-4` tablist.
  - Each tab is `h-11 max-w-40 truncate px-1 text-sm border-b-2`: active 700 white with a white underline, idle 400 `white/70` with a transparent border. Tabs carry the text shadow and the white focus ring.
  - The tabindex is roving. Every tab's `aria-controls` is the pager's `panelId`. Arrow keys use automatic activation (focus follows the selection), Home/End jump to the ends, and nothing wraps.
  - The active tab is scrolled into view (`inline: 'center'`), instantly under reduced motion.
  - Pointers stop at the row's root.
- **`LikeButton` (D-124).**
  - New props: `tone` (`'default' | 'overMedia'`) and `glyphSize` (default 20).
  - `overMedia` sets `text-white` idle and `text-like [&_svg]:fill-like` liked on the button itself. It also adds the glyph drop shadow, a translucent white press and the white focus ring.
  - The docblock records why a wrapper override like the story viewer's `[&_button]:text-white` would repaint a liked heart white.
- **`compactCount`.** `Intl.NumberFormat(locale, { notation: 'compact', maximumFractionDigits: 1 })`. It returns `null` below 1 and for non-finite input.
- **`ReelRail` (UI-D-87, D-129, D-130).**
  - A `w-12` column at `right-2`, `bottom-[calc(var(--safe-bottom)+88px)] md:bottom-6`.
  - Items, top to bottom:
    - a 44 px plain `<a>` holding `Avatar size="md"` with `ring-2 ring-white`;
    - the host's like node over its count slot;
    - comment (`MessageCircle` 28) over its count slot;
    - share (`Send` 28), last, and only when `onShare` is given.
  - Count slots are always rendered, are `aria-hidden`, and stay empty at `null`.
- **`ReelCaption` (UI-D-88, D-129, D-131).**
  - When expanded, a veil (`absolute inset-0 z-[2] bg-black/50`) renders before the `z-[3]` block. It fades in over 200 ms via `starting:opacity-0`, with no transition under reduced motion.
  - The author is a truncating 16/700 plain link.
  - The chip is a 44 px `<a>` around a `h-7` pill, named by the host's aria label.
  - The caption paragraph uses `whitespace-pre-wrap break-words`, with a white/bold/underline link override.
  - Collapsed, the paragraph has `line-clamp-2`. "… mais" appears only when a layout-effect or ResizeObserver measure finds `scrollHeight > clientHeight`.
  - Expanded, the paragraph is `max-h-[40vh] overflow-y-auto overscroll-contain` with `touch-action: pan-y` and ends with an inline "menos".
  - The component never slices the text and has no pause callback.

## Decisions Made

- **Re-selecting the active lane is a no-op.** Otherwise a tap on the current lane would make the host reset and refetch the list the member is watching.
- **Over-media press and focus.** The over-media tone and the rail's comment/share buttons replace `IconButton`'s grey hover disc and brand focus ring with a translucent white press and the white ring (UI-D-97). The grey disc on video is the same defect the tone exists to fix.
- **Measure only while collapsed.** When expanded, the 40vh box may itself overflow, and that is scrolling, not a reason to offer "mais". The effect also re-runs on `children`, because new text can overflow at an unchanged clamped height, and no resize reports that.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Held arrow keys do not repeat a lane selection**
- **Found during:** Task 1.
- **Issue:** Each lane selection makes the host load a lane, and an auto-repeating arrow would fire a load per repeat. This is the flooding T-05.3-13 guards against for the pager's own keys.
- **Fix:** Keydown events with `event.repeat` are `preventDefault`ed but select nothing.
- **Files modified:** `packages/modules/reels/ui/ReelsLanes.tsx`
- **Committed in:** 7384e89

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Over-media hover and focus classes**
- **Found during:** Task 1 and Task 2.
- **Issue:** `IconButton`'s base classes put a `hover:bg-bg-hover` grey disc and a `ring-brand` focus ring with a `ring-offset-bg` halo on every control. Over video that is a grey blot and brand ink, which UI-D-39/UI-D-97 rule out.
- **Fix:** The over-media tone and the rail's comment and share buttons override these with `hover:bg-white/10 active:bg-white/20 focus-visible:ring-white focus-visible:ring-offset-0`. The default `LikeButton` tone is unchanged.
- **Files modified:** `packages/modules/feed/ui/LikeButton.tsx`, `packages/modules/reels/ui/ReelRail.tsx`
- **Committed in:** 7384e89, fabeecf

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] Test-harness fixes in the Task 2 GREEN commit**
- **Found during:** Task 2 GREEN.
- **Issue:**
  - The Write tool had turned the ` ` escapes in `reel-rail.test.tsx` into literal U+00A0 characters in the RED commit. The plan requires the escape to be spelled out (the AC5 grep), and a literal NBSP is invisible in review.
  - `toHaveTextContent` folds U+00A0 into a plain space, so the correct "8 mil" failed.
  - The ResizeObserver callback's state update ran outside `act()`.
- **Fix:**
  - The escapes are restored as ` `, and `́` in the caption test.
  - The slot's raw `textContent` is asserted.
  - The observer callback is wrapped in `act()`.
  - No case's intent changed.
- **Files modified:** `packages/modules/reels/tests/reel-rail.test.tsx`, `packages/modules/reels/tests/reel-caption.test.tsx`
- **Committed in:** fabeecf

**4. [Interpretation] Extra cases beyond `<behavior>`**
- **Added:**
  - The lanes: layout classes, ArrowLeft from the last tab, pointer isolation, and scroll-into-view in both motion modes.
  - The rail: the 44 px avatar ring, share calling `onShare`, and 28 px glyphs.
  - The caption: the empty string treated as no text, the tap on text toggling both ways, the resize re-measure, the link override and pointer isolation.
- **Why:** They pin UI-D-84/87/88 truths the plan lists but `<behavior>` does not enumerate.

**5. [Interpretation] Styling details not fixed by the plan**
- **Hidden scrollbar:** `scrollbar-none` is kept for precedent (`StoriesStrip`, `Tabs`). `[scrollbar-width:none]` is added so the scrollbar is hidden even if no `scrollbar-none` utility is defined.
- **`top`/`bottom` offsets:** these are Tailwind arbitrary classes rather than inline styles, so the `md:top-4`/`md:bottom-6` classes can override them. An inline style would always win.
