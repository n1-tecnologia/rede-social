<objective>
Playback and data for Reels in the app tier: the video element, the one batched token mint, and the
web half of the feed's video list.

`ReelVideo` is the full-bleed vendor element with no vendor chrome, muted at mount, looping, fitted by
aspect ratio, and immune to the muted preference other players persist; it hands the host a
controller the pager's gesture calls synchronously, and reports refusals and errors upward without
ever showing a raw message. The server actions give the host a page of Reels (as ready-to-render
views that reuse the feed card's own mapping) and mint up to three playback credentials in one
round trip, because Next queues server actions and a per-video mint would stall behind likes.

Purpose: the playback half of REELS-06 and the data half of REELS-03/05, ready for the host (plan 08).
Output: `ReelVideo` with its test, `reels-actions.ts` with its test, `lib/reels.ts` with its test, and
`getFeed`'s `media` parameter.
</objective>
