## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **WARNING — Caption "… mais" / "menos" toggles are about 21px tall** (`packages/modules/reels/ui/ReelCaption.tsx:176-199`, `TOGGLE` = `text-sm font-bold text-white/70` with no min-height). They are the keyboard and assistive path to expanding the caption, and they fail the 44px rule every other Reels control meets. Tapping the caption text also toggles, which softens this for pointer users. **Fix:** add `min-h-11 inline-flex items-center` to the absolute "… mais" button. For the inline "menos", add `py-3 -my-3` so the line height stays the same.
2. **WARNING — The active-lane underline is detached from the label** (`packages/modules/reels/ui/ReelsLanes.tsx:142-147`: `h-11 border-b-2` on the button). The 2px white border draws at the bottom of the 44px hit box, so the gap between the text and the underline is about 13-15px. The print shows a tight underline. **Fix:** keep the button at `h-11`, remove its border, and put the border on an inner `<span className="border-b-2 pb-1">` around the label, so the hit area and the drawn underline are separate. Confirm against `reels-design.png`.
3. **WARNING — The empty-state CTA is hand-rolled instead of using the `Button` primitive** (`apps/web/components/reels/ReelsHost.tsx:921-925`: a `<Link>` with inline `bg-brand … rounded-xl h-11 px-5`). If the shared Button's brand variant, radius or hover changes, this CTA will drift from the rest of the product. **Fix:** render it with the shared Button's link/`asChild` form (variant brand, size md), as the stage-error retry already does with `Button variant="outline"`.

Minor: replace `right-[3px]` with `right-1` on the tick column (`ReelsPager.tsx:523`), as the UI-checker suggested. Also, `ReelsHost.tsx` is 1,029 lines, which makes future visual changes risky to review. This is not a UI defect.

## Applied (quick 260927-ebk, 2026-09-27)

The scores above are the audit's and were not re-rated after these fixes.

1. **Fix 1 applied** (`packages/modules/reels/ui/ReelCaption.tsx`). "… mais" gets its hit area from a pseudo-element (`after:absolute after:inset-x-0 after:-inset-y-3`), and "menos" gets a 24 px upward pad (`pt-6 -mt-6`). Both reach 44 px, and neither the layout nor the expanded caption's scroll range changes. These differ from the suggested classes on purpose: `min-h-11` on the bottom-anchored "… mais" would lift the label or stretch the gradient, and `py-3 -my-3` on "menos", which sits on the scroll container's last line, would add scroll overflow. Test: `reel-caption.test.tsx` › '"… mais" and "menos" reach a 44 px hit area without moving their label (UI-REVIEW fix 1)'.
2. **Fix 2 applied** (`packages/modules/reels/ui/ReelsLanes.tsx`). The underline is now drawn by an inner label span (`border-b-2 pb-1`, plus `truncate min-w-0`). `h-11` and the tab semantics stay on the button. Tests: `reels-lanes.test.tsx` › 'the active tab is white 700 with the underline on its label span; an idle tab is white/70 with a transparent one (UI-REVIEW fix 2)' and 'a 60-character label is the full accessible name and truncates at max-w-40'.
3. **Fix 3 applied** (`apps/web/components/reels/ReelsHost.tsx`). The empty-state CTA is now `LinkButton` (brand, md) to `/criar`, the codebase's Button-as-Link, since `Button` has no `asChild`. It keeps the white focus ring with no offset. Test: `ReelsHost.test.tsx` › 'an author gets "Criar publicação" to /criar in the empty state'.

The Minor items (the `right-[3px]` / `w-[3px]` ticks and the size of `ReelsHost.tsx`) were not applied.

## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (4/4)
- `apps/web/messages/pt-BR/reels.json` has every `reels.*` key in the contract table: nav, region, lanes.label/all, sound.unmute/mute, play/pause, rail.author, caption.less, position, previous/next, empty.title/body and errors.load/retry/playback/loadMore. The strings match exactly.
- Reused keys are used, not duplicated: the "mais" toggle uses `feed.caption.more`, the empty CTA uses `feed.empty.cta`, and the actions and counts use `feed.*`.
- A grep for literal JSX text and literal `aria-label="…"` in the Reels TSX found nothing.
- There are no generic labels ("OK", "Submit"). The error copy is specific and each message has its own retry.

### Pillar 2: Visuals (3/4)
- Hierarchy follows the print. Author 16/700, then the chip (12/700 on `bg-white/20`), then the caption (14/400) in `ReelCaption.tsx:140-170`. The rail order is avatar, like, comment, share with 28px glyphs and `ring-2 ring-white` on the avatar (`ReelRail.tsx`).
- Icon-only controls all have names: the sound button, rail IconButtons with `label`, the desktop chevrons, and the focus-alias pause control (`ReelsPager.tsx:446`).
- Media chrome is declared by the manifest (`BottomNav.tsx:83` `data-theme="dark"`, TopBar suppressed), as UI-D-81 specifies.
- Defect: the lane underline offset (Fix 2).
- The user passed visual fidelity against the print in UAT on 2026-09-27. That pass is noted, but this audit could not confirm it with screenshots.

### Pillar 3: Color (4/4)
- `bg-brand` appears in one place (`ReelsHost.tsx:923`, the author-only empty CTA), which is the single accent allowed on this surface. Caption links override linkify's brand colour with `[&_a]:text-white` (`ReelCaption.tsx:167`).
- Hardcoded colours are limited to the spec's veil gradient (`ReelsPager.tsx:142`) and the shipped text shadow `rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`. Both are declared **[proto]** values.
- Focus rings over media are `ring-white` throughout, not `ring-brand`.

### Pillar 4: Typography (4/4)
- Sizes: `text-sm` ×6, `text-xs` ×2, `text-base` ×1. Weights: `font-bold` ×8, `font-normal` ×2. The prototype's 11px text and 500/600 weights were correctly normalised to 12/700.

### Pillar 5: Spacing (3/4)
- These match the spec: `right-2` rail, `gap-4` rail and lanes, `left-16 right-16` lanes, `right-[72px]` caption, `bottom-[calc(var(--safe-bottom)+88px)]`, `top-[calc(var(--safe-top)+8px)]`, the desktop column `md:h-[calc(var(--screen-h)-48px)] md:max-w-[calc(100%-160px)] md:mt-6`, and the chevrons at `left-[calc(100%+24px)]`.
- Off-grid: `right-[3px]` and `w-[3px]` in `ReelsPager.tsx:523,530`. The spec allows these as **[proto]**, but they were flagged at checker time and are still unchanged.
- The underline gap (Fix 2) is a spacing symptom as well.

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (3/4)
- The Reels screen handles every state it needs: the route `loading.tsx` spinner, lane-level loading (`ReelsHost.tsx:~955`), empty (with/without the lane row per D-120), stage error with retry, the per-video `ReelPlaybackError` pill (h-11), a load-more toast, a like-revert toast, the paused/blocked 56px badge, and reduced-motion variants (`motion-reduce:*`).
- The desktop chevrons are `disabled:opacity-40` at the ends. The sr-only pause control meets WCAG 2.2.2.
- Defect: the caption toggle touch targets (Fix 1).
- Already dispositioned and not re-scored: WR-04 (trackLike residual) and WR-05 (multi-pointer gesture) are being fixed in a quick task, and WR-06 is deferred to the phone UAT.
