# Phase 12.1 Plan 13: Pure web logic of steps and conditions Summary

**Pure TypeScript for the member's stepper and the condition sentence: a visibility rule that delegates to the contracts, a visible-aware form state with a step scope, the step path (first, returning, edit), the erase list and the pt-BR disjunction, all unit-tested and rendering nothing.**

## Accomplishments

- The form state sees only what the member sees (D-631, Pitfall 10): `flaggedIds`, `validateAll`, `wireAnswers` and `mergeFreshForm` skip hidden questions, a revealed required one is judged and sent, and every existing call (no `scope`) keeps working unchanged. `mergeFreshForm` keeps typed drafts of surviving questions, returns the fresh form (so `stepId`, `condition` and `steps` come with it) and flags a newly required question only while it is visible.
- `visibleQuestionIds(form, drafts?)` reads openers from the drafts (converted by the same `toWire` the save uses, then `answerTokens`) or from the stored answers; every row of `PROFILE_FORM_VISIBILITY_CASES` gives the table's verdict through it (17 parity tests).
- The member's path: `stepPath` skips steps whose questions are all hidden and empty steps (D-628), the returning mode lists only steps with a visible, required, unanswered question (D-630, UI-D-657), `showSteps` and `showProgress` keep a form that does not need steps looking like Phase 12 (UI-D-653), and `firstStepWithMissing` picks the step to jump to after Concluir (D-629).
- `answersToErase` returns exactly `{ id, title }` for each question with a stored answer that the draft closes, in form order (D-626); a test asserts the exact keys and that the answer text never appears in the output (T-12.1-11).
- The readers' two rules: `storedVisibleQuestionIds` for "Minhas respostas" and `isNotApplicable` for the staff "Nao se aplica" (D-634, UI-D-659).
- `describeCondition` and `formatDisjunction` give the builder row and any reader the values of the condition sentence ("A", "B" ou "C") through `Intl.ListFormat`.
