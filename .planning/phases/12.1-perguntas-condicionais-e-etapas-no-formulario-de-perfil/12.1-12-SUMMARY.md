## Accomplishments

- `staffAnswersSchema` rows gain `condition: profileFormConditionSchema.nullable()` (strict kept); `answers.ts` selects `condition_question_id` and `condition_values` in the staff query only and returns `condition` or null. `publicAnswersSchema` and the public query have no hunk in the diff. A contract test also asserts that a public row with a `condition` key is still refused.
- Tracer: a member answers an opener, an ACTIVE and an ARCHIVED public dependent, closes them through the real save route; the store, the public block (another member reads it, by id and value) and the staff view carry neither answer, the staff row lists the active dependent unanswered with its condition, a bystander's three answers are untouched, and a positive control read before the change shows the answers.
- Parity: 18 integration cases (one per `PROFILE_FORM_VISIBILITY_CASES` row) plus a table sanity case. Each seeds an opener of the right type, a conditional REQUIRED question and the stored opener answer through the admin SQL, then asserts `isQuestionVisible` over `answerTokens`, `app.profile_form_missing` (1 exactly when visible) and `app.profile_form_purge_closed()` under the member's claims (deletes the dependent exactly when hidden, never the opener) all equal `row.expected`.
- CSV: after the closure the header (one column per question, archived included) and the row count are unchanged, Ana's closed cells are empty, her opener cell reads the new choice, a neighbour's cells are intact, and two exports are byte-identical.
- Readers: the public block, the staff view, the admin summary and the member form read return identical payloads twice; a purge with nothing to delete leaves rows and `updated_at` unchanged; a held save (claims, `lock_active_v2`, upsert, purge) is read as the whole old state, then after commit the whole new state; readers hammering the staff view during the real save route never see the new opener with the old closed answer.
- Concurrency: condition edit and step move, each in both commit orders (member holds then admin waits, admin holds then member waits), the admin refused `condition_locked` after the member's answer, the member refused `form_changed` after the admin's edit, retries consistent; six rounds of real routes raced (only 200 or 409 `form_changed`, never 500 or `40P01`); three rounds of two saves of one member racing.
- Move copy: two steps, an opener (highest uuid), an active and an archived conditional (lowest uuids) and three answers are copied; the planner raises no `unsortable_tables`; steps, questions (step and condition resolved inside the target) and answers match; a second copy inserts nothing.
- Fan-out: `Phase 12.1 (PFORM-40)` brings a project at the Phase 12 head with questions, an archived one and answers to head: every question has the one default step titled `PROFILE_FORM_DEFAULT_STEP_TITLE`, a tenant without questions gets no step, answers are identical (ids, values, timestamps), a legacy-shaped insert lands in the same step, the ledger lists the three versions (read from the files), the project is `current`, a second run applies nothing, and the helper, default-step, `_v2` lock and purge functions exist.
