# Phase 12.1 Plan 17: The builder's conditions, markers, refusals and impact reading Summary

**An admin can now make a question conditional from its sheet (native Etapa select, a switch, only legal openers grouped by step, Sim/Não or checkbox answers), sees the dependency web in the list ("Condicional" with its sentence, "Abre N perguntas"), is shown a list of dependents whenever an opener edit is refused, and can only remove, never change, the condition of an answered question, after reading how many members it will newly ask.**

## FLAG FOR IGOR: sketch finding F-9 (wording)

The UI-SPEC sentence for the step field refusal of an OPENER repeats `{dependent}`:

> “{dependent}” depende desta pergunta, então ela precisa ficar na etapa de “{dependent}” ou em uma antes.

I used it **verbatim** (`profileForm.admin.editor.step.errors.mustPrecede`, pinned in `profile-form-12-1-conditions-messages.test.ts`) and did not reword it. It reads as "the dependent depends on this question, so this question must stay in the dependent's step or earlier", which is hard to parse because the same title appears twice, and it is also slightly wrong in substance: a question moved to another step lands at the END of that step, so if the dependent is in the target step the opener would come after it. The client check therefore only accepts a step strictly BEFORE every dependent's step (the server's D-623 is the authority), while the sentence says "na etapa de ... ou em uma antes". A suggested rewrite for Igor to approve: "“{dependent}” depende desta pergunta, então ela precisa ficar em uma etapa antes da de “{dependent}”." Nothing was changed without his word.

## Accomplishments

- **Condition editor** (`ConditionEditor.tsx`, `editor-state.ts`, `QuestionEditorSheet.tsx`): the block "Onde e quando aparece" follows the two switches. `eligibleOpeners` offers only ACTIVE dropdown, multiple choice, single choice or yes/no questions that carry no condition, come earlier than the question (a moved or new question is measured at the end of its step), are not the question itself, and are not archived; the list of opener types is the contract's `PROFILE_FORM_CONDITION_OPENER_TYPES` (no second copy). `validateCondition` gives the two inline messages; `checkStepChange` is the D-623 snap-back. Saving sends `condition` and `stepId` through the existing create, update and replace actions; a 409 `condition_invalid` shows the stale line, clears the choice and refreshes.
- **Markers and opener block** (`QuestionList.tsx`): one neutral pill per row and the "Só aparece se ..." line (`describeCondition` plus `formatDisjunction`; yes/no as plain unquoted words), also in the accessible description; the archived list is unchanged. An opener's sheet shows "Esta pergunta abre N perguntas:" with up to five titles, "e mais N" and the hint, and no condition editor (an opener has no condition).
- **DependentsAlert** (`DependentsAlert.tsx`): `role=alert`, the 09.4 geometry, 14/700 title, 12/400 hint, plain `ul` named "Perguntas que dependem desta", up to 5 then "e mais N". The sheet pre-checks archive, delete and retype ("Trocar tipo" no longer opens the grid for an opener) from the list the page holds and renders the same alert from the server's 409 `dependents` (actions now carry the list on `conflict` and on a new `hasDependents` remove result). `OptionsEditor` shows `Split` 14 in place of remove for a used option, the count caption, the sr-only text and the note; rename and reorder still work (asserted: ids stay stable). `ArchivedList` toasts "Para reativar ... reative antes ..." and opens no dialog, including for the server's `condition_invalid` race.
- **Answered questions and impact** (`LockedCondition` in `ConditionEditor.tsx`, `ImpactDialog.tsx`): with answers and no condition the switch is a `Lock` row with the ICU plural and the shipped replace button; with a condition it shows the sentence, the line and a ghost "Remover condição" that only marks the draft ("Desfazer" restores it). Removing the condition of a REQUIRED question reads `GET /impact` with `change=remove_condition` first (asserted by call order), a failed read writes nothing and toasts, the brand dialog "Remover a condição?" opens only for `n > 0`, and the write sends `condition: null`. Every add, make-required, reactivate and replace dialog of a required conditional question ends with "Só conta quem vê esta pergunta: ...", and the create/replace read carries `conditionQuestionId` and `conditionValues` (comma-joined by the action).
- **Catalog**: every UI-SPEC string verbatim under `profileForm.admin` (`editor.where/step/condition/opens`, `editor.options.used*`, `row.*`, `dependents.*`, `archived.openerArchived`, `impact.unconditional*`, `impact.conditionalNote`), pinned string by string through the real formatter in `profile-form-12-1-conditions-messages.test.ts`.

### Plan-vs-reality notes (not rule deviations)

- **Top "Adicionar pergunta" and the Etapa select.** The select shows the last step by default but the create body omits `stepId` unless the admin changed it or opened the sheet from a well, because plan 16's test (and D-629) say the top button names no step.
- **Sheet without the question list.** `questions` is optional; without it the sheet shows no condition block (the Phase 12 tests keep their markup). The builder always passes it.
- **Make required and remove the condition in one save** reads the `make_required` impact (the stored condition), not `remove_condition`; the plan only specifies the required-stays-required removal. Rare combination, no data risk (the server recomputes on write).
- **Changing (not removing) a condition on a required question** reads no impact; the plan scopes the impact to removal, and a question that already has answers cannot change its condition anyway.
- **Used option.** Because a used option has no remove button at all, its "A opção ... abre outras perguntas" alert only appears from the server's 409 (a race on an option the page did not know was used); asserted that way.
- **Typecheck.** The Task 3 `<verify>` runs `pnpm turbo typecheck`, which triggers a `next build`; per the run's rules I ran `next typegen && tsc --noEmit` instead (exit 0).
