# Phase 12.1: Perguntas condicionais e etapas no formulário de perfil - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-05
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The tenant admin of the profile form (Phase 12) gains two things. First, a question can be CONDITIONAL: the admin says "show this question only if [an earlier question] was answered [with one of these options]", so that one answer opens further questions. The first client's case: "Do you own a clinic, work in a clinic, or are you learning?" and only "I own a clinic" opens the clinic questions. Second, the form is split into STEPS (grouped questions): the member answers one step at a time and moves on with "Avançar".

This phase delivers: the model (steps, the condition on a question), the SQL gate that ignores a required question the member does not see, the changed save (per step), the admin builder (steps, condition editor, preview that follows both), and the member screens (stepper on first fill and on return; one scroll with step titles on edit from the profile).

What this phase does NOT deliver: conditions on a whole step, chains of conditions (A opens B opens C), conditions that read a number, date, text, acceptance or rating answer, a "go to step" jump or an early end of the form, a notification when the form changes, state/city and file answer types (still deferred from Phase 12), anything in the "Rede social" phases 10-11.

Origin: the team asked on 2026-11-05 for "an option on multiple choice or yes/no questions that works as a condition on the answer", and for "splitting into steps, groups of questions to answer and advance", because the first client's profile questions have a block that only opens for a given answer that is not yes/no.

This phase REVERSES D-582 of Phase 12 (one scrollable screen, no sections) and takes "question sections and conditional questions" out of Phase 12's deferred list.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The condition rule
- **D-620:** Only a question of type dropdown, multiple choice, single choice or yes/no can OPEN other questions. Number, date, text, phone, e-mail, link, acceptance and rating never open anything. — **Reversibility:** reversible — widening the list later is one more validator, no table change.
- **D-621:** The condition lives on the question that APPEARS (the "shown" question), not on the opener and not on the step: "show only if [opener question] is [option, or any of these options]". It is stored as the opener's question id plus the set of option ids (for yes/no, the set holds the answer true or false). For a multiple-choice opener the shown question appears when the member ticked ANY option of the set. A step whose questions are all hidden disappears by itself (D-628). — **Reversibility:** costly — the columns, the contract schema and the SQL gate all read this shape.
- **D-622:** ONE LEVEL only: the opener must itself be always visible (it carries no condition), so a shown question never depends on another shown question. The gate stays a plain join with no recursion. — **Reversibility:** reversible — widening to a chain changes the validation rule and the SQL, not the tables.
- **D-623:** The opener must come BEFORE the shown question: in the same step at an earlier position, or in an earlier step. A write or a reorder that would put the opener after the question it opens is refused with a coded error. Otherwise the member would face a question whose opener they have not answered yet. — **Reversibility:** reversible.

### What happens to answers that stop applying
- **D-624:** When the member changes the opener's answer so that some shown questions close, the answers of those closed questions are DELETED in the same transaction that saves the opener (same save, same snapshot). What is stored is always what the member sees. If they go back, they answer again. Because nothing hidden is ever stored, the public answers block, the staff view, the CSV and the impact count need NO visibility filter and a stale answer cannot leak to other members. — **Reversibility:** costly — "stored equals visible" is a promise to members and removes the filter from every reader; reversing it means adding the visibility filter to each read path and the privacy tests of D-593.
- **D-625:** A question that already has answers cannot GAIN or CHANGE its condition (a new database lock in the family of PF001..PF005, same path as D-587: create a new question and archive the old one). REMOVING a condition is allowed at any time: it widens who sees the question, discards nothing, and when the question is required it reopens the form for members who lack the answer (D-585), with the impact dialog counting them. — **Reversibility:** costly — one more trigger code and a service rule; loosening it means deciding what to do with answers that no longer apply.
- **D-626:** The member is never surprised by D-624: before a save that would delete answers (an opener changed on "Avançar" or on "Salvar"), the screen tells them which answers will go. The wording and shape (confirmation dialog versus inline notice) are left to the UI-SPEC and its sketch; the constraint is that no answer is deleted without the member having been told in that interaction. — **Reversibility:** reversible.

### Steps
- **D-627:** A STEP is a tenant-owned row with a title and a position; EVERY question belongs to exactly one step. The questions that exist when this ships are moved into one default step, and a form with a single step renders as today: one screen, no progress bar. The builder shows steps as cards holding their questions and allows moving a question between steps. — **Reversibility:** costly — a new table, a column on the questions, a backfill migration that fans out to every tenant project (09.2, 09.4), and the builder's shape.
- **D-628:** A step with no visible question for this member is skipped and does not count in the progress ("Etapa 2 de 3" is computed over the steps the member actually has). — **Reversibility:** reversible.
- **D-629:** "Avançar" validates the step and SAVES it. Closing the app mid-form returns the member to the first step that still has something missing. The save must therefore stop refusing "a required question still unanswered" for questions OUTSIDE the saved step: today `saveAnswers` recounts `app.profile_form_missing_self()` after writing and refuses the whole save when anything required is missing (Phase 12 member.ts, D-585 recount). The step save keeps the per-value validation and the `formEtag` conflict check, drops that recount for other steps, and reports `missingRequired`; the hard gate (D-581) still releases only when nothing visible and required is missing, so the member leaves the form only after the last step closes. — **Reversibility:** costly — this changes the contract of `PUT /v1/me/profile-form/answers` that Phase 12 published and its tests; Phase 13's native shell reuses these web screens, so it inherits the change.
- **D-630:** EDIT from the profile ("Minhas respostas") is ONE scrollable screen with every step stacked under its title and a single "Salvar", not the stepper. RETURN after the admin adds a required question shows only the steps that have something missing for that member. First fill is the stepper. — **Reversibility:** reversible — presentation only.

### Effects on the Phase 12 rules
- **D-631:** D-585 is extended, not replaced: completeness is still computed and never stored. A question counts as owed when it is active, required, has no answer row AND is VISIBLE to the member: it has no condition, or its opener has an answer row whose value is one of the condition's options. The rule stays one SQL function (`app.profile_form_missing`) behind the same `_gate` twins, one round trip, no per-type logic and no recursion (D-622). A tenant with the module off still pays one primary-key probe. — **Reversibility:** costly — the SQL function is the hot path of every request and its twins are published to every tenant project.
- **D-632:** A question that still OPENS others cannot be archived, cannot change its type and cannot lose an option that a condition references (even an option with no answer). The service refuses with a coded 409 that carries the list of dependent questions; the admin removes the condition or archives the dependents first. Reactivating a shown question whose opener is archived is refused the same way. — **Reversibility:** reversible — it is a refusal; relaxing it later is a service rule.
- **D-633:** Limits: the 30 active questions of Phase 12 stay (the first client's form fits), plus a maximum of 10 steps per form. The number lives in `PROFILE_FORM_LIMITS` and in a database CHECK that reads it, like the others. If the client's list grows past 30 the limit moves in a small migration. — **Reversibility:** reversible.
- **D-634:** The admin impact dialog ("X members will have to answer") counts only members who SEE the question: the opener's answer matches the condition and they lack the answer. The CSV keeps one column per question (archived included) with an empty cell where a question never applied. The public answers block, the staff view and the "Minhas respostas" section need no new filter (D-624). — **Reversibility:** reversible.
- **D-635:** The sketch gate applies (UI-04): sketch 016 has no steps and no condition editor, and sketch 017 belongs to Phase 13, so this phase needs a new sketch (018) of the screens it adds: the builder with step cards, the condition editor on a question, the dependent badge and the refusal lists, the stepper, the delete-answers warning, the one-scroll edit with titles, and the admin preview following steps and conditions. The executor never writes the approval. — **Reversibility:** reversible.
- **D-636:** The purge of D-624 reaches ARCHIVED dependent questions too: when a member changes the opener's answer, the stored answers of every closed shown question are deleted, active or archived, in the same transaction. The member cannot see an archived question (RLS), so it cannot be confirmed on screen; D-626 lists only the ACTIVE titles. This is a deliberate exception to D-588 ("archived answers are kept"), narrowed to answers that no longer apply and only as a consequence of the member's own act. Reactivating an archived question under an opener never needs a bulk delete, because nothing that does not match is ever stored. Decided by Igor on 2026-11-05 (research A1, open question 1). — **Reversibility:** costly — same promise as D-624 (stored equals visible); keeping archived answers would add the visibility filter to every reader and a bulk delete on reactivation.

### Claude's Discretion
- Whether the "Outro" option of a choice question can open questions (default: no, the set holds real option ids only).
- How the "Há perguntas novas" notice (D-586) counts a new OPTIONAL conditional question: for members whose opener answer does not match it is not "new for them".
- Whether the CSV header or a column carries the step title; whether a step has an optional description under its title.
- The exact database codes (next after PF005), the refusal codes added to `PROFILE_FORM_ISSUES`, and the shape of the dependents list in the 409.
- The default step's name, step reorder ergonomics (drag between steps, move up/down), whether an empty step can exist in the builder (a step with no question is never shown to members).
- How `formEtag` covers steps and conditions so a member whose form changed under them gets the same `form_changed` conflict (D-629).
- Progress indicator style, Voltar behaviour (back keeps what was saved), animation of a question appearing after the opener is answered.
- pt-BR copy of every new string, in the existing `profileForm.*.json` catalogs.
- Requirement ids: the planner adds PFORM-27 onwards for steps, conditions, the gate extension and the per-step save, and maps them in REQUIREMENTS.md.

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `app.profile_form_missing(tenant, membership)` and its `_self` and `_gate` twins: the one place the gate rule lives; D-631 changes its third branch only.
- `profile_form_question_guard` trigger and `lock-rules.ts`: the pattern for D-625 and D-632 (a trigger code as backstop, a service check first, a coded 409).
- `parseAnswer` and the discriminated answer schema: unchanged; conditions read the stored answer value, they do not add a type.
- `PROFILE_FORM_ISSUES` and the pt-BR map: where the new refusal codes go.
- The builder's `QuestionList`, `QuestionEditorSheet`, `OptionsEditor`, `PreviewSheet` and `ImpactDialog`: the surfaces that gain steps, the condition editor and visibility-aware counts.
- `formEtagOf` and the `form_changed` 409 that carries the fresh form: the conflict path for a form edited while a member is mid-step.

### Established Patterns
- Completeness is computed, never stored (D-585); a gate never mutates membership state or revokes the session (D-498).
- Answers are kept by the database: ON DELETE RESTRICT on the question, triggers for the locks (D-587, D-588). D-624 is the one deliberate exception, and only for answers that no longer apply, only as a consequence of the member's own change.
- Tenant-owned rows carry `tenant_id` first in every index with RLS on every table; a new table gets the same policy shape as `profile_form_questions` (active rows readable by the three real roles, written only by `admin_tenant`).
- Migrations are expand-only with the old function twins left for one release; hand SQL goes in `--custom` migrations; the schema source of truth is Drizzle.
- Screens the prototype lacks go through a UI-SPEC and a sketch gate (UI-04); all strings come from the pt-BR catalog.

### Integration Points
- `requireAuth`: the visibility join runs inside the existing single membership lookup; it must not add a round trip nor grow for a tenant with the module off.
- `GET /v1/me/profile-form` and `PUT /v1/me/profile-form/answers`: the member form gains steps and the conditions of each question; the save gains a step scope.
- `/v1/profile_form` admin routes: steps (create, rename, reorder, delete when empty), the condition on a question, the dependents refusal.
- `/me/bootstrap` block `profileForm.pendingRequired`: counts visible required questions only.
- The `completar-perfil` screen and `/perfil/editar`: stepper versus one scroll.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The first client's question, in the team's words: whether the person "tem clínica, trabalha em clínica ou está aprendendo"; only "tem clínica" opens the new questions. It is a single choice with three options, not a yes/no, which is why the condition is on an option and not on a boolean.
- The team's phrasing of the yes/no case: if the answer is yes, new questions open; if no, the form goes on to the next question. Same mechanism: the condition on the shown question reads "yes".
- The questions that came from the client (via the team) are not in the repository. The planner should ask Igor for the list, or build the seed and the e2e on the clinic example, and must not invent the client's wording.
- Style: mobile-first in the prototype's language (Manrope, light ground, white cards, 12px radii, 44px touch targets, the tenant's brand as the single accent), pt-BR only; a question that opens should appear in place, right after its opener.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- A condition on a WHOLE step ("show the step 'Sua clínica' only if..."), the Google Forms style jump. Rejected for now in favour of the condition on the question (D-621); revisit if admins repeat the same condition on many questions of one step.
- Chains of conditions (A opens B opens C), up to a few levels. Rejected for the hot path (D-622); widening is a rule change, not a table change.
- Conditions that read a number, date, text, acceptance or rating answer.
- Keeping the closed answers hidden (and restoring them if the member goes back) instead of deleting them (D-624). Rejected for privacy and simplicity; would need the visibility filter on every reader.
- A "go to step" jump or an early end of the form.
- Notifying members (notification center or Web Push) when the admin changes the form.
- State/city and file/photo answer types (deferred since Phase 12).

None of the above was a todo; no pending todo matched this phase.
