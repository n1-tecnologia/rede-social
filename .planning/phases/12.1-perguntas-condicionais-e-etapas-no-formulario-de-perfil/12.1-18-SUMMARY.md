# Phase 12.1 Plan 18: Admin preview follows steps and conditions Summary

**"Como o membro vê" now runs the member's own stepper over the admin's live questions: Avançar works with no answers and calls nothing, a role=status line counts the steps and questions a member would see under the ticked answers, "Recomeçar" clears the preview, and the last step ends in the outline "Fechar".**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 25 min
- **Completed:** 2026-11-05
- **Tasks:** 2 (1 tracer, 1 auto)
- **Files modified:** 6 (2 created, 4 modified)

## Accomplishments

- `ProfileForm` preview mode uses the stepper when the form has two or more steps (`showSteps`), otherwise Phase 12's single card byte for byte. In the preview "Avançar" and "Voltar" only change the current step (no `validateAll`, no flagged state, no erase dialog, no action), Enter in a field advances the same way, and the last step shows the outline "Fechar" (with "Voltar" beside it when there is a previous step).
- `PreviewSheet` adds the extra banner sentence, a `role="status"` 12/400 line with `ListChecks` 16 ("Com estas respostas, o membro vê {steps} e {questions}.", ICU plurals) and a ghost `Button size="sm"` with `RotateCcw` 16 "Recomeçar" (44px hit area, `text-text`, not brand). Counts come from the same `walkPath` / `visibleQuestionIds` the member uses, so a step that appears or is skipped shows in the count and in "Etapa x de y".
- Strings `skipNote`, `summary`, `restart` added to `profileForm.admin.json` under `preview`, verbatim from the UI-SPEC, and pinned by `profile-form-12-1-preview-messages.test.ts`.
