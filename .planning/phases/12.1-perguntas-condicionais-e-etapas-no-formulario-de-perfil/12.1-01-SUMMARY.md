# Phase 12.1 Plan 01: Shared contract for conditions, steps and visibility Summary

**One pure TypeScript visibility rule (answer tokens + any-of overlap), an 18-row parity table for the SQL twin, the step and condition limits, seven new refusal codes and the additive condition, step and layout schemas, with every existing read shape untouched.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 15 min (start time was not captured at spawn; estimated from first and last commit)
- **Tasks:** 3 of 3
- **Files modified:** 3
- **Commits:** 4 (RED, GREEN, two feat)

## Accomplishments

- `answerTokens` maps the three stored shapes to tokens (boolean to `'true'`/`'false'`, option ids for choices); "Outro", an empty set, a string, a number and null give none. `isQuestionVisible` and `visibleSteps` are pure and implement D-621/D-628 exactly (unknown or empty opener hides; a step with no visible question is absent; step order kept).
- `PROFILE_FORM_VISIBILITY_CASES` carries 18 rows (yes/no true and false both ways, string-not-boolean, single choice, mixed list, multiple choice any-of and none, Outro on single and multiple, Outro text with option ids, empty set, unanswered, unknown option id); the contracts test runs each through the rule.
- `PROFILE_FORM_LIMITS` gained `steps: 10` and `stepTitle: 60`; `PROFILE_FORM_ISSUES` ends with `condition_locked, condition_invalid, condition_order, has_dependents, step_not_empty, last_step, unknown_step` (28).
- New schemas: `profileFormConditionSchema` (strict, 1 to 20 unique tokens of 1 to 64 chars), `memberStepSchema`, `adminStepSchema`, `createStepBodySchema`, `updateStepBodySchema`, `layoutBodySchema` (at most 10 steps, 30 ids, no repeated id), `dependentSchema`, `hasDependentsDetailsSchema`.
- Optional `stepId`/`condition` on create, update (null removes) and replace; optional `stepId` on `saveAnswersBodySchema` (still strict); `answerEtag(questions, steps?)` keeps all earlier outputs.
- The module package re-exports every new symbol and nothing else.
