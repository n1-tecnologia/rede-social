### Tenant scoping and lanes
**Source:** `withTenantTx(ctx, ...)`, tenant ONLY from `ctx.tenantId`, explicit `tenant_id = ${ctx.tenantId}::uuid` predicates, RLS policies from the questions table. Every new policy/route needs a cross-tenant negative test (pgTAP + isolation inventory).

**Source:** `app.profile_form_missing_self` (claims from `app.tenant_id()` / `app.user_id()`), `set search_path = ''`, `revoke ... from public`, `grant execute ... to authenticated, api_user, service_role`.

`isQuestionVisible` (contracts, pure) is the single TS rule; SQL twin is `app.profile_form_answer_tokens` plus `&&`; parity test (contracts unit + integration) pins both plus the purge.
