# Phase 12.1 Plan 11: The member form with steps and conditions and the per-step save Summary

**The member read now carries steps and conditions under a formEtag that follows them, and the save is scoped to a step, validates by the same visibility the SQL gate uses, ignores values for hidden questions, deletes (in the same transaction) every answer that stopped applying, active or archived, and reports what is still owed instead of refusing for it.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 35 min (the start time was not captured at spawn; estimated from the first and last runs)
- **Tasks:** 3 of 3 (Task 1 is the tracer)
- **Files:** 1 created, 9 modified (plus 1 sibling test, see Deviations)

## Accomplishments

- Contract (non-additive, every consumer fixed in the same plan): `stepId` and `condition` on `memberQuestionSchema`, `steps` on `memberFormSchema`, `missingQuestionIds` on `saveAnswersResultSchema`.
- `member.ts` read: `QuestionRow` carries `step_id` and the condition columns; the lock reads `app.profile_form_lock_active_v2()`; `readFormSteps` keeps the steps that hold an active question; `formEtagOf(rows, steps)` hashes `id:revision:step_id` per question plus the shown steps' `id:revision`.
- `member.ts` save: scope by `stepId` (unknown, empty or foreign is 400 `unknown_step`, a key outside the scope is `unknown_question`); effective answers (stored overlaid with the submitted parsed values) feed `isQuestionVisible` in two passes; hidden values are dropped without error; a visible required question without a key or a stored answer is `required` only inside the scope; unchanged values are not rewritten; `app.profile_form_purge_closed()` runs in the same transaction; `reportMissing` computes `missingQuestionIds` in TypeScript and cross-checks its length against `app.profile_form_missing_self()` (a difference is 409 `form_changed` with the fresh form).
- `newOptionalCount` now requires the member to see a conditional optional question (`q.condition_values && app.profile_form_answer_tokens(o.value)`).
- `me.ts` route descriptions name steps, conditions, the optional `stepId`, the ignored hidden values, the purge and `missingQuestionIds`.
- Web, compile-only: fixture builders default `stepId`/`condition`/`steps`; `previewForm(questions, steps = [])` maps `stepId`, `condition` and the steps that hold a question; `QuestionBuilder` and `PreviewSheet` gained an optional `steps` prop. No markup, class, string or element was added.

**3. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] Per-member advisory lock**
- **Found during:** Task 2 design
- **Issue:** the purge deletes rows in no particular order, so two simultaneous saves of one member could deadlock (the canonical write order does not cover the purge) and PFORM-33 requires a consistent store whichever wins.
- **Fix:** after the `FOR SHARE` lock, `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('profile_form:member:<membership>', 0))` serialises the member's saves. It sits after the question lock and admin edits never take it, so no lock cycle exists. A 3-round two-connection case passes.
- **Files modified:** `packages/core/server/profile-form/member.ts`
