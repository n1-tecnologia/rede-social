# Phase 12.2 manual proofs (M1 to M8)

This file is the ledger of the proofs only the owner can run: a real phone, a hosted tenant project, a real
tenant move, a browser run that needs more disk than the build machine had, the reading of choices the design
review left open, and the later cleanup. Everything an agent could build is built and proved on the local stack
(plans 01 to 20); what is left here cannot be faked, so nothing in this file is ever recorded as done by an agent.

**The rule.** Every row ships `pending`. A row becomes `passed` only when Igor pastes the evidence of its steps
and the date, in the row, himself. An agent never edits a Status line and never writes evidence it did not
see. A phase whose ledger has a `pending` row closes `human_needed`, never green.

**The evidence rule.** Paste shapes, counts, statuses, HTTP codes, exit codes and the words the screens show.
**Never** a key, a password, a token, a connection string, an e-mail address of a member, a CSV cell or a
project ref. Replace a ref by `<ref>` and a host by `<host>` in what you paste.

**The procedures** are in `docs/DEPLOY.md`, "Phase 12.2 release (conditional branches)" (written by plan 21).

## M1 — The builder, the Fluxo and the member block on a real phone

- **Proves:** that the Lista with its blocks, the Ramo select and "Ramos desta pergunta", the branch sheet, the
  Fluxo and the member's block work on a real device against the deployed app. Phones cannot reach
  `*.localhost`, so no local run stands in for it. The plans proved the structure, the classes, the wiring and
  the text in jsdom or happy-dom, and the pixels only on the sketch; this row is the real-browser look the plans
  marked "still needs a real-browser look".
- **Needs:** the homolog deploy of the release in `docs/DEPLOY.md` "Phase 12.2 release", the module switched on
  for one homolog tenant, an admin account, two member accounts and a phone (a home-screen install is welcome,
  not required). Use iOS Safari and Android Chrome, in the light theme and the dark one, once with a custom
  tenant brand.
- **Steps (the story):**
  1. As the admin, build the team's clinic example: a single-choice question with three options; "Criar ramo"
     for the first option; two questions added into it through the footer; make one of them a Sim ou não question
     and give it a branch on "Sim" with one question; give that one a third level.
  2. The Lista reads as three indented blocks under their openers, with the pill "Abre N perguntas" on each
     opener. Press "Reordenar": the branch arrows move a block, the question arrows move a question inside its
     block and never change its branch. Press "Concluir".
  3. Change the view to "Fluxo": the edges carry the answers, a tap on a node opens the question sheet, a tap on
     an edge opens the branch sheet, and nothing scrolls sideways.
  4. Open "Ver como o membro vê": ticking the opener shows the whole block at once; "Sim" opens the nested one.
  5. As the first member, answer the opener: the block appears as one under one rail and focus stays on the
     opener; the nested block appears inside it; the progress line follows.
  6. As the first member, change the opener: the erase dialog lists the titles of every level, flat and in form
     order, "Rever respostas" saves nothing, "Apagar e continuar" saves and the answers are gone.
  7. As the admin, open the member: the closed questions read "Não se aplica".
  8. As the second member, answer another option: the block never shows.
- **The real-browser look, by plan and screen** (tick each on the device and paste "worked" or what you saw; the
  frame names are those of sketch 019):
  - **Member form (plan 14).** The 200 ms motion of a block opening and closing (jsdom only proves a block
    leaves after its exit); the rail and the spacing against `membro-ramo-aparece`, `membro-ramo-aninhado`,
    `membro-ramo-fecha`, `membro-ramo-outra-etapa`; the 214 px input width at 320 px (`membro-ramo-320`,
    `membro-apagar-ramo-320`); the dark theme (`membro-ramo-escuro`); the real focus order and the
    screen-reader reading of the live region.
  - **Builder Lista and reorder (plan 15).** The rail and the white cards against `lista-ramo-simples`,
    `lista-ramo-aninhado`, `lista-ramo-etapas`, `lista-ramo-vazio`, `lista-ramo-varios`; the 234 px deepest card
    at 320 px (`lista-ramo-320`); the dark theme (`lista-ramo-escuro`); the reorder frames
    (`lista-reordenar-ramo`, `lista-reordenar-ramo-anunciado`); the focus order and the screen-reader reading of
    the nested lists and the live region.
  - **Question sheet and the Ramo select (plan 16), on iOS and on Android.** The native picker of the Ramo
    select and its optgroup labels; the rows against `editor-ramo-seletor`, `editor-ramo-bloqueado`,
    `editor-ramo-ocultos`, `editor-ramos-*`, `ramo-novo`, `ramo-novo-simnao`, `ramo-erros`,
    `ramo-descartar-dialogo`; the 320 px frames `editor-ramo-320` and `ramo-nome-longo-320` (20 options, a
    60-character name, a long opener title, the save button reachable by scroll); typing a 60-character name on a
    phone (the `maxLength` question of M6).
  - **Branch sheet, locked state, undo and archive dialogs (plan 17).** The native opener select and its
    optgroups; the locked row, the divided block and the two dialogs against `ramo-editar`,
    `ramo-editar-trocar-pergunta`, `ramo-bloqueado`, `ramo-erros`, `ramo-desfazer-dialogo`,
    `ramo-desfazer-impacto`, `ramo-arquivar-dialogo`, `ramo-arquivar-recusa`; 320 px with 20 options; the restore

  - **Refusals and WR-06 (plan 18).** The grouped alert, the disabled "Mostrar no perfil do membro" switch row and
