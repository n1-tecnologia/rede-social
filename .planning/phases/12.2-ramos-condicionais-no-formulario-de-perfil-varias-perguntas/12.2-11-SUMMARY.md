# Phase 12.2 Plan 11: Counts through the branch chain Summary

**The form summary and every impact kind now count only members who see a question through its whole branch chain, composed from the one shared SQL fragment, with two new kinds (undo_branch, reactivate_branch) whose numbers equal what the gate holds after the change is applied.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 12 min (about 19:03 to 19:15 local, 2026-11-06)
- **Tasks:** 3 (Task 1 tracer, Tasks 2 and 3 TDD)
- **Files:** 3 changed in the plan range (1 created, 2 modified), 1191 insertions and 72 deletions

## Accomplishments

- **Summary through the chain (Task 1, tracer).** `getSummary` is the exported `summaryStatement` run as one statement: the `req` CTE carries `id, tenant_id, branch_id` of the tenant's required, active questions and a member is complete when no required question visible by `questionVisibleSql({ question: 'r', tenant: 'r.tenant_id', member: 'm.id' })` lacks an answer. The 12.1 single-level restatement is gone. A depth-3 clinic fixture with members at depth 0, 1, 2, 3 and a stale deeper answer proves `complete` equals the number of members for whom `app.profile_form_missing` is 0, at every step.
- **Impact through the chain (Task 2).** `getImpact` reads its scope from a one-row derived table `p` (`tenant_id`, `branch_id`, `question_id`, all bound parameters) so the fragment receives identifiers only. `create_required` and `replace` take an ACTIVE `branchId` (else the bare 404) or count everyone; the retired condition parameters are 400 `invalid`; `make_required` and `reactivate` count members who see the question and lack an answer; `remove_condition` counts members who do not see it today and lack an answer. `SEES_THROUGH`, the single-level `LACKS_ANSWER` pairing and `parseConditionValues` are gone. Every result is `count(distinct members.id)`, exactly the key `members`.
- **Undo and reactivate (Task 3).** `undo_branch`: members who lack an answer to a required active question directly in the branch and do not see it (`not branchChainOpenSql`). `reactivate_branch`: members who see and lack an answer to a required question archived with the branch's own stamp, in a subtree branch that carries the same stamp (compared in SQL, microsecond for microsecond, like the service). Both are compared with the gate by applying `POST /branches/{id}/undo` and `/reactivate` and counting the members whose gate count rose.
- **Route documentation.** `impactRoute` documents `branchId`, the retired parameters, the two new kinds, and the 400 and bare 404 behaviour; the summary route describes the chain rule.

**2. [Plan vs approach] The tenant reaches the shared fragment as a column, not as "a bound parameter inside the raw text"**
- The plan says to wrap the fragment with `sql.raw` "with the tenant bound as a parameter". The fragment (plan 04) validates its inputs as plain identifiers by design (T-12.2-11), so a value cannot travel in raw text. The tenant is therefore a column of a one-row derived table (`r.tenant_id` from the filtered CTE, `p.tenant_id` from `IMPACT_SCOPE`) fed by a bound parameter. The acceptance intent holds and is asserted: the rendered text never contains the tenant id, and the params do.

**3. [Plan vs approach] `turbo typecheck` not used**
- Its task graph depends on `^build` (forbidden here). `tsc --noEmit` per package (core, api, module profile-form) exits 0.

**4. [Scope] Retired parameters refused by key rejection**
- To keep `grep -c conditionQuestionId admin.ts` at 0 as the acceptance criterion requires, the 400 for the retired parameters is "any query key besides change, questionId, branchId", not a named check.

**Total deviations:** 4 (1 test fix, 3 approach notes). **Impact:** none on scope.

## Known limit

A member who lacks no direct required answer but would newly see a required question of a nested branch only through a stale answer to its opener is not counted (the shared fragment cannot express a chain with one level removed). The depth-3 drift test (undo of B1 with stale members) confirms the number equals the gate's delta in the case the dialog is meant for.
