## Decisions Made

- The builder story seeds the opener and the plain question and builds everything else through the screens, like the 12.1 spec; building a three-option single choice through the options editor would add a fragile step that proves nothing about branches.
- The question added through a footer is found by the opener's title in the footer's accessible name (`Adicionar pergunta ao ramo: Se “<opener>”`), so the nested footers are never confused with the first.
- A new question is created optional in the builder story, so no impact dialog ("N membros vão precisar responder") interrupts it whatever members the lab holds.
- The four-row hygiene read sums to zero across both databases, which implies each row is zero.
