# Phase 12.2 Plan 20: Retire the per-question condition Summary

**The 12.1 per-question `condition` is gone from the contracts, the server reads, the integration fixtures, the web and the Playwright harness; `branchId` and `branches` are required on every read, and the repository grep gate lists only the retired columns' own schema, migrations, SQL tests, conversion fixtures, the SELF_REF_ORDER entry and DEPLOY.md.**

## Accomplishments

- Contracts: deleted `profileFormConditionSchema`, `ProfileFormCondition`, `isQuestionVisible`, `visibleSteps`, `parseConditionValues`, the `condition` key of the create, update, admin, member and staff shapes and the two condition parameters of `impactQuerySchema`; `branchId` is `uuid | null` and required on `adminCore`, `memberQuestionSchema` and each staff row; `branches` is required on `adminQuestionListSchema`, `archivedQuestionListSchema`, `memberFormSchema` and `staffAnswersSchema`. The 18-row `PROFILE_FORM_VISIBILITY_CASES` table stays and now runs through `answerTokens` plus a token-overlap check.
- Server: `admin.ts`, `member.ts` and `answers.ts` stop emitting `condition: null`; `conditionRetired()` and the retired-parameter check of `getImpact` are removed; the module routes' descriptions and README stop naming the per-question condition.
- Integration fixtures: `seedProfileFormQuestion` takes `branchId` only; answers, save-step and export seed a branch with `seedProfileFormBranch`.
- Web: `describe-condition.ts` and `isNotApplicable` deleted; fixtures, `readImpactAction`, `SaveFieldKey`, `PreviewSheet`, the admin and profile reads and their tests speak branches; five unread catalog groups removed with their pins updated.
- Playwright harness: `seedBranch`, `branchId` on `seedQuestion`, `clearForm` and `snapshot` cover branches (a deferred-constraints transaction, as the integration sweep does), the clinic and wide forms seed through a branch, and spec 1 builds the branch through the builder ("Criar ramo", "Salvar ramo", "Adicionar pergunta ao ramo") while 4b opens the branch sheet instead of the condition editor.

## Deliberately kept (not the retired pair)

- **Database columns, the Drizzle line, every migration, `supabase/tests` 220/226, `migrate-all.test.ts`, the `SELF_REF_ORDER` entry and its test, `docs/DEPLOY.md`:** the plan forbids touching them; they go in the cleanup release.
- **`remove_condition`** (impact change name) and the API codes **`condition_invalid`, `condition_locked`, `condition_order`**: still the vocabulary of the branch lanes (a question leaving its branch; an opener that is archived; the order rule), pinned by the contracts and by the integration suites.
- **`PROFILE_FORM_CONDITION_OPENER_TYPES`** (constant name pinned by its test) and the web names **`ConditionDescription`, `conditionOptionsText`, `editor.impact.conditionalNote`, `impact.unconditional*`**: they name the sentence of a branch's opening answers, not the retired shape.
- **`FormQuestions.branches?`** in `visibility.ts` and the optional `branches?` props of `ArchivedList` and `PreviewSheet`: component-level leniency for callers and tests that build a partial form; the contract itself now requires the key.
- `QuestionList.tsx`, `ArchivedList.tsx` and `StaffAnswersBlock.tsx` needed no edit: no reader of the old pair was left in them.

See `key-decisions`. In short: the strict contract is the guard for a retired `condition` key (generic 400), the label logic of conditions was folded into `describe-branch.ts`, and unread catalog keys were removed only after a grep proved nothing reads them.
