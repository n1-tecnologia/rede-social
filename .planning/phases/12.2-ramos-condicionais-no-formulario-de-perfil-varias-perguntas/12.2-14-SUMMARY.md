# Phase 12.2 Plan 14: The member form on the branch tree Summary

**The member form now draws a branch's questions as one block under one shared rail that appears and leaves as a unit (nested branches nest their rails), the one-scroll edit announces blocks that open in other steps by step title, the erase dialog and the staff "Não se aplica" follow the whole branch chain, and `ProfileForm.test.tsx` is rewritten onto branches and green.**

## Performance

- **Tasks:** 3 (Task 1 tracer, Tasks 2 and 3 TDD), 6 commits (3 RED, 3 GREEN)
- **Files:** 15 changed (4 created, 10 modified, 1 deleted), 1284 insertions and 164 deletions, all under `apps/web`
- **Duration:** not recorded (see frontmatter)

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (tracer).** `blocksOf` walks a stack of open blocks over each question's chain (`branchChainOf`, reversed to outermost first): consecutive questions of one branch share a block, a nested branch is a block inside its parent right after the question that opens it, two branches of one opener stay two blocks, and a root question closes everything. `RevealedBranch` replaces `RevealedField` (deleted): `div[data-revealed-branch]` with `flex flex-col gap-6 border-l-2 border-border pl-3`, inside a 200ms `AnimatePresence` that is instant under reduced motion and never animates on first render. `ProfileForm` keeps the form's branches in its view, filters with `branchId` in place of `condition`, renders each card through `blocksOf` with a small recursive renderer, and passes `view.branches` to `validateAll`, `wireAnswers` and `answersToErase`. `QuestionField` is now always a plain field. Tracer verify re-run end to end (human_verify_mode is end-of-phase and the verify is automated only), then expansion.
- **Task 2.** `announceReveal` takes the changed question: in the one-scroll edit the questions of its own step keep the 12.1 strings and every other step is announced per step, in step order, with the two new catalog strings; the stepper stays silent for other steps. The catalog gained `stepper.revealedOtherStep` and `stepper.hiddenOtherStep` (UI-SPEC wording), pinned verbatim in the new `profile-form-12-2-member-messages.test.ts`. New component cases: nested rails, close-everything-below in one pass (also with motion on), a middle branch closing only what is below it, a draft typed under a closed nested block returning on reopen and absent from the wire, a flagged question keeping its danger block and the rail, erase lists at depth 3 (flat, form order, no stored answer means not listed, seven titles show five and "e mais 2 perguntas", scroll body kept).
- **Task 3.** `StaffAnswersBlock` uses `isNotApplicableInTree` with a `branches` prop; the payload's branches reach it from `MemberAnswersBlock.tsx`. Floor tests: a three-deep tree with long titles and labels, every control in a block is a 44px target, no motion wrapper under reduced motion, no half-step spacing class in `ProfileForm.tsx`, `RevealedBranch.tsx` or `QuestionField.tsx`.
- **Baseline cleared.** `ProfileForm.test.tsx` was rewritten from the retired per-question `condition` onto branches (a local `gate()` helper builds the branch the questions name) and its 25 failing tests are green.
