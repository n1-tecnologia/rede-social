# Phase 12.2 Plan 06: The branches' SQL half Summary

**One expand-only custom migration that adds the deferrable key pair, the v3 and branch lock functions, replaces the gate and the purge with a fixed three-level chain join, converts any 12.1 per-question condition into a branch, and installs the question, branch, tree (PF008, PF009) and legacy (PF010) guards, with a Vitest that pins the SQL depth literal to the TypeScript constant. Written, never applied.**

## Accomplishments

- `profile_form_branches_opener_fk` on `(tenant_id, opener_question_id)` and `profile_form_questions_branch_fk` on `(tenant_id, branch_id)`, both NO ACTION and `DEFERRABLE INITIALLY IMMEDIATE`, hand SQL after the header, absent from the Drizzle schema and the snapshot (`drizzle-kit generate` still says "No schema changes").
- `app.profile_form_lock_active_v3()` (the v2 columns with `branch_id` instead of the two condition columns, same order and `for share of q`) and `app.profile_form_lock_branches()` (`id, opener_question_id, open_values, revision, archived_at`, never the name, `order by b.created_at, b.id for share of b`).
- `app.profile_form_missing(uuid, uuid)` replaced with the same signature and attributes; cases 1 and 2 byte for byte; case 3 left-joins `q` to `b1, o1, b2, o2, b3` and owes a required unanswered question when it has no branch or every branch on its chain is open (answer to the opener overlaps `open_values`). `app.profile_form_purge_closed()` replaced with the negation, claims scoped, active and archived questions alike, no `archived_at` anywhere in its body.
- The conversion `do` block: per tenant with a legacy condition, `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('profile_form:' || tenant, 0))` (the `adminLock` key), the privacy-safe switch of a public legacy dependent of a private opener, one nameless branch per distinct (tenant, opener, sorted values), then every legacy dependent (archived included) pointed at its branch. The legacy columns, `revision` and `updated_at` are never touched.
- `app.profile_form_question_guard()` restated: PF007 first and re-targeted to active branches (the "gain a condition" clause deleted), the early return, PF001 to PF005 text unchanged, PF006 at the end of the answered branch (`new.branch_id is not null and new.branch_id is distinct from old.branch_id`; leaving a branch passes).
- `app.profile_form_branch_guard()` (BEFORE UPDATE on the branches, PF006 on a change of opener or of the SET of opening values when any question of the branch, active or archived, has an answer), `app.profile_form_tree_guard()` (PF008 whole-tenant `b1 .. b4` join, then PF009 whole-tenant public-under-private join, wired as an AFTER row trigger on both tables) and `app.profile_form_legacy_condition_guard()` (PF010, the last trigger of the file).
- Grants: revoke from PUBLIC on all eight functions, `authenticated, api_user, service_role` on v3, branch lock and purge, the gate keeps its existing grant, `revoke truncate` on the branches table.
- `packages/core/tests/profile-form-branch-depth.test.ts`: 9 cases that parse the migration (comments stripped) and assert the gate and purge have `branchDepth` aliases, the PF008 test has `branchDepth + 1`, PF008/9/10 each appear once as an errcode and PF001 to PF010 all appear, the order facts above, and that every function sets an empty `search_path`.
