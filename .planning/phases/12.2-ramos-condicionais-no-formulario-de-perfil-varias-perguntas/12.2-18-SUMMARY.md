# Phase 12.2 Plan 18: Refusals, privacy guard and archived branches Summary

**An opener's archive, delete, retype and option removal now refuse with the branches that block it, each over its question titles (client pre-check and server 409 draw the same alert); a public question under a private opener is refused everywhere (switch disabled with the nearest private opener named, opener and branch edits refused with the public titles); and the Arquivadas tab shows archived branches as groups with "Reativar ramo", its impact, the 30-question limit and one-sentence refusals.**

## Performance

- **Tasks:** 3 (Task 1 tracer, Tasks 2 and 3 TDD), 6 commits (3 RED, 3 GREEN)
- **Files:** 19 changed, all under `apps/web`, 3260 insertions and 339 deletions
- **Duration:** not recorded (see frontmatter)

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (tracer).** `DependentsAlert` takes `groups`: each branch is a `text-xs font-bold break-words` line (the `ul` below it is named by that line through `aria-labelledby`, so no new string) over a plain list of its question titles; five titles in total across the groups, then "e mais N" (ICU plural, `=0` never renders), a branch with no question or one the five already used shows its label only; geometry and `role="alert"` are unchanged and the flat list stays for `archiveBranch` and the privacy kind. The hint is chosen by kind. `editor-state` now answers over branches: `dependentBranchesOf(tree, openerId, labelOf)` (active branches, active member titles, list order), `groupsFromServer` (label built from the SERVER's name, opener and answers, so a branch the page does not know yet still gets its sentence), `optionUsage` (delegates to `referencedOptionCounts`), `refusalOfUpdate(question, draft, groups, tree)`; the 12.1 `dependentsOf` and its per-question `optionUsage` are deleted. `actions.ts` keeps `details.branches` (parsed with the contract's own shape, which strips unknown keys, dropped whole if it breaks the contract) on the update conflict and on `hasDependents`. The sheet wires it for archive, delete, retype (under the type row) and option removal, from the tree it holds and from the 409 on a race; the option row's caption is `Abre N ramos` through the existing `usedBy` key.
- **Task 2.** `VisibilitySwitches` gains `privateOpener`: the "Mostrar no perfil do membro" switch is `disabled` and `aria-checked` false, and its helper is the privacy sentence with the opener's title. `privateOpenerOf(tree, branchId)` walks the chain nearest first (tests at depth 1, 2, 3 and with two private openers). `privacyTitlesIf(tree, change)` builds the tree as it would be after the change and subtracts the violations the form already holds, using the existing `publicUnderPrivateTitles`. The question sheet refuses, before any request, turning an opener's own switch off while a public question hangs below at any depth, with the `privacy` alert and the public titles; the branch sheet refuses a new opener that would expose a public question; a server `public_under_private` with `titles` renders the same alert in both sheets, and without titles the branch sheet falls back to its failure line, never an empty alert.
- **Task 3.** `archivedLayout` (pure, in `editor-state.ts`) turns the archived read into groups and question entries: a question went with a branch when its `branchId` is the branch and its `archivedAt` equals the branch's. `ArchivedList` draws a group as a header card (`Split` 14, label 14/700 `break-words line-clamp-2`, "Arquivado em {data} · N perguntas" over the whole block, a ghost `size="sm" hitArea` "Reativar ramo" with `aria-label` "Reativar ramo: {label}") over a rail of rows (no own "Reativar") and nested groups (no "Reativar ramo"); a question archived alone keeps its card with "Estava no ramo “{label}”." and reactivates through the question path. "Reativar ramo" refuses an archived opener by sentence, refuses past the limit by the shipped limit line, reads `reactivate_branch`, opens the shipped impact dialog (`ImpactDialog` kind `reactivateBranch`, same strings as a reactivation) and writes through `reactivateBranchAction` only from the dialog's confirm (zero members writes directly). A question inside a branch that is still archived is refused with "Para reativar “{title}”, reative antes o ramo “{label}”, que decide se ela aparece."; the 12.1 sentence stays for a plain question whose opener is archived (a race).
