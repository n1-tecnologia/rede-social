# Phase 09.4 Plan 15: Owner choice and customer token in the new-tenant wizard Summary

**"Projeto da plataforma / Projeto do cliente" choice in Dados with a memory-only scoped token, optional expiry and open how-to; the pre-check and the creation carry them once, every token refusal lands under its own field, and the Resumo and dialog name the owner without ever echoing the token.**

- **Completed:** 2026-10-27T19:12:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (1 tracer, 2 auto; all TDD)

## Accomplishments

- Tracer: the owner `SegmentedControl` sits before the ref; the customer choice shows `ProjectTokenFields` and the open `TokenHowTo`, swaps the group helper, and "Continuar" sends ref, token and date once. Platform drafts send no new field, byte for byte. Tracer verified end to end before expansion.
- `TenantDraftProvider` persists `owner` and `tokenExpiresAt` and keeps the token in memory only (cleared by reset, an owner switch and the creation answer); a reload asks for the token again and `RequireDraftData` sends a tokenless customer draft back to Dados.
- `actions.ts` maps the pre-check reasons to fields (`token_expiry_invalid` under the date, other `token_*` under the token, `not_found` reading the customer wording) and the creation maps the API's closed token codes the same way; the token is never logged or returned.
- Resumo and the confirmation show "Dono do projeto", the fixed "Token informado, guardado com segurança" line and "Vence em DD/MM/AAAA" (string-formatted, no timezone shift); the three customer failure sentences and their code line come through the shared `reasonCatalogKey`.
- A full-path test over the real provider proves the token reaches nothing but the two actions: no text, attribute, storage, console call or fetch address.

## Decisions Made

- The token stays out of `TenantDraft` entirely (a separate `useState`), so adding keys to the draft later can never persist it.
- Creation codes `org_mismatch` and `unchecked` under `token` have no field to blame and no catalog key; they remain the generic retryable failure rather than inventing copy outside UI-SPEC.
- `today` for the date field's bounds is computed in the browser in `America/Sao_Paulo` (the platform's day), avoiding a server prop for a field that only renders after a client-side choice.
