<objective>
The interaction layer every later content module copies (FEED-04, FEED-05, FEED-06, MOD-03): one `feed_likes` table with nullable target foreign keys and partial unique indexes, one `feed_comments` table whose single reply level is enforced by a composite self-referencing foreign key rather than a trigger, trigger-maintained counters that survive soft deletes, and the six typed domain events Phase 7's notification centre will consume.

Purpose: this is the conventions plan. Phase 5's story likes and story comments reuse these exact tables through the reserved `story_id` slots; Phase 7 builds notification rows from these exact payloads; Phase 8's moderation widens these exact routes. Getting the shape right here is worth more than any single feature in the phase.
Output: the two tables with their constraints and triggers, eight service functions, eight routes, the pgTAP negative cases the roadmap names as acceptance checks, and the integration suite that proves idempotency under a retry and under concurrency.

**Decision — the reply cap is declarative, not a trigger.** A `before insert` trigger that reads the parent row is a read-then-write with no lock: two concurrent inserts can both observe `parent.parent_id is null` and both succeed, producing the three-level thread the constraint was supposed to forbid. The composite foreign key `(parent_id, parent_depth) -> (id, depth)` is enforced by an index and cannot race, needs no `security definer` function and no `search_path` hardening. RESEARCH verified all three refusal paths against this project's local Postgres.
</objective>
