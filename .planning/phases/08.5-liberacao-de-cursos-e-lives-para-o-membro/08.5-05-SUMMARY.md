**Four store access reads for courses and lives (card tags and buy sections, the community reads' twins over the kernel lock seams), and a product detail and purchase answer that name the published courses and non-cancelled lives a product unlocks for every caller, a locked live read through `app.course_live_cards()`.**

## Accomplishments

- `GET /v1/store/course-access` / `GET /v1/store/live-access`: one row per gated published course (`published_at desc`) or linked live (`starts_at asc`, a cancelled one kept), with `locked` computed in the statement through `LOCKED_COURSE_IDS` / `LOCKED_LIVE_IDS` and `archivedTag` when no linked product is active.
- `GET /v1/store/courses/{courseId}/access` / `GET /v1/store/lives/{liveId}/access`: `locked`, `gated`, `archivedTag`, `buyableProducts` (active, `created_at desc, id desc`) and manager-only `products`; a locked live still answers 200 because existence comes from `app.course_live_cards(liveId)`; a member's draft course, an unknown or foreign id, and anything with `courses` off are the bare 404.
- `productDetailSchema.unlockedCourses` / `unlockedLives` (every caller) and `purchaseResultSchema.courses` / `lives`: published courses only, non-cancelled lives only, `[]` while `courses` is off; a first purchase and a replay answer the same lists.
- The four routes inventoried and proved across tenants (`phase 08.5 sweep: store course and live access`), and the store README records the routes, the new keys and the MOD-02 debt extended to `app.course_live_cards()`.
