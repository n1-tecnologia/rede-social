## Decisions Made

Planning decisions 1-7 were implemented as locked (see `key-decisions`). Two execution-time readings:

- **Module number** is the rank among the live modules the curriculum shows (non-empty, = `module_seq`). An empty live module produces no outline row and therefore never shows, so numbering by it would leave a visible gap ("Módulo 01, Módulo 03"); this also keeps "Libera ao concluir o Módulo NN" consistent with the module rule, which looks at the previous NON-EMPTY module.
- **`Cache-Control: no-store`** on both learn routes (the caller's progress and an opened paid lesson's content are in the body).
