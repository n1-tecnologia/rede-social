# Phase 08.5 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Scope.** This phase opens the school to members. It adds these member surfaces:
- the **Cursos** BottomNav tab, with Reels moving to the TopBar;
- the member `/cursos` page: area tabs **Cursos · Lives**, the course poster grid and the lives list;
- the member **course page**: curriculum, padlocks, buy section, progress, "Começar" / "Continuar";
- the **lesson player**: video with resume, completion, materials, module list, previous and next;
- the **lock page** of a lesson the member cannot open;
- the member **live page**: countdown, join window, replay, padlock and buy section.

It also changes these admin and store surfaces:
- "Ver como aluno" on the hub row and on the admin course page, plus the preview banner;
- the lesson form's new switch "Exige concluir a aula anterior";
- the product page's unlock list (communities, courses, lives);
- "Compra concluída", with the return to a locked course or live.

**Design authority.** `reference/frontend-design/` draws four of these member screens. They are ported, not redrawn (PROTOTYPE.md: components are ported into module packages, never refactored in place):
- the catalogue `app/membros/cursos/page.tsx`;
- the course page `app/membros/cursos/[courseId]/page.tsx`;
- the player `app/membros/aulas/[lessonId]/page.tsx`;
- the lives list `app/membros/lives/page.tsx`.

The components ported are `CourseCard`, `LessonRow`, `LessonThumb`, `ModuleCover`, `ProgressBar` and `ProgressRing`. The lock rules are `lib/members-progress.ts` `resolveLock`, used as a label formatter only.

**Porting deltas.** Three prototype habits are replaced by decisions that are already locked:
- **The poster ratio stays 3:4.** The prototype's `CourseCard` is 4:5. UI-D-409 fixed 3:4 and the admin form previews 3:4 (D-420).
- **"Premium" becomes the store's padlock.** The prototype's "Premium" pill, grayscale cover and gold CTA become the 08.2 padlock tag, the cover in colour and the store buy section (D-357, D-421, D-424).
- **No gold or emerald tokens.** `pill-gold` and `pill-emerald` become `StatusPill` and the over-media pill (PROTOTYPE.md §2, token rename).

Prototype extras out of scope this phase are not drawn: certificates, reminders, "Destaque", the stats strip, attendee counts and the countdown grid.

**Prototype-less surfaces.** These go through the sketch gate (D-365, D-417), `.planning/sketches/010-phase-08.5-designed-screens`:
- the live page;
- the course page's buy section and lock notices, and the lesson lock page;
- "Ver como aluno";
- the lesson form switch;
- the product page unlock list and the generalised "Compra concluída";
- the Reels TopBar slot.

§ Sketch gate below lists the design question and the frames to draw.

**Inherited design language.** The design language, spacing scale, type scale and colour model are **inherited unchanged** from the approved `02`–`08.4-UI-SPEC.md`. This document restates them for the checker and records only the Phase 08.5 deltas.

**Upstream decisions.**
- `08.5-CONTEXT.md` decisions are cited as **D-419..D-434**, and the roadmap criteria as **SC 1..6**. None is re-litigated.
- `08.5-RESEARCH.md` is cited as **R-Pattern n** / **R-Pitfall n** / **R-A n**. Its draft requirements are **COURSE-16..COURSE-27**, **STORE-23**, and the amended **STORE-06** / **STORE-07**.
- Earlier visual decisions keep their numbers: UI-D-35, UI-D-69, UI-D-81, UI-D-206, UI-D-288, UI-D-366, UI-D-369..UI-D-375, UI-D-384, UI-D-386, UI-D-389..UI-D-411, UI-D-414, UI-D-417.
- The UI questions this phase opens are decided below as **UI-D-423..UI-D-445**. They continue after 08.4's UI-D-422, the highest id in any UI-SPEC (checked 2026-10-15 with `grep -rhoE "UI-D-[0-9]+" .planning | sort -t- -k3 -n -u | tail -1`).

**Research open questions settled here.**
1. **A cancelled live's page after its planned end.** It still renders and says "Cancelada". It is not a 404 (R-Open Question 3, UI-D-441).
2. **Lesson images in the curriculum.** They use the course cover. There is no per-lesson Mux still, and WR-08 stays deferred (R-A10, R-Open Question 2, UI-D-444).
3. **Reels on the TopBar.** With courses on, Reels becomes a TopBar slot. It is the leftmost slot of the right group, and `/reels` reached from it keeps the dark media chrome (R-Pitfall 6, UI-D-423).

**Assumptions made in this session.** This is a yolo session. Each assumption is stated here instead of being asked, and the planner may overturn any of them with one line:
- **A-UI-1. The member area switch is the underline `Tabs` (`HubTabs`), and the categories are the chips.** D-420 says "two-chip catalogue". The CONTEXT code insights point at `HubTabs.tsx` / `tabs.ts` ("the AreaTabs pattern for the member chips"). The prototype's member area also uses an underline row (`AreaTabs`). Two chip rows stacked would read as one filter, which is 08.3's A-UI-6. So the area is the `Tabs` row and the categories are a `Chip` row, the same frame the admin hub ships (UI-D-390/391).
- **A-UI-2. "Comprar para liberar" is the label for one buyable product and for several.** It is a CONTEXT copy anchor. With one product it is a link to the product page. With several it opens the "Opções de acesso" sheet. The purchase is still confirmed only on the product page (UI-D-375's rule). The community page keeps its shipped "Ver produto" / "Ver opções".
- **A-UI-3. Tapping a locked lesson navigates to that lesson's URL, which renders the lock page.** D-424 asks that a tap "shows the padlock notice with the same buy action". The lock page is that notice, and it is the same surface a remembered or shared lesson link lands on. That makes one surface instead of a sheet plus a page.
- **A-UI-4. Reels sits leftmost in the TopBar slot group, before Loja.** Notificações, Suporte and Loja keep their pixel positions (the UI-D-366 rationale).
- **A-UI-5. A locked course card still shows its progress bar once the member completed a lesson.** A non-buyer can complete free lessons (D-429). D-421 says the locked card shows "everything it would show unlocked", and D-430 says the bar shows "once the member has completed at least one lesson". Both are read literally.
- **A-UI-6. With "Aula aberta" on, the "Exige concluir a aula anterior" switch is disabled, renders off and is sent `false`.** The evaluator ignores it on a free lesson anyway (D-429), and a disabled "on" switch would claim a lock that never happens.
- **A-UI-7. The live page mounts the replay player only when the member taps "Assistir ao replay".** That is one playback credential per intent, not per page view (R-Anti-Patterns "one mint per player open").

Exceptions (all inherited or geometric, listed for the checker):

**Interaction truths outside the 8-category state taxonomy.** The planner lifts these as plain truths; they are listed here so they are not lost:
- **E07, E09:** the web never decides a lock. Every label comes from the server's `lockReason` through the pure formatter, and the formatter has no `localStorage` and no clock.
- **E09:** progress ticks are at most one every 15 s while playing, plus pause, end and `pagehide` (beacon). No tick is sent in preview or for an `admin_tenant`.
- **E10:** the DOM never contains `externalUrl` outside the window or for a locked live. The page refreshes at `joinOpensAt` and `endsAt` from a `serverNow` delta (UI-D-436).
- **E05–E10:** no locked lesson's description, video id, poster, material name or material id is present in the RSC payload (D-423). The member components never receive those props for a locked row.
- **E11:** The eye link on published rows, the outline "Ver como aluno" above "Despublicar", and the banner on every preview page, with links that keep `?ver=aluno`.
- **E01:** the chrome lookup reads topbar items. `/reels` from the TopBar renders without the mobile TopBar and with the dark BottomNav.

**Contract requirements this document places on the API** (for the planner; each is a projection of R-Pattern 2–10, not a new rule):
- **Catalogue card:** `id`, `title`, `coverAssetId`, `category {id,name}`, `level`, `lessonCount`, `durationSeconds`, `completedCount`, `locked`. Separately, `categories[]` limited to those with a published course.
- **Course read:** header fields, `locked`, the progress summary (`done`, `total`, `percent`, `nextLessonId`, `firstLessonId`, `firstFreeLessonId`), and the per-module (`id`, `title`, `position`, `requiresPrevious`, `lockReason`) and per-lesson (`id`, `title`, `position`, `durationSeconds`, `hasVideo`, `isFree`, `lockReason`, `completedAt`, `lastPositionSeconds`) outline.
- **Lesson read:** content and materials only when open, the module and lesson numbers, prev and next ids with their lock reasons, and the course `instructor` and photo.
- **Live read:** `status`, `locked`, `joinUrl` (window only), `joinOpensAt`, `endsAt`, `serverNow`, `startsToday`, and `replay` (lesson id, asset id, duration, materials, own progress) only when open and recorded.
- **Store access read:** `locked`, `archivedTag` and the buyable products for the buy sections.
- **Product detail (member):** `courses[]` and `lives[]` with thumbs and the live date.
- **Purchase result:** `courses[]` and `lives[]`.

If the planner drops a field, the surface omits that piece rather than estimating it.
