# Phase 08.5: Liberação de Cursos e Lives para o Membro - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-15
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase builds the member side of the tenant school that Phase 08.3 authored and Phase 08.4 linked to products. Members get the following.

- **Entry.** A Cursos BottomNav tab that opens a two-chip catalogue: Cursos and Lives.
- **Courses.** Published courses appear in a poster grid. A course linked to a product the member has not bought carries a padlock, exactly as a private community does (08.2).
- **Course page.** The page shows the curriculum. Free lessons ("Aula aberta") play for everyone. The other lessons open once the member holds an entitlement to one of the course's products.
- **Player.** A member with access watches the lesson video, opens its materials and writes their own progress. From this phase on the sequential lock ("exige concluir o anterior") is enforced against that progress, per module and per lesson, and the API refuses a skip.
- **Lives.** The tenant's lives appear in an agenda, each with its own page. A locked live hides its join link and replay until bought, with the same padlock and buy path as a course.
- **Store.** The product page and the purchase success state also name the courses and lives a product unlocks.
- **Threat closure.** This phase closes T-08.3-05 (lesson playback by a remembered asset id) and T-08.3-08 (a material's `/original` bytes by a remembered asset id). Both deferred items in `08.3/deferred-items.md` must reach `status: resolved`.
- **Isolation.** The two-tenant suite, pgTAP and integration tests cover every new member route, the progress writes and the changed policies. Staff keep their current access.

**Out of this phase** (the member area, the next phase, to be described by the user): tracks for members, the school overview (Início), the Progresso screen, "continue watching" rows, streaks, certificates, live reminders and notifications about new courses or lives.

The roadmap entry (`.planning/ROADMAP.md` § Phase 08.5) holds the 6 draft success criteria. This discussion confirms them and refines SC 1, SC 4 and SC 5 through the decisions below. Requirements are TBD in the roadmap. The researcher drafts new COURSE-* member requirements (COURSE-16 onward), and the planner amends STORE-06 and STORE-07 for D-425.

### Locked course page
- **D-423: A locked course's page shows the whole curriculum as titles with padlocks.**
  - What a non-buyer sees: module titles and order, each lesson's title, duration, free tag and lock state. Free lessons play in full, with their materials.
  - What never leaves the server for a locked, non-free lesson: its description, video asset id, playback credential, thumbnail, material names and material ids.
  - Today the restrictive `course_lessons_course_gate` policy hides those lesson rows entirely, so this needs a titles-only read. The researcher chooses the mechanism (a `SECURITY DEFINER` outline function scoped to the caller's tenant, or a column-safe projection). It must keep the T-08.3-05 and T-08.3-08 closures airtight and every 190-courses pgTAP assertion green.
  - **Reversibility:** costly. The read shape becomes the member contract, and the restrictive policy's posture changes.
- **D-424: The buy path is the 08.2 top section, plus a tap on a locked lesson.**
  - Below the course header, "Comprar para liberar" opens the product when one buyable product links the course. It opens the product picker when there are several, and the section is hidden when none is buyable (all archived).
  - Tapping a lesson locked by purchase shows the padlock notice with the same buy action.
  - Tapping a lesson locked by sequence shows "Libera ao concluir …" (the prototype's `resolveLock` labels), with no buy action.
- **D-425: The product page and "Compra concluída" list everything the product unlocks.** This amends STORE-06 and STORE-07.
  - The product page lists the active communities, published courses and non-cancelled lives the product unlocks, each linked once owned.
  - "Compra concluída" offers "Ir para a comunidade", "Ir para o curso" or "Ir para a live" when there is exactly one target. With several it lists them, and with none it shows "Fechar".
  - A purchase started from a locked course or a locked live returns there, now unlocked (STORE-07 posture).
- **D-426: An archived or unpublished course disappears for every member, buyers included.**
  - The course leaves the catalogue, and its pages answer the kernel not-found.
  - Progress rows are kept and come back if the course is republished.
  - D-413 is unchanged. There is no "Arquivado" view for buyers in this phase.

### Completion and sequential lock
- **D-427: A lesson completes automatically near the end of its video, and also by a "Marcar como concluída" button.**
  - A video lesson completes when the member reaches the completion threshold of its duration (default 90%, see Claude's Discretion).
  - The button completes a lesson without video, and works as a shortcut on a video lesson.
  - The user accepted that the button can complete a video lesson without watching it.
  - Completion is written through the API into `course_lesson_progress` (own row, the primary key arbitrates a repeat).
  - The server refuses completion of a lesson the member cannot open: a locked course and not free, or locked by sequence.
- **D-428: The sequential lock exists per module and per lesson.**
  - `course_lessons` gains `requires_previous`, through an expand-only migration. The lesson form gains the switch "Exige concluir a aula anterior", forced off on the first lesson of the course, mirroring the module rule.
  - A module with `requires_previous` stays locked until every live lesson of the previous module is complete.
  - A lesson with `requires_previous` stays locked until the previous live lesson in course order is complete.
  - The API refuses playback, materials and progress writes for a lesson locked by sequence. The web shows the lock, and the server is the authority.
  - **Reversibility:** costly. A new column, a new admin control and a server-evaluated rule.
- **D-429: A free lesson ignores the sequential lock and writes progress, even for a non-buyer.**
  - "Aula aberta" always plays, even behind a locked module.
  - A non-buyer's progress on it is stored, so after a purchase it already counts as complete and can unlock what follows.
- **D-430: The member sees their progress on the course page, in the lesson list and on the card, and the video resumes.**
  - The course page shows a progress bar and percent, plus a "Começar" / "Continuar" button to the next uncompleted lesson the member can open (the prototype's `courseProgress().nextLesson`).
  - Each lesson row shows a completed check.
  - The player resumes from `last_position_seconds`.
  - The catalogue card shows a progress bar once the member has completed at least one lesson.
  - The denominator is the course's live lessons, free lessons included.
  - Porting `ProgressBar` (and `LessonRow` / `LessonThumb`) from `reference/frontend-design/components/members/` belongs to this phase.

### Lives for the member
- **D-431: The Lives chip lists lives happening now and scheduled lives first, then recorded lives that have a replay.**
  - "Ao vivo agora" and scheduled lives come first, nearest first. Recorded lives with a replay follow, newest first.
  - A live that ended without a replay is not listed.
  - A cancelled live shows a "Cancelada" tag until its planned end has passed, then leaves the list.
  - A card shows the cover, the date and time in the tenant's timezone, the duration and the status.
  - A locked live is listed with the padlock tag, like a course.
  - Today the restrictive `course_lives_live_gate` policy hides a locked live's whole row from a member lane. That has to become a projection where title, cover, description and times are visible and `external_url` and the replay never are.
  - **Reversibility:** costly. The policy posture changes and becomes the member contract.
- **D-432: For a member with access, the join link leaves the server only from 15 minutes before the start until the planned end.**
  - Before that window the live page shows "Começa em …" with the date.
  - After it ends the page shows the replay, or nothing.
  - A member without access never receives `external_url`.
  - The admin keeps seeing the link in the admin form as today.
- **D-433: Either access to the live or access to the replay lesson's course opens the replay.**
  - A member with access to the live watches the replay on the live page even without the course. That path ignores the course's sequential lock.
  - A member with the course watches it as a normal lesson inside the course.
  - The lesson-asset gate (`app.media_asset_hidden`, the new lesson branch for T-08.3-05) must accept either door.
  - **Reversibility:** costly. Two doors on one asset, encoded in the gate function and its pgTAP truth table.
- **D-434: Each live has its own page.** The page lives under `/cursos/lives/{liveId}`, sharing the URL with the admin's live page per the route-layout default in Claude's Discretion.
  - It shows the cover, title, description, date and duration.
  - It shows "Entrar na live", "Começa em …" or the replay player.
  - When the live is locked, it shows the padlock and the D-424 buy section.
  - It is the destination for "Compra concluída", for shared links and for the live cards.

### Claude's Discretion
Each item has a stated default. The planner may refine it but should not reverse it without asking.

**Routes and roles**
- **Route layout.** The admin already owns `/cursos/[courseId]`, `/cursos/[courseId]/aulas/[lessonId]` and `/cursos/lives/[liveId]`.
  - Default: one URL per entity, with the view chosen by role. A manager gets the admin page with a "Ver como aluno" toggle (for example `?ver=aluno`), and a member gets the member page. Links from the Loja, notifications and shares then work for everyone.
  - The planner may give the member a flat lesson route (`/cursos/aulas/[lessonId]`, the prototype's flat `/membros/aulas/[lessonId]`) or move the admin pages under a sub-path, if it keeps every 08.3 e2e green.
- **`support_tenant`.** Default: sees the member views fully open (the gate answers `'{}'` for staff claims), and their own progress is written like a member's. Only `admin_tenant` writes none (D-422).

**Progress and locks**
- **Completion threshold and progress cadence.** Default: completion at 90% of `duration_seconds`.
  - `last_position_seconds` is written at most every 15 s while playing, plus on pause, end and `pagehide`, idempotently.
  - The server clamps the position to the asset duration.
  - Completion is one-way in this phase, with no "desmarcar".
- **Error vocabulary.** Default: an unpublished or unknown course or lesson is the bare 404. A lesson locked by purchase is `403` with `details.courses = 'lesson_locked'`. A lesson locked by sequence is `409` with `details.courses = 'lesson_sequence_locked'`, so the web can show the right notice.
- **Sequential lock across modules.** Default: "the previous lesson" is the previous live lesson in course order, which may be in the previous module. The first lesson of a module that has `requires_previous` is governed by the module rule.
- **Where the lock is evaluated.** Default: one SQL function per course (lesson id → lock reason) used by the routes, the playback gate and the progress write. The prototype's `resolveLock` stays in sync with it as a pure helper for the web. The researcher may choose otherwise if one source of truth remains server-side.

**Media and threats**
- **T-08.3-08 closure.** Default: extend `app.media_asset_hidden` to lesson materials (hidden when the lesson is hidden by purchase or by sequence), and have the `/original` path ask it for `file` kinds.
  - Accepting the residual is not an option: SC 3 says the phase closes it.
  - The member's material open goes through the 10-minute signed `…/materials/{materialId}/url`, which leaves `courses.manage` for a member lane with access.

**Edge cases**
- **Replay of a lesson in an archived or unpublished course.** Default: the replay keeps playing from the live page while the lesson itself is live (not soft-deleted), because the live is its own door (D-433). The planner may tighten this to "the course must be published" if the gate stays simpler.
- **A lesson under a soft-deleted module** (the 08.3-05 residual). Default: for a member it counts as deleted. It is not listed, not playable and not counted in progress.
- **Progress write on the live-page replay.** Default: it writes the same `course_lesson_progress` row (it is the same lesson), so a later course purchase sees it.
- **Materials on the live-page replay.** Default: they open through the same door as the video.
- **Live join window.** Default: 15 minutes before `starts_at`, until `starts_at + duration_min`, computed by the shared `liveStatusAt` helper on the server.
