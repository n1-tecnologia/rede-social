# Phase 05.2 Plan 10: The Seen Ring and the Resume Index Summary

**Seen state now lives on the server and follows the member to every device. A new `story_views` table records each story the viewer SHOWED. `POST /v1/stories/views` is member-reachable, always answers 204 and only writes in the caller's lane. Every story read carries the caller's own `viewerSeen`, at no extra statement. The Início tenant circle wears the brand ring and "… Há stories novos." while anything live is unseen, greys the moment the last one was shown, and opens at the first unseen story.**

## Accomplishments

- **`story_views`**, its own create-only migration (`20260926002810_story_views.sql`):
  - RLS and `story_views_tenant_isolation`.
  - `story_views_uq (tenant_id, user_id, story_id)` in the measured order. It is the idempotency arbiter, and it is what the ring's subplan probes.
  - The schema docblock states the privacy rule: only the caller's own rows are read, there is no event and no count, and "quem viu" is V2.
- **The write and the read (R-D-J):**
  - `markStoriesSeen` is ONE insert-select from `stories` in the caller's lane, with `on conflict do nothing`. It logs counts only.
  - The route is member-reachable (auth and module only), declared before `/{storyId}`, and answers 204 for every accepted body.
  - `storyProjection` now takes `ctx` and adds `viewer_seen` as an `exists` with a CONSTANT tenant predicate. All six callers moved.
- **The web:**
  - `markStoriesSeenAction` validates with the contract first, chunks at 50, and is silent: no `redirect`, no `revalidatePath`, failures logged by shape.
  - `StoriesSurface` keeps a session seen set and a buffer. The buffer flushes on close, on a group change (the viewer now reports the group key), at 10 ids and on `visibilitychange → hidden`.
  - The tenant circle's ring, name and resume index come from one pure `tenantSeenState`, used on the server's first render and on every close.
- **Proof:**
  - pgTAP 120 (arbiter, FK, cascade, catalogue, and the EXPLAIN pin on 400 stories and 20,000 views), plus 010/020.
  - Isolation `b8.` and the strip budget.
  - The seed carries both ring states, and a browser walk covers the seed state, the resume, the optimistic grey-out and the persistence across a reload.
