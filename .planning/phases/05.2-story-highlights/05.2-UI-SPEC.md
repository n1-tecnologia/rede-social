# Phase 05.2 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**What this phase is.** Stories start working like Instagram's (05.2-CONTEXT §Phase Boundary). The Início row changes from "one circle per story" to **[+ Seu story] [tenant circle] [highlight circles…] [manage circle]**. The community Destaques row becomes that community's named highlights. A new curation surface appears: a per-place manage screen, an edit sheet, and one shared highlight sheet in two modes. The viewer becomes group-aware, and the composer's "Publicar em" row becomes "Destaque". The design language, spacing scale, type scale, colour model and catalog conventions are **inherited unchanged** from the approved `05-UI-SPEC.md` and `05.1-UI-SPEC.md` (which inherit `02`/`03`/`04`). This document restates them for the checker and records only the 05.2 deltas.

**Upstream decisions.** `05.2-CONTEXT.md` decisions are cited as **D-100..D-116** and are not re-litigated. `05.2-RESEARCH.md` discretion resolutions are cited as **R-D-A..R-D-Q**, and its patterns and pitfalls as **R-Pn** / **Pitfall n**. Phase 5 and 05.1 visual decisions are cited as **UI-D-25..UI-D-58**. Questions CONTEXT left to Claude that are purely visual or interaction questions are decided here as **UI-D-59..UI-D-80**, continuing the numbering as CONTEXT instructs.

**Reversals this contract makes explicit.** Three earlier visual decisions are superseded here, because the product decisions behind them were reversed upstream:
- **UI-D-27** ("zero rings per story state") is superseded by UI-D-61. D-105 reverses D-79.
- **UI-D-30**'s "end of sequence closes the viewer" is superseded by UI-D-65. D-107 reverses the rule.
- **UI-D-41** (the "Fixar em comunidades" pin sheet) is superseded by UI-D-67. D-116 retires pins.

**UI-D-54..UI-D-58** (the 05.1 composer row, its sheet, its render rule, the landing and the archived race) are rewritten for highlights as UI-D-68..UI-D-71. The docblocks that encode the old rules must be rewritten in the same plan that changes the behaviour, so no comment contradicts the code: `StoriesStrip.tsx:18` (D-78), `StoryCircle.tsx:18-23` (D-79, R §Code Map), `StoryViewer.tsx:218-246` (end closes / start restarts) and `PinStorySheet.tsx` (UI-D-41).

**Design review (Phase 2 D-33).** Every surface in this phase is prototype-less except the highlight circle itself (`reference/frontend-design/app/(app)/community/[communityId]/page.tsx:85-105`, `lib/mock/community.ts:34`). **A sketch package (`/gsd-sketch 05.2`) rendered from this contract must be reviewed with the design team before the UI waves are coded.** It must show:
1. the admin and member Início rows (seen and unseen tenant ring, empty highlight, manage circle);
2. the community row;
3. the manage screen, populated and empty;
4. the edit sheet with its cover and "Adicionar stories" steps;
5. the highlight sheet in checklist mode and in single-select mode, including the "Novo destaque" step;
6. the composer's "Destaque" row in its three states;
7. the viewer's "Destacar" control.

The planner puts that review behind a `checkpoint:human-verify` before the first UI plan.

## Interaction & Accessibility

- **Links for routes, buttons for actions:** "+ Seu story", the manage circle and empty-highlight circles are `<a>`. Circles that open the viewer, sheet rows (single-select), the composer row, "Destacar", the drag handle and every sheet control are `<button>`. A `Switch` is never nested in a button (UI-D-67).
- **Accessible names are the visible catalog strings** or the listed `aria-label` keys. The seen state is carried in the tenant circle's name (UI-D-61), not only in colour.
- **Focus:** a sheet returns focus to its opener (BottomSheet). Body-step swaps move focus to the step's heading, and "Voltar" returns it to the control that opened the step. After "Criar destaque" on the manage screen, focus lands in the new highlight's edit sheet title. After a keyboard reorder, focus stays on the moved handle. The viewer's focus trap and return-to-originating-circle are unchanged, and the originating circle is the group's circle.
- **Live regions:** one polite region per surface. Viewer position (UI-D-65), reorder position (UI-D-73), upload progress at 25/50/75/100 (Phase 3). Toasts `role="status"`, and the composer alert `role="alert"`.
- **Motion:** `Reorder` layout animation and the viewer's group slide use shipped easings. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` makes both instant. The story clock is unaffected. All motion imports `motion/react`.
- **No role comparison in the web tier:** the manage circle, the manage routes, "Destacar", the composer row and the edit controls read `bootstrap.permissions.includes('stories.story.manage')`; "+" reads `stories.story.publish` (plus manage on communities, UI-D-53). The API re-enforces everything (R-D-K).
- **No `Date.now()` in render:** story dates in the edit sheet, picker and cover step are server-formatted (UI-D-14).
