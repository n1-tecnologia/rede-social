<objective>
Publishing asks for a highlight, and a community page shows its highlights (D-111..D-115,
HIGHLIGHT-04). At the API a story can be born inside an existing highlight — or inside a highlight
created in the same transaction — with 05.1's one-write rule and permission seam carried. The
composer's "Publicar em" row becomes the one "Destaque" row: zero taps to publish tenant-wide, and
from a community's "+" it arrives pre-filled with that community's first highlight — or, when the
community has none, it will not let the admin publish into "Nenhum" without seeing the choice. The
community page then shows only its highlights, which is where such a story lands.

Purpose: HIGHLIGHT-04 and HIGHLIGHT-02's publish route; retires 05.1's "Publicar em" row.
Output: the extended publish contract and transaction, the rewritten composer and community row,
and the rewritten 05.1 e2e flows.

Budget note: over a 100k projection because the composer's landing page must show the story it
landed on — the publish API, the composer and the community row ship together or the UAT replay
breaks between plans.

Task-count note: its three tasks (a raw tag count reads 4 because it also counts the tasks wrapper element) stay one plan because the publish API, the composer that calls it and the community row where the story lands are the one publish-into-a-highlight flow the e2e replays — split, the composer would land the admin on a page that cannot show the story.
</objective>

<planning_decisions>
1. **Destination first (05.1 planning decision 4, carried).** With a destination the transaction
   issues: highlight (or place + place lock) → asset → story insert → [highlight insert] → item
   insert → projection. A refused destination costs no asset validation and never follows a
   rolled-back insert. Without one, exactly today's three statements.
2. **`communityId` stays accepted until plan 11** (it still writes a pin), but nothing in the app
   sends it after this plan; the refine allows at most ONE of `communityId`, `highlightId`,
   `newHighlight`.
3. **The action learns the landing place from the composer.** `publishStoryAction(input, landing: {
   communityId: string | null })` revalidates `/inicio` and, for a community destination,
   `/comunidades/{id}`; `landing.communityId` is uuid-validated and used for nothing else.
</planning_decisions>
