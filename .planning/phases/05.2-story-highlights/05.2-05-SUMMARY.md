# Phase 05.2 Plan 05: The Grouped Story Viewer Summary

**`StoryViewer` now plays a row of circles. A tap past a group's last story enters the next circle, previous mirrors next, a swipe skips a whole circle, and the viewer closes after the row's last story. Every Início highlight circle opens its own stories, oldest first, expired ones included. They are loaded lazily through a new server action, prefetched at the previous group's last story, and keyed per segment so a story in two circles never shares state.**

## Accomplishments

- **The group-aware viewer (UI-D-65, R-D-M).** The position is the pair `(g, i)`. Next and previous cross group boundaries and mirror each other. The row's first story restarts and the row's last closes. A horizontal swipe skips a group, while ArrowLeft and ArrowRight keep tap semantics. `[]` groups are skipped in both directions. A `null` group shows the loading frame (header, one empty segment, `Loader2`, clock paused) and calls `onNeedGroup`. A `failed` group shows the group error with a retry. Close and swipe stay live over both frames.
- **Pitfall 4 end to end.** Every per-segment state map, handler, React key and progress-bar key is `${group.key}:${item.id}`. The host's play and comment-count registries are keyed per segment too.
- **`onSegmentShown(storyId)`** fires once per showing of the current segment once its media is ready. It never fires on mount or for a neighbour. Plan 10's seen state can use it as is.
- **The lazy highlight read.** `loadHighlightItemsAction` → `getHighlight` → `GET /v1/stories/highlights/{id}`. Items are mapped with the strip's own `storyViewerItem`, and `/inicio`'s SSR carries no highlight items. `StoriesSurface` keeps loaded groups for the page's life, dedupes in-flight reads, and marks and retries failures.
- **Every caller moved in the same commit.** Início passes `[tenant, …highlights]`, and its highlight circles are now `open` descriptors for groups 1..n. The community pinned row passes one `pins` group and the deep link one `story` group, so both open exactly as before.
- **The browser proves it.** Bastidores opens on the expired story (D-103, STORY-04 in the viewer). The row runs from the tenant circle into Bastidores and closes past its end. A left swipe skips to Bastidores, and a left-third tap returns to the tenant circle's last story.

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter. The two with product consequences:

- **Swipe = group skip, for every caller.** On the deep link and the community pinned row (a single group), a left swipe now closes the viewer and a right swipe returns to the first story. Before, both stepped one story. R-D-M's rule is stated per viewer, not per caller. The tap, the arrows and the clock are unchanged there.
- **Highlight groups never change the URL**, even when reopened with their items loaded. A refresh therefore always lands on `/inicio`, which can rebuild the row.

- **Files modified:** apps/web/components/stories/StoryViewerHost.tsx
