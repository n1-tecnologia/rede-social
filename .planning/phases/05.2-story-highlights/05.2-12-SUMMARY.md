## Decisions Made

- **Step 6 watches two images and pins the rest seen.** Under the fake provider, a video segment is never shown and so never recorded. Pinning the seeded video as seen is the same technique `stories.spec.ts`'s watch-through uses (R-D-I). The two unseen stories are the seed's newest image and step 2's photo, both asserted to be `image`.
- **The re-cover needs a contrast.** A highlight holding only the expired story already wears that frame automatically. Adding step 2's photo first makes the automatic cover that photo, so choosing the expired frame changes the row's `img` and the assertion can fail.
- **The second device is a new browser context with copied device options.** `browser.newContext()` does not inherit the project's `use`, so viewport, UA, scale, `isMobile` and `hasTouch` are copied from `testInfo.project.use`. Service workers stay blocked, as in every spec here.
- **Deep links are avoided for watching.** Every watch opens the viewer from the row, because a story watched through `/stories/{id}` is not recorded as seen (known, out of scope).
