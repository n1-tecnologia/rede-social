<planning_decisions>
1. **Group shape.** `StoryViewerGroup = { key: string; items: readonly StoryViewerItem[] | null;
   failed?: boolean; header: { name: string; avatar: ReactNode } }`. `items === null` means "not
   loaded"; `[]` means "loaded and empty" (skipped). `StoryViewerItem` keeps `authorName`, `avatar` and
   `timeLabel` unchanged — the host fills them per group (tenant: logo + display name; highlight:
   cover disc + title), and `header` is used only while a group has no items to show.
2. **Previous = exact mirror of next (R A2).** Tap-back at a group's first story lands on the previous
   group's LAST story; if that group is not loaded yet, the loading frame shows and the viewer lands
   on its last story when the items arrive.
3. **History entry.** Opening the tenant group still pushes `/stories/{id}` of the story it opens on;
   opening a highlight group pushes the current URL unchanged, so back closes either way and a refresh
   never lands on a route that cannot rebuild the row.
4. **One label for position:** `labels.position(group, current, total)` replaces the two-number
   version; the catalog key `stories.viewer.positionGroup` replaces `stories.viewer.position`.
</planning_decisions>
