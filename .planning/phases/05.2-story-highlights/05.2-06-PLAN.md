<objective>
The one highlight sheet D-110 asks for, and its first door: "Destacar" in the viewer.

The module gains `HighlightSheet` — the pin sheet's proven toggle machine (revert in place, in-flight
no-op, re-seed on target change) rebuilt around NAMED highlights grouped by place — in both modes
(the checklist the viewer and "Seus stories" use, and the single-select the composer uses in plan
08), plus the shared "Novo destaque" title step. The web gains the sheet's server-side plumbing
(one read that composes the catalogue, the story's memberships and the place names; one action per
toggle). Then a curator can open ANY story in the viewer, tap "Destacar", and put it into — or take
it out of — any highlight, with the story paused underneath.

Purpose: HIGHLIGHT-02's first route (D-110 route 1) and the component routes 2 and 3 reuse.
Output: the module sheet and title step with their tests, the sheet actions and view builder, the
viewer pill and overlay.

Budget note: over a 100k projection because the sheet, its plumbing and its first door are one
vertical slice; splitting the door from the plumbing would ship actions no screen calls.
</objective>

<planning_decisions>
1. **One read opens the sheet.** `loadHighlightSheetAction(storyId)` runs the catalogue, the story's
   memberships and the active communities list in parallel on the server and returns place groups
   with their labels — the viewer never assembles names on the client, and the sheet never opens on
   an unverified state (UI E09 loading).
2. **The viewer's toggles do not revalidate** (the like-action rule: re-rendering `/inicio` behind an
   open viewer would rebuild the row under the member's finger). The add/remove actions take a
   `revalidate` flag; the viewer passes `false`, "Seus stories" (plan 07) passes `true` and the action
   then revalidates the affected place (`/inicio` or `/comunidades/{id}`).
3. **Words stay in the app.** The module ships no strings; `StoryViewerHost` reads the sheet's labels
   with `useTranslations('stories')` on the client (the composer's precedent), so the three
   composition points pass only `canCurate`.
</planning_decisions>
