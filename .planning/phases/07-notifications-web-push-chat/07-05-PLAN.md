<objective>
Members who said they are going are reminded a day and an hour before the event, and every member hears about a new or reactivated event. The same seam delivers all four kinds.

**As a** member who confirmed `Vou`, **I want to** be reminded a day and an hour before my event starts, **so that** I actually show up. This story comes from EVENT-07 and ROADMAP Phase 7 SC 1 ("event reminders (24 h and 1 h before, from a scheduled job, only to members who confirmed)").

<planning_decisions>
1. **Arm in the producer's transaction, check at fire time** (RESEARCH Pattern 8, decided over a periodic scan). A scan would have to read events across tenants (admin lane, kernel-only), and a definer reachable from the tenant lane would leak other tenants' event times. Deferred per-event jobs armed in-tx need neither.
2. **The events module gets its own system context** (`eventsSystemCtx`: zero user, `role: 'member'`, `requestId: 'job:events'`). It cannot import the notifications module's, and reading `events` plus `event_attendances` needs no staff role.
3. **Fire-time `now` is injectable.** `runEventReminder(payload, nowMs)` is the handler body, and the `JobDefinition` passes `Date.now()`, so integration tests drive moved, late and cancelled cases without waiting (the `runInviteSendJobs` precedent).
4. **The 30-minute late tolerance** is fixed as the constant `EVENT_REMINDER_LATE_TOLERANCE_MINUTES = 30` in the events contracts.
5. **Reactivation dedupe uses the sink's `sinkAt`** (07-01 planning decision 7): `events.event_reactivated:{eventId}:{sinkAt}`.
</planning_decisions>
