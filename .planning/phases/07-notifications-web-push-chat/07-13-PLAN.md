<objective>
Close the third red of the exit gate: on desktop, `feed.spec.ts` "a double tap on the gallery likes exactly ONCE" fails intermittently (both full 07-11 runs, 1 of 3 alone, 1 of 4 in the verifier's `--repeat-each=4`). Its trace shows the gesture firing only `unlikePostAction`.

The orchestrator asked for a finding, not an assumption: can a member's double tap within about 1 s of load unlike a post (a product bug, fixed in product code), or does only the test need settling? Evidence gathered at planning time points at the test's shared state.

- The feed's double tap is the SAME toggle as the heart (`PostCard`: one toggle, three entry points). On an already-liked post it sends an unlike by design.
- `mobile-chromium` and `desktop-chromium` run this case one after the other on the same seeded member and gallery post.
- The case's cleanup asserts only the optimistic "Curtir", and the page closes right after, so its unlike can be lost.
- A like left over from an earlier run then turns the next double tap into exactly the unlike-only trace 07-11 recorded.

This plan proves or refutes that before it changes anything, and it never settles or loosens the test to hide a product failure.

Purpose: the gate's like cases measure the like path, not the residue of the previous run, and the product question gets a recorded answer.
