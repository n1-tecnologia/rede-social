<objective>
The staff side of support. The team sees every member's conversation in one list ordered by activity, can open and answer any of them, and shares one read state, on a phone and in the desktop split view.

**As a** member of my organisation's support team, **I want to** see every member conversation ordered by what happened last, open any of them and reply, and know which ones still wait for us, **so that** no member is left without an answer. This story comes from ROADMAP Phase 7 SC 3 ("`support_tenant` sees an inbox of member conversations ordered by last activity with unread indicators and can open and reply to any of them") and CHAT-03.

Purpose: it completes the chat loop on 07-08's inbox API and shared read state, reusing 07-09's pane and pieces. The inbox, the staff header and the split are [designed] (sketch items 1 and 2 plus the UI-D-263 header), so both tasks wait for the D-33 approval.
Output: `InboxRow`, the staff `ThreadHeader` variant, the shared `/suporte` layout with the split view, the staff thread route, the inbox BFF route, and the staff e2e.
