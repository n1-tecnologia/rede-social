## Phase Goal

**As a** member of my organization's community, **I want to** upload my photo from my phone, edit my name and bio, and find the other members by name, **so that** the community is made of recognisable people rather than anonymous accounts.

<objective>
The people-finding screens (PROF-02, PROF-03) plus the D-02 first-access nudge (PROF-01): `/membros` is a mobile-first, searchable, keyset-paginated list of the tenant's active members reached from the "Membros" row on `/perfil` (R-11 — deliberately **not** a fifth nav tab, because D-40's bar already reaches four by Phase 6), `/membros/[membershipId]` is the deliberately plain other-member profile (photo, name, bio and nothing else), and `/inicio` gains a dismissible card that invites a member who has neither a photo nor a bio to complete their profile.

Purpose: closes criterion 2 on the screen side and delivers the last user-visible half of PROF-01's nudge. It also ports the last two prototype components this phase needs (`SearchBar`, `UserListItem` → `MemberRow`) into `@tria/ui` and `apps/web/components/profile/`, which Phase 8's member management reuses unchanged.
Output: `SearchBar` in `@tria/ui`, `MemberRow` and `ProfileNudgeCard`, the `/membros` and `/membros/[membershipId]` routes with their loading and not-found shells, the `/inicio` composition point, the `members` pt-BR catalog, and a Playwright spec with a throwaway tenant big enough to prove a second page.

**The `[designed]` surfaces here — the directory page and the nudge card — went through the D-33 review and are specified in `03-UI-SPEC.md` (status `approved`, reviewed 2026-09-21).** Do not re-decide geometry, copy or state behaviour; every value in this plan is a citation of that contract.

</objective>
