## Phase Goal

**As a** member of my organization's community, **I want to** upload my photo from my phone, edit my name and bio, and find the other members by name, **so that** the community is made of recognisable people rather than anonymous accounts — and **as** the tenant's `admin_tenant`, **I want to** upload a phone-recorded video that just plays for everyone, **so that** the platform accepts every kind of media a real community produces without ever leaking one community's files to another.

<objective>
The phase's closing plan: the orphan sweeper that makes the broker's lifecycle complete (R-07), the two-tenant isolation suite extended to every surface this phase added (criterion 4's hard constraint), one mobile smoke spec that walks the whole phase goal, the `pnpm verify` exit gate, and the honest record of what could not be verified without a Mux account and a real device.

Purpose: TENANT-04's proof and the phase's exit criteria. `SCHEMA-CONVENTIONS` §(j) rule 2 makes extending the isolation suite mandatory for every new table, endpoint, bucket and topic — this phase added three tables, nine endpoints, one bucket and one provider, and the suite must grow with them. The sweeper closes the one lifecycle gap 03-01 deliberately left open: `deleteAsset` marks a row and leaves the bytes, and an abandoned upload leaves a `pending` row forever.
Output: `packages/core/server/media/sweep-job.ts` with its `purgeAsset` service half, the worker registration, the extended isolation suite in both Vitest and pgTAP, `apps/web/e2e/phase3-smoke.spec.ts`, the `docs/DEPLOY.md` runtime-state record, and a green `pnpm verify`.

**Decision — the sweeper re-arms itself; `boss.schedule()` stays unused.** RESEARCH R-07 is explicit and the repo has no scheduler in use: `kernel.domain-verify` paces itself at a ~10-minute cadence with deferred `startAfter` re-arms (`packages/core/server/jobs/boss.ts:148-151`). Introducing a second mechanism here would mean two ways to run periodic work, and the one the team already debugs would not be the one the sweeper uses.

**Decision — `purgeAsset` deletes the prefix, then the provider asset, then the row, in that order.** A row is the only pointer to the bytes; deleting it first would orphan them. Deleting bytes first means a failure leaves a still-collectable row, which the next run retries — the same "failure leaves a recoverable state" discipline 02-09 established for `checkDomain`'s side effects.
</objective>
