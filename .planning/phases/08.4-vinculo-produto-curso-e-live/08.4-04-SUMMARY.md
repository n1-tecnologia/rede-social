# Phase 08.4 Plan 04: Product form course and live pickers Summary

**The product form now has "Cursos liberados" and "Lives liberadas" blocks, gated on the courses module plus `courses.manage`. They save `courseIds` / `liveIds` only when present or changed, and they open the extended `CoursePickerSheet`, whose metas are built on the server. Refusals show under their own block, a failed list never reads as an empty school, and focus follows each removal.**

## Accomplishments

- `CoursePickerSheet` gains `meta`, `thumbShape` (`wide` = `w-12 aspect-[16/10]` and wide skeletons) and `emptyIcon`. With none of them passed it renders as in 08.3, and the track form suite is unchanged and green.
- Two walked list loaders:
  - `listProductFormCourses` uses the `all` filter (4 pages of 50).
  - `listProductFormLives` reads upcoming then past (each 4 pages of 50) and drops cancelled rows.
  - Both answer `{ items, failed, capped }` and log the shape only.
- New views `productFormCourseView` ("Rascunho" on drafts) and `productFormLiveView` (`liveDateParts` in the tenant timezone plus the shipped status words). `productFormDefaults(product, t, timeZone)` now maps the detail's `courses` / `lives`.
- Both pages compute one gate: `courses` module plus `COURSE_PERMISSIONS.manage`. When it is off they pass `null` and nothing is fetched or sent.
- The form clones the communities block as `LinkBlock`:
  - Selection and dirty check are kept per block. Newly picked rows come first.
  - The create body carries each key when its block is present. The patch carries a key only when its set changed.
  - The lock preview stays communities-only (`lockPreviewAction` count unchanged at 3).
- Refusals, degraded lists and focus:
  - The four issue codes show as `role="alert"` field errors under their block. The cap comes from `STORE_MAX_LINKS`.
  - A failed list read keeps the saved rows and shows "Não foi possível carregar …" in the sheet.
  - An empty school shows the GraduationCap or Radio empty state, with no action.
  - After an `X`, focus moves to the next row, else the previous one, else the open button.
  - All three blocks are inert while the save runs.
- `store.courses.json` (root `store`) holds every new string. `messages.test.ts` checks the root, that no leaf is shared with `store.json`, every placeholder, and the formatted outputs.

## Deviations from Plan

### Design-consistency adjustment

**1. The picker sheet's meta rows keep the 14/400 title**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The Task 1 action text asks for a "14/700 truncating title" in a sheet row that carries a meta. UI-SPEC UI-D-415 sets no weight for the sheet title, and the shipped 08.3 sheet uses 14/400. The course sheet mixes draft rows (with meta) and published rows (without meta), so a bold title on meta rows only would give one list two title weights.
- **Fix:** Meta rows keep `text-sm font-normal` for the title and add the 12/400 `text-text-secondary` `truncate` meta under it. The block rows (UI-D-414) do use the 14/700 title.
- **Files modified:** packages/modules/courses/ui/CoursePickerSheet.tsx
- **Committed in:** ff077d9a

---

**Total deviations:** 1 (a design-consistency adjustment, no scope change).
**Impact on plan:** None on behaviour or on the contract. If the design owner wants bold titles on every sheet row, it is a one-class change.
