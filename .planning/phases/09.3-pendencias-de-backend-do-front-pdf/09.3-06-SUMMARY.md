## Resolved: the P41 plan pin (was `blocked: policy plan regression`)

`supabase/tests/180-store.sql` test 121 (P41, index usability of `feed_posts_tenant_created_all_idx` for the member-lane merged feed) went red under the new policy in the full pgTAP run only. Attribution at the time: HEAD without plan 06 green; with plan 06 only test 121 red; the file alone green; a variant policy with `cardinality(...) = 0 or` still red; with the new policy dropped the pin was already marginal after pgTAP residue. Cause: every restrictive clause of the form `community_id is null or community_id <> all (unknown array)` is estimated with a tiny selectivity, so adding one more flips an ordered index walk under `limit 11` to a bitmap scan plus sort in that fixture.

**Owner decision (Igor):** keep the policy exactly as committed and re-pin P41 by measuring the index's usability with `set local enable_bitmapscan = off` / `reset enable_bitmapscan` around that EXPLAIN (commit `5c5c392c`, the only change to `180-store.sql`, +11 lines, no assertion text loosened). After it the full suite was green and stayed green on this resume. `201-feed-social-gate.sql` was never touched.
