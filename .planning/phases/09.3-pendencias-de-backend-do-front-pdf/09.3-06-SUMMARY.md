**Why it happens (analysis, not a fix):** every restrictive clause of the form `community_id is null or community_id <> all (unknown array)` is estimated with a very small selectivity (the planner assumes a ten-element array). Adding one more shrinks the estimated result of the merged-feed statement from 78 to 30 rows, so an ordered walk of `feed_posts_tenant_created_all_idx` under `limit 11` looks more expensive than a bitmap scan on `feed_posts_tenant_author_idx` plus a sort. The policy text is already the InitPlan form decision 7 asks to check first. 090-feed.sql, 130-reels.sql and 201-feed-social-gate.sql (the other three EXPLAIN-asserting files) stayed green with the policy active.

**What I did NOT do:** loosen or edit 180-store.sql, change the policy into a shape the plan did not specify, or add a planner hint. Whether the right answer is a different policy shape, a plan-support function, a statistics change, or re-pinning P41 is the owner's call, since the owner chose this backstop knowing it touches the hot-path RLS.

**State left behind:** the work is committed locally (`a833bdc3`), unpushed, and applies cleanly from scratch (`db:reset`). Until this is resolved the full pgTAP run is red on one pin, so plan 06 must not ship. The local database was reset and seeded with the committed migrations at the end.
