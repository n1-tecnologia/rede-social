## Finding for the owner (planning decision 6, carried from plan 05)

The Loja keeps links to archived communities when a product is edited, and the product form cannot list them. So for a community archived while an active product links it, the way out of the refusal is: reactivate the community, remove it from the product, archive it again, then delete. The approved copy does not say "reactivate first". This plan ships the copy as approved; the wording is for the owner to decide, and plan 09 carries it as a manual UAT row.
