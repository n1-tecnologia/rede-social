# Phase 09.3 Plan 05: Delete a community Summary

**`DELETE /v1/communities/{communityId}` soft-deletes an archived community behind a tenant-scoped `FOR UPDATE` read, refuses a live one as `not_archived` and a still-unlocked one as `unlock_active` with up to five product names plus the total, and leaves the Loja and every highlight row untouched.**

## Accomplishments

- Tracer through every layer: contract vocabulary and refusal schema, the service, the route behind the literal `communities.community.manage`, the isolation classification and sweep, and an end-to-end test where an admin archives then deletes a community and it leaves both lists and answers the bare 404 on the detail.
- The two refusals and the no-oracle rule: the locking read carries the tenant predicate and runs before any state check, so an active or archived lab community answers the same bytes as an unknown uuid, in the sweep and in case 12.
- The Loja refusal: names newest first with an `id desc` tie-break (proved with two products at the same instant), at most 5 names, exact total, archived products and a disabled Loja do not block, names returned byte for byte (accents, a 4-byte emoji, markup, the 80-code-point maximum).
- The lock race is proved deterministically: an uncommitted link insert holds the community row through its foreign key, the delete is shown to wait for 400 ms, the commit is released, and the delete answers `unlock_active` listing the product. With `FOR UPDATE` removed the case fails (mutation checked, then restored).
- Hidden, not erased: the highlight rows keep their counts, the place read is the bare 404, the catalogue and the curator sheet ids drop the highlight; the one leak found (`listStoryHighlightIds`) got the one-line filter, and reverting it makes case 10 fail.

## Finding for the owner (planning decision 6, not a plan change)

The Loja deliberately keeps links to archived communities on a product edit (the CR-02 rule in `replaceLinks`: the product form lists only active communities). So for a community archived while an active product links it, the admin's way out of `unlock_active` is: reactivate the community, remove it from the product in the product form, archive it again, then delete. The approved refusal copy ("Tire a comunidade dele primeiro") does not say "reactivate first". D-554 is implemented exactly as locked; the wording is for the owner to decide in a later phase and appears as a manual UAT row in plan 09.
