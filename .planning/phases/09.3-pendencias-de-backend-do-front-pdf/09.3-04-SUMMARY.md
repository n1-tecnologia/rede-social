# Phase 09.3 Plan 04: Delete an event Summary

**`DELETE /v1/events/{eventId}` soft-deletes any event of the tenant with one guarded UPDATE (204, bare 404 on a miss, silent, every row kept), and one `create or replace` migration keeps the member's "Sua atividade" attendance counting a deleted event.**

- **Duration:** 9 min

- **Completed:** 2026-10-26T21:15:14Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 14 (4 created, 10 modified)

## Accomplishments

- Tracer through every layer: the service function, the route behind the literal `events.event.manage`, the isolation classification and sweep, and an end-to-end test where an admin delete makes the event answer the bare 404 on ten surfaces while its secrets and the member's answer stay.
- Silence proved by snapshot: after a delete the bus saw nothing, the notifications table, the `events.reminder` and `notifications.fanout` job counts and every kept row (answer, check-in, photo, attempts, secrets) are identical; the old bell row is still in the member's list with `removed: false` while its link is the bare 404.
- Edge items under real concurrency: two DELETEs give `[204, 404]`, a PUT, a member read and an RSVP racing a DELETE never leave a live event, a 500 or a lost answer; a tied neighbour keeps its place and a cursor issued before the delete resumes at the neighbour.
- Migration `20261026211152_dashboard_activity_keeps_deleted_events.sql` (generated with `pnpm db:generate --custom`): the previous function byte for byte minus the `e.deleted_at is null` line in the `att` CTE, `e.status = 'active'` kept, the `revoke all` re-stated.
- pgTAP 146 (16 assertions) and four new 198 assertions (109-113 range). Applied against the previous function the two new "still counts" assertions fail (111 and 113), so they do discriminate.
