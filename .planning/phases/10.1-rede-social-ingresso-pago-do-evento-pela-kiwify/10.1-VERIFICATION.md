# Phase 10.1: Kiwify event ticket - Verification Report

**Phase Goal:** An event can be linked to a Kiwify product (not a store product of the system); a member's presence is confirmed only after that product's purchase is approved, matched by the gateway product id and the buyer's e-mail; a refund or chargeback removes the presence and frees the seat; a payment that arrives at a full event still confirms; an event with no linked product keeps the automatic RSVP.
**Verified:** 2026-11-13T13:00:00Z
**Status:** gaps_found
**Re-verification:** No - initial verification

## Verdict in one paragraph

The payment path is built and wired end to end: mapping kind `event`, guard trigger and member-lane policies, the definers for grant/revoke/courtesy/doors/reads, the apply job, the Pagamentos and Participantes surfaces, the notification, and the web form/zone/doors. The behavior is proved by 322 passing pgTAP assertions (re-run here), 41/41 turbo typecheck+test at HEAD (recorded by the orchestrator) and an 8/8 Playwright spec. One must-have clause is actually falsified: the database does not stop a member from rewriting `origin` and `order_ref` on their own paid or courtesy row (CR-01, reproduced). It is not reachable through the application API or the intentionally closed Data API, so it is a defense-in-depth failure of an explicit "the database must refuse" requirement (TICK-06 / D-741), but it is a real one and one migration plus a pgTAP case closes it. Owner proofs (P1..P19, including TICK-24) remain pending by design. Status is therefore `gaps_found`, not `human_needed`.
