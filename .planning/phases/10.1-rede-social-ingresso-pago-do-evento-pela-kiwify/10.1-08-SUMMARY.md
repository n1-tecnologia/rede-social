# Phase 10.1 Plan 08: The web's server-side view-models for the paid event Summary

**Pure, clock-free view-models turn the paid-event API facts into the finished zone, tag, banner, check-in section, attendee marks and delivery rows the screens of plans 11 to 14 will draw, with every buy href forced through an https-only guard and the production build proving no server-only import leaks.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 9 min
- **Completed:** 2026-11-12 (UTC 2026-11-13T02:34Z)
- **Tasks:** 3 (the third is a gate and wrote no code of its own)
- **Files modified:** 10 (3 created, 7 modified), 83 KB of diff

## Accomplishments

- `eventSaleState` gives the member's zone as one fact from `nowMs`: cancelled, ended, under way, unavailable, full, buy. The precedence, the millisecond boundaries (one ms before `starts_at` is buy, the start is salesClosed, `ends_at` is empty) and the capacity boundaries (`capacity - 1` buys, `capacity` is sold out, a null capacity never is) are unit cases.
- The card gains the `Pago` tag, the price-first note (`R$ 80,00 · Últimas 3 vagas`) and the `, evento pago` / `, evento pago, R$ 80,00` accessible-name suffix; the detail gains the tag, the sale zone, the confirmed banner in its three variants, the refunded lines and the paid cancelled body; the action state gains the zone, origin, validated href and price label and never the meeting URL.
- The check-in pass gets the `paid` section (precedence done, cancelled, closed, paid, notOpenYet, open), the enter gate maps `ticket_required` to the `ingresso` notice, and attendee rows carry the origin, `Reembolsado` and `Acima do limite` pills in the fixed order with `removeCourtesy` / `release` as the only actions.
- `apps/web/lib/event-ticket.ts` holds `safeCheckoutHref` (https only, no credentials), the price label and parser, the Ingresso section state machine and the server-issue to field-key map.
- `eventRowView` words a ticket delivery (Ingresso product line, removed-event line, the outcome effect lines, the two not-confirmed lines with the gateway name); `getEventMapping` and `loadCourtesyHolders` are the two new server loaders.
