# Phase 10.1 Plan 13: Closed doors, bell row, ticket deliveries and manual release Summary

**A member without a presence is turned away at both doors with a sentence and a validated buy link, the payer gets a "Presença confirmada" bell row, and the admin reads ticket deliveries with their reasons and can release a Sem conta one by hand through a Ticket dialog with the ended and cancelled refusals.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 12 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-13T12:00Z (approximate; the start time was not captured)
- **Completed:** 2026-11-13T12:12Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 25 (3 created, 22 modified)

## Accomplishments

- **Online door (task 1, tracer).** `entrar/route.ts` sends `ticket_required` to `?motivo=ingresso`; `aviso/page.tsx` now draws the fourth reason (the plan 08 exclusion is gone) with `Ticket` 28, "Este evento tem ingresso", the body, then the brand "Comprar ingresso" anchor only when `eventSaleState` says `buy` (one `loadEvent` read, any failure draws the screen without it), then "Ver evento". Unknown, repeated, wrong-case or absent `?motivo=` still redirect silently to the detail.
- **Venue door.** `CheckinState` draws the `paid` section (`Lock` 28, "O check-in é só para quem tem ingresso.") with the same actions stack; `CheckinForm` maps the API's `payment_required` to that sentence inline plus a refresh; `checkInEventAction` accepts the new closed code. A non-holder never gets the code form or the "abre 1 hora antes" sentence (the precedence was plan 08's, now drawn).
- **Bell row.** `payments.ticket_confirmed` renders as an actor-less `Ticket` disc, `<b>Presença confirmada</b> em “{event}”.`, link to `/eventos/{id}` degrading to `/eventos`, retracted variant the shipped sentence; a title with `<script>` renders as text.
- **Deliveries and release.** `EventsList` passes the adapter name into the row view (the two not-confirmed effect lines need it), shows the ticket product line, and opens a `Ticket` brand dialog "Confirmar presença de {email} em {evento}?" for event deliveries. Success settles the row from the server answer with the event-naming toast; `no_membership` leaves the row; `event_ended` and `event_cancelled` toast their sentence, confirm nothing and refresh.
- Tests: web 371 files / 8767 passed, module-payments 119 passed, module-notifications 187 passed, payments-neutral 12 passed.
