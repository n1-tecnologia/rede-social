# Phase 10.1 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Scope.** The tenant can sell an event. `admin_tenant` links a gateway product to the event; a member without a presence buys it at the gateway and the presence appears by itself once the webhook confirms. This document contracts only what is new or changes:

1. the **EventForm "Ingresso" section**: switch, product code, purchase link, reference price, and its module-off, disconnected, locked and recurring states (UI-D-741 to UI-D-743);
2. the **poster card** and the **event detail hero**: the "Pago" tag and the optional price (UI-D-744, UI-D-745);
3. the **detail action zone** of a paid event for a member without a presence, every state, the buy control and the live swap to confirmed (UI-D-746 to UI-D-748);
4. the **"Presença confirmada" block** (paid, courtesy, before the ticket), the refunded and cancelled variants, and the give-up rules (UI-D-749, UI-D-750);
5. the **closed doors**: online "Entrar", the in-person check-in route, and stale-tab refusals (UI-D-751 to UI-D-753);
6. **Participantes** (admin): origin marks, "Liberar presença", removing a courtesy, the "Acima do limite" marks (UI-D-754 to UI-D-757);
7. the **Pagamentos hub "Eventos recentes"**: ticket deliveries, reasons, and "Liberar manualmente" for tickets (UI-D-758, UI-D-759);
8. the **"Presença confirmada em {evento}"** bell row and push (UI-D-760);
9. loading, error, accessibility, themes, glyphs, catalogs and trust rules (UI-D-761 to UI-D-766).

The poster, the hero, the info grid, the check-in banner, the calendar pair, the "Garantir minha vaga" flow of a **free** event, the check-in ticket for a member with a presence, the Participantes chips and code card, the Pagamentos shell and tabs, the store product form and the notification list geometry are **reused unchanged** from `06-UI-SPEC.md`, `09.1-UI-SPEC.md` and the Phase 7 notification rows. **An event with no linked product is not touched by any rule below.**

**Design authority.** `reference/frontend-design/app/(app)/events/[eventId]/page.tsx` (checked 2026-11-12) draws, for a paid ticket: a `SpotsPill` ("Esgotado" / "Últimas N vagas"), an "Inscrição confirmada / Ingresso … · pagamento aprovado" green block, and a gold CTA. Phase 6 already ported the spots wording (the poster note, the "Vagas" info cell, the disabled "Vagas esgotadas" CTA) and dropped the confirmed block. Therefore:

- **[proto delta]** (one surface): the **"Presença confirmada" block** (UI-D-749) is derived from the prototype's "Inscrição confirmada" block (`CheckCircle2` 20, `rounded-xl` tinted green panel, 14/700 title, 12/400 tertiary line), minus the example ticket code and the invoice, and re-voiced because the presence comes from a real payment, a courtesy or an earlier answer.
- **[reused]**: the spots note and cell, the disabled brand CTA, the checked-in banner, the poster tag ground, the Pagamentos row and the confirm dialog.
- **[designed]** (everything else): the form section, the "Pago" tag, the paid action zone, the closed-door screens, the origin marks, the release sheet, the ticket delivery rows and the bell row. They are drawn in the prototype's language from primitives that ship in `@rede-social/ui`, `@rede-social/core/ui` and the module UI packages.

The design language, spacing scale, typography scale and colour model are **inherited unchanged** from the approved `02` to `10-UI-SPEC.md`. This document restates them for the checker and records only the Phase 10.1 deltas.

**Upstream.**
- `10.1-CONTEXT.md` decisions are cited as **D-740..D-758** (locked, never re-asked). Carried-forward ids keep their own numbers (D-201..D-219 events, D-488..D-509 payments).
- **UI decision ids claimed: UI-D-740..UI-D-766, inside the separate block UI-D-740..UI-D-779.** The highest id in any UI-SPEC is **UI-D-720** (Phase 10), checked 2026-11-12 with `grep -rhoE "UI-D-[0-9]+" .planning | sort -t- -k3 -n -u | tail -1`. Phase 11 has not been discussed and will continue from UI-D-721; to keep the two runs from colliding, this phase mirrors its decision block (D-740..D-779) and owns UI-D-740..UI-D-779.

## Motion & Accessibility

- **No new motion.** The swap to confirmed is a re-render; sheets and dialogs use the shipped transitions. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` nothing changes.
- The paid zone sits in a **persistent `aria-live="polite"` wrapper**; the confirmed block is announced when it appears. Static notices (locked, over-limit, module-off) are text present at load, not live regions. Field errors use the form's inline error slots (`role="alert"` where the form already does).
- Every interactive element is at least 44px: `h-12` brand buttons, `hitArea` on every `sm`, the switch row `min-h-11`, rows `min-h-14` or `min-h-18`.
- "Comprar ingresso" is a link that opens a new tab and says so to assistive technology; it carries no `data-no-prefetch` need (it is external) and never fires by render (D-218).
- Dialog focus: the safe action (cancel) first for danger dialogs, focus returns to the opener; a confirmed row action leaves focus on the row; a swap moves no focus.
- The marks are words; "Pago", "Cortesia", "Reembolsado" and "Acima do limite" are never distinguished by colour alone.
