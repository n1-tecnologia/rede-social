## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Plan inconsistency] "A member's own Vou over capacity is `event_full` and after start `rsvp_closed` in the same event"**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** in a MAPPED event, step 3b (placed by the plan and the research before steps 5 and 7) fires first, so a member's Vou there is always `payment_required`, which is also the right answer: the paid path passes both the limit and the start, so paying is the way in.
- **Fix:** the member negatives `event_full` and `rsvp_closed` are proved as controls in FREE events beside the paid-path positives (pgTAP 2b, `events-capacity` #9), and the mapped-event member Vou is asserted `payment_required`. No guard change.
- **Files modified:** `supabase/tests/233-event-tickets.sql`, `apps/api/tests/integration/events-capacity.test.ts`

**2. [Rule 2 - robustness] Bookkeeping exit compares the whole row, placed before the event read**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** the plan described a column list; a list silently exempts any future column and, placed after the event read, a refund flag on a soft-deleted event would raise `event_not_found` inside the apply job.
- **Fix:** `to_jsonb(new) - <5 keys> = to_jsonb(old) - <5 keys>`, right after step 1.
- **Commit:** `b29a002f`

**3. [Rule 3 - repo state] `_journal.json` loses its trailing newline when drizzle-kit rewrites it**
- Restored by hand in both migration commits, as in plan 01.

**Total deviations:** 3 (one plan wording clarified, one hardening, one cosmetic). **Impact:** none on the contract; behaviour matches every truth in the plan.
