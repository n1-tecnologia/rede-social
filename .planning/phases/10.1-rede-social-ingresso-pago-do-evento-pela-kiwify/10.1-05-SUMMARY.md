# Phase 10.1 Plan 05: The webhook half of the paid event Summary

**An approved purchase of an event product now confirms the buyer's presence through the plan 04 definers and tells them once; a refund or chargeback removes it; Eventos recentes and Liberar manualmente understand ticket deliveries, unconfirmed accounts included.**

## Accomplishments

- `applyInTx` dispatches on the mapping kind with a `switch` whose default is `unsupportedMappingKind(kind: never)`; the store branch is no longer the fallthrough. `applyTicket` follows the order of `applyStore`: reference and event check, the type branch (only `purchase_approved` grants, only `purchase_refunded` and `chargeback` revoke), the e-mail check, the live-member lookup of any role, the stale fact, then the definer call with an explicit tenant, mapped outcome by outcome to a returned settlement. No business case can end `failed`.
- `payments.ticket_confirmed` is emitted after commit beside `payments.purchase_confirmed`; its notification source notifies only the payer (in-app and push, title the tenant, body `Presença confirmada em {evento}` cut on a word to 100 graphemes, url `/eventos/{id}`), with the event id and title as the only facts.
- The deliveries list carries `kind: 'event'` and `eventTitle` for a ticket delivery only (optional keys of the strict schema), uses `app.event_title_for` for the product line, and shows `event_ended` / `event_cancelled`.
- `grantEventManually` accepts a `sem conta` ticket delivery: it finds the live member by the stored e-mail, re-runs the stale check, calls `app.event_gateway_grant` through the admin lane with the admin-release flag (the one way an unconfirmed account is confirmed), settles the delivery in the same transaction and tells the payer. The store path is byte-identical.
- 54 tests added: 30 apply cases (the tracer among them), 2 cross-tenant webhook cases, 9 list and release cases, 2 notification cases and 11 module and contract unit cases.
