<objective>
The gate reaches every feed consumer of a locked community's content (ROADMAP SC 4 and SC 7, decisions (8), (10) and (11)).

**As a** member who has not bought a community's product, **I want to** see that community's newest post as a sample and nothing else of it anywhere, **so that** the store's promise is real: hiding it in the UI is never enough (SC 7). Derived from the ROADMAP goal and SC 7.

Plan 01 made the community page stop at the sample. RLS admits the sample on purpose, so Início and Reels must exclude it explicitly (Pitfall 3); interactions must be refused with a distinguishable answer on the sample and with a bare 404 on hidden content; the share target must say "locked" without revealing the post; and the comments and likes tables need the same restrictive guard so a forgotten route still cannot leak or write.

Output: the access vocabulary, the optional contract keys, two migrations (the definer lookups and the generated restrictive policies), the service predicates and guards, pgTAP additions, and `store-gate.test.ts`.

**Commits.** No `Co-Authored-By` trailer. Stage only the files listed.
</objective>
