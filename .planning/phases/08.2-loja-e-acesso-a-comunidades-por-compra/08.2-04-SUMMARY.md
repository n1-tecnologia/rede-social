# Phase 08.2 Plan 04: Highlights, Notification Fan-out and Video Playback Gated Summary

**A locked community's content no longer leaves through the three side doors outside the feed. Its highlights answer the bare 404 at the stories place seam. The notification worker sends a gated community's posts only to holders (`app.community_viewer_ids`) and drops reply and comment-liked rows for a recipient the community is locked for. Playback tokens are refused for a video that `app.media_asset_hidden` reports hidden.**

## Accomplishments

- **Highlights (D-355, STORE-18).** `resolveHighlightPlace` now selects `(c.id = any (LOCKED_COMMUNITY_IDS)) as locked` in its existing community probe. A `read` of a locked community throws the same bare 404 as a missing one (D-23). Write intents are untouched. The gate function reads the store flag itself, so no TS flag read joins the transaction. The stories README lists the kernel file.
- **Notifications (STORE-19, Pitfall 6).** The worker lane is `support_tenant`, which the lane form of the gate lets through, so the resolvers ask about recipients instead. `post.published` with a community asks `app.community_viewer_ids`: null keeps `members`, and an array becomes a `users` audience, empty meaning nobody. This covers reels in a community too. `comment.liked` and `comment.created` read `p.community_id` and drop the recipient when `lockedCommunityIdsFor(recipient)` contains it. Push follows in-app because the push channel pushes exactly the in-app recipients.
- **Playback (D-356, T-08.2-10).** `playbackTokens` runs `select app.media_asset_hidden(asset)` inside the transaction that reads the asset, and treats `hidden` like a miss before any token is minted. `serveVariant` is unchanged and carries a comment citing Assumption A6 / Open Question 2.

## Decisions Made

- The gated audience applies to any post with a gated community, whatever its kind. A video post in a community is `feed.reel` (D-226), not `feed.community_post`, and without this its excerpt would still reach every member by push.
- Every gate probe fails closed. A missing viewer row means nobody, a missing lock row means locked, and a missing hidden row means hidden. These cases cannot happen with the real functions, but the unit fakes pin the behaviour.
- The `notifications` cases use their own locked community and product (`trancada-avisos`). Publishing into the 08.2-03 locked community would have moved its sample, and the `agreement` and `playback` cases depend on that sample.
- The revoke in the test is a direct ledger update through `adminSql`. The grant and revoke routes belong to a later plan.
