<assumption_delta_decision>
The assumption-delta detector fired (`pluralization` on "Second round, 2026-10-08", `optional` on "each optionally linked", `chosen` on the product-choice pop-up). The first and last are prose false positives (a second discussion round; UI wording). The substantive change under them: community read access was DERIVED from tenant membership alone (COMM-02, D-73: "every member sees every community", an absence of any predicate) and now depends on membership AND, for a product-linked community, an entitlement.
- **Primary noun:** "the communities locked for this viewer", answered by ONE kernel-named SQL seam, `app.community_locked_ids()` (lane form) and `app.community_locked_ids_for(user)` (explicit form), whose body the store module supplies from `store_product_communities ⋈ store_entitlements ⋈ tenant_modules`.
- **Decision: promote.** The seam becomes the single answer every consumer asks (RLS on `feed_posts`/`feed_comments`/`feed_likes`, the feed, stories and media services, the notification worker). The old rule "membership ⇒ every community" is demoted to one variant of it: the seam returns the empty set when the store is off or a community has no product. Not add-alongside: Option A (materialising access into `community_members` beside the membership rule) is rejected because it would keep two sources of truth that can drift (RESEARCH §Entitlement Storage).
- **Invariant test (accepted):** "for every combination of viewer role × link state × entitlement state × store flag, every consumer agrees with `app.community_locked_ids_for(viewer)`". Here it is the pgTAP 180 truth table; plan 03 adds the feed, interaction and share-target agreement cases and plan 04 the highlights, notification-audience and playback cases, all in `apps/api/tests/integration/store-gate.test.ts`.
</assumption_delta_decision>
