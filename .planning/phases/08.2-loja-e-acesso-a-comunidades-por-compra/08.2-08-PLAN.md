<objective>
The purchase pop-up (D-358, D-361, UI-D-370, UI-D-371): the one place a member confirms a purchase.

**As a** member, **I want to** tap "Comprar", confirm in a pop-up and land on the communities I just unlocked, **so that** buying feels like part of my community app, not a checkout from somewhere else. Derived from SC 3 and D-358.

Output: the `PurchaseDialog` module component, the server action and API client call, the product page's action zone for a product the viewer does not hold, the return path to a locked community, every refusal state, and the `purchase` e2e cases.

**Commits.** No `Co-Authored-By` trailer. Stage only the files listed.
</objective>
