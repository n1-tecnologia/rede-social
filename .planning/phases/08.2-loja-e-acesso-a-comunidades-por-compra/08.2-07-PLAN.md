<objective>
The member's and the manager's way into the store: the TopBar slot, the Loja grid with its chips, the product page, the Configurações row, every catalog string of the phase, and the seed that turns the store on for rede-demo.

**As a** member, **I want to** open "Loja" from the top bar, browse my community's products and open one to see what it unlocks; **as an** `admin_tenant`, **I want to** reach the same screen from Configurações and see my archived products, **so that** the store is one screen for everyone. Derived from SC 3 and D-350..D-353, D-362.

The purchase pop-up (plan 08), the locked community (plan 09), the product form (plan 10) and Compradores (plan 11) build on these pages. The action zone of a product the viewer does not hold ("Comprar"/"Obter") is built in plan 08 together with the pop-up it opens.
