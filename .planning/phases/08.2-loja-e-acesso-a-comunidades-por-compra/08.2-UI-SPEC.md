# Phase 08.2 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Design authority.** `reference/frontend-design/` has **no store, product, checkout, price, buyers or locked-community screen** (ROADMAP § Phase 08.2 UI hint, checked 2026-10-08: no loja, checkout, carrinho or price UI). The closest visual language is the prototype's courses grid with a locked "Premium" course (`reference/frontend-design/app/membros/cursos/page.tsx`, `components/members/CourseCard.tsx`: a 4:5 poster, a veil, a pill at the top left, a centred lucide `Lock`). Every Phase 08.2 surface is therefore **[designed]** in the prototype's language from primitives that already ship: the communities list and page (`CommunityCard`, `CommunityHeader`, `CommunityPickerSheet`), the events poster and detail page (`EventPoster`'s 4:5 geometry and over-media pill, the "Gerenciar evento" card), the Participantes list (`AttendeeRow`), the admin search (`SearchBar`, `getAdminMembers`), `ConfirmDialog`, `BottomSheet`, `Chip`, `StatusPill`, `Skeleton`, `EmptyState`, `InfiniteScroll`. The design language, spacing scale, type scale and colour model are **inherited unchanged** from the approved `02`–`08-UI-SPEC.md`. This document restates them for the checker and records only the Phase 08.2 deltas.

**Sketch gate (D-365).** Unlike Phases 8 and 08.1, this phase **has** a sketch gate: the seven prototype-less surfaces are drawn in `.planning/sketches/008-phase-08.2-designed-screens/` from this contract (store grid with chips; product page; purchase pop-up and its success state; locked community page with sample post, placeholders, count line, top section and product-choice sheet; locked and archived list-card tags; product form with the lock-warning dialog; "Compradores" with "Conceder acesso"). The product owner approves it (provisionally) before any UI task starts, and the approval line in the sketch README is **pasted by the user** (project practice). Where the sketch and this document disagree after approval, the approved sketch wins and this document is amended in the same commit.

**Upstream decisions.** `08.2-CONTEXT.md` decisions are cited as **D-350..D-365** and the roadmap decisions as **(1)..(11)**; none is re-litigated. `08.2-RESEARCH.md` is cited as **R-§section** and its draft requirements as **STORE-01..21**. Earlier visual decisions keep their numbers (UI-D-35, UI-D-38, UI-D-42, UI-D-50, UI-D-201, UI-D-204, UI-D-206, UI-D-269, UI-D-270, UI-D-283, UI-D-284, UI-D-288). The questions CONTEXT left to Claude's Discretion, and the UI questions RESEARCH handed to this document (R-§UI Surfaces "What the UI-SPEC must cover", Open Question 5), are decided below as **UI-D-366..UI-D-388**. The block starts after CONTEXT's D-365 so it cannot collide with D-350..D-365, 08's UI-D-269..UI-D-288 or 08.1's UI-D-320..UI-D-327. No question was put to the user: CONTEXT locks every user-owned choice and the session runs in auto mode.

**Discretion defaults adopted unchanged** (CONTEXT § Claude's Discretion): comments on the sample post show the count but never the list; a hidden-post link lands on the locked page; access is lifetime until revoked; product management is `admin_tenant` only; a locked community with no posts shows the normal empty state plus the buy section; store ordering is newest first; one shared BRL formatter.
