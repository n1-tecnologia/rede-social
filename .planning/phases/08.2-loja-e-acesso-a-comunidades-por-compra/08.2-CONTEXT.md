# Phase 08.2: Loja e Acesso a Comunidades por Compra - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-08
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase adds a per-tenant store, the `store` module, which `super_admin` turns on or off in `tenant_modules`.

- **Products.** `admin_tenant` creates, edits, archives and lists products. A product has a name, a description, an image and a price in BRL, stored as integer cents. Each product optionally unlocks communities (many-to-many).
- **Purchase.** A member opens "Loja", picks a product and confirms it in a pop-up. There is no payment gateway yet. Confirming creates an order shaped for a future gateway, and the order grants an access entitlement.
- **The gate.** A community linked to at least one product is locked for a `member` without an entitlement to one of its products.
  - A locked community appears in the list with a padlock.
  - Its page shows only the newest post, read-only, followed by locked placeholders and a section that leads to the store.
  - Its posts disappear from Início and Reels.
  - The API and RLS enforce the gate on every consumer of the community's content.
- **Admin tools.** Admins see who bought each product and can grant or revoke access by hand.
- **Isolation.** The two-tenant isolation suite covers the new tables, routes and the gate. It includes 08.1's shared-identity fixture.

Out of this phase: the payment gateway, which is a later phase that plugs into the same order.

The roadmap entry (`.planning/ROADMAP.md` § Phase 08.2) holds the 8 success criteria and the user's decisions (1)–(11) from 2026-10-08. They are carried forward below and not re-opened.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Carried forward (locked before this discussion — do not re-open)
- **Roadmap decisions (1)–(11), 2026-10-08.** Full text in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` § Phase 08.2 **Notes**.
  1. Products and communities are independent. A community with no product is open to all members, and a product may unlock no community.
  2. A locked community has a padlock tag in the list. Its page shows the newest post and locked placeholders, under a section that leads to the store.
  3. Archiving a product keeps access already bought, and the community gets a "produto arquivado" tag.
  4. `super_admin` turns the store on or off per tenant. With it off, every community is open.
  5. `admin_tenant` sees the buyers and can grant or revoke access by hand, only for members of their own tenant.
  6. Rollout keeps every member's current access, because existing communities have no product.
  7. Linking an open community to a product locks it for every member without an entitlement.
  8. The visible post is the newest one.
  9. The top section opens the product when there is one buyable product, opens a pop-up when there are several, and is hidden when none is buyable.
  10. A non-buyer can only read. Every interaction is blocked.
  11. A locked community's posts disappear from Início and Reels.
- **D-338 (Phase 8):** moderation belongs to `admin_tenant` only. The same posture applies to product management (see Claude's Discretion).
- **D-339 / D-340 (Phase 8):** the admin panel grows out of Configurações → Administração, and actions are reachable both in context and from the panel.
- **D-309 (08.1):** each tenant app is isolated. A purchase belongs to one membership (one tenant). An identity that bought in tenant A gains nothing in tenant B.

### Where Loja lives
- **D-350: Loja is a top-bar icon.**
  - The store module's manifest `nav` entry uses `placement: 'topbar'` with a shopping-bag icon, next to Notificações and Suporte.
  - The 5-tab BottomNav (Início, Comunidades, Reels, Eventos, Perfil) does not change.
  - With the module off, the icon does not render, through the existing `bootstrap.modules` and `buildNav` path.
- **D-351: The store is a 2-column card grid.**
  - Each card shows the image, name and price ("Grátis" when the price is R$ 0).
  - A product the member owns carries a "Comprado" tag.
  - The card language follows the prototype's courses grid (`reference/frontend-design/components/members/CourseCard.tsx`).
- **D-352: Members filter their purchases with a "Todos | Comprados" chip pair at the top of Loja.**
  - There is no separate "Minhas compras" screen.
  - An owned product's page links to the communities it unlocks.
- **D-353: Buying a product that unlocks no community only records the purchase.**
  - The order and entitlement are stored, the product shows "Comprado", and the buyer appears in the admin's buyers list.
  - Delivery happens outside the app.
  - The product page says nothing about communities when there are none.

### Locked community page
- **D-354: At most 3 locked skeletons below the newest post, plus a real count line.**
  - The count line reads "+ N publicações exclusivas".
  - The server sends only the number. Text, media and comments of hidden posts never leave the server.
- **D-355: A locked community's highlights row is hidden for non-buyers.**
  - The row does not render.
  - The community highlights route (`comunidades/[communityId]/destaques`) and the stories `/highlights*` API refuse for that community.
  - Highlight covers never reach a non-buyer.
- **D-356: The newest post is a free sample a non-buyer can read in full.**
  - Its video plays, its images open and its attachments open.
  - Only interactions are blocked: like, comment, share and any other action, server-side as well as in the UI.
- **D-357: The Comunidades list card for a locked community shows everything it shows today, plus a padlock tag.**
  - "Everything" means the cover, name, description and post count. The cover stays in colour, with no grayscale.
  - The card adds the "Produto arquivado" tag when that applies.

### Buy, grant, revoke
- **D-358: After "Confirmar", the pop-up turns into a "Compra concluída" success state.**
  - With exactly one unlocked community, it offers "Ir para a comunidade".
  - With several, it lists them.
  - With none, it shows only "Fechar".
  - When the purchase started from a locked community's top section, the member returns to that community, now unlocked.
- **D-359: Revoking ends the entitlement, and the member can buy again.**
  - The order stays in history with a revoked status.
  - The product shows "Comprar" again for that member.
  - This is the shape a gateway refund will reuse.
- **D-360: Manual grant and revoke live on the product's buyers list ("Compradores").**
  - The screen copies the event participants pattern (`apps/web/app/(app)/eventos/[eventId]/participantes/`).
  - "Conceder acesso" opens a search over the tenant's members.
  - Revoke sits on each row.
  - Each row is tagged "Comprado" or "Concedido", so a grant is told apart from a purchase.
  - There is no grant or revoke in the member admin sheet in this phase.
- **D-361: Orders snapshot amount and currency, and R$ 0 products are allowed.**
  - Editing a price affects only new purchases.
  - A R$ 0 product shows "Grátis" and the button "Obter", and uses the same confirmation pop-up and order path.

### Product admin and design
- **D-362: Admins manage products inside Loja and from a "Loja" row in Configurações → Administração.**
  - Inside Loja, `admin_tenant` sees "+ Novo produto", an "Arquivados" chip, and edit and "Compradores" actions on each product (in context, like communities).
  - The Configurações row opens the same view.
- **D-363: Product–community links are edited only in the product form.**
  - The product form has a community multi-select.
  - The community edit form shows a read-only "Liberada pelos produtos: X, Y", with links to those products.
  - There is one write path for links.
- **D-364: Saving a link that newly locks an open community asks for confirmation.**
  - The confirmation is a danger-tone `ConfirmDialog` reading "N membros perderão acesso a [Comunidade] até comprarem ou receberem acesso".
  - N is the number of members who would lose access.
  - It appears only when a newly linked community had no product before.
- **D-365: Sketch gate before UI work.**
  - The prototype-less surfaces get a sketch, `.planning/sketches/008-phase-08.2-designed-screens`, in the D-33 / UI-04 pattern used in Phases 4–7. They are:
    - the store grid with chips
    - the product page
    - the purchase pop-up and its success state
    - the locked community page (newest post, skeletons, count line, top section, product-choice pop-up)
    - the locked and archived list-card tags
    - the product form, including the lock-warning dialog
    - the "Compradores" list with "Conceder acesso"
  - The product owner approves it (provisionally) before the UI tasks start.
  - A UI-SPEC is also written.
  - Per project practice, the approval line in the sketch README is pasted by the user.

### Claude's Discretion
Each item has a stated default. The planner may refine it but should not reverse it without asking.
- **Comments on the visible newest post.** Default: non-buyers see the comment count but not the comment list. Comments are community content, and every comment endpoint (list, replies, thread) refuses for a non-buyer.
- **A share or notification link to a hidden post (`/post/{postId}`) opened by a non-buyer.** Default: it renders the locked state with the buy section, never the post. `getPost` refuses server-side.
- **Access duration.** Default: lifetime until revoked. The order and entitlement model leaves room for an expiry later, but nothing expires in this phase.
- **Who manages products.** Default: `admin_tenant` only, for product CRUD, the buyers list, grant and revoke. `support_tenant` still sees every community in full (success criterion 4) but has no store admin.
- **A locked community with no posts.** Default: the normal empty state plus the top buy section.
- **The entitlement storage.** The researcher and planner decide whether the gate reads the existing, unused `community_members` seam or a new product-entitlement table joined through product–community links. The rule: the RLS gate must stay cheap (indexed lookups) and must not break any existing two-tenant isolation assertion.
- **The product image media purpose.** The planner decides whether to reuse `cover` or add a `product` value to `MEDIA_PURPOSES`.
- **Store ordering.** Default: newest first. The planner may reuse the communities `position` / `PUT /order` pattern if it is cheap.
- **Money formatting.** Add one shared BRL formatter (`Intl.NumberFormat('pt-BR', { style: 'currency', currency: 'BRL' })`) in contracts or ui. Nothing exists today.

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- **Confirmation pop-up.** `packages/ui/src/overlays/ConfirmDialog.tsx` (`tone` brand or danger, async `onConfirm`) serves the purchase pop-up and the lock warning. `packages/ui/src/overlays/BottomSheet.tsx` serves the product-choice pop-up.
- **Product image upload.** `apps/web/components/media/useSignedUpload.ts` and `apps/web/lib/upload.ts`, with the API in `apps/api/src/routes/media.ts`. The image-only example to copy is the cover field in `apps/web/app/(app)/comunidades/CommunityForm.tsx`.
- **CRUD to copy.** `comunidades/nova`, `[id]/editar`, `CommunityForm.tsx` and `actions.ts`. Events has the same shape (`eventos/novo`, `EventForm.tsx`).
- **Buyers list.** Copy `apps/web/app/(app)/eventos/[eventId]/participantes/ParticipantsList.tsx`. The member search for "Conceder acesso" can reuse `apps/web/lib/admin-members.ts`.
- **Unused seam.** `community_members` (unique tenant, community and user; role member or moderator) in `packages/modules/communities/db/schema.ts`.

### Established Patterns
- **Module toggle.**
  - Add `store` to `TOGGLEABLE_MODULES` in `packages/contracts/src/modules.ts`, plus a migration that widens the `tenant_modules` CHECK.
  - Use `requireModule('store')` (`packages/core/server/modules/require-module.ts`; disabled returns 404 `MODULE_DISABLED`; order is `requireAuth -> requireModule -> permission`) and `moduleFlags.isEnabled(ctx, 'store')` for in-code checks.
  - Register the module in `apps/api/src/modules/registry.ts` and `apps/web/lib/registry.tsx`.
  - The `super_admin` toggle UI already exists in `apps/web/app/(platform)/plataforma/tenants/[id]/modulos/`.
- **Module packaging.** Each feature is a self-contained package (`packages/modules/<name>/{db,server,ui,module.ts}`). The store becomes `packages/modules/store`, and the gate is exposed to other modules through a published contract, never through their internals (MOD-02).
- **RLS.** Today every table has only `tenantIsolationPolicy` (`packages/core/db/rls.ts`). The gate is the first per-user content policy on `feed_posts`, so keep it indexed and cheap.
- **Money.** No price, cents or currency handling exists anywhere yet.

### Integration Points (every consumer of community content the gate must cover)
- **Feed service** (`packages/modules/feed/server/service.ts`):
  - `listFeed` (~373, Início and Reels with its media filter)
  - `listCommunityFeed` (~426)
  - `listVideoCommunities` (~501, Reels lanes)
  - `getPost` (~548, the `/post/[postId]` page and share target)
  - likes, comments, replies, threads and comment likes, in `packages/modules/feed/server/routes.ts`. None of them checks the community today.
- **Share link.** Built in `apps/web/lib/feed-view.tsx` (~260) as `${origin}/post/${id}`. `packages/modules/feed/ui/sharePost.ts` handles the client side.
- **Notifications.** `packages/modules/feed/server/notifications.ts` sends `communityPost` with `audience: { type: 'members' }` (~100). The fan-out in `packages/modules/notifications/server/fanout-job.ts` must exclude non-entitled members for locked communities.
- **Story highlights.** `packages/modules/stories/server/service.ts` (`resolveHighlightPlace`, `placePredicate`) and the `/highlights*` routes.
- **Community API and UI.**
  - API: `packages/modules/communities/server/routes.ts` and `service.ts`.
  - Web: `apps/web/app/(app)/comunidades/` (`CommunitiesList.tsx`, `[communityId]/CommunityPosts.tsx`, `CommunityHeader.tsx`, `destaques/`).
  - Card: `packages/modules/communities/ui/CommunityCard.tsx`.
- **Navigation.** `packages/core/ui/nav.ts` (`buildNav`, the `iconFor` lucide map needs a bag icon). It is built in `apps/web/app/(app)/layout.tsx` and rendered by `apps/web/components/shell/LiveShell.tsx`.
- **Admin entry.** The rows in `apps/web/app/(app)/configuracoes/page.tsx` (~103–128) gate on `bootstrap.modules` and permissions.

## Specific Ideas

- **Locked visual language.** The prototype's courses with a "Premium" course at the end of a track (`reference/frontend-design/app/membros/cursos/page.tsx`, `CourseCard.tsx`). The locked list card keeps its cover in colour (D-357). Grayscale is not used there.
- **Copy anchors (pt-BR).**
  - Store and product: "Loja", "Comprar", "Obter", "Grátis", "Comprado", "Todos | Comprados", "Compra concluída", "Ir para a comunidade".
  - Locked community: "+ N publicações exclusivas", "Produto arquivado".
  - Admin: "Compradores", "Conceder acesso", "Concedido", "Liberada pelos produtos", "Arquivados", "+ Novo produto", and "N membros perderão acesso a [Comunidade] até comprarem ou receberem acesso".
  - The UI-SPEC finalises wording.
- **Future gateway.** The order's status, amount, currency and provider fields are shaped so the gateway phase changes only how an order becomes paid. Revoke (D-359) is the shape a refund will take.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- **Payment gateway.** A later phase. It plugs into the order created here (roadmap).
- **Sale notification to the admin, and grant or revoke notifications to the member.** These are new notification types and are not built in this phase.
- **A delivery note for products with no community.** An admin-written text or link shown to the buyer after purchase. Offered and declined for now (D-353).
- **Grant and revoke from the member admin sheet.** A "Produtos" section in `MemberAdminSheet`. Only the buyers list has it in this phase (D-360).
- **Editing product links from the community form.** Read-only there for now (D-363).
- **Expiring or subscription access.** Access is lifetime until revoked in this phase.
- **A separate "Minhas compras" screen with dates and amounts.** The chip filter covers it for now (D-352).

</deferred>
