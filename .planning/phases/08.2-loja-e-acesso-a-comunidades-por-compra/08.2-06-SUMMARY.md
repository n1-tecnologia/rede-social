# Phase 08.2 Plan 06: Grant, Revoke, Buyers and the Two-Tenant Isolation Proof Summary

**An `admin_tenant` can now list who holds each product (`GET …/buyers`, names from the member's profile in this community), grant it to any live member (`POST …/grants`) and revoke anyone's access (`DELETE …/entitlements/{id}`). Two tenant-pinned SECURITY DEFINER functions do the writes and re-check the admin claim themselves. Revoke keeps both ledger rows as history, and the member may buy again. The phase's isolation proof now covers all twelve store routes and one identity that belongs to two communities.**

## Accomplishments

- **Definers (`*_store_grants.sql`).** `app.store_grant(product, membership)` returns `granted | already_active | not_found | forbidden`. It resolves the membership in `app.tenant_id()` only (active, not blocked, not deleted), inserts `source 'grant'` with `granted_by_user_id = app.user_id()` against the active arbiter, and answers the existing active row on conflict. `app.store_revoke(product, entitlement)` returns `revoked | not_found | forbidden`. It revokes the ACTIVE entitlement of THAT product and, for a purchase, its order. Both functions check the role claim first and return their outcome rather than raising.
- **API.** Three routes, each behind `requirePermission('store.product.manage')`. `listBuyers` confirms the product and counts its holders in one statement, then reads one keyset page `(created_at desc, id desc)`, LEFT-joining the live membership and its `member_profiles` row. `grantAccess` and `revokeAccess` each make one definer call and map the outcome after the transaction: `not_found` is the bare 404 and `forbidden` is a 403. Logs carry ids and outcomes only.
- **Proof.** `store.test.ts` has a new `buyers, grant and revoke` describe with 6 cases. The phase 08.2 sweep covers the three new routes, and its inventory has 12 entries. pgTAP 180 gains sections 8 and 9 (plan 98 → 134). `isolation.test.ts` has three `08.2 store:` cases for the shared identity T. ## Decisions Made

- The definers check the role claim before any lookup. A non-admin lane gets `forbidden` whatever ids it sends, so the answer reveals nothing about what exists. A null `tenant_role` claim is `forbidden` even for an admin user (pgTAP row).
- A grant may target an archived product: archiving only refuses NEW purchases (08.2-05). It may also target any live membership of the tenant, staff included, as the plan's predicate states.
- The buyers list keeps a blocked holder's name, because that membership row is live. Only a removed (`deleted_at`) membership nulls the three identity fields together, which is the events attendee precedent.
- If a concurrent revoke frees the arbiter between the grant's insert and its read-back, the grant retries, at most three times. This keeps `entitlementId` non-null on both 200 answers.

## Flagged Assumption (P34, STORE-09)

STORE-09 is read as follows. "Lifetime" means `expires_at` exists and is never set or read in 08.2. A revoke is a status change, never a deletion. A grant to a member who already holds an active purchase entitlement answers `already_active` and writes nothing. Revoking a purchase also revokes its order, and revoking a grant touches no order. Archiving never revokes (08.2-05). Each clause is exercised by a test above.
