## Claude's Discretion

Each item has a default, recorded in CONTEXT.md:
- Comments on the visible newest post: count only, no list.
- A share or notification link to a hidden post: locked state, never the post.
- Access duration: lifetime until revoked.
- Who manages products: `admin_tenant` only.
- A locked community with no posts: empty state plus the buy section.
- Entitlement storage: `community_members` versus a new table, left to research and planning.
- The product image media purpose.
- Store ordering: newest first.
- A shared BRL formatter.

## Deferred Ideas

- Payment gateway (a later phase)
- Sale notification to the admin, and grant or revoke notifications to the member
- A delivery note for products with no community
- Grant and revoke from the member admin sheet
- Editing product links from the community form
- Expiring or subscription access
- A separate "Minhas compras" screen
