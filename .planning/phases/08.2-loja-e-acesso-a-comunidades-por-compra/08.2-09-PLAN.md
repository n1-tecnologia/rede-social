The locked community, as a member meets it (decisions (2), (8), (9), (10); D-354..D-357; UI-D-372..UI-D-376).

**As a** member who has not bought a community's product, **I want to** see it marked "Exclusiva" in the list, read its newest post, see how much more there is, and find the way to the product, **so that** I know what I would get and how; **and** when a share or a notification points into it, **I want to** land on that same page rather than on an error. Derived from SC 4 and SC 7.

Output: the read-only post card and the card badge slot (feed and communities module UI, extended), the locked-page pieces (store module UI), the composed list and page in `apps/web`, the share-target redirect, the mid-session lock handling, and the `locked community` e2e cases.
