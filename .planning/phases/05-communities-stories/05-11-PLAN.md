<objective>
Close the video half of GAP 2 of `05-VERIFICATION.md`: a video story in the viewer never progresses
and never auto-advances, and nothing in the suite or the seed can see it.

**As a** member, **I want to** watch a video story and have it play through and hand me the next one,
**so that** the sequence works the same way whichever medium my organisation chose.

`StoryVideo` attaches the element's own `canplay`, `playing`, `timeupdate` and `error` in an effect
keyed on `[tokens, onPlayRef]`. `MuxPlayer` arrives through `next/dynamic(…, { ssr: false })`, so on
the commit where `tokens` first becomes non-null the `mux-player` custom element does not exist yet.
`querySelector` returns null, the effect returns early, and neither dependency ever changes again —
`onPlayRef` is a stable `useCallback([])` in `StoryViewerHost`. So `videoProgress[currentId]` stays 0
forever, the bar never moves, and `goNext` is never reached. The pause/mute effect below it has the
same shape and the same defect.

Purpose: make the bridge resilient to the mount it was always going to lose the race with, and put
three shipped routes back inside the gate that is supposed to watch them.
Output: a mutation-observed attachment, a bridge contract test, a video story that really advances a
progress segment inside the real viewer, and a static-route gate that knows about `/stories`.
</objective>
