<objective>
The last slice: the editorial act that lets a 24-hour broadcast become a community's permanent
highlight, and the one screen in the phase with no prototype at all — the admin's own story history,
which is also where pinning and deleting live.

**As an** admin, **I want to** pin the stories worth keeping to the communities they belong to,
**so that** a member who arrives next week still sees them — and **as a** member, **I want to** find
a community's highlights as the circle row the design already drew, **so that** the community's
history is one tap from its page.

Purpose: STORY-04 is the requirement that makes a story more than ephemeral, and it is the one that
proves the phase's assumption-delta decision — that a story's visibility belongs to a SURFACE rather
than to a global clock. This plan also carries the phase exit gate.
Output: `story_community_pins`, pin/unpin, the community Destaques row, `/stories/meus` with its row
menu, the two doors UI-D-29 specifies, the phase smoke witness, and a green `pnpm verify`.

Depends on everything before it: 05-04 (the community page whose Destaques slot this fills), 05-05
(the stories module and the strip component this reuses), 05-06 (the viewer a Destaques circle
opens) and 05-07 (the comment surface a pinned expired story must still offer).
</objective>

|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-48 | Elevation of privilege | the pin, unpin and delete routes | high | mitigate | The literal `requirePermission('stories.story.manage')` on each; the history's affordances are read from the bootstrap's permission array and are UX only. A member receives 403 (asserted) |
| T-05-49 | Tampering | pinning a story to another tenant's community | high | mitigate | BOTH ids are resolved inside the same `withTenantTx` transaction before the insert, and the pin row carries its own `tenant_id` under RLS; the cross-tenant case is asserted in pgTAP and in the integration suite with positive controls |
| T-05-50 | Information disclosure | a pinned expired story reachable by a member | medium | accept | This is the REQUIREMENT (STORY-04): a pin is a deliberate editorial act that extends visibility, and the admin's unpin is the control.
