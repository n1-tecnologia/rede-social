<objective>
The screen stories exist for: full-bleed media on black, a clock that fills a bar, and a thumb that
moves through a sequence without ever having to aim.

**As a** member, **I want to** tap a story and watch the whole sequence hands-free, pausing when I
want to read a caption and liking the ones I like, **so that** catching up on what my organisation
posted today costs me ten seconds and no decisions.

Purpose: this is the one genuinely new interaction in Phase 5 — nothing in the tree is a timed
auto-advancing full-screen pager. The gesture model is the prototype's own reels pager with the axis
flipped, and the timing model is a single frame-loop over elapsed time, because pause, resume and
skip are the three things a CSS animation cannot do.
Output: `useStoryClock`, `StoryProgressBars`, `StoryViewer`, the `/stories/[storyId]` route, story
likes end to end with their trigger-owned counter, and the mobile e2e that drives the gestures.

Depends on 05-02 (the approved drawing — the viewer is prototype-less) and 05-05 (the module, the
strip and the `feed_likes.story_id` foreign key).
</objective>
