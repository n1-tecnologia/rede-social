<objective>
The one genuinely new mechanism in Phase 5: a product rule the roadmap states twice — "story
comments cannot be liked or replied to" — expressed as a fact the database cannot be talked out of,
and the comment surface that rule governs.

**As a** member, **I want to** leave a comment on a story and read what others said, **so that** a
24-hour post can still be a conversation — and **as the** product, I want that conversation to be
flat by construction rather than by the UI happening not to draw a button.

Purpose: an application check passes its own tests while the constraint is missing. This plan lands
the declarative pair the research falsified against real Postgres, closes the latent three-valued
CHECK hole Phase 4 left in the same statement, and gives the story's flat conversation its own
ascending index rather than sorting in memory.
Output: the `target_kind` machinery with its backfills, the two new machine codes, the ascending
story-comment read, the flat `CommentsList` variant over the viewer, and the pgTAP battery where
every negative carries its positive control.

Depends on 05-05 (the `stories` table and the two foreign-key slots) and 05-06 (the viewer whose
paused boolean the sheet feeds).
</objective>
