<objective>
The other half of COMM-01 and COMM-03: the screen a member lands on when they tap a community card,
and the screens an admin uses to bring a community into existence, correct it, and retire it.

**As a** member, **I want to** open a community and read everything published in it, **so that** I
can follow the part of my organisation I actually care about — and **as an** admin, **I want to**
create, edit and archive communities from my phone, **so that** I can organise what we publish
without a laptop.

Purpose: 05-01 proved the list; this plan makes a community a place. It also settles the one piece
of Claude's discretion CONTEXT handed over without a decision — what archive MEANS — by the only
variant that costs zero extra index and zero denormalisation, and it installs the two trigger-owned
columns the list's ordering and the card's count have been promising since 05-01.
Output: `/comunidades/[communityId]`, `/comunidades/nova`, `/comunidades/[communityId]/editar`,
`PATCH /v1/communities/{id}`, the `post_count`/`last_activity_at` trigger, and the archive semantics
with their pgTAP proof.

Depends on 05-01 (the module and the table), 05-02 (the approved drawing of the form — a
prototype-less surface may not be coded before its D-33 review) and 05-03 (`listCommunityFeed` and
the suppression flag the post list needs).
</objective>
