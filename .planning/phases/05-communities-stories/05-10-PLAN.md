<objective>
Close the image half of GAP 2 of `05-VERIFICATION.md`: the phase's headline surface does not work,
and the phase's green unit suite structurally cannot see it.

**As a** member, **I want to** tap a story and watch it, **so that** catching up on my organisation
takes a thumb and five seconds rather than a scroll and a guess.

Two things are wrong on the image path and one thing is wrong with the suite. `MediaImage`'s mount
effect calls `onReady` whenever the image is already decoded, with `onReady` in its own dependency
array; `StoryViewer` manufactures fresh `onLoad`/`onError` objects on every render through a
render-time factory; `controls.onLoad` sets a fresh object into state. Once the image decodes, the
pair spins without bound — a verifier probe reproducing exactly that composition ran to a JavaScript
heap out-of-memory after about 168 seconds. Separately, an empty variant ladder makes `MediaImage`
return its fallback without calling `onFailed` at all, and because the clock is gated on
`currentState === 'ready'` the viewer sits in `loading` forever with no progress, no auto-advance, no
error copy and no retry. And `story-viewer.test.tsx:101` stubs the `media` prop with a plain `<div/>`,
which is precisely why all of it shipped green.

Purpose: make the viewer's image path settle, make its failure path report, and give the suite eyes.
Output: a `MediaImage` whose effect deps cannot change per render, a `StoryViewer` that stops
destabilising its children, badge and retry taps that mean one thing, and three tests that would each
have caught one of these.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** `assumption-delta` fired `kind: pluralization`, term `fallback`, over the phase scope
(the D-73/D-74 merged-predicate fallback prose). The term is also the name of a real prop this plan
touches: `MediaImage`'s `fallback` node.

**Noun now primary:** *a media render outcome*. Its current model is a two-state one — either the
image renders, or the `fallback` node does — with `onReady`/`onFailed` as the outward report.

**The candidate generalization:** the gap shows a THIRD outcome exists and is reachable: *there was
never anything to render* (an empty variant ladder). Today it collapses into the fallback branch
without a report, which is the defect. Does that make "outcome" a three-valued noun the component
should model explicitly — a `status` union promoted to the primary representation, demoting the
boolean `failedId`?

**Decision: `no-change`.** Keep the two-state model and the `failedId` key; route the empty ladder
into the EXISTING failure outcome and its existing `onFailed` report.

**Rationale:** from every consumer's point of view the third case is already the second one — there
is no image and the fallback renders. The viewer's `mediaState` union is `loading | ready | error`
and the empty ladder belongs in `error`; introducing a fourth value would give the viewer a state
with no distinct copy, no distinct affordance and no distinct behaviour. The gap is that a real
outcome was not REPORTED, not that the vocabulary was too small. Promoting a three-valued status here
would be adding a representation alongside a still-correct one, which is the move the reference rule
warns against.

**Reversibility:** `reversible`. If a later phase needs to tell "no bytes yet" apart from "bytes
failed" in the copy, the union can be widened then, when there is a second sentence to justify it.
</assumption_delta_decision>
