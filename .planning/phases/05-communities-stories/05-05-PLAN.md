<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** the orchestrator's assumption-delta detector fired on `optional` — "image or video up to
~60 s via the streaming vendor, **optional** caption". Judged against the real phase scope, that cue
is weak (a brand-new nullable column on a brand-new table), but it sits next to a genuine
pluralisation this phase performs, and that is the one worth recording.

**The noun that is now primary:** *a story's visibility on a surface* — the (story, surface) pair —
rather than *a story's lifetime*. Phase 5 takes a single global fact ("a story is visible for 24 h")
and splits it into two independent visibilities: the `/inicio` strip, which is time-bounded, and a
community's Destaques row, which is pin-bounded and indefinite (STORY-04).

**Decision: `promote`.** The general representation is promoted to primary: each surface's query
carries its own predicate, and the pin row in `story_community_pins` (05-08) IS the expiry override.
The old singular is demoted to a detail of exactly one surface — `expires_at > now()` appears only in
the strip's read. Deliberately NOT chosen: `add-alongside`, which here would have meant an
`is_pinned` boolean (or a `pinned_until`) on `stories` beside the still-authoritative `expires_at`.
That shape contradicts the generalised intent — STORY-04 says "one or MORE communities", so a single
column could never represent the fact — and it would have to be kept in sync with the join forever.

**Companion invariant test (accepted, planned in 05-08):** *a pinned story is visible on its
community page for every value of `now()`* — asserted in `supabase/tests/110-communities-stories.sql`
under a clock the test controls, with the expired-and-pinned pair as its fixture. That assertion goes
red the instant a future phase reintroduces a global "is this story still alive" predicate on the
community path.
</assumption_delta_decision>

<objective>
The second module of the phase, and the half of stories that does not need a viewer: the table whose
expiry is a predicate rather than a job, the strip that puts 24 h of the tenant's broadcast at the
top of `/inicio`, and the full-screen publish flow that gets content into it from a phone.

**As an** admin, **I want to** publish a photo or a short video to my organisation's members in
under a minute from my phone, **so that** something happening right now reaches them before it stops
being now — and **as a** member, **I want to** see what is live at the top of my home screen, **so
that** I do not miss it.

Purpose: STORY-01 and STORY-03 are the whole of stories except watching one. Getting the expiry
shape right here — a volatile default rather than a generated column, and an index that both filters
and orders on the same column — is what makes the viewer, the history and the pins all read from one
index later.
