<objective>
The moment `feed_posts.community_id` — reserved with its own index in Phase 4 and docblocked as
Phase 5's inheritance — finally carries values, on both sides: the read (one merged, strictly
chronological feed, index-served, with a module-flag fallback) and the write (one composer with one
optional destination picker).

**As a** member, **I want to** see my organisation's community posts mixed into the same
chronological feed as its tenant-wide posts, each labelled with where it came from, **so that** I do
not have to visit every community to find out what is new.

Purpose: criterion 1 of the phase is this sentence, and the research falsified the assumption it was
planned on — the Phase 4 composite index cannot serve a query with no predicate on `community_id`.
Landing the third index together with the predicate, and pinning it with an `EXPLAIN` assertion, is
what stops the merged feed from being a sequential scan that is invisible at pilot volume.
Output: the FK, the third index and its plan assertion, the D-73/D-74 predicate switch, the D-71
label, and the D-72 `Publicar em` picker inside the existing `/criar`.

Depends on 05-01 (the `communities` table the FK points at) and 05-02 (the catalog rows this plan's
copy sits beside, and the approved drawing of the picker's sheet body).
</objective>
