# Phase 05 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Design authority.** `reference/frontend-design/` is the visual source of truth for everything it covers (D-66, UI-01). Values marked **[proto]** are extracted verbatim from `app/(app)/community/page.tsx`, `app/(app)/community/[communityId]/page.tsx`, `app/(app)/reels/page.tsx`, `app/globals.css` (`.card-magazine`) and `lib/constants.ts`. The **five** surfaces D-66 names as genuinely prototype-less — (1) the stories strip, (2) the story viewer, (3) the story publish flow, (4) the community create/edit/archive form, (5) the pin-a-story-to-a-community flow — are marked **[designed]** and use only tokens and primitives already shipped in `@tria/ui` / `@tria/core/ui` / `@tria/module-feed/ui`. The design language, spacing scale, typography scale and colour model are **inherited unchanged** from the approved `02-UI-SPEC.md`, `03-UI-SPEC.md` and `04-UI-SPEC.md`; this document restates them for the checker and records only the Phase 5 deltas.

**Upstream decisions.** `05-CONTEXT.md` decisions are cited as **D-nn** (this phase owns D-66..D-85); `05-RESEARCH.md` patterns/assumptions as **R-Pn** / **A-n**. Nothing in those documents is re-litigated here. Questions both left open and that are purely visual are decided below as **UI-D-25..UI-D-47** (numbering continues from `04-UI-SPEC.md`, which used UI-D-07..UI-D-24).

**Review pattern (D-33, UI-04).** The five `[designed]` surfaces go through `/gsd-sketch 5` (static HTML mockup rendered from this contract) and design-team approval **before they are coded**. `[proto]` surfaces — the community list card, the community page header, the Destaques circle row, the post list inside a community — do not. The same package is the vehicle for telling the design team that **PROTOTYPE.md open question 1 is answered "yes"** (D-68: the highlights circles become the pinned-stories UI and now open the viewer), for confirming the interactive circle's larger hit target (Open Question 3 in `05-RESEARCH.md`), and for confirming **UI-D-31** (double-tap-to-like is removed from the viewer).

## Stories strip contract — `[designed]`, D-78 / D-79 / D-80, UI-D-25..UI-D-28

**Placement.** `@tria/module-stories` registers `home: [{ order: 5 }]`; `StoriesStrip` renders inside `HomeSlots` on `/inicio` above the feed slot. `stories` declares **no nav entry** (D-40/D-80). With `stories` disabled the slot is absent and `/inicio` closes up.

**Row.** `flex gap-4 px-4 py-3 overflow-x-auto scrollbar-none` **[proto]**, items `shrink-0 flex flex-col items-center gap-1.5`. Identical on desktop inside the 680px column (UI-D-47). Horizontal overscroll is contained (`overscroll-x-contain`) so a swipe at the row never triggers a browser back-gesture.

**Circle.** 64×64 `rounded-full overflow-hidden bg-bg-tertiary` holding a `MediaImage` at the `story` variant ladder, inside a `p-0.5 rounded-full border-2` ring — `border-brand` in the strip, `border-border` on a community page (UI-D-27). Label below: 12/400 `text-text-tertiary max-w-16 truncate`, the **server-formatted** relative time (UI-D-14 — no `Date.now()` in render). Each circle is a `<button>` that opens the viewer at its index; the whole row is `role="list"` with `role="listitem"` children.

**Order and grouping.** One circle per active story, newest first (D-78). No per-publisher grouping, no seen/unseen state (D-79). A story whose media asset is not `ready` never enters the strip (R-P8).

**Admin's leading circle.** Rendered first, only when `bootstrap.permissions.includes('stories.story.publish')` — **never a role comparison in the web tier**. Geometry per UI-D-28; it is a `<Link>` to `/stories/publicar`.

**Empty.** Member: the component returns `null` and the slot collapses (UI-D-26). Admin: the own-circle alone.

**Loading.** Three 64px `Skeleton` circles with 12px label bars at the same geometry, so the swap to content does not shift the feed below.

**Error.** A failed strip read renders **nothing** — the strip is never the reason `/inicio` shows an error, and the feed below is unaffected. The failure is logged, not surfaced; a member who cannot see a story that expires in an hour is not helped by a retry button above their feed.

## Story viewer contract — `[designed]`, STORY-02, UI-D-30..UI-D-34

**Shell.** `fixed inset-0 z-50 overflow-hidden bg-black` with `style={{ touchAction: 'none' }}` **[proto]**; `--statusbar-ink: #ffffff` set on `document.documentElement` on mount and **removed on unmount** **[proto]**. Route: `/stories/[storyId]` rendered as an intercepting modal over `/inicio` (and over the community page when opened from Destaques), so the back gesture dismisses it and a shared/deep link still resolves to a full page.

**Pager.** `translateX(calc(${-i * 100}% + ${dragging ? dx * 0.35 : 0}px))` with `transition: transform 0.42s cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.715, 0.205, 0.99)` while not dragging **[proto]**. Neighbours `|k − i| ≤ 1` stay mounted **[proto]** — this is also the pre-buffer for the next story's image decode / HLS manifest.

**Gestures.** Dominant-axis lock at `LIMIAR = 60` **[proto]**; one gesture never does both. Horizontal → previous/next. Vertical **down** → dismiss (the same 60px threshold, with the rubber band). Vertical up → no-op. Tap zones on the media layer: **left third = previous, right two-thirds = next** (the larger target matches the dominant direction). A "tap" is a `pointerup` within 200 ms and 10px of the `pointerdown`; anything longer is a **hold**, which pauses the clock and the video on `pointerdown` and resumes on `pointerup`/`pointercancel`.

**Clock.** One `requestAnimationFrame` loop for the whole sequence (R-P9's shape). Image duration `STORY_DURATION_MS = 5000` **[proto]**, ported into `@tria/module-stories/contracts` so the viewer and the tests cite one constant. Video duration is the asset's own, driven by the player's `timeupdate` — never a fixed timer, which desynchronises the instant the network stalls. `paused` is a single boolean fed by three sources: the hold gesture, the open comment sheet (D-82), and document visibility (`visibilitychange`), so backgrounding the PWA never burns a story.

**Progress bars.** `absolute left-0 right-0 z-[3] flex gap-1 px-2` at `top: calc(var(--safe-top) + 8px)`; one `h-0.5 flex-1 rounded-full bg-white/35` segment per story in the sequence, the active one filled to `progress` with a `bg-white` inner span, past segments at 100%, future at 0%. `aria-hidden` — the announced state lives on the viewer's `aria-live` region ("Story {i} de {n}").

**Boundaries.** Next at the last story **closes the viewer** (never loops, D-78). Previous at the first story **restarts the current story's clock**. A story that expires mid-view plays out its own segment and is simply absent from the next strip read — it is never removed under the member's finger.

**Media.** `object-contain` centred on black (UI-D-33), with the prototype's two-stop veil. Video: the shipped `VideoPlayer` with `playsInline`, `muted` by default and the UI-D-34 autoplay-blocked branch. Mute toggle: 44×44 `IconButton` on `bg-black/35` at the top-right **[proto]**, `VolumeX`/`Volume2` 20.

**Overlay, top to bottom.** (1) progress bars; (2) a header row at `top: calc(var(--safe-top) + 24px)`, `px-4`: `Avatar sm` + author display name 14/700 white + the relative `<time>` 12/400 white/75, with the mute toggle and a 44×44 `X` "Fechar" on the right; (3) the media (tap zones); (4) a bottom block at `bottom: calc(var(--safe-bottom) + 12px)`, `px-4`: the caption 16/400 white `drop-shadow` `line-clamp-3` — tapping it expands and **pauses** — then the action row of UI-D-32.

**Like.** The shipped `LikeButton` in its over-media variant: 44×44, `Heart` 20 stroke 1.5, white when idle, `text-like fill-like` when liked, the same optimistic-then-authoritative reconciliation as the feed. **No double-tap** (UI-D-31). On failure: revert + the generic error toast, no inline message.

**Comments.** The shipped `CommentSheet` slides over the viewer and the clock pauses while it is open (D-82). It renders `CommentsList` with the new `flat` prop: **no "Responder", no replies toggle, no comment like control** (STORY-05). Ordering is oldest → newest (D-83), with new comments landing at the bottom. Closing the sheet resumes the clock at its stored `elapsed`. The API's and the database's refusals are the actual control; the absent affordance is only UX.

**Reduced motion.** `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses the pager's `transform` transition to an instant swap and disables the like pulse. **The auto-advance clock stays** — it is content pacing, not decoration, and removing it would make the viewer unusable.

**Accessibility.** `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` with a focus trap and Escape-to-close; focus moves to the close control on open and returns to the originating circle on close. Keyboard: ← / → navigate, Space toggles pause, Escape closes. Every icon-only control is 44×44 with a required `label`; the progress bars are `aria-hidden` and the sequence position is announced through a single `aria-live="polite"` region.

## Story publish & history contract — `[designed]`, STORY-01 / STORY-04, UI-D-39..UI-D-41

**Publish `/stories/publicar`.** Two states per UI-D-39. Upload states are the Phase 3 machine verbatim (`idle → preparing → progress → processing → done → error`): a 4px `bg-brand` bar, "{percent}% enviado" 12 tertiary `tabular-nums`, a ghost "Cancelar envio", `aria-live="polite"` at 25/50/75/100. `purpose: 'story'`; the pick-time gate is UX, and the server re-validates.

**Publishing while a video transcodes is allowed** (the D-53 / Phase 3 precedent): the story row is created immediately, the strip does not show it until the asset is `ready`, and "Seus stories" shows it with the `Processando` pill and the processing note. **The 24 h window starts at publish, not at `ready`** — state it in the note rather than silently losing story life. A video over the ~60 s cap is refused **asynchronously** (Pitfall 5): the story lands in "Seus stories" with the `Recusado` pill and the `media` catalog's duration copy, and never appears in the strip. A story is **not editable after publishing** (D-81) — delete and republish.

**History `/stories/meus`.** Admin-only, `requirePermission('stories.story.manage')` composed on the bootstrap. Rows per UI-D-40, newest first, keyset-paged on the same envelope. "Ver story" opens the viewer as a single-item sequence (the expired story still renders; the strip is what filters, not the viewer).

**Pin flow.** The `PinStorySheet` of UI-D-41. A pinned expired story stays likeable and commentable (A-4) — the viewer does not gate interaction on `expires_at`, and there is no affordance that 400s.

## Motion & Accessibility

- Springs and easings are the shipped ones. Phase 5 adds only the pager transition `transform 0.42s cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.715, 0.205, 0.99)` **[proto]** and the progress-bar fill, which is a `width` written by the rAF clock rather than an animation. All component motion imports `motion/react` — **never `framer-motion`** (Pitfall 12).
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: the pager swap is instant, the like pulse is disabled, `Skeleton` shimmer is static, the circle rows use `scroll-behavior: auto`. The story clock is unaffected (UI-D-30).
- Every icon-only control is 44×44 with a required `label`; decorative glyphs, middots and the progress bars are `aria-hidden`.
- The viewer is a modal dialog with a focus trap, Escape, and focus return to the originating circle; the circle rows are `role="list"`; the sequence position is announced once through `aria-live="polite"`.
- Like/unlike announces through `aria-pressed`; counts sit in a polite live region so a screen reader hears the change without re-reading the overlay.
- Errors render with `role="alert"`; toasts with `role="status"`.
- No `Date.now()` in render (UI-D-14 — every relative time on a circle, a card or a history row is formatted server-side); no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` anywhere (D-54 — a story caption is plain text with render-time linkify).
- Images: `loading="lazy" decoding="async"` on every cover and circle thumbnail except the strip's first three, which are eager (above the fold on `/inicio`).
