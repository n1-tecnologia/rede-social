<assumption_delta_decision>
Noun that is now primary: the participant set of a conversation (`chat_participants`), with `created_by_user_id` demoted to a detail of the support kind. Decision: `add-alongside`. The pair columns restate, for `kind = 'direct'` only, what the two participant rows already say, because a unique constraint over a SET of two rows is not expressible; they are accepted debt. What would force a later promote: group chats (`kind = 'group'`, a set of N) or a second direct conversation per pair (D-720 lifting), at which point uniqueness leaves the table and the pair columns go. The invariant test the checkpoint suggests is in Task 3: every paired direct has exactly its two pair users as participants.
</assumption_delta_decision>
