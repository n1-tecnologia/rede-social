# Phase 11 Plan 01: Sketch 025 mockup Summary

One self-contained mockup of all 64 prototype-less Phase 11 frames (reports and block, Conversas and the direct conversation, profile and directory entry points, the admin queue rows, the bell rows and pushes, three annotation frames), ready for plan 02's README and Igor's review.

## What was built

- Task 1 (0953902b): group G1, 24 frames: the 17 frames carried from sketch 017 redrawn with the Phase 11 deltas, plus story, story comment and community menus, the five report titles, the two message privacy lines and the social-on block body.
- Task 2 (86155b8c): groups G2 and G3, 24 frames: the bubble, Conversas list and previews, the direct conversation and its composer states (open, closed, blocker, removed, refused send), not-found, the 320 geometry, the profile zone and the directory row.
- Task 3 (387aa8f5): groups G4 and G5 and the annotations, 16 frames: queue rows for story, story comment, community, text and voice message, the resolve sheets and archive dialog, the two bell rows, the three pushes, the state matrix, the screen-reader table and the A-UI-1..A-UI-22 table with an empty answer column.

The file has four tenant brands (Azul, Verde, Coral, Amarelo claro) plus Marinho escuro, light and dark, a 390/320 width switch, a fictitious clock (Thursday 19 November 2026, 14:20) and a visible index with the total frame count. Frames were rendered in Chromium to check for horizontal overflow and clipped fixed-height phones; the two found were fixed (button stack collapsing at 320, one clipped empty-state phone).
