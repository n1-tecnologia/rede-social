### Locked Decisions

- **D-846:** A message carries either text or one recording, never both. There is no caption; a member who wants words with a voice message sends a text message after it. The text rules of Phase 7 are unchanged (1 to 2000 characters after trim). Reversibility: costly.
- **D-847:** Everyone who may send a text message in a chat may send a voice message: the member in the support chat, the staff replying, and every participant of a direct or group conversation once Phase 11 exists. Same permission as text (`chat.support.contact` for the member, the staff reply route for the team); no new permission and no new module toggle.
- **D-848:** A recording lasts at most 5 minutes (300 seconds). Enforced three times: the recorder stops at it, the upload contract declares a maximum duration for the audio kind, and the server refuses a longer one when the upload completes. A recording shorter than about one second is discarded by the recorder without sending (the threshold is Claude's Discretion).
- **D-849:** One tap on the microphone starts recording. While it records, the composer shows a trash button (cancel), the elapsed time and a send button that stops the recording and sends it in the same tap. No listen-before-send, no hold-to-record. Reversibility: reversible.
- **D-850:** At 5:00 the recording stops by itself and the member chooses to send or discard it; nothing is sent without that choice. At 4:30 the composer says 30 seconds remain.
- **D-851:** The microphone shares the slot of the send button. Empty field = microphone; text = the usual send button. The composer stays one row at 320 px.
- **D-852:** The microphone permission is requested at the first tap on the microphone, never when the page loads. When refused or absent, a plain pt-BR sentence is read and the text composer keeps working.
- **D-853:** The bubble shows a simple player: play/pause, a draggable progress bar, the duration as `m:ss`. One voice message plays at a time; starting another stops the previous one. No waveform. Reversibility: reversible.
- **D-854:** Playback speed 1x, 1.5x, 2x with a small button in the player; applies to that player and is not stored.
- **D-855:** No "listened" indicator and no transcription. Receipts stay in V2-CHAT-02.
- **D-856:** A voice message stays as long as its conversation exists. No expiry and no new sweep; the existing media sweeper keeps removing uploads that were never completed.
- **D-857:** A voice recording counts in the tenant's storage quota under the same rules as the other media (tenant ceiling and the member's share), through the existing media quota code, with no separate budget.
- **D-858:** Only the participants of the conversation can open the audio (for the support chat, the member and the tenant's staff who can read cases). A member of the same tenant who knows the media id must get nothing, and no user of another tenant can. The kernel's media read gate (`app.media_asset_hidden`) hides only assets carried by posts, stories and lessons today; this phase adds the chat case to it, as the story gate did. Reversibility: costly.
- **D-859:** Nobody deletes or edits a sent voice message, exactly as nobody does for text today (append-only, no UPDATE or DELETE policy for lanes). No delete action in this phase.
- **D-860:** Wherever a message's text is shown as a preview, a voice message reads "Mensagem de voz" followed by its duration as `m:ss` (inbox row, "Meus chamados" card, push body staff and member). - **D-862:** The refusals that apply to a text message apply to a voice message in the same order and with the same codes: a closed case, a blocked or removed member, and a conversation the sender is not part of.

## Summary

The kernel already does 80% of the work: a signed single-PUT upload (a 5-minute recording is at most a few MB, under `RESUMABLE_THRESHOLD_BYTES`), `completeUpload` with magic-byte checks, the tenant/member quota that sums every non-video byte, a `processing` -> `ready` worker path (`kernel.media-derive-variants`), the hourly sweeper that also runs module `sweepFunctions`, and `GET /v1/media/{id}/original` as a same-origin 302 to a signed Storage URL. Nothing in the repo records or plays audio, and two facts the CONTEXT assumed are different from what the code does: (a) `GET /v1/media/{id}/original` asks only `courseAssetHidden`, **not** `app.media_asset_hidden` (the feed gate is asked only by the video `playback` route), so "adding the chat case to `app.media_asset_hidden`" would protect nothing until `serveVariant` also asks it; the 08.5 precedent (a second kernel-owned seam, `app.course_asset_hidden`) is the right shape; (b) the native microphone text exists only in `scripts/app-release/config.ts:55`, not in `native.ts` (which holds a schema), and `csp.test.ts` does not change (audio is served under the existing `media-src`).

**The recording format decision (the one that matters).** MediaRecorder output is not portable: Chromium writes `webm/opus` with **no Cues index ever** and (before Chrome 140, or when `timeslice`/`requestData()` is used) no Duration; Firefox writes `ogg` or `webm` Opus; Safari writes `mp4` AAC and, from 18.4, also `webm`; Chrome can write `mp4` only when the platform has an OS encoder. A file as recorded therefore has no trustworthy duration (needed for the server-side 300 s check and the UI's stored-duration bar), is not reliably seekable, and Opus plays in iOS Safari only from 18.4. **Recommendation: store what the browser recorded as the upload object, then normalize it in the worker (the existing derive job, with an `audio` branch) to one AAC-LC mono `audio/mp4` file with `+faststart`, and measure its duration with ffprobe.** One output format that every target plays, an exact duration the server owns, and a seekable file with Range support. Cost: ffmpeg becomes a system dependency of the one Docker image (a new runtime dependency that needs Igor's approval), `ffmpeg` on dev and CI machines, and about 2 to 6 seconds between the end of the upload and `ready`. The fallback (store as recorded, three accepted mimes, hand-written container parsing, client-declared duration bounded by size) is described under Open Questions and is worse on every axis except "no new dependency".

**Message model.** `chat_messages.body` becomes nullable, with two new nullable columns `media_asset_id` (FK) and `voice_seconds` (a copy of the server-verified duration), a CHECK that exactly one of text or recording is present, a partial unique index on `(tenant_id, media_asset_id)` (an asset is used once, and a lost-response retry becomes idempotent), and the INSERT policy extended to require an asset that is the author's own, `ready`, `audio`/`voice`, undeleted and whose duration equals `voice_seconds`. The two chat triggers read no `body` (verified), so nothing there changes; the case transitions read no body (D-861 holds). New API surface is **two new routes** (member and staff voice send) rather than a union on the text routes, so the text contract and its tests stay byte-identical and the previous API keeps working between migration and deploy.

**Primary recommendation:** ship `audio`/`voice` as a new media kind and purpose, normalize in the worker with ffmpeg to AAC `audio/mp4`, put duration on the message row (`voice_seconds`), gate reads through a new kernel seam `app.chat_asset_hidden` (invoker function over the chat RLS) asked by `serveVariant('original')`, add two voice send routes, and make the player assign `audio.src` synchronously inside the tap handler. Deploy order for this phase is migrations, then API and worker, then web.

### Pattern 1: Normalize in the worker, one format out
**What:** `completeUpload` for `kind = 'audio'` checks size and content type, reads a 16-byte prefix (the existing `prefixOnly` path), checks the signature the declared mime demands, sets `status = 'processing'` and enqueues the existing derive job. `deriveAssetVariants` gains an audio branch (today it returns `skipped` for any non-image) that downloads the object, runs ffmpeg, ffprobes the output, overwrites the same key, and writes `mime`, `bytes`, `duration_seconds`, `status = 'ready'`.
**When to use:** always for `audio`.
**Why overwrite `original`:** `mediaVariantParamSchema` only allows `original` and `w<width>`, so a second key would need a contract change; nothing can fetch a not-ready voice asset except its owner (the gate hides it from everyone else), and the owner has no UI path to it.
**Example (verified locally; flags are stable across ffmpeg versions):**
```ts
// packages/core/server/media/voice.ts  (sketch; values verified by running the command, ffmpeg 9.0.2)
// Input demuxer and decoder are pinned from the signature, never probed: -f matroska|mov|ogg, -c:a opus|aac|libopus
// -t 301 bounds the READ; the output duration (ffprobe) decides the verdict.
await execFileP('ffmpeg', [
  '-hide_banner', '-nostdin', '-v', 'error',
  '-protocol_whitelist', 'file',
  '-f', demuxer,                       // 'matroska' (webm), 'mov' (mp4), 'ogg'
  '-i', inPath,
  '-vn', '-sn', '-dn', '-map', '0:a:0',
  '-t', '301',
  '-c:a', 'aac', '-b:a', '48k', '-ac', '1', '-ar', '44100',
  '-movflags', '+faststart',
  '-fs', '8000000',
  '-f', 'mp4', '-y', outPath,
], { timeout: 20_000, killSignal: 'SIGKILL', maxBuffer: 1 << 20 });
const { stdout } = await execFileP('ffprobe', ['-v', 'error', '-show_entries', 'format=duration', '-of', 'json', outPath]);
```
Local evidence: a 70 s Opus-in-WebM written like a browser stream (`-live 1`, so no duration and no cues) reported `duration=N/A` from ffprobe, normalized in 0.35 s wall to a 435,829-byte `aac`, 44100 Hz, mono, `mov,mp4,m4a` file with `duration=70.000000` [VERIFIED: command run this session]. Five minutes is therefore about 1.5 s of CPU. Input is read through a temp file in a `mkdtemp` directory removed in `finally` (a `+faststart` MP4 needs a seekable output, so the output cannot be a pipe).

### Pattern 2: A second kernel read-gate seam, built over the chat RLS
**What:** `app.chat_asset_hidden(p_asset uuid) boolean`, declared by a kernel migration as a stub (`select false`, the 08.5 `course_asset_hidden` shape) and given its body by the chat module with `create or replace`. The body is **SECURITY INVOKER** so the chat tables' own RLS decides:
```sql
-- hidden = a voice asset the caller neither uploaded nor can read through a chat message
select exists (select 1 from public.media_assets a
                where a.id = p_asset and a.tenant_id = app.tenant_id() and a.purpose = 'voice')
   and not (
        exists (select 1 from public.media_assets a where a.id = p_asset and a.owner_user_id = app.user_id())
     or exists (select 1 from public.chat_messages m where m.media_asset_id = p_asset));   -- RLS: participant or staff on support
```
`serveVariant('original')` changes one expression: `select (${courseAssetHidden(assetId)} or ${chatAssetHidden(assetId)}) as hidden`, still ONE round trip and fail-closed (`gate?.hidden !== false` is a miss). Non-voice assets answer false, so nothing else changes.
**Why not edit `app.media_asset_hidden` (the literal D-858 wording):** (1) `serveVariant` does not ask it for `original` today [VERIFIED: packages/core/server/media/service.ts:884 `tx.execute<{ hidden: boolean }>(sql\`select ${courseAssetHidden(assetId)} as hidden\`),` and :984 `sql\`select (${mediaAssetHidden(assetId)} or ${courseAssetHidden(assetId)}) as hidden\`,` which is the video playback route], so the change would have no effect on audio; (2) the 08.5 migration states the reason in writing: "a courses migration re-creating the feed body would inline it, and a later feed redefinition would silently drop the lesson branch" [VERIFIED: supabase/migrations/20261015194744_course_asset_gate_seam.sql]. The user-visible outcome of D-858 is identical; flag it to Igor as an implementation deviation, not a product one.
**Why invoker:** a definer restates the staff-role literal list and adds a third copy for the role-drift test; an invoker function inherits `chat_conversations_select`, `chat_participants_select` and `chat_messages_select`, including Phase 11's direct conversations, where staff are not participants and so cannot open the audio.

### Pattern 3: Voice is a second message shape on the same service path
**What:** `insertMessage(tx, ctx, conversationId, side, content)` takes `{ kind: 'text', body } | { kind: 'voice', mediaAssetId }`. `sendSupportMessageOnce` and `replyToConversation` keep their structure (case open or reopen, participant ensure, member-state refusals, CS004 retry into a new case, `emit('chat.message_sent')`), so D-862's order is inherited, not re-implemented. Two thin routes call them with the voice input.
**Order inside a voice send:** member state (blocked or removed, staff route) -> asset pre-validation (own, `audio`/`voice`, `ready`, not deleted, not already used; `processing` is the retriable `voice_not_ready`) -> insert (RLS and CHECKs as defense in depth) -> trigger (CS004 closed). The pre-validation exists only to give stable codes: a failed RLS WITH CHECK is an anonymous 42501.
**Idempotency without a client key:** the partial unique index makes an asset single-use. Before inserting, the service reads `chat_messages` by `media_asset_id` (the author's own row is visible); if found, it answers that message (same `SendResult` shape, `case.opened = false`, `reopened = false`). A concurrent double send loses on `23505` and the service re-reads in a NEW `withTenantTx` (the aborted transaction cannot be reused, the same rule as the CS004 retry).

### Pattern 4: The player assigns `audio.src` synchronously inside the tap
**What:** first tap sets `audio.preload = 'none'`, `audio.src = '/v1/media/<id>/original'`, calls `audio.play()` in the same handler. The browser requests the same-origin Next handler, which forwards the cookie and passes the 302 (`apps/web/app/v1/media/[assetId]/[variant]/route.ts`), then follows it to Storage and sends `Range` for seeks. **Do not** `await` an API call to obtain a link and then call `play()`: WebKit's autoplay policy wants `play()` in the user gesture [ASSUMED, device proof]. UI-SPEC's "the link is requested on the first tap" is satisfied by the media request itself.
**Refresh after expiry or error:** append a cache-buster (`?r=<n>`) so the browser's `private, max-age=1500` cached 302 is bypassed (the Next handler ignores the query), then restore `currentTime` on `loadedmetadata`.
**Unsupported state:** detect it from the element's `error` event (`MediaError.code === 4`, `MEDIA_ERR_SRC_NOT_SUPPORTED`), never from `canPlayType`, which happy-dom answers with `''` for every type [VERIFIED: ran `new Window()` from happy-dom 20.14.5 in `packages/modules/chat`: `canPlayType audio/mp4 -> ""`, `MediaRecorder undefined`, `mediaDevices undefined`].

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- **Storing the MediaRecorder file as the playback source.** No Cues and often no Duration; the bar maximum would depend on `audio.duration` (Infinity) and seeking would scan from the start [CITED: blog.addpipe.com/duration-in-webm-videos-produced-by-chrome].
- **A union body on the existing text routes.** Their `defaultHook` lifts `body_required`/`body_too_long` from the issue message; an `invalid_union` issue would degrade every text error to a generic 400 and break the pinned contract tests. Add routes.
- **Emitting `voice: null` or `body: null` on text rows.** The previous web parses `messageRowSchema` strictly; every new key must be `.optional()` and **omitted** (not null) on text rows, and `body` stays a string (`''` for a voice row) on the wire. The host view model keeps `body` and `voice` mutually exclusive (UI-SPEC); only the wire differs.
- **A SECURITY DEFINER gate that restates the staff role list.** A third copy to drift (pgTAP 152 and the role-drift test pin the literals).
- **`useSignedUpload` for the take.** It re-encodes images, shows toasts and cannot resume at the asset after a failed send; compose its parts instead.
- **Letting the service worker intercept the audio request** (see Pitfall 2).
- **`canPlayType` gating** (see above).

## Don't Hand-Roll

| Problem | Don't Build | Use Instead | Why |
|---------|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Audio duration of a browser recording | EBML/MP4/Ogg parsers | `ffprobe` on the normalized output | WebM has no Duration or Cues; fragmented MP4 has none in `mvhd`; three parsers on the request path is the larger risk |
| Re-muxing for seekability | `ts-ebml`, `fix-webm-duration` (new npm packages that patch a Blob) | ffmpeg `-movflags +faststart` | One tool, server-owned, no client trust |
| Signed uploads, quota, sweeper, 302 serving | A new audio storage path | `startUpload` / `completeUpload` / `serveVariant` / `kernel.media-sweep-orphans` | Already tenant-scoped and tested |
| Read authorization of a voice file | A restated participant/staff predicate | The chat RLS, through an invoker function | One source of truth, covers Phase 11 |
| Idempotent send | A client key table | `unique (tenant_id, media_asset_id)` + pre-read | Makes retry safe with no new state |
| Orphan collection | A new job | A module function listed in `sweepFunctions` (`app.chat_voice_orphans_sweep`) run by the existing hourly sweeper | Same mechanism as `chat_cases_auto_close` |
| Range requests and seeking | A streaming endpoint | Supabase Storage signed URL + the element's own Range logic | Bytes must never transit Cloud Run |
| Preview word building | SQL string concatenation | Select `voice_seconds`; build the words in TS (`notification-copy.ts`) and the host catalog | The UI-SPEC and D-860 put copy in catalogs and the push builder |

**Key insight:** every "audio problem" here is already a solved problem of another tool (ffmpeg for containers, Storage for Range, RLS for access); the phase is plumbing a new `kind` through code that has exactly one place per concern.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: MediaRecorder output cannot be trusted as a file
**What goes wrong:** the player bar and the server's 300 s check have no duration; Chrome's WebM has no Cues so seeking reads from the start; Safari's MP4 may be fragmented.
**Why:** MediaRecorder writes the header before it knows the length. Chrome 140 added `Duration` for non-chunked recordings only; "the `Cues` metadata is not present regardless" [CITED: blog.addpipe.com/duration-in-webm-videos-produced-by-chrome]. Safari 18.4 added `webm/opus` recording beside its `mp4` and can write fragmented MP4 [CITED: webkit.org/blog/16574/webkit-features-in-safari-18-4/]. Chrome writes MP4 only when the platform has an OS encoder, and Firefox does not record `video/mp4` [CITED: MDN isTypeSupported page and the Mozilla bug it links, via search results].
**How to avoid:** normalize on the server (Pattern 1); never read `audio.duration` for the bar (UI-SPEC A-UI-13 already says so).
**Warning signs:** `ffprobe` prints `duration=N/A` on an upload; the seek bar jumps.

### Pitfall 2: The service worker breaks Safari audio
**What goes wrong:** rule (a) of `apps/web/app/sw.ts` sends every same-origin `/v1/*` request through `NetworkOnly`, which includes `<audio src="/v1/media/.../original">`. Safari probes media with a `Range` request and needs a proper 206; a SW in the path is the documented cause of Safari media failures, and the WebKit guidance is to let the browser handle audio and video itself, detecting them with `Request.destination` [CITED: web.dev/articles/sw-range-requests; bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=184447; dev.to/philnash/service-workers-beware-safaris-range-request].
**Current code:** [VERIFIED: apps/web/app/sw.ts:56] `const NETWORK_ONLY_PREFIXES = ['/auth/', '/v1/', '/api/', '/m/', '/serwist/'];` and the file header states "anything else is left to the browser (no route)".
**How to avoid:** add `request.destination !== 'audio'` (and `video`) to rule (a)'s matcher so the request matches no route and the browser's native networking (which preserves Range) handles it. Images through `/v1/media` keep working unchanged. This must be proven in the installed iOS PWA by Igor.

### Pitfall 3: The kernel gate that does not gate
**What goes wrong:** extending `app.media_asset_hidden` as D-858 literally says leaves `GET /original` open to any tenant member who knows the id.
**How to avoid:** Pattern 2, plus a pgTAP and integration negative: a same-tenant member who is not a participant gets the bare 404; staff of the support thread gets 302; a user of another tenant gets 404; the uploader gets 302 before sending.

### Pitfall 4: A member (or admin) can delete a sent voice message through the media API
**What goes wrong:** `DELETE /v1/media/{id}` lets the uploader (and any `admin_tenant`) soft-delete the asset; the sweeper purges the bytes an hour later, and the thread keeps a bubble that can never play. This contradicts D-859.
**Current code:** [VERIFIED: packages/core/server/media/service.ts `assertMayRetire`: "`return` if `isOwnerOrAdmin(ctx, row)`" with `isOwnerOrAdmin = row.ownerUserId === ctx.userId || ctx.role === 'admin_tenant'`].
**How to avoid:** `deleteAsset` answers a refusal for `purpose = 'voice'` always (the orphan sweep is the only way a voice asset goes away). The FK `chat_messages.media_asset_id` stays `NO ACTION`, so a purge that tried to delete a referenced row fails loudly in the sweeper's try/catch instead of silently orphaning a bubble.

### Pitfall 5: Orphans eat the quota
**What goes wrong:** a take whose send failed permanently, or was discarded after the upload, leaves a `ready` voice asset no message references. The kernel sweeper collects only `pending` (24 h) and `deleted`/`rejected` (1 h) [VERIFIED: sweep-job.ts docblock "A `processing` or a `ready` asset is therefore outside the predicate BY CONSTRUCTION"]. Bytes count in the tenant ceiling and the member share (`kind <> 'video'` sums) [VERIFIED: member-share.ts `sql\`${mediaAssets.kind} <> 'video'\``].
**How to avoid:** `app.chat_voice_orphans_sweep(p_batch int)`, a module-owned definer function in the chat manifest's `sweepFunctions` (the exact `chat_cases_auto_close` pattern: cross-tenant by design, each write pins the row's own `tenant_id`, `for update skip locked`, executable by `service_role` only) that soft-deletes `voice` assets that are `ready` or `failed`, older than 24 h, with no referencing message; the existing sweeper then purges the objects an hour later. This is one name added to a list, no new queue or job, but it does change D-844's wording "reused as they are": flagged under Open Questions.

### Pitfall 6: Server Actions queue behind each other
**What goes wrong:** Next runs a page's Server Actions one at a time, so a poll that is an action and a text send interleave at action granularity. A voice send implemented as "start, PUT, complete, then poll `fetchAssetStatusAction` every 2 s, then send" works but wastes round trips and the first poll delay is 2 s [VERIFIED: apps/web/components/media/useAssetReadiness.ts `READINESS_FIRST_DELAY_MS = 2_000`].
**How to avoid:** make the voice **send** the poll: the API answers `409 voice_not_ready` while the asset is `pending` or `processing` (retriable) and the client repeats the same send every 700 ms for up to ~20 s; the unique index makes every repeat safe. No readiness hook, no extra route, and the upload progress ends at the PUT while "finishing" covers the rest (UI-D-846's "finishing step").

### Pitfall 7: The 5:00 stop and background timers
**What goes wrong:** a `setInterval` that decides "300 s elapsed" is throttled in a hidden tab (Chrome slows timers; minutes later to once a minute), so a take can overshoot and the server refuses it as too long.
**How to avoid:** measure with `performance.now()` from the recorder's own `start` event and also check it in each `dataavailable` (use `start(1000)`; media events are not timer-throttled). The server accepts up to 301 s and clamps the stored value to 300 (the recorder's stop is not sample-exact and AAC adds priming).

### Pitfall 8: iOS Safari behaviours that contradict the UI-SPEC defaults [ASSUMED, all Igor's device proofs]
1. **A-UI-7 says hiding the tab does not stop a recording.** On iOS, capture is suspended when the page is backgrounded (call, lock screen, app switch) and the track fires `mute` without `ended`; the recorder keeps running and writes silence. Treat a track `mute` that lasts more than ~1 s as an interruption (keep what was recorded when at least 1 s, as A-UI-7 already says) in addition to `ended` and `MediaRecorder` `error`.
2. **Playing after recording** can come out quiet or on the earpiece until the microphone is released; stop every track immediately on every exit (UI-D-845 already requires it).
3. **Permission is asked again on every page load** (UI-D-842 already notes it).
4. **`MediaRecorder.start(timeslice)`** has had empty-chunk quirks in Safari; ignore zero-size chunks and fall back to a plain `start()` if no chunk arrives.
5. **Installed PWA and `getUserMedia`:** works in standalone on current iOS; the Phase 13 shell loads these screens in a WebView (D-600: "Expo with a WebView is the candidate"), where microphone capture needs the OS usage string (the native text change below) and the WebView's capture permission; Phase 13 must list "record a voice message inside the app" in its acceptance.

### Pitfall 9: Mime parameters
`Blob.type` from MediaRecorder is `audio/webm;codecs=opus`. `startUpload` does `if (!limit.mimes.includes(body.mime)) throw validationFailed('type_not_allowed')` and `completeUpload` compares `info.contentType !== row.mime`. Strip parameters on the client (`type.split(';')[0]`) before the start action and before the PUT's `content-type`, and keep the bucket allow-list to the three base types.

### Pitfall 10: Expand-only is not the same as "the previous API keeps working with voice rows"
The previous API would return `body: null` for a voice row and its text insert path is untouched. Voice rows only exist after the new web sends one, and the new web ships after the API in this phase's order, so no previous-API read ever meets a voice row except in a mixed tab window; the release note must say so (below).

### Pitfall 11: One Docker image, three roles
`apps/api/Dockerfile` builds one image for `api`, `worker` and `migrate` [VERIFIED: Dockerfile header "One image, three roles (D-18, D-544)"]. ffmpeg lands in all three. Measure the image size delta and cold start of the API revision in Wave 0; if unacceptable, use the pinned static binary instead of apt.

### Pitfall 12: Admin media library
`GET /v1/media` lists every asset to an `admin_tenant` (`listAssets` is admin-only, filterable by kind and purpose) [VERIFIED: service.ts `if (ctx.role !== 'admin_tenant') throw new ApiError(403, 'FORBIDDEN');`]. With Phase 11, an admin must not see private direct-message audio metadata. Exclude `purpose = 'voice'` from `listAssets` unconditionally.

### Known Threat Patterns for this stack

| Pattern | STRIDE | Standard Mitigation |
|---------|--------|---------------------|
| Non-audio file declared as audio (polyglot, a video, an image, a PDF) | Tampering | mime allow-list at start; bucket allow-list at the PUT; content type compared at complete; 16-byte signature per declared mime; ffmpeg `-f` pinned and `-map 0:a:0` (a file with no audio stream fails `transcode_failed` and the object is removed); video and subtitle streams are dropped |
| ffmpeg demuxer or decoder vulnerability on hostile input | Elevation of privilege | pinned `-f` and `-c:a`, `-protocol_whitelist file`, `-nostdin`, no shell (`execFile` with an argument array), temp directory per job, 20 s kill timeout, `-fs 8000000`, runs in the worker role only, keep the Debian package updated (security repo) |
| Oversize or overlong file | DoS | 6 MiB cap at start and complete; `-t 301` bounds the read; output duration above 301 s is refused `duration_too_long`; stored duration clamped to 1..300; DB CHECK `voice_seconds between 1 and 300` |
| Media id from another conversation or tenant used in a send | Spoofing / Info disclosure | INSERT policy requires `owner_user_id = app.user_id()` and the caller's tenant; the pre-validation answers one `voice_invalid` for unknown, foreign and used assets (no existence oracle); the unique index blocks reuse across conversations |
| Same-tenant member who learns a media id opens someone else's audio | Information disclosure | `app.chat_asset_hidden` through `serveVariant('original')`; bare 404 for a hidden asset, the same answer as a miss; pgTAP and integration negatives |
| Signed URL leak | Information disclosure | accepted residual: the URL is a bearer for up to 1 hour and `signRead` memoises it per key for 50 minutes across callers, but only after the gate passed for that caller; the 302 is `private`; voice is no more exposed than a private photo. A stricter option (unmemoised `signReadFor(key, 1800)`) exists if Igor wants it for voice only |
| Member sends to a conversation they are not in | Elevation of privilege | unchanged text rules: participant requirement in the INSERT policy; the staff route requires `chat.support` and a support conversation |
| Quota exhaustion by uploads (including orphan takes) | DoS | tenant ceiling and member half-share count every non-video byte; orphan sweep after 24 h; **no rate limiter exists on `/v1/media/uploads`** (shared residual of every upload kind; flagged, not introduced here) |
| Admin or member deletes a sent recording | Repudiation / Tampering | `deleteAsset` refuses `voice`; FK `NO ACTION`; no UPDATE or DELETE policy on messages (D-859) |
| Admin media library exposing private audio metadata | Information disclosure | `listAssets` excludes `voice` |
| Recording continues after leaving the screen (microphone left open) | Privacy | stop every track on every exit; covered by unit tests with counting fake tracks |
