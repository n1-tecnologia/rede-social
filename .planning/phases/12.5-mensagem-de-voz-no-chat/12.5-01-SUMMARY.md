- The media contract now carries `audio` (last of `MEDIA_KINDS`) and `voice` (last of `MEDIA_PURPOSES`), `VOICE_MAX_SECONDS = 300`, `VOICE_MIMES`, `MEDIA_LIMITS.audio.voice` and `PURPOSE_WIDTHS.voice = []`; `maxBytes` equals `RESUMABLE_THRESHOLD_BYTES` so a take is always one signed PUT.
