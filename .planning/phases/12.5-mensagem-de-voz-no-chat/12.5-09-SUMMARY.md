# Phase 12.5 Plan 09: Voice previews, the case label and the push words Summary

**A recording now carries its stored length in the inbox row and the "Meus chamados" card, names its case "Mensagem de voz" on every surface, and pushes "Mensagem de voz · m:ss" in the slot a text preview takes, with the words built in TypeScript and a deleted recording read as deleted and never pushed.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 10 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-17T23:15:58Z
- **Completed:** 2026-11-17T23:26:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (task 1 the tracer)
- **Files modified:** 10 (2 created, 8 modified)

## Accomplishments

- `inboxRowSchema` and `caseCardSchema` `lastMessage` gain OPTIONAL `voiceSeconds` (1..300) and `voiceDeleted` (`true`); a text preview has neither key and is byte-identical to before. For a recording `preview` is `''` and the host builds the words from the stored length.
- `subjectLabelOf(subject, firstBody, firstIsVoice)`: typed subject, else the constant `CHAT_VOICE_COPY.label` when the first message is a recording, else the cut first text. The member's two header reads, the card, the staff inbox row, the staff thread header and the earlier-cases list all feed `first_is_voice`.
- `notification-copy.ts` gains `CHAT_VOICE_COPY`, `formatVoiceClock` (clamped to 0..300, rounded, never `NaN`) and `voicePreview`; `resolveMessageSent` computes ONE preview and passes it to the unchanged builders. No template changed, no bell row, no kernel import, no event, no job.
- A recording whose asset is gone: the source returns no intent (T-12.5-38); lists keep `voiceSeconds` and add `voiceDeleted: true`.
