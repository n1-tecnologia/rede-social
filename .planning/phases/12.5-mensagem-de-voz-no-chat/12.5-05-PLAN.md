## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| member lane to `POST /v1/chat/support/voice-messages` | the body is untrusted: a media id, never a duration, a side or an author |
| member to another member's or tenant's asset | an id learned elsewhere must not be usable in a send |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| T-12.5-15 | Spoofing | a media id of another member, another conversation or another tenant in a send | high | mitigate | explicit `owner_user_id` and tenant predicates in the pre-validation, the database policy and the unique index as defense in depth, ONE `voice_invalid` for every unusable asset, isolation case beside a positive control |
| T-12.5-16 | Tampering | a forged duration or side in the body | high | mitigate | strict schema refuses unknown keys; the duration is copied from the asset row inside the insert statement; the side is derived from the permission |
| T-12.5-17 | Elevation of privilege | staff or an unauthenticated caller on the member route | high | mitigate | `requirePermission('chat.support.contact')`; tests assert 403 for staff and 401 without a token |
| T-12.5-18 | Denial of service | a retry storm of one take or a concurrent double send | medium | mitigate | the single-use index makes every repeat safe and the loser re-reads; the twenty-way test proves one message |
| T-12.5-19 | Information disclosure | `voice_invalid` versus `voice_not_ready` revealing that an id exists | medium | accept | `voice_not_ready` is returned only for the caller's OWN asset (the ownership predicate comes first), so it reveals nothing about another member's asset |
</threat_model>
