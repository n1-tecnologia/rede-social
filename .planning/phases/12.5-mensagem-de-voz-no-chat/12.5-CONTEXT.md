# Phase 12.5: Mensagem de voz no chat - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-17
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Anyone in a chat can record a voice message on the spot and send it, and the other side plays it inside the conversation. Today a chat message is text only: `chat_messages.body` is required (1 to 2000 characters, a DB CHECK) and nothing in the repository records, uploads or plays audio. This phase adds a microphone to the shared composer, a recorder, an audio kind in the media pipeline, a player in the message bubble, and "Mensagem de voz" wherever a message's text is shown as a preview (inbox row, case cards, push).

It reuses what Phase 7 and the kernel already built: the conversation and its gapless `seq`, Realtime id-only signals, the unread badge, the push, and the media pipeline (`media_assets`, signed uploads, the private `media` bucket, `GET /v1/media/{id}/original`).

Requested by Igor on 2026-11-17 ("o user precisa conseguir gravar um áudio ali na hora e já enviar"). Inserted after Phase 12.4 because it touches the same composer, previews and push copy, and because Phase 11 (direct messages) will build on the composer this phase finishes. It runs before Phase 11 and Phase 13.

**"All chats" means one shared implementation.** The only chat that exists with routes and screens today is the support chat (`kind = 'support'`). Direct and group conversations (`kind = 'direct'`, `'group'`) are built by Phase 11 on the same composer, the same message model and the same bubble, so they inherit voice messages with no extra work, and Phase 11 must list "voice in direct chats" in its acceptance and must not plan the recorder or the player again. This phase builds no direct-message screen.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### What a voice message is
- **D-846:** A message carries either text or one recording, never both. There is no caption; a member who wants words with a voice message sends a text message after it. The text rules of Phase 7 are unchanged (1 to 2000 characters after trim). — **Reversibility:** costly — the message row gains a media reference beside `body` and the contracts, previews and push read the new shape, so adding a caption later changes the CHECK and every reader.
- **D-847:** Everyone who may send a text message in a chat may send a voice message: the member in the support chat, the staff replying, and every participant of a direct or group conversation once Phase 11 exists. It uses the same permission as text (`chat.support.contact` for the member, the staff reply route for the team); there is no new permission and no new module toggle.
- **D-848:** A recording lasts at most 5 minutes (300 seconds). The limit is enforced three times: the recorder stops at it, the upload contract declares a maximum duration for the audio kind, and the server refuses a longer one when the upload completes. A recording shorter than about one second is discarded by the recorder without sending (the threshold is Claude's Discretion).

### Recording and sending (the composer)
- **D-849:** One tap on the microphone starts recording. While it records, the composer shows a trash button (cancel), the elapsed time and a send button that stops the recording and sends it in the same tap. There is no step to listen before sending and no hold-to-record gesture. — **Reversibility:** reversible — a listen-before-send step can be added to the recorder later without touching the message model.
- **D-850:** At 5:00 the recording stops by itself and the member chooses to send or discard it; nothing is sent without that choice. When 30 seconds remain (at 4:30) the composer says so.
- **D-851:** The microphone shares the slot of the send button. With the text field empty the button is a microphone; as soon as there is text it is the usual send button. The composer stays one row at 320 px.
- **D-852:** The microphone permission is requested at the first tap on the microphone, never when the page loads. When it is refused or the device has no microphone, the member reads a plain pt-BR sentence and the text composer keeps working.

### In the conversation (the bubble)
- **D-853:** The bubble shows a simple player: a play and pause button, a progress bar the listener can drag, and the duration as `m:ss`. One voice message plays at a time; starting another stops the previous one. There is no waveform. — **Reversibility:** reversible — a waveform needs the peaks stored or computed per message, and can be added later as an optional column or a client-side computation.
- **D-854:** The listener can change the playback speed between 1x, 1.5x and 2x with a small button in the player. The choice applies to that player and is not stored.
- **D-855:** There is no "listened" indicator and no transcription. Per-message read or played receipts stay in V2-CHAT-02.

### Storage, access and quota
- **D-856:** A voice message stays as long as its conversation exists. There is no expiry and no new sweep; the existing media sweeper keeps removing uploads that were never completed.
- **D-857:** A voice recording counts in the tenant's storage quota under the same rules as the other media (the tenant ceiling and the member's share of it), through the existing media quota code, with no separate budget.
- **D-858:** Only the participants of the conversation can open the audio (for the support chat, the member and the tenant's staff who can read cases). A member of the same tenant who knows the media id must get nothing, and no user of another tenant can. The kernel's media read gate (`app.media_asset_hidden`) hides only assets carried by posts, stories and lessons today; this phase adds the chat case to it, as the story gate did. — **Reversibility:** costly — the gate is read by every media request, so a later change touches the hot path.
- **D-859:** Nobody deletes or edits a sent voice message, exactly as nobody does for text today (the chat is append-only, with no UPDATE or DELETE policy for lanes). The sender and the staff have no delete action in this phase.

### Previews and cases (defaults assumed, Igor may overturn before execution)
- **D-860:** Wherever a message's text is shown as a preview, a voice message reads "Mensagem de voz" followed by its duration as `m:ss`. This covers the inbox row, the "Meus chamados" card, the case subject fallback and the push body (staff and member). The copy lives in the pt-BR catalog and in the push copy builder.
- **D-861:** A voice message may be the first message of a new case. With no typed subject the case label falls back to "Mensagem de voz". The category above the composer works as today (`Outro` preselected). A voice reply to a `Resolvido` case reopens it and a voice message after `Fechado` opens a new case, because the case transitions read no body (D-805, D-801, D-802).
- **D-862:** The refusals that apply to a text message apply to a voice message in the same order and with the same codes: a closed case, a blocked or removed member, and a conversation the sender is not part of.

### Carried forward from earlier phases (informational)
- **D-221:** [informational] Reversed for the support kind by D-800 of Phase 12.4; the chat stays one list of messages per case.
- **D-225:** [informational] The text body rules of Phase 7 (plain text, 1 to 2000 characters, line breaks kept, URLs auto-linked, no markdown, no link preview) stay for text messages and are not changed here.
- **D-800:** [informational] Cases and their states (Phase 12.4); a voice message goes through the same case transitions as text.
- **D-811:** [informational] "No attachments in this phase" of Phase 12.4 stays true for files; a voice message is a recording made in the composer, not an attachment picker, and arbitrary file attachments remain V2-CHAT-02.
- **D-844:** [informational] The hourly kernel sweeper and the chat manifest's `sweepFunctions` are reused as they are; this phase adds no job.

### Claude's Discretion
The researcher and the planner decide these; Igor is not asked unless a choice changes what a member sees.
- **Recording format and playback.** The browser decides the container: `webm` with Opus on Chromium and Firefox, `mp4` on Safari. Decide whether to store what the recorder produced (allowing several audio mime types in the bucket allow-list, the contract and the magic-byte check) or to transcode in the worker to one format; confirm that every stored format plays in Chromium, Firefox and Safari on desktop and phone before choosing. Nothing here can be tested on a real iPhone locally, so the plan must say which proofs are Igor's.
- **Media model.** A new `audio` media kind and a purpose such as `voice`, or the existing `file` kind with a new purpose; where the duration is captured and checked; whether `chat_messages.body` becomes nullable with a "text or recording" CHECK, or a `kind` column is added; the RLS INSERT check that the referenced asset belongs to the author and is `ready`; keep the change expand-only so the core message rows are not rewritten.
- **Upload path.** Reuse `useSignedUpload` and `lib/upload.ts`. A 5-minute Opus recording is about 1 to 2 MB and an AAC `mp4` about 2 to 3 MB, both under the 6 MB single-PUT threshold, so TUS should not be needed; confirm.
- **Serving.** `GET /v1/media/{id}/original` answers 302 to a 1-hour signed Storage URL; check that a 5-minute audio plays, seeks and resumes inside that hour and with the `Cache-Control: private` header.
- **Optimistic bubble.** What the sender sees while the upload runs, on failure and on retry; whether a failed upload keeps the take for one more try.
- **Recorder details.** The short-recording threshold, the live indicator while recording (counter and optional level bars), what happens when the app loses focus or a call interrupts, and the 30-second warning wording.
- **Previews in SQL.** The branch at each place that derives text from `body` (inbox row, case card, subject fallback, push selects), the "Mensagem de voz" copy and the duration source.
- **Module housekeeping.** `packages/modules/chat/README.md` must list every new kernel import, event and job (`apps/api/tests/unit/module-readmes.test.ts`); new routes need entries in `apps/api/tests/isolation-inventory.ts`; the contract tests that pin `MEDIA_LIMITS`, the bucket allow-list pgTAP (`supabase/tests/070-media-bucket.sql`) and `apps/web/lib/csp.test.ts` change with the new mimes.
- **Native shell.** The microphone permission text of the app shell (`packages/contracts/src/native.ts`, `scripts/app-release/config.ts`) is worded for video sound only; change it so Phase 13 ships a text that covers voice messages.

</decisions>

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `ChatComposer` is one props-only component for the member and the staff (a textarea and one send button, `onSend(body: string): Promise<boolean>`); the microphone takes the send button's slot (D-851).
- `MessageBubble` renders `body` through `linkify`; the player is a second branch beside it.
- `ThreadPane.onSend` already does the optimistic bubble and the case-aware send for both sides; the voice path follows it.
- The kernel media pipeline already does signed single-PUT uploads, `completeUpload` size and content-type checks, the quota rules and `GET /v1/media/{id}/original` with a 1-hour signed URL.
- `useSignedUpload` and `lib/upload.ts` (PUT, TUS above 6 MB) are the browser side.
- The CSP already allows `blob:` and the Supabase origin in `media-src`, and nothing in `next.config.ts`, `proxy.ts` or `vercel.json` sets a Permissions-Policy that blocks the microphone.

### Established Patterns
- The chat is append-only for lanes: no UPDATE or DELETE policy, and the grants are revoked; messages get a gapless `seq` from a BEFORE INSERT trigger; Realtime sends ids only and the data always comes through the API.
- Media rows are tenant-scoped and written through the admin lane; reads are tenant-wide by policy, so privacy for a kind comes from the `app.media_asset_hidden` gate (the story gate is the model).
- Previews are derived in SQL from `m.body` in five places (inbox row, case card, case subject fallback, staff and member push), and the push copy is built in `notification-copy.ts` (cut at 100 graphemes).
- A module lists its kernel imports, events and jobs in its README, and a unit test enforces it; new routes are listed in the isolation inventory.
- Drizzle holds the schema and `drizzle-kit generate` produces expand-only migrations applied by the Supabase CLI; every table keeps `tenant_id` and RLS.

### Integration Points
- A migration for `chat_messages` (a media reference and either a nullable `body` with a "text or recording" CHECK or a `kind` column), for the media kind and purpose CHECKs, for the bucket allow-list and for the media read gate.
- The member send route (`POST /v1/chat/support/messages`) and the staff reply route (`POST /v1/chat/conversations/{id}/messages`) accept a voice message.
- The message projection, `messageRowSchema` and `ChatMessageView` gain the recording's media id and duration.
- The previews and push copy gain the "Mensagem de voz" branch in each place named above.
