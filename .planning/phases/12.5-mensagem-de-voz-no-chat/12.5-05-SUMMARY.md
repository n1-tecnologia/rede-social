# Phase 12.5 Plan 05: The member's voice send Summary

**A member's recording now goes from a signed upload and the real ffmpeg derive to a stored voice message in a numbered support case, once per take with no client key, through the new `POST /v1/chat/support/voice-messages` and the published chat contracts the next plans parse.**

## Accomplishments

- `POST /v1/chat/support/voice-messages` (permission `chat.support.contact`, strict `{ mediaId, category?, subject? }`) beside the unchanged text route; the text schemas, routes and their tests are byte-identical apart from the additive `voice` key and the two appended issues.
- `voice-sql.ts`: `voiceGoneSql(alias)`, `loadVoiceAsset` (the caller's id compared explicitly), `storedVoiceMessage` and `assertVoiceUsable` (the refusal order: not-ready 409 only for the caller's own asset, then ONE `voice_invalid`).
- The service inserts a recording with `insert ... select` from the caller's own asset, so `voice_seconds` is the server-measured `duration_seconds`; a vanished asset at the insert is the same `voice_invalid`. `sendSupportMessage` keeps its signature and behaviour (its body moved into `supportSendInTx`, shared with the voice send).
- Idempotence: the caller's own message for the asset is read first (answer with `opened`/`reopened` false, no second `chat.message_sent`), and the loser of a concurrent double send re-reads in a new transaction after the `23505` of `chat_messages_tenant_media_asset_uq`. The CS004 retry loop is the text one.
- `chat-voice-fixtures.ts` for plans 06 to 09: real upload, in-process derive, cleanup that deletes messages before assets.
