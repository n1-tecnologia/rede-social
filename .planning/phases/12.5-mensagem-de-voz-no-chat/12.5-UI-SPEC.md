# Phase 12.5 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Scope.** A voice message is recorded in the shared composer and played in the shared bubble (D-846..D-862). This document contracts only what is new or changes on screen:

1. **The composer:** the microphone that shares the send button's slot, the permission request and its refusals, the recording state (counter, trash, send), the 4:30 notice, the 5:00 stop, an interrupted recording, the too-short discard, and how a recording ends when the screen changes (UI-D-840..UI-D-845).
2. **Sending:** the optimistic voice bubble with upload progress, ordering, the failed bubble (retry and discard), and the same refusals as text (UI-D-846, UI-D-847).
3. **The bubble player:** geometry, the two sides, every state (idle, loading, playing, paused, seeking, ended, error, unsupported), one audio at a time, the speed button, a long conversation with several audios (UI-D-848..UI-D-851).
4. **The case flow:** a voice message as the first message of a case, a voice reply to a `Resolvido` case, the staff reply that takes a case, the closed and blocked strips (UI-D-852).
5. **Previews** in the inbox row, the "Meus chamados" card, the case subject fallback and the push (UI-D-853), the **staff side and the desktop split** (UI-D-854).
6. Accessibility, catalogs and the native shell text, what the UI never shows or trusts (UI-D-855..UI-D-857).

Everything else is **reused unchanged** from `07-UI-SPEC.md`, sketch 007 and `12.4-UI-SPEC.md` (sketch 023): the thread, the case bars and strips, the opener, the sender label above a bubble, day separators, the gapless list, the inbox and "Meus chamados" geometry, the dialogs, sheets and toasts. **The direct and group conversation screens (Phase 11) are not drawn here**; they inherit the composer and bubble states below, and Phase 11 lists "voice in direct chats" in its acceptance and never plans the recorder or the player again (CONTEXT, domain).

**Design authority.** The design team's prototype (`reference/frontend-design/`) has **no voice message**: nothing here is ported. Every surface is **[designed]** from primitives that already ship, in the language of the shipped composer (the round 44px send button, the `rounded-2xl` field) and bubble (`bg-brand text-on-brand` own, `bg-bg-secondary` other).

**The design language, spacing scale, typography scale and colour model are inherited unchanged** from `02` to `12.4-UI-SPEC.md`. This document restates what the checker needs and records only the Phase 12.5 deltas. **Phase 12.5 adds no token, font, size, weight, library or dependency** (the recorder and the player are browser APIs: `MediaRecorder`, `getUserMedia`, `HTMLAudioElement`).

| **UI-D-849** | **Player states and rules** (D-853). One state machine per bubble, drawn identically on both sides. **Idle:** `Play`, thumb at 0, the time line shows the total "**0:42**". **Loading** (after a tap, before sound): the disc shows `LoaderCircle` (`motion-safe:animate-spin`; static `Play` at `opacity-50` under reduced motion), `aria-busy`, the bar disabled; a tap on the disc **cancels** and returns to idle; if no sound starts within about 15 seconds it becomes the error state. **Playing:** `Pause`, the thumb advances, the time line shows "**0:12 / 0:42**". **Paused:** `Play`, the thumb stays, same time line. **Seeking:** dragging the thumb (or the keyboard) updates the time line to the target live and the sound jumps on release; a playing message keeps playing through a seek. **Ended:** back to idle visuals (thumb at 0, total shown, `Play`), **no auto-advance to the next message** (A-UI-11). **Error** (the link failed, expired after its hour, the file is gone, the network dropped): the disc becomes `RotateCw` (name "Tentar novamente"), the time line is replaced by "**Não foi possível carregar a mensagem de voz.**" 14/400, the thumb **stays where it was**, the bar is disabled; ## Motion & Accessibility

- **One new animation, and it is built in.** The recording dot pulses with Tailwind's own `motion-safe:animate-pulse`; the loading disc spins with the shipped `motion-safe:animate-spin`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the dot is static and the disc shows a dimmed `Play`. The thumb and fill move with the audio's clock with **no transition**; the mic-to-send swap and every state change are instant.
- **Speech is polite, never assertive**, from one persistent status region in the composer (UI-D-855). The counter is never live; whole-minute, 4:30 and 5:00 announcements replace per-second noise.
- **Stable names.** The voice bubble is one group named "Mensagem de voz, 42 segundos"; the controls inside keep the names of UI-D-855.
- **Focus.** The slot button keeps focus from the mic to the send state and back; trash is Shift+Tab from send; a live change, a new message or playback moves no focus; no keyboard shortcut is bound.
- **Touch.** Every new control is 44px (`HIT_AREA`, `hitArea`, `h-11 w-11`); the bar's touch area is 44px tall and does not overlap its neighbours (8px gaps, the pills' areas extend 2px).
- **Text safety.** Every string is a React text node; the player shows no file name or URL.
