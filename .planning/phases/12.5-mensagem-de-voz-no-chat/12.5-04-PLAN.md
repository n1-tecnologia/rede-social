## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| member or staff lane to `chat_messages` | a lane may insert only as itself, into a conversation it belongs to, and only its own ready voice asset |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-12.5-10 | Spoofing | a media id of another member or another tenant used in a send | high | mitigate | the INSERT policy requires owner, tenant, kind, purpose, `ready`, not deleted and an equal duration; the unique index blocks reuse across conversations; pgTAP matrix with the cross-tenant negative beside its positive control |
| T-12.5-11 | Tampering | a message with both or neither of text and recording, or an out-of-range duration | high | mitigate | `chat_messages_content_chk` and the replaced body CHECK, validated, refused for every writer including the admin lane |

| T-12.5-13 | Elevation of privilege | a member sends into a conversation they are not part of | high | mitigate | the shipped participant predicate of the policy is unchanged and re-asserted with a voice row |
| T-12.5-14 | Denial of service | the migration locks or rewrites `chat_messages` | medium | mitigate | expand-only: nullable relax, two nullable columns, a partial index over an empty set; the CHECKs are NOT VALID then validated; no table rewrite |
