# Phase 12.5 manual proofs (P1 to P34)

This file is the ledger of the proofs only the owner can run: recording and playing a voice message on a real iPhone
(Safari and the installed app), on an Android phone and on desktop Firefox, the cross-play between them, an incoming
call during a recording, seeking and resuming inside the signed link's hour and after it, the installed app's service
worker, the screen-reader walk, the held-out visual rows, the sketch 024 approval record, the run of the end-to-end
spec, the image measurement on Cloud Run, every hosted release step and the first-day checks, the full `pnpm verify`
(it resets and seeds the local database, so it is his), and the reading of the assumptions, defaults and wording the
plans left to him. Everything an agent could build is built and proved on the local stack (plans 01 to 17: pgTAP,
integration, unit and component tests, and a Playwright spec that is written and listed but was never run); what is
left here cannot be faked, so nothing in this file is ever recorded as done by an agent.

**The rule.** Every row ships `pending`. A row becomes `passed` only when Igor pastes the evidence of its steps and
the date, in the row, himself. An agent never edits a Status line and never writes evidence it did not see. A phase
whose ledger has a `pending` row closes `human_needed`, never green. (Project memory: a blanket UAT pass is checked
against `docs/DEPLOY.md` before it is recorded, and a phone cannot reach `*.localhost`, so a row that needs a phone
needs a homolog or production host.)

**The evidence rule.** Paste shapes, counts, statuses, HTTP codes, exit codes and the words the screens show.
**Never** a key, a password, a token, a connection string, a webhook token, an e-mail address of a member, a project
ref, a signed link or a recording (not the file, not a link to it). Replace a ref by `<ref>` and a host by `<host>` in
what you paste; name a tenant "tenant 1", "tenant 2".

**The procedures** are in `docs/DEPLOY.md`, "Phase 12.5 release (voice messages)". The release order of this phase
differs from 12.4: migrations, then the API and the worker from the REBUILT image (it holds ffmpeg), then the web.

**The design approval is yours.** Sketch `024` (the design gate) carries `approved: true`, kind **provisional**,
recorded on your word "aprovado" of 2026-11-17 in the session, not by an executor, with no line-by-line answers. It
was read as "keep every default as drawn": A-UI-1 to A-UI-26, A-UI-1 (the case label with no duration) and A-UI-2 (the
preview "Mensagem de voz · 0:42" with a microphone glyph) included. It stays provisional until you walk the built
screens (rows P1 to P3, P12 to P20 and P21).

## P1 - Record and play on an iPhone in Safari (VOICE-36)

- **Proves:** that a member records, sends and plays a voice message on the browser nobody can test locally. Three things
  are unproven until now: that WebKit records and which mime it produces (`audio/mp4`, or `audio/webm` on Safari 18.4
  and later: the recorder tries `audio/webm;codecs=opus` first, then `audio/mp4`, then `audio/ogg;codecs=opus`; if the
  iPhone proof shows the WebM path misbehaving, flip the probe so `audio/mp4` comes first, a one-line change in
  `useVoiceRecorder.ts`), that the first tap on the play disc plays at once (the player assigns the source inside the
  tap, assumption A1), and that the speaker is not quiet or on the earpiece after a recording (assumption A2: the
  microphone is released on every exit).
- **Needs:** a homolog or production tenant with the `chat` module on, a member account, a team account, an iPhone.
- **Steps:**
  1. As the member, open `/suporte`, tap the microphone, allow the prompt (Safari asks again on every page load), speak
     for ten seconds, tap send. Note the mime if you can read it in the web inspector; otherwise note the Safari version.
  2. As the team, on another device, open the case and play the message: it plays on the first tap.
  3. As the member, play a team recording on the iPhone: the first tap plays; the volume is the normal speaker volume.
  4. Record again right after playing: the speaker is not quiet and the recording starts.
- **Evidence to paste:** the iPhone model and iOS version, the Safari version, the mime if known, whether each first
  tap played, whether the volume was normal, and the words of any refusal the screen showed.
- **Status:** pending

select cardinality(allowed_mime_types) as mimes,
       allowed_mime_types @> array['audio/webm', 'audio/mp4', 'audio/ogg'] as has_audio, public
