**A live is now an agenda entry whose state is the clock's answer through ONE helper: six `/v1/courses/lives*` + `/lessons` routes where `liveStatusAt` projects `scheduled | live | recorded` per request and `cancelled_at` is the only stored state (set once by `coalesce(cancelled_at, now())`, never a delete), an `https`-only `externalUrl`, a product gate on `course_live_products` and a replay pointing at a live lesson of the tenant settable only once the live has ended; a Lives tab beside Trilhas with Próximas · Passadas chips and rows carrying the tenant-timezone date line and the server's pill; and a live form that borrows the events form's date/time pair, builds the instant in the tenant's zone with `Intl` alone, gates by product, picks the replay from a sheet grouped by course and cancels through the danger dialog into a read-only state — proved by 6 integration cases, 61 unit cases and 3 e2e cases on two projects.**

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The live form's submit was dead while the link, the start or the duration was invalid**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (the first e2e run: `locator.click` waited 120 s on a disabled "Agendar live" after typing the `http://` link)
- **Issue:** Task 2 gated `submittable` on `valid` (title AND start AND duration AND url), so the E13 error states ("start, duration and url errors under their fields") were unreachable by a click — the button simply stayed disabled, contradicting the UI-SPEC and the e2e truth ("an `http://` link shows the url error").
- **Fix:** the submit arms on a title alone (the TrackForm rule) and `submit()` judges the structured fields on the attempt, returning before any write while `valid` is false; the errors render under their fields.
- **Files modified:** `apps/web/app/(app)/cursos/lives/LiveForm.tsx`
- **Verification:** the 15 `LiveForm` unit cases still pass (cases 3, 4 and 6 are exactly the errors-on-attempt path); the lives e2e passes 6/6 then 16/16 with browse
- **Committed in:** `aca3ad89` (the Task 3 commit)

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `scripts/check-static-routes.sh` not run locally (the Task 2 verify's last step)**
