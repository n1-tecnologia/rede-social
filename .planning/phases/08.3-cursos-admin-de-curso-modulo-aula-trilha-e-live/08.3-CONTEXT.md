# Phase 08.3: Cursos: Admin de Curso, Módulo, Aula, Trilha e Live - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-13
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase adds the per-tenant school, the `courses` module, which `super_admin` turns on or off in `tenant_modules` (off by default, like `store`). It is **admin-first**: the whole catalogue is authored from the tenant admin, and the member-facing side is deliberately NOT built here, but its data model is.

- **Courses.** `admin_tenant` creates, edits, publishes, unpublishes and archives courses: title, tagline, description, category, level, cover, instructor (name + optional photo), "what you will master" bullets, optional certificate hours.
- **Modules and lessons.** Ordered modules inside a course, ordered lessons inside a module, reorderable. A lesson has a title, a description, an optional video uploaded directly to Mux through the existing pipeline, a "free sample" flag and up to 10 materials uploaded through the existing attachment pipeline.
- **Access, modeled not surfaced.** A course is open to every member or linked to one or more store products (08.2 entitlements). A module may require the previous one. A per-membership lesson progress table exists. RLS, policies and pgTAP cover all of it; no member screen reads it yet.
- **Tracks.** Title, subtitle, audience, cover and an ordered list of published courses.
- **Lives.** An agenda entry: title, description, cover, start, planned duration, external link, optional products, optional replay lesson. State derived from the clock. No streaming in the app.
- **Isolation.** The two-tenant suite covers every new table, route and storage prefix, with 08.1's shared-identity fixture.

Out of this phase: member catalogue and player, progress UI, certificate PDF emission, in-app live streaming, live reminders, quizzes, lesson comments, drip scheduling. The member-facing port of the prototype's `app/membros/**` is the following phase.

The roadmap entry (`.planning/ROADMAP.md` § Phase 08.3) holds the 8 success criteria and the user's decisions of 2026-10-13 (admin first, tracks and lives as the cuttable last plans). They are carried forward and not re-opened.

### Name, route and navigation
- **D-400: The module is `courses`, the route prefix is `/cursos`, the label is "Cursos".** Catalog namespace `courses` (`apps/web/messages/pt-BR/courses.json`). `/membros` stays the member directory (03-05). — **Reversibility:** costly — the key lands in the `tenant_modules` CHECK, the permission string, the route inventory and every link; renaming later is a migration plus a sweep.
- **D-401: The member entry (next phase) is a BottomNav tab, and Cursos wins the slot over Reels.** With `courses` on, Cursos takes the third tab and Reels moves to the TopBar as an icon; with `courses` off, nothing changes. A fixed rule, no per-tenant setting. **In this phase the manifest declares no `nav` entry**, so members see no tab and no icon; the following phase adds `nav: { placement: 'tab', ... }` and the demotion rule in `packages/core/ui/nav.ts` (`buildNav`). Recorded now so the manifest and the nav code are shaped for it.
- **D-402: The admin enters through a "Cursos" row in Configurações → Administração that opens `/cursos`.** `/cursos` is the hub: for a manager it shows a chip sub-nav **Cursos | Trilhas | Lives** (the prototype's `AreaTabs` pattern, built from `packages/ui` `Chip`/`Tabs`), each tab a list with its "Novo" action and in-context edit/archive. There is **no `/configuracoes/cursos`** tree; the next phase adds the member tabs (Início, Progresso) to the same hub. A `member` reaching `/cursos` in this phase gets the kernel not-found (the route is permission-gated until member screens exist).
- **D-403: Permission `courses.manage`, `admin_tenant` only by construction** (D-338 posture, store's WR-03 lesson): routes authorise by the permission, SQL write policies by the `admin_tenant` claim, and the registry unit test pins the composition.

### Access, lock and product
- **D-404: A course links to zero or more store products; an entitlement to any of them opens it; no product means open to every member.** Many-to-many `course_products`, written only through the course form's product multi-select (one write path, the STORE-03 posture; the product form does not edit course links in this phase). The gate reuses the store's entitlement model and the kernel-seam pattern of `community-gate.ts`: the kernel names `app.course_locked_ids()` (stub: nothing locked), the store module gives it a body. — **Reversibility:** costly — the seam's SQL signatures are declared by a kernel migration and consumed by RLS predicates; the next phase's member policies are written against them.
- **D-405: `courses` does not require `store`.** No `requires: ['store']` in the manifest. With the store off, the product multi-select is absent from the course form, every course is open, and links already stored stay dormant (the STORE-11 behaviour for communities). Turning the store on later re-activates them.
- **D-406: The sequential lock is modeled and editable now, enforced later.** A module carries `requires_previous` ("Exige concluir o módulo anterior") that the admin toggles in the module form. Nothing evaluates it in this phase; the member phase resolves it against progress (the prototype's `resolveLock`).
- **D-407: A lesson carries `is_free` ("Aula aberta"), chosen per lesson by the admin.** Meaningful only on a product-gated course (a sample a non-buyer may watch); zero free lessons is valid. Modeled now; surfaced in the member phase.
- **D-408: Progress is modeled per membership, unused in this phase.** A `course_lesson_progress` table keyed by `(tenant_id, membership/user, lesson_id)` with `completed_at` and `last_position_seconds`, RLS own-or-admin, covered by pgTAP. No route writes it in 08.3 beyond the isolation tests.

### Lesson content
- **D-409: Video is optional and, when present, comes only from a direct Mux upload.** A lesson may be text plus materials (the prototype's "prática"). When it has a video it is one `media_assets` row created through the existing direct-upload flow (`apps/web/components/media/VideoUploadField.tsx`, the `VideoProvider` seam); `duration_seconds` and the thumbnail come from the ready asset and are never typed. Playback is signed (gated content must not be a shareable URL). **No external YouTube/Vimeo links.** Cost was examined on 2026-10-13 (Mux vs S3+CloudFront+MediaConvert vs Cloudflare Stream): delivery dominates, S3 is not cheaper at any quality that keeps adaptive bitrate and does not transcode, Cloudflare Stream is the cheap swap behind the same seam if delivery cost ever matters; the user confirmed Mux. — **Reversibility:** reversible for "optional"; "Mux only" is a product decision recorded with its cost basis in `<specifics>`.
- **D-410: Materials — up to 10 per lesson, 50 MB each, any kind the pipeline accepts** (`attachment` purpose: PDF, spreadsheet, slides, images), in the private bucket, served by short-lived signed URLs. The admin sees name, kind and size, reorders and removes. The 50 MB cap is the Supabase Free plan limit already enforced by the pipeline.
- **D-411: Categories are a per-tenant list managed by the admin — name + cover image, no icon.** A course has zero or one category. The prototype's six facial-harmonisation categories and lucide icons are mock content, not product. Category management lives inside the Cursos tab (a "Categorias" action), not in Configurações.
- **D-412: The instructor is free text plus an optional photo** (a `media_assets` image). No membership FK: guests who are not members, and the admin themselves, fit; nothing breaks when a person leaves.

### Lifecycle, tracks and lives
- **D-413: Only the course has a publication state — `draft` → `published` → `archived`, with unpublish back to `draft`.** Modules and lessons inherit it. Publishing is refused while any lesson's video asset is not `ready` (a clear inline notice names the lessons). Archive never deletes (communities posture). Unpublishing a course that already has progress rows keeps them. — **Reversibility:** costly — the state is the predicate the next phase's member read policies are written against.
- **D-414: Reordering uses up/down arrows on each row**, persisted through an idempotent `PUT .../order` that replaces the whole order of a parent (modules in a course, lessons in a module, courses in a track), the communities pattern. No drag-and-drop.
- **D-415: A track is an ordered path over published courses only.** Title, subtitle, audience ("Para quem está começando"), cover, ordered `course_ids`. A course may belong to several tracks. An archived or unpublished course drops out of the track at read time (the link row stays, so republishing restores it). A track has no lessons and no certificate hours of its own; it sums its courses.
- **D-416: A live is an agenda entry whose state is derived from the clock.** Fields: title, description, cover, `starts_at`, `duration_min`, `external_url` (any `https` URL: YouTube, Zoom, Meet, anything), optional products (same gate as courses), `replay_lesson_id` (nullable, set by the admin after the live, points to a lesson of the tenant). `scheduled | live | recorded` are computed from `starts_at + duration_min`, never stored; `cancelled_at` is a stored state, never a delete. No streaming in the app, no reminders in this phase.
- **D-417: Every admin surface goes through the sketch gate** (D-365 pattern): `.planning/sketches/009-phase-08.3-designed-screens` plus a UI-SPEC, approved (provisionally) by the product owner before UI tasks; the approval line in the sketch README is pasted by the user. Surfaces: the `/cursos` hub with chips and the three admin lists; the course form (products multi-select, category, instructor, cover, outcomes, hours); the curriculum editor (modules and lessons with arrows, `requires_previous`, publish-blocked notice); the lesson form (video upload states, free flag, materials list); the category manager; the track form with course picker and order; the live form with cancel and replay picker; the archive/unpublish confirmations; the Configurações row.

### Claude's Discretion
Each item has a stated default. The planner may refine it but should not reverse it without asking.
- **Media purposes.** Default: add `lesson` (video) to `MEDIA_PURPOSES`; reuse `cover` for course, category, track and live covers and for the instructor photo; `attachment` for materials. The planner may choose one new `course` purpose instead if the width ladder differs.
- **Gate seam shape.** Default: a second kernel seam `course-gate.ts` with `app.course_locked_ids()` / `_for(uuid)` mirroring `community-gate.ts`, stubs in a kernel migration, bodies in the store module's functions migration. The planner may instead generalise the existing seam to a `(entity, id)` form if it keeps every 08.2 isolation assertion green.
- **Field caps** (measured on the trimmed string, the store's code-point note applies). Default: course title 120, tagline 160, description 5,000, outcomes ≤ 10 items of ≤ 120, certificate hours 0–999; module title 120, summary 500; lesson title 120, description 2,000; category name 60; track title 120, subtitle 160, audience 60; live title 120, description 2,000, duration 5–600 min.
- **Level.** Default: a fixed optional enum `iniciante | intermediario | avancado` (prototype `CourseLevel`), no admin-managed levels.
- **Lesson type.** Default: no `type` column; "video" vs "prática" is derived from the presence of a video asset, and "replay" is a live pointing at the lesson.
- **Course cover.** Default: one image per course; the prototype's two crops (3:4 poster, 16:10 hero) and `coverPosition` are handled by CSS object-fit in the member phase. The planner may add `cover_position` (`top | center`) if it is one column and no UI.
- **Deleting modules and lessons.** Default: soft delete (`deleted_at`) because progress rows will reference them; the admin UI says "Remover" and the row leaves every list.
- **Categories removal.** Default: a category with courses cannot be removed (409); an empty one can.
- **Signed material URLs.** Default: 10-minute TTL, minted by `GET /v1/courses/lessons/{id}/materials/{materialId}/url` behind the manage permission in this phase (the member lane arrives with the next phase).
- **Video encoding tier.** Default: the provider's basic 1080p tier (free encoding on Mux). A per-tenant 720p cap is a deferred cost lever.
- **A `member` on `/cursos` or on a `/v1/courses/*` route in this phase.** Default: web not-found; API `403 FORBIDDEN` from the permission guard (the module is on, the role lacks the permission). Member read routes come next phase.
- **Course ordering in the admin list.** Default: newest first with a `draft | published | archived` chip filter; no manual order of the catalogue in this phase.

</decisions>

## Specific Ideas

- **The prototype is the vocabulary.** Field names and semantics follow `types/member.ts`: durations stored in seconds, hours always derived; `unlocksAfter` becomes the boolean `requires_previous` on the module; `lockReason: 'premium'` becomes "linked to a product"; `isFree` stays; `Live.replayLessonId` stays; live status derived, never stored.
- **Copy anchors (pt-BR), finalised in the UI-SPEC:** "Cursos", "Trilhas", "Lives", "Categorias", "Novo curso", "Novo módulo", "Nova aula", "Nova trilha", "Nova live", "Rascunho", "Publicado", "Arquivado", "Publicar", "Despublicar", "Arquivar", "Aula aberta", "Exige concluir o módulo anterior", "Liberado pelos produtos", "Materiais", "Instrutor", "Carga horária", "O que você vai dominar", "Replay", "Agendada", "Ao vivo", "Gravada", "Cancelada", "Não é possível publicar: N aulas ainda processam o vídeo".
- **Cost basis recorded for D-409 (2026-10-13).** Mux 1080p basic: encoding free, storage US$ 0.003/min/month, delivery US$ 0.001/min after 100,000 free minutes/month. A 200 h catalogue with 1,000 students watching 20 h/month ≈ US$ 1,100/month, almost all delivery; a 20 h pilot with 100 students ≈ US$ 24/month. S3 + CloudFront at 1080p ≈ 3x the delivery cost and needs MediaConvert (~US$ 0.05/min uploaded) plus a pipeline that already exists for Mux. Cloudflare Stream ≈ parity at scale, no free tier; it is the documented swap behind `VideoProvider`.
- **The admin's mental model:** one hub, three tabs, "Novo" at the top of each list, edit in context, publish from the course page once every video is ready.
