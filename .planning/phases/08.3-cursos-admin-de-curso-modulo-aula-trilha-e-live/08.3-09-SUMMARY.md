# Phase 08.3 Plan 09: The lesson form, the video states and the materials Summary

**The admin authors a lesson on `/cursos/{courseId}/aulas/…`: an optional video that goes browser → Mux through the kernel field under the `lesson` purpose and is shown in five states (the processing card says the form may be saved and the course page is what waits; the ready preview prints the asset's own `mm:ss`), the "Aula aberta" switch whose helper reads gated or open from the course's products, and up to ten materials uploaded browser → Storage, labelled by kind and size from the asset, reordered with arrows, removed, capped, saved as ONE ordered set with the lesson and opened through a ten-minute URL minted per tap.**
