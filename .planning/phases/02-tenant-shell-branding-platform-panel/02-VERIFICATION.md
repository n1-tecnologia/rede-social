### Gaps Summary

The phase goal is achieved in the codebase for every roadmap criterion on its normal path: the served brand per host (pre- and post-login, phone and desktop, build-output gate green), the panel provisioning flow (create → list → settings → module toggle → attach/verify a domain → invite → accept, all against the local stack under Playwright), the shared-UI port with the prototype brand removed, and the PWA/mail/catalog surface.

One declared must-have is only partially true: **plan 02-09's domain poller cadence and 7-day expiry do not survive a provider error.** `checkDomain` swallows `domainProvider.verify` failures and answers `pending` without re-enqueueing the `kernel.domain-verify` job and without applying `verifyDeadlineAt`; the job wrapper only re-arms on a thrown error. With the real Vercel provider (the expected production configuration) a single 429/5xx/timeout stops automatic verification for that host until a super_admin manually presses "Verificar agora", and the host never expires. The fake provider never throws, so the local gate cannot see it and no test pins it. This is the review's CR-01; it contradicts the 02-09 truth and is recorded as a `partial` gap with a concrete fix (re-arm + deadline check inside the error branch's transaction, plus one integration case with a throwing provider stub). Nothing else in the review contradicts a must-have on its normal path; the remaining warnings are listed as advisory anti-patterns and surfaced for prioritisation in human item 12.

Two truths are uncertain and need a human: the design team's review of the panel screens (SC3 — only the product owner's provisional approval is recorded) and the real-device standalone launch (SC4 — provable only on a hosted HTTPS origin). The hosted provider/mail/CI proofs remain explicit backstops for Phase 01.1.
