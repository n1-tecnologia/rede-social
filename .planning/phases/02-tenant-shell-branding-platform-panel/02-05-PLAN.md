## Phase Goal

**As a** member of a tenant, **I want to** open my organization's own domain and see its branded, installable app on phone and desktop — branded already on the login page — **so that** the community feels like my organization's, not TRIA's. **As** TRIA's `super_admin`, **I want to** provision a tenant end-to-end (branding, modules, custom domain, first admin) from the platform panel on the platform domain, **so that** no tenant setup touches the database.

<objective>
The provisioning API slice: a `super_admin` creates, lists, inspects, rebrands, suspends and reconfigures a tenant's modules over `/v1/platform/*`, with the first-admin invite stored as pending and sent automatically once a verified primary host exists. Services live in the kernel platform lane, routes are restructured into `routes/platform/*` so later plans (domains 02-09, branding 02-13) mount their own files.

Purpose: after this plan a super_admin can provision a tenant end-to-end through the API (ROADMAP criterion 2 minus domains/UI), and the flags cache proves ROLE-04 without a redeploy.
Output: `platform/{tenants,modules,invites}.ts` services, `routes/platform/{index,tenants}.ts`, `platform-tenants.test.ts`, extended `modules.test.ts`.
</objective>
