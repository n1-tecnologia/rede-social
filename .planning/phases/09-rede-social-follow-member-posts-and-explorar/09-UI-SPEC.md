# Phase 09 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Scope.** With the toggleable module **"Rede social"** (`social`) on, members follow each other and publish feed posts, and a new **Explorar** view shows the posts of the people a member follows. Início stays the organization's voice. This document contracts only what is new or changes:

1. the **Início · Explorar** chip row and the **"Encontrar pessoas"** entry (UI-D-491, UI-D-492);
2. **Explorar** itself: list, empty states, own posts, the moderator's view (UI-D-493 to UI-D-497);
3. the **Seguir / Seguindo** button (UI-D-498);
4. the **"Siga para ver"** locked post screen and the mid-session refusal (UI-D-499, UI-D-500);
5. the **member profile** with counts, the **private lists** and the **directory for members** (UI-D-501 to UI-D-503);
6. the **member composer** variant of `/criar` (UI-D-504);
7. the **platform panel** `social` row, its confirmation and its limits form (UI-D-505);
8. the **notice** for a comment on a member post, the **moderation log** sentence, **Reels** and the **Admin badge** (UI-D-506 to UI-D-509).

The post card, comment sheet, like, share, link card, video player and upload states are **reused unchanged** from `04-UI-SPEC.md`. Nothing in them is re-specified here.

**Design authority.** `reference/frontend-design/` is the source for three surfaces, marked **[proto]**: `components/profile/FollowButton.tsx`, `ProfileStats.tsx` and `UserListItem.tsx`, plus `app/(app)/profile/{followers,following}/page.tsx` for the list screens. The prototype's `app/(app)/explore/page.tsx` is **not** the model (CONTEXT canonical refs): Explorar here is a following-only feed. Every other surface in this phase is **[designed]** in the prototype's language, from primitives that already ship in `@rede-social/ui` and `@rede-social/core/ui`. The design language, spacing scale, typography scale and colour model are **inherited unchanged** from the approved `02`–`08.7-UI-SPEC.md`. This document restates them for the checker and records only the Phase 09 deltas.

**Upstream.**
- `09-CONTEXT.md` decisions are cited as **D-466..D-484**. `09-RESEARCH.md` is cited as **R-Pn** (patterns), **R-OQn** (open questions) and **R-SOCIAL-nn / FOLLOW-nn / EXPL-nn** (its draft requirements).
- The UI decisions this phase opens are **UI-D-491..UI-D-512**. They continue after 08.7's **UI-D-490**, the highest id in `.planning`, checked 2026-10-19 with `grep -rhoE "UI-D-[0-9]+" .planning | sort -t- -k3 -n -u | tail -1` → `UI-D-490`.
- Questions that CONTEXT and RESEARCH left open and that change what the screen shows are decided below with a stated default and repeated in **§ Pending User Confirmation**.

**Sketch gate (D-33, D-365).** The **[designed]** surfaces go through `/gsd-sketch 9` (static HTML drawn from this contract) and design-team approval **before they are coded**: the chip row with "Encontrar pessoas", the three Explorar empty states, "Siga para ver", the member profile counts block, the private lists, the member composer, and the platform `social` row with its sheet and confirmation. The **[proto]** surfaces (`FollowButton`, the counts row, `UserListItem`) do not need it. The record goes to `.planning/sketches/MANIFEST.md` as `012-phase-09-designed-screens`.

**Non-interactive session.** No question could be asked in this run. The shadcn gate and every open item below were decided with a stated default, so the orchestrator can confirm or overturn each one with one line.

## Spacing Scale

The standard scale (4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64) from `02`–`08.7-UI-SPEC.md`. **Every new element Phase 09 draws is on this scale.** The one non-scale value, 12px, is a scoped exception for shipped geometry (below), not a token.

| Token | Value | Usage in Phase 09 (new elements only) |
|-------|-------|----------------------------------------|
| xs | 4px | Gap between the audience line's glyph and text; the counts cells' number-to-label step (`gap-1`) |
| sm | 8px | The chip row's `gap-2`; the "Encontrar pessoas" button to the tabs |
| md | 16px | Page and row gutter (`px-4`), the counts block's `py-4`, the profile action block's `px-4 pt-4`, the locked card's `gap-4` |
| lg | 24px | The locked card's padding (`p-6`) and its top offset (`pt-6`); the desktop content gutter |
| xl | 32px | Not used by a new element |
| 2xl | 48px | Not used by a new element |
| 3xl | 64px | `EmptyState` icon circle and `py-16` (shipped) |

Exceptions:
- **44px** minimum touch target for every icon-only control and every `sm` button through `hitArea` (UI-D-443, inherited). The follow button on a row is 36px visually with a 44px hit area.
- **80px** (`Avatar xl`) and **24px** (the lock disc) on the locked screen; **96px** is not used. **`w-24` (96px)** the fixed follow-button width on a row; **360px** the breakpoint at which "Encontrar pessoas" collapses; **260px** the locked screen's body and button max width; **400px** the profile button's desktop max width. All multiples of 4.
- **2px** hairline geometry only (borders, focus ring). It is a stroke width, not a spacing value; no gap, padding or margin is 2px. The prototype's `gap-0.5` in the counts cells is **not** carried over: it becomes `gap-1` (4px, UI-D-501).
- **36px** the visual height of the shipped `Button size="sm"` (the follow button on a row); the `hitArea` brings the target to 44px. Not a spacing value.
- **680px** the desktop content column, inherited.
- **12px (`gap-3`, `py-3`) — scoped exception, shipped geometry only.** It applies to exactly these places and nowhere else: (1) the feed column's card gap in `FeedList` (`gap-3`); (2) the internals of the shipped `EmptyState` (`gap-3`); (3) the member row anatomy of `MemberRow` / `MembersList` / `MembersSkeleton` (`flex min-h-14 items-center gap-3 px-4 py-3`), which the split row of UI-D-502 and UI-D-503 repeats. **Justification, stronger than "inherited":** (a) Explorar renders the very same `FeedList` and `PostCard` components as Início, so a different gap would make the same card column space differently on the two tabs of one page, and the contract states that these components are reused unchanged; (b) the split row replaces `MemberRow` inside the same `/membros` list and under the same `MembersSkeleton`, so any other padding would shift the layout when the skeleton resolves and would give one directory two row heights depending on the viewer; (c) the shared primitives are shipped in `@rede-social/ui` and `apps/web/components/profile`, and re-gridding them is a change to 02–08.7 surfaces outside this phase's scope (PROTOTYPE.md: components are ported, not refactored in place). **No new element uses 12px:** the counts block (`py-4`, `gap-1`), the lock disc offset (`-bottom-1`), the locked card (`gap-4`, `p-6`), the chip row (`gap-2`) and the profile action block (`px-4 pt-4`) are all on the standard scale. A future phase that re-grids the shared primitives removes this exception.

## Motion & Accessibility

- **No new motion.** The follow button uses the shipped `transition-colors`; there is no spring, no burst, no count-up. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` nothing changes (nothing animated is added).
- Every icon-only control is 44×44 with a required `label`. Every `sm` button carries `hitArea`. The follow button's accessible name contains its visible text (UI-D-498).
- The chip row is a `tablist` of links (the shipped `Tabs`): ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home and End move through the roving tabindex and navigate; the active key is decided on the server from `?aba=`.
- The locked screen's lock disc and every empty-state icon are `aria-hidden`; the avatar link carries its own name.
- Counts on another member's profile are plain text, read as "{count} seguidores" by assistive technology through the cell's text order; on `/perfil` the link's `aria-label` carries the full phrase.
- After a successful follow on the locked screen, focus moves to the post's region heading; after "Remover publicação" succeeds, focus moves to the next card.
