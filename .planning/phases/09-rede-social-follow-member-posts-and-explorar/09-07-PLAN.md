2. **`visibility = 'followers'` in the statement is what keeps T-04-54 for organization posts**, and the live-author conjunct keeps the plan-04 default (a blocked author's posts are not moderated until unblocked), flagged for the user.

4. **Push copy follows the house convention** (title = tenant, actor in the body); UI-D-506's title/body split is not expressible in `NotificationPushHint` and the kernel is not changed by this phase.

6. **The `removal` key is emitted only when it is `'moderation'`** (the 08.2 Pitfall 10 rule), never as `null`.
