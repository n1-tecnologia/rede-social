<objective>
Member posts exist, and only the right people can read them. After this plan a member of a tenant with `social` on publishes a post that carries a stamped audience (`followers`), the post is visible to its author, to the people who follow the author and to the tenant's `admin_tenant` (and to the system lane), is invisible to everyone else - at the database, not only in a route - and shows up in the new Explorar scope of the feed and never in Início. The first notification rule (a member post notifies nobody) ships with it.

Task 1 is the tracer: ONE member post through every layer - the visibility column and the two restrictive policies (generated migration), the member stamp in `createPost`, the Início predicate, the Explorar scope with the simple `= any(followed set)` form, the notification guard and a real publish/follow/read check on rede-lab. Task 2 is the Wave 0 plan-measurement spike that decides the final Explorar query form and indexes (recorded, consumed by plan 04). Task 3 is the [BLOCKING schema] proof in Postgres: pgTAP 201.

Decisions implemented: D-471 (own posts are mixed into the author's Explorar), D-473 (a member post is born with `community_id` null; the API-level refusals of attachments and community arrive in plan 06), D-476 (a member post notifies nobody), D-483 (off hides every member post without deleting), D-484 (a blocked author's posts leave every follower's lane). The stamped audience supersedes CONTEXT's default of deciding "admin post or member post" from the author's role at read time (CONTEXT allowed it: "unless research finds a reason to stamp it"; RESEARCH finding 1: ADMIN-02 can promote and demote, so a read-time role would move old posts between Início and Explorar).

Purpose: authorization that depends on who is asking is the highest-risk thing this phase adds. The restrictive policy is the backstop that makes a route that forgets a guard fail closed; the service predicates (this plan and plan 04) are the precise rule.
Output: one migration, the policy and column definitions, the service and route changes, the notification guard, the spike record, the integration file and pgTAP 201.

<planning_decisions>
1. **Stamp the audience** (`feed_posts.visibility`, `'tenant' | 'followers'`). Reasons: a role change must not retroactively move old posts; a stamped column can be a literal partial-index predicate and a cheap RLS test; pgTAP can prove it. The CHECK `feed_posts_visibility_shape_chk` makes "a followers post has no community" a database fact.
2. **The staff exemption is `admin_tenant` plus the system user, not `support_tenant` by claim.** RESEARCH Pattern 2 exempted both staff roles because system lanes run with the `support_tenant` claim, and then had to document that a real support user could read member posts through RLS. Every system lane that reads `feed_posts` (notifications worker, dashboard build, courses) runs as the zero user, so exempting the zero user and `admin_tenant` keeps them working while a real support user gets no exemption (D-475: support gets neither the staff Explorar nor the removal). The exemption also requires `app.social_enabled()`, so "off hides everything" (D-483) holds for the admin too. Recorded as a deliberate deviation in RESEARCH's own Pitfall 2 alternative.
3. **Two fragments, one meaning.** `memberPostVisible(canModerate)` is the service predicate (`p.visibility = 'tenant'` or social enabled AND the author is live AND (the viewer follows or is the author, or `canModerate`)); the policy is the RLS form. They are written side by side in the same two files so a reviewer sees them agree.
4. **The first Explorar form is the simple one.** The tracer uses `author_user_id = any (SOCIAL_VISIBLE_AUTHOR_IDS)`; Task 2 measures it against the bounded LATERAL form and plan 04 adopts the recorded winner and creates the indexes. No index is created in this plan, on purpose: the spike decides the definitions.
5. **No `removal` field and no moderator mutation yet.** `canModerate` is plumbed through the list read here (plan 04 finishes the moderator view); the soft-delete branch and `deleted_by_user_id` writes are plan 07.
6. **The notification guard ships with the first member post** (not in plan 07) so that no test, seed or run between plans can broadcast a member's post to the whole tenant (T-09-24).
</planning_decisions>

<assumption_delta_decision>

Primary noun: the post's stamped audience (until now a feed post meant "the organization's voice, authored by admin_tenant"; FEED-08's `settings.postingPolicy` was the only seam, and Phase 9 makes the author a member as well). Decision: `promote` - the stamped `visibility` ('tenant' | 'followers') replaces the implicit "authored by admin_tenant = organization voice" and supersedes the posting-policy flag; it is written by the service from the author's role at publish time, never derived at read time, and is the predicate every audience, policy and partial index reads. Rationale: a role change (ADMIN-02) must not re-audience old posts, and a stamped column keeps RLS, indexes and pgTAP cheap and provable.
