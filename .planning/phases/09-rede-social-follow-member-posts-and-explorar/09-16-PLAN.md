<planning_decisions>
1. **Rename and widen the byte sum (WR-03 part 1).** The role is read from the uploader's CURRENT membership, as before, so an admin's uploads never count and a promoted member stops counting. An avatar is small, so counting it costs a member nothing in practice; the point is that no purpose is a side door. Applies with `social` off too: the condition is the role, not the module.
2. **Pending member videos are charged at their cap (WR-03 part 2).** Chosen over 'one in flight per member' because D-474 forbids a per-member quota in this phase and because an abandoned pending upload lives 24 h (the pending sweeper), which would lock that member out of video for a day. The cost is soft like the rest of the quota (EP-77) and releases on ready (replaced by the measured duration), rejected, failed, retired or swept. Recorded in the DEPLOY open decision by plan 09-17.
3. **The verdict is a pure function.** `judgeDuration` takes the member snapshot (or null), the static cap and the reported duration and answers `ok`, `too_long` or `unmeasurable`; `applyReady` stays the only writer. The unit test needs no database.
4. **`failed` with `transcode_failed`, not `rejected`.** `rejected` means the member was refused for a reason they can act on (too long); an unmeasurable asset is a processing failure, and `transcode_failed` is already in the closed `MEDIA_ISSUES` set with web copy, so no catalog string is added (the web check-ui-literals gate stays untouched).
5. **The declared size is the snapshot (WR-08).** The start path already judged `body.size` against the cap in force; `media_assets.bytes` stores it. Enforcing `info.size <= row.bytes` at complete is the photo analogue of the video `duration_cap_seconds` snapshot with no new column. It also closes the under-declaration route around the share: the budget was charged at the declared size, and an object larger than declared is now refused. For admin and for non-`post` member uploads the static limit still applies, unchanged.
6. **Requirement wording.** SOCIAL-05 in REQUIREMENTS.md says the share covers member-owned `post` uploads; this plan widens it to every member purpose by the user's recorded choice. REQUIREMENTS.md is not edited here (the orchestrator owns it); flagged in the hand-off for a wording update at close.
7. **Reversibility.** All three tasks are reversible: service-local behavior behind unchanged signatures, no schema, no contract.
</planning_decisions>
