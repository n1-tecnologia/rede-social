<planning_decisions>
1. **A kernel seam for the policy**, in the `setPermissionResolver` pattern: the media broker (kernel) cannot import the social settings schema (MOD-02), so it calls `memberMediaPolicyFor(ctx)` which the app registry fills with `effectiveKeys` plus a tolerant parse of the `social` settings. The seam returns null when `social` is not effective, and null means "no member allowance".
2. **The new refusal codes get their own set** (`FEED_MEMBER_ISSUES`, `details.member`), not new members of `FEED_MEDIA_ISSUES` or `FEED_COMMUNITY_ISSUES`: the web switches on those two exhaustively ("a new refusal code cannot compile until it has copy"), and plan 09 is where the composer gets its copy.
3. **The sub-budget counts only member-owned `post`-purpose uploads** (avatars excluded), against half of each ceiling, in the same admin transaction as the existing sum. It is the one addition beyond the written decisions, justified by RESEARCH Pitfall 9, kept to one constant and reported to the user.
4. **Settings are written by merge**, never by replacing the blob (`settings = settings || patch`): a stray key from another phase survives, and the legacy feed blob is untouched.
5. **The 42501 mapping is for members only.** A member whose insert is refused by the policy during the cache window gets 403; any other 42501 stays an internal error so a real policy bug is never masked as a permission refusal.
6. **Bytes of a member video are judged at start** (the declared size), as the admin's are today; the provider reports no byte count at ready, so duration is the authoritative cost control.
</planning_decisions>
