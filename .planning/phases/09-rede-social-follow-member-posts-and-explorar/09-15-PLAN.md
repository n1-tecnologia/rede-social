<planning_decisions>
1. **The recipient test is the kernel seam, not a second mechanism.** `resolvePostCommented` needs no recipient test (the recipient IS the author, read from the row). The reply recipient is the ROOT comment's author, who may be a former follower, and the worker lane cannot read follows through RLS or through the claims-based seam. The explicit-user definer of plan 09-14, wrapped by `socialCanReadFor` in the kernel seam file, mirrors `lockedCommunityIdsFor` (08.2) exactly; the feed names no social table.
2. **Fail closed on the visibility value.** Anything other than `tenant` is treated as a followers post, so an unexpected value or a fixture without the column is refused rather than notified.
3. **The cheap tests run first.** `social_on` and `author_live` come from the same statement as the reply, so a refused reply on those grounds costs no extra statement; only a followers reply that passes them pays the one definer probe. Organization-post replies keep exactly today's statement count.
4. **Rows written before a revocation are not retracted** (Assumption A5 of 08.2, unchanged); this plan only stops writing new ones.
5. **No schema change.** The SQL half shipped in plan 09-14; this plan checks that the function exists before it starts.
</planning_decisions>
