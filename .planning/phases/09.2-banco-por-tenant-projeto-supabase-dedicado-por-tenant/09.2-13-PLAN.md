<objective>
Turn 'create the project, load the schema, configure everything' into eight idempotent, resumable steps run by a durable job, so an operator who has an empty Supabase project needs to paste only its ref.

Each step can run again without damage, records its status and a reason code (never provider text) in the registry, and a failure stops the run where it happened; 'Tentar de novo' resumes at the first step that is not done. The tenant stays `preparing`, and accepts no login, until the eighth step passes. The steps use the provider seam of plan 12 and the migration runner of plan 02, so every one of them is testable here against the fake provider and the second local database; nothing in this plan touches a real Supabase project.

Tracer: a tenant is bootstrapped through all eight steps on the second local database and then served by an ordinary request. It exercises provider, runner, sealing, registry, queue, job, mirror and routing together, which is exactly what plans 03-12 built.

Heavy plan, justified: the engine, the eight step bodies, the job and the Auth template are one mechanism; the two migrations (a tenant-set expand and a central column) are small and gated in Task 3.

<planning_decisions>
1. **Claim by row, not by lock.** A step is claimed with a conditional `update … returning` (pooler-safe: no session-level advisory lock survives a transaction pooler) and a 15-minute lease on `running`.
2. **Steps are functions of (project, tenant, provider, connections).** They read what they need from the registry and write only their own results; the `invite` and `tenant` steps operate on the tenant(s) bound to the project (exactly one in production; plan 14 reuses them for a second tenant joining a ready non-production project).
3. **Where the first admin's e-mail lives until step 8.** The central migration adds `platform.tenant_projects.pending_invite jsonb` (`{ email, createdBy }`), written by the create call (plan 14), read and cleared by step 8; it is personal data in the control plane for the length of the bootstrap only.
4. **Hooks stay disabled** (Open Question 2, assumption A-13-1): URIs are set and `enabled: false`, as production does today; enabling is a deliberate per-tenant step documented in DEPLOY.md.
5. **No DDL through the Management API.** All SQL runs over the stored DDL connection (session pooler, `postgres`), per RESEARCH Pitfall 2.
6. **`API_PUBLIC_URL`.** The hook URI needs the API's public origin: `assertProductionEnv` (plan 12) also requires `API_PUBLIC_URL` when `PROJECT_PROVIDER=supabase` (added here to `env.ts`).
</planning_decisions>
