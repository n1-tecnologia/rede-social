<objective>
The last two member doors: take the event into a calendar, and find the next one (and its check-in) on Início.

**As a** member, **I want to** add an event to my phone's calendar and see the next event on the home screen, which becomes the check-in shortcut when the doors open, **so that** I don't forget it and I can check in from where I already am. This story comes from the ROADMAP Phase 6 goal clauses "add events to their calendar" and "members see what is coming".

Purpose: EVENT-06 is a pure web-tier serializer. Its only subtle part is that the online LOCATION is our own `/entrar` (D-211), which keeps calendars gated and counting. D-202's card is the home-slot composition (D-42) plus the UI-D-203 boundary refresh, so a member standing at the venue sees 'Fazer check-in' appear on the screen they already have open.
Output: `events-calendar.ts` (tested byte by byte), the `.ics` route, the calendar pair, `GET /v1/events/next`, `NextEventCard`, `eventsHome` at order 7, and the refresh island.

Task 1 (calendar) is not design-gated. Task 2 codes the Início card (surface 5 of the six) and halts on its precondition until 06-02's review is recorded.
</objective>
