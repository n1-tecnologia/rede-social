<objective>
EVENT-04 online: `Entrar` opens the meeting and counts the member present, gated at click time, and it is safe against prefetchers and redirect abuse.

**As a** member joining an online event (from the app or from my calendar), **I want to** tap `Entrar` and land in the meeting, **so that** I get in without hunting for a link and the organiser sees I attended. This story comes from the ROADMAP Phase 6 goal clauses "check in on the day" and "the admin sees who showed up".

Purpose: `/eventos/{id}/entrar` is the one URL shape that leaves the app and lives in calendars (D-211), so its behaviour is effectively permanent (D-56). This plan builds it once with the gate in the database, the side effect behind a POST at the API, and a production-build proof that nothing but a human tap fires it.
Output: `app.events_enter`, `POST /v1/events/{id}/enter`, the `GET /eventos/{id}/entrar` route handler, the `aviso` refusal page, the online rows of the action zone, the widened continue path, and the prefetch spec.

Task 1 is not gated. Task 2 codes the online variant (surface 4 of the six) and halts on its precondition until 06-02's review is recorded.
</objective>

<planning_decisions>
1. **The function returns `(outcome, meeting_url, attendance_status, starts_at)`.** `attendance_status` gives the service `walkIn` for the event payload, and `starts_at` completes it, so no second read is needed. The `meeting_url` column is NULL for every outcome except `forward`, `recorded` and `already`.
2. **Outside the window with Vou: forward, record nothing** (D-218's recommended default, recorded). Entering early works but counts nothing, and the outline `Entrar` before the window says exactly that (UI-D-209).
3. **Residual CSRF is accepted.** A cross-site top-level navigation to `/entrar` inside the window, carrying the Lax cookie, can check in a victim who is a member of that tenant. The impact is one spurious "present" on the victim's own row, visible to the admin, with no data exposed. Blocking it would need an interstitial, which breaks the one-tap calendar requirement (D-210/D-211). It is recorded as accepted risk T-06-39.
</planning_decisions>
