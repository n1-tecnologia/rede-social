<planning_decisions>
1. **H is read uncached from `tenant_domains`** (`verifiedTenantIdForHost(host)`: `verified_at is not null`), never from the 60 s host cache, so a stale positive or negative cache entry can neither brand nor refuse a mail. Auth mails are low volume.
2. **Row 4 is D-317 exactly:** only `recovery` is branded without membership or invite; invite and every other link type keep the D-315 refusal. The requester of a recovery never sees the mail, and the form's answer is constant, so the brand reveals nothing to them.
3. **Row 6 (H + non-link type) is neutral**, not "the other membership's brand": a code-only mail tied to B's host must not wear A's colours, and nothing about B may be asserted without a membership.
4. **The decision is a pure function** of pre-read facts, unit-tested row by row; `resolveMailTenant` only gathers the facts.
</planning_decisions>
