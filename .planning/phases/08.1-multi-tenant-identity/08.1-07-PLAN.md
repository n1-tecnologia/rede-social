<planning_decisions>
1. **Realtime host binding: not bound (CONTEXT discretion, decided with RESEARCH).** Realtime evaluates its policy against the Supabase JWT only; no hook can know the host at sign-in, and whether request headers reach the policy is undocumented (RESEARCH A4). A member of A and B who subscribes to B's topics from A's origin receives ids-only signals they are entitled to anyway. The required proofs are the closed cases: a tenant without membership, a blocked membership, an invited or removed one.
2. **One shared identity per describe, roles differ by tenant** (`member` in A, `admin_tenant` in B), so role-per-host and data isolation are proved by the same fixture.
3. **The release-1 gate runs the whole `pnpm verify`** (with `TURBO_CACHE=local:r`, project memory: the turbo cache fills the disk), not a subset: release 1 is what production will run first.
</planning_decisions>
