<objective>
The generic-host half of D-308. Production tenants always have their own domain, so on a tenant host the host picks the community. On localhost and Vercel Preview there is no tenant host, and an identity in two communities needs to choose. 08.1-01 already made the API answer `TENANT_CHOICE_REQUIRED` and honour `x-tenant-choice`; this plan gives the web the picker, the cookie and the header, and completes the generic-host sign-up join from 08.1-02.

**As a** developer or tester on a Preview or local URL who belongs to two communities, **I want to** choose which community to open after logging in, **so that** I can exercise every community from one address, while production tenant apps never show the choice. Derived from ROADMAP 08.1 success criterion 3 and CONTEXT D-308.

Output: `GET /v1/join/communities`, the picker page and action, the header forwarding, the shared cookie constant and the generic-host e2e.

**Commits.** No `Co-Authored-By` trailer. Stage only the files this plan lists.
</objective>
