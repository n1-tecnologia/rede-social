# Phase 10 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Scope.** With the toggleable module **"Rede social"** (`social`) on, members also publish stories and create their own communities, and a community accepts publications only from its owner. This document contracts only what is new or changes:

1. the **Explorar story row**: the member's own circle, one circle per followed author, the moderator's every-member row (UI-D-697 to UI-D-701);
2. the **member story publish screen**, its destination step and the visibility notice (UI-D-702 to UI-D-705);
3. the **create-community entry**, the limit-reached state and the member form (UI-D-706, UI-D-707);
4. the **community list** order, the **"Criada por"** label on the card and in the header (UI-D-708, UI-D-709);
5. the **creator-only publishing controls** and the member composer inside a community (UI-D-710, UI-D-711);
6. the **creator lifecycle**, highlights curation and the **moderator's** story and community menus (UI-D-712 to UI-D-714);
7. the **platform panel** community limit (UI-D-715);
8. the states tied to the **module switch** and to a **blocked creator** (UI-D-716, UI-D-717);
9. picker and lane consequences, loading and vocabulary (UI-D-718 to UI-D-720).

The post card, comment sheet, like, share, link card, video player, upload states, the community card geometry, the community form chrome, the story viewer chrome, the highlight manage screen and edit sheet, the `Seguir` button and the Explorar feed are **reused unchanged** from `04`, `05`, `05.1`, `05.2`, `05.3` and `09-UI-SPEC.md`. Nothing in them is re-specified here.

**Design authority.** `reference/frontend-design/` was checked on 2026-11-10. It has **no stories system** (`app/(app)/feed/page.tsx` states "nem stories ... em lugar nenhum"; only `STORY_DURATION_MS` and `STORY_EXPIRY_HOURS` exist in `lib/constants.ts`) and **no member-created community form**. Its community page (`app/(app)/community/[communityId]/page.tsx`) draws one related element: a `por {OWNER.name}` line (12px tertiary, beside an 80px overlapping avatar and a verified badge), which D-67 removed in Phase 5. Therefore:

- **[proto delta]** (one surface): the **"Criada por {name}" line** of the community header (UI-D-709) is derived from that prototype line, minus the overlapping avatar and the badge.
- **[designed]** (every other surface): all stories surfaces, the own circle with "+", the member publish screen, the destination and visibility notice, the create entry and limit notice, the owner-only control matrix, the moderator menus, the platform field. They are drawn in the prototype's language from primitives that ship in `@rede-social/ui`, `@rede-social/core/ui` and the module UI packages.
- **[reused]**: `CommunityForm` (sketch 003, approved), the highlight screens (sketch 004), the viewer (003/004), Explorar (sketch 012). Only their deltas are drawn here.

The design language, spacing scale, typography scale and colour model are **inherited unchanged** from the approved `02` to `12.3-UI-SPEC.md`. This document restates them for the checker and records only the Phase 10 deltas.

**Upstream.**
- `10-CONTEXT.md` decisions are cited as **D-701..D-718** (locked, never renumbered or reversed). Carried-forward decisions keep their own ids (D-466..D-484, D-89, D-67, T-05-03, T-05-25).
- The UI decisions this phase opens are **UI-D-697..UI-D-720**. They continue after Phase 12.3's **UI-D-696**, checked 2026-11-10 with `grep -rhoE "UI-D-[0-9]+" .planning | sort -t- -k3 -n -u | tail -1` -> `UI-D-696`.
- Product decisions that cannot be drawn exactly as written, or that left a visible gap, are **not changed**: they are recorded in **§ Open questions for the owner** at the end, each with the default this contract draws meanwhile.

**Sketch gate (D-33, D-365).** The **[designed]** surfaces go through a reviewed sketch **before UI tasks are written** (full list at the end, § Sketch gate). This session wrote no sketch and edited no sketch README or approval.

**Non-interactive session.** No question was asked. Every default adopted is repeated in **§ Pending User Confirmation** and **§ Open questions for the owner**, so each can be confirmed or overturned with one line.

## Motion & Accessibility

- **No new motion.** The row, the sheets, the dialogs and the viewer use the shipped transitions; the badge and the byline are static. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` nothing changes except what 05.2 already collapses.
- Every icon-only control is 44x44 with a required `label`; the retry circle is a real control with its own accessible name. The seen state of an author circle is carried in its accessible name, not colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
- The row is `role="list"` with `role="listitem"` children and a region label; it is keyboard-scrollable and its md-up scroll buttons are 44x44. The own circle is the first tab stop of the row.
- The limit notice is static text (no live region): it is on the page when the page loads. The form's limit and `403` messages use the shipped `role="alert"` card. The destination refusals use the composer's top `role="alert"` card.
- Switch-on confirm: focus lands on the dialog's cancel button (the safe action), returns to the switch on close; the switch's state is the confirmed one only.
- After a moderator's "Excluir story" succeeds, focus moves to the viewer's next story (or the close control on the last), as the shipped host does.
