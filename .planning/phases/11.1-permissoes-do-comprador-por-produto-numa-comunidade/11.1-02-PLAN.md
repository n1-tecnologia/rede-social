<assumption_delta_decision>
Noun that is now primary: the **link row** (product x community), which already was the unit of ACCESS (any active linked product unlocks) and now is also the unit of GRANT (the five flags). Decision: `promote`. The flags go on the link row, not on the product (D-891) and not in a second table alongside it; access stays "a link exists for an active entitlement" and the grant is the same join with `bool_or`. No old singular representation is left beside the new one. The invariant test the checkpoint suggests is in Task 2: for every community and every action, the scope map, `app.community_action_allowed` and `app.community_action_denied_ids` agree with the union computed straight from the links and active entitlements.
</assumption_delta_decision>
