# Phase 11.1 Plan 16: The buyer's story flow Summary

**A buyer saves and removes only their own stories in highlights the administrator opened: the destination sheet draws a group per community with its open highlights and no "Novo destaque" (one optional `canCreate` flag per group, the rule per group never per member), every "Destacar" door and the community's "+" vanish when nothing is open, the D-716 warning is untouched, and a refused write answers with the shipped neutral sentences and a re-read that removes the closed row.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 12 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-25T15:06:00Z (approximate)
- **Completed:** 2026-11-25T15:18:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 16 (1 created, 15 modified; 3 production files in the module and pages beyond tests)

## Accomplishments

- `HighlightSheetPlace.canCreate` (optional): the single-select sheet draws a group's "Novo destaque" row only when it is not `false`. `/stories/publicar` builds the member's groups with `offeredPlaces`: a group the member cannot create in (the API's own `viewer.canCurate` of that community is false) carries `canCreate: false`, a community with nothing open is dropped, and `?comunidade=` is resolved against the groups that are actually offered. The administrator's groups are returned exactly as built.
- Every door of a member reads one finished fact: `memberCanHighlight` (an owned active community, or the catalogue lists a highlight). `/stories/meus` and the Explorar row's viewer menu use it, so with no open highlight "Destacar" is absent and no sheet is ever drawn empty. The community page already gated the "+" on `viewer.actions.story` and returned the shipped null band, so it needed tests only.
- The two checklist hosts re-read the destinations after a member's refused write (other than a full highlight): the switch reverts with the shipped toast, the closed row leaves the list, and with nothing left the sheet closes and "Destacar" leaves the menu. A read that finds no group and no community of their own never opens the sheet (no "Criar destaque" for a buyer).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] "Destacar" in the story viewer's own-story menu**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** the viewer's "Destacar" for a member comes from `ExplorarBody` (`memberMenu.canHighlight`), which read only `ownsActiveCommunity`; a buyer with an open highlight would never be offered it, and a stale door would persist. `ExplorarBody.tsx` is not in the plan's file list.
- **Fix:** `ExplorarBody` now reads `memberCanHighlight`, the same fact as `/stories/meus` (the catalogue is read only when ownership said no).
- **Files modified:** `apps/web/app/(app)/inicio/ExplorarBody.tsx`, `apps/web/lib/stories.ts`
- **Committed in:** `1d7bd8ae`

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] A buyer must never reach an empty sheet or "Criar destaque"**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** both checklist hosts route a member's empty sheet to `createHref ?? '/comunidades'` with the text "Crie um destaque ..."; for a buyer (no owned community, so no `createHref`) a highlight closed between the page render and the tap would draw a create affordance (T-11.1-58).
- **Fix:** a member read with no group and no `createHref` does not open the sheet (shipped generic toast) and removes "Destacar" for the visit; the same check runs after a refused write.
- **Files modified:** `StoryHistoryList.tsx`, `StoryViewerHost.tsx`
- **Committed in:** `3327d351`

**3. [Plan wording] Shipped test M9 of the viewer fixture changed**
- The shipped case "the author's Destacar opens the highlight sheet" returned no group and no `createHref`, a shape no owner of an active community can produce; it now carries the owner's `createHref`. Behaviour of the case (the sheet opens for that story) is unchanged.

**4. [Plan wording] Test file not in the plan's list**
- `apps/web/app/(app)/stories/meus/page.test.tsx` was created (no test of that page existed) to pin that `member.canHighlight` comes from `memberCanHighlight` and that the administrator's list never reads it. The plan's case (f) (`social` off redirects to `/inicio`) is the shipped rule of `/stories/publicar` (P6, already tested); `/stories/meus` has no social check today and none was added.

**5. [Plan wording] `story-view.ts` untouched**
