# Phase 11.1 Plan 11: the server-half security sweep Summary

**A refund through `app.store_gateway_revoke` ends every buyer permission at the next request while everything the buyer wrote stays readable, a revoke racing a write is a bare 403 on two connections, a buyer's post tells nobody through the worker, and no function, gate or route answers across tenants or leaks a product or a flag, with 19 integration cases and 83 pgTAP assertions that found no defect.**

## Accomplishments

- The refund path end to end through the real definer: purchase and revoke are called as `service_role` exactly as `applyStore` does. After the refund the scope map is empty at the next statement, like and comment answer the locked page (the community has no other product), post and story save answer the bare 403, and every post, comment, like and highlighted story the buyer wrote is still returned to a second buyer and still `deleted_at is null`. A re-purchase restores the grant and a snapshot of every row the buyer wrote is byte-identical before and after.
- The admin revoke route agrees with the gateway; the refund, the chargeback and the cancel share one call site (pinned on `packages/core/server/payments/apply.ts`); a second product held keeps the community open and the refund of the first is the bare 403 in every lane.
- The administrator removes the buyer's post and comment through the shipped moderation (one `post_removed` and one `comment_removed` log row, anchored by subject id) and the highlight item through the shipped takedown, the story surviving.
- The race on two raw connections: the buyer's lane reads the grant (true) inside a READ COMMITTED transaction, a second connection commits the revoke, and the NEXT like insert is refused 42501; a revoke after the insert leaves the row and never waited on a lock. At the API level a revoke landing between the guard and the write is the bare 403 on like, comment heart and comment, and a staff 42501 stays a 500.
- The notification negative: the buyer's post enqueues its fan-out job, the worker's own handler is run on it, and no `notifications` row, no push job and no `payload`/`dedupe_key` mentioning the post exists; the staff post of the same community notifies the holders and not the locked reader.
- The cross-tenant matrix, both directions (A against B and B against A): like, comment, comment heart, post, item add and remove, the open switch, open counts, community detail and list, post detail and comments, the catalogue, product reads and `communityLinks` writes each answer the bytes of an unknown id, and tenant A's own call passes beside each. Host mismatch is `TENANT_HOST_MISMATCH` in both directions. One shared user, two same-named and same-slug communities, never answers across the claims. A throwaway tenant that owns nothing answers empty or false and never errors.
- pgTAP sections 20 to 22 (83 assertions): the three functions and the five gates against tenant B's rows and under tenant B's claim, the empty and same-name cases at the database level, and a catalog meta check that fails when a function that reads the flags or a policy that calls one is not named in the matrix.
- The serialised no-leak sweep: 13 community, feed, comment, highlight and story reads and 4 product surfaces, as a buyer and as a locked reader, carry no foreign product id or name (NFC and NFD), no entitlement id, no flag key in snake or camel case, no `buyer_scope`, no `openHighlight`, no `openToBuyers`; the curator and manager reads of the same objects do carry `openToBuyers` and `communityPermissions`, so the sweep is not blind.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Plan wording] The "no post.published fan-out job" assertion is not what the system does**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (case 9); plan 08 had already recorded it
- **Issue:** the event bus enqueues a `notifications.fanout` job for every `post.published`, a buyer's included. The guarantee is the other half: the worker's resolver answers `[]` for `visibility <> 'tenant'`.
- **Fix:** the case asserts the job exists, runs the real handler on it, and asserts zero notification rows, zero push jobs and zero mentions of the post, beside the staff post that notifies. The Realtime half is marked human judgment (D10).
- **Committed in:** `58ae5a66`

**2. [Plan wording] The locked refusal, not the bare 403, answers like and comment after the last product is refunded**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** the plan says "the bare 403 (or the locked refusal when no other product remains)"; in the code a post and a story save are the bare 403 either way and only like and comment carry the locked refusal.
- **Fix:** the cases assert exactly that split, and a second product held gives the bare 403 in every lane.
- **Committed in:** `80874830`

**3. [Plan wording] A comment heart racing the LAST product is 403 or 404**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (case 8)
- **Issue:** the revoke of the last product locks the community at the write statement and the comment is already hidden by the locked read, so the heart answers 404 instead of the 42501 mapping.
- **Fix:** with a second product held (community stays open) the race is the bare 403 on like, comment heart and comment; with the last product the answer is asserted as 403 or 404, never 500, no row written. Not a defect: nothing is written and nothing leaks.
- **Committed in:** `58ae5a66`

**4. [Rule 3 - own test bugs, fixed before commit] four harness mistakes**
- a refund of the order was missing from the race revoke (a live order blocks a re-purchase), the new empty tenant needed the `feed` module, the staff catalogue read replaces a member read in the empty tenant, and a failed statement inside `postgres.js begin` rejects the whole transaction (the race catches it outside). No production code was involved.
