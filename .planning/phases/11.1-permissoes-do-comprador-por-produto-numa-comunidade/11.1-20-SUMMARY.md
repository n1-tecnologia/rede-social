# Phase 11.1 Plan 20: Refused word scoped to the member lane Summary

**`createPostAction` reads the lane lazily from the viewer's composed permissions, so only a buyer's pinned member composer gets the neutral word `refused`; a staff 403 is generic and logged again, and BPERM-28..30 read Pending until Igor's ledger rows pass.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 6 min of agent time (start not clocked to the second; run ended 2026-11-25T19:15Z)
- **Tasks:** 3 (1 tracer, 2 auto)
- **Files modified:** 5 (4 web, 1 planning)

## Accomplishments

- WR-02 closed: the word `refused` was produced for every 403 on a create, so an administrator's or staff member's unexpected refusal lost its only log line. It is now produced only when the body names a community AND `isMemberComposer(bootstrap.permissions)` holds, the pair `/criar` uses to pin the composer. Anything else is generic with `console.error('feed.post.write_failed', ...)` and no community revalidation.
- `attemptPostWrite`'s `mapRefused` accepts a boolean or a function returning a boolean or a promise, awaited only on a bare 403 after the named-issue and bootstrap-redirect checks. A rejecting predicate counts as false. The edit path passes nothing and is untouched.
- Traceability fixed: BPERM-28 (ledger P1-P4, P10-P16, P20-P22, P31), BPERM-29 (P5) and BPERM-30 (P6-P9) read unchecked and Pending in both places, each pointing at `11.1-MANUAL-PROOFS.md`. Row numbers were confirmed against the ledger's Requirement column.
