# Phase 11.1 manual proofs (P1 to P34)

This file is the ledger of the proofs only the owner can run: the administrator's product form and the "Aberto a
compradores" switch on a real phone at 320 and 390 px, a buyer's post from the community page and a story saved into
an open highlight on the tenant's own domain, a refund through the real gateway while a buyer's screen is open, the
VoiceOver and TalkBack walks of the permission group, the switch and the row after a control vanishes, the held-out
visual rows, the two Playwright runs, the sketch 026 approval kind and its defaults, the planner's assumptions and
product calls, every hosted release step and the first-day checks, and the full `pnpm verify` (it resets and seeds
the local database, so it is his). Everything an agent could build is built and proved on the local stack (plans 01
to 17: pgTAP, API integration, unit and component tests, and two Playwright specs that are written and listed but
were never run); what is left here cannot be faked, so nothing in this file is ever recorded as done by an agent.

**The rule.** Every row ships `pending`. A row becomes `passed` only when Igor pastes the evidence of its steps and the
date, in the row, himself. An agent never edits a Status line and never writes evidence it did not see. A phase whose
ledger has a `pending` row closes `human_needed`, never green. (Project memory: a blanket UAT pass is checked against
`docs/DEPLOY.md` before it is recorded, and a phone cannot reach `*.localhost`, so a row that needs a phone needs a
homolog or production host.)

**The evidence rule.** Paste shapes, counts, statuses, HTTP codes, exit codes and the words the screens show. **Never**
a key, a password, a token, a connection string, a webhook token, an e-mail address of a member, a project ref, a signed
link or a recording. Replace a ref by `<ref>` and a host by `<host>` in what you paste; name a tenant "tenant 1",
"tenant 2".

**The procedures** for the release rows are in `docs/DEPLOY.md`, "Phase 11.1 release (buyer permissions per product)".
The release order is: migrations on every tenant database, then the API and the WORKER together, then the WEB (the
inverse of Phase 11: every new response key is optional and emitted only at a non-default value, so the previous web
never meets one).

**The design approval and what it is.** Sketch `026` (the design gate) carries `approved: true`,
`approved_by: Igor (recorded by Claude on Igor's instruction)`, `approved_at: 2026-11-25` and
`approval_kind: provisional`. Igor wrote "aprovado" in chat, the README still read `approved: false`, and on his
answer "altere voce e continue" the orchestrator wrote the approval keys; no agent edited them since. It stays
provisional until you walk the built screens (rows P1 to P4, P10 to P12, P20 to P22) and a designer reviews it (D-33,
row P16). The row for sketch 026 in `.planning/sketches/MANIFEST.md` still reads `pending`; it was not touched and
needs your word to update (row P15).

## P1 to P4 - The first walk on a real phone (BPERM-28)

- **Proves:** that an administrator can set a product's permissions and open a highlight, and that a buyer can then post
  and save a story, on a real phone at both adjacent widths. The four rows are the same walk twice: P1 and P2 are the
  administrator's half (steps 1 and 2) at 320 and 390 px, P3 and P4 the buyer's half (steps 3 and 4).
- **Needs:** a homolog or production tenant on its own domain with `store`, `social` and `feed` on, an administrator,
  a buyer account that holds one linked product, a community whose product list starts with NO linked community (the
  empty start), a highlight on that community.
- **Steps, in this order (the order is part of the proof):**
  1. The administrator opens the product form on the phone, links the community (the empty start: no link existed),
     ticks "Postar na comunidade" and "Postar story", saves, reopens the product and checks the ticks held.
  2. The administrator opens the community's highlight, turns "Aberto a compradores" on in the edit sheet, and sees
     the "Compradores" pill on the manage list.
  3. The buyer opens the community page: the post entry and the story entry appear; the buyer publishes a post from
     the community page, which shows only on the community page.
  4. The buyer saves one of their own stories into the open highlight; a member who can see the community reads it.
- **Evidence to paste:** the device and OS versions, the width, the words the screens showed at each step, whether the
  ticks held after the save, whether the post appeared only on the community page, whether the story appeared in the
  highlight. Never a link, a name or an e-mail.
- **Status of P1, P2, P3, P4:** pending (each is its own row in the table above)

## P5 - A refund through the real gateway with a buyer's screen open (BPERM-29)

- **Proves:** that a refund ends a buyer's permissions at the next refresh while the server refuses the action in the
  meantime. BPERM-29 is `unclassified` in the plan; the invariants pinned instead are the five steps below and only
  you mark the row.
- **Needs:** a real tenant with a payment gateway, a buyer holding exactly one linked product with `like`, `comment`
  and `post` ticked, the buyer's phone, an administrator.
- **Steps:**
  - (a) The refund goes through the real gateway on a real tenant.
  - (b) The buyer's screen still shows its controls until the next refresh, and an action the buyer taps in the
    meantime is refused by the server with the neutral sentence (no product name, no reason).
  - (c) After the refresh the controls are gone and the locked page returns if no other product remains.
  - (d) What the buyer published stays where it is.
  - (e) The administrator can still reach it through the moderation that already exists; this release adds nothing
    that hides or removes it (D-887).
- **Evidence to paste:** the status code or word the refused action showed, whether the controls were gone after the
  refresh, whether the locked page returned, whether the post and the saved story stayed.
- **Status:** pending
