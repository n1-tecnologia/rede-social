# Phase 11.1 Plan 07: the communities half of the member-visible facts Summary

**`viewer.actions` on the community list and detail is now the union of the buyer's flags, composed at the single site `viewerOf` from a scalar InitPlan column over `app.community_buyer_scope()`, with `post` and `story` also obeying `social`, the open highlight and the archived state, and the list still one statement.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 9 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-25T12:05:00Z (approximate)
- **Completed:** 2026-11-25T12:14:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 3 (one service file, one integration file, one pgTAP file)

## Accomplishments

- `communityProjection` selects `(select app.community_buyer_scope()) -> c.id::text as buyer_scope`. The list and the detail stay one statement each; a total parser (`parseBuyerScope`) reads the value, and a missing or malformed one reads as no entry.
- `viewerOf` composes the five booleans for an organization community with a buyer entry: `like`, `comment`, `share` are the three flags; `post` is `scope.post` and the viewer holding `feed.post.create`, or the organization rule; `story` is `scope.story`, an open highlight of that community and the viewer holding `stories.story.publish`, or the story rule. Staff, a locked or unlinked community, store off and a member-created community have no entry and keep today's values. The edit, curate, publish and owner fields are untouched.
- `CommunityViewerFlags.canPostMember` added (from `feed.post.create`), so with `social` off a buyer's `post` and `story` come out false whatever is ticked, and turning `social` back on restores them with no data change.
- 11 integration cases (tracer, controls, matrix a to h, budget) and a 7-assertion pgTAP file pinning the InitPlan shape by plan text and by call count (one call for twelve rows).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Archived community offers neither post nor story**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (case g: "an archived community ... reads today's values")
- **Issue:** The plan asks an archived community to read today's values, but the scope function (plan 02) deliberately keeps archived communities in the map, so `viewerOf` would have drawn a post and a story door the insert gate and `resolveCommunityTarget` refuse.
- **Fix:** the buyer's `post` and `story` additionally require `row.status === 'active'`; like, comment and share follow the flags (nothing changes until the administrator edits the product, D-890). Proved by case g with the flags ticked.
- **Files modified:** `packages/modules/communities/server/service.ts`, `apps/api/tests/integration/buyer-permissions-viewer.test.ts`
- **Committed in:** `b690c13c`

**2. [Plan wording] `canPublishStory` already existed**
- The plan says `CommunityViewerFlags` gains `canPostMember` and `canPublishStory`; `canPublishStory` was already there (Phase 10 review WR-02), so only `canPostMember` is new. It is optional and fail-closed because `events.test.ts` and `order.test.ts` build the flag object by hand.
