## Accomplishments
- `toProductLinksBody` sends `communityIds` unless a selected link differs from what the product stores (new link: defaults), then `communityLinks` with all five booleans; both outputs parse through `productInputSchema` and `productPatchSchema`, and a stored non-default link ticked back to the defaults is not lost.
- Every screen decision of the web plans 12 to 17 is a tested pure function: `linksChanged`, `showSocialOffNote`, `showNoOpenHighlightNote`, `postActionsView`, `highlightOpenState`, `highlightOpenSectionVisible`, `openToBuyersToastKey`. None compares a role, a product or an entitlement (grep 0).
- 19 new catalog keys in `store.json`, `stories.json` and `feed.json`, verbatim from the UI-SPEC; the catalog test pins sentence, placeholder, order, adjacency and the neutrality of the two member-readable keys.
