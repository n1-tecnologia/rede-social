# Phase 11.1 Plan 05: buyer permissions contracts Summary

**Per-link buyer flags on the product body and manager read (`communityLinks`, `communityPermissions`, `community_links_conflict`), the optional `actions` fact on a post's community, the highlight `openToBuyers` switch with its `open_unavailable` issue and the open-counts schemas, all optional and strict so a previous client keeps working in both directions.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 8 min of wall clock (the start stamp was not captured; estimated from the first command)
- **Completed:** 2026-11-25T01:22:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 11

## Accomplishments

- Store: `productCommunityLinkSchema` (`.strict()`, five required booleans, `z.uuid('community_invalid')`), `communityLinks` on create and patch capped by `STORE_MAX_LINKS`, `community_links_conflict` for both-together and duplicate ids, the patch's "not empty" refine counting the new key, and `communityPermissions` on `productDetailSchema` with the manager-only, differs-from-defaults docblock. `productCommunitySchema` is untouched and a test proves no flag key is accepted on it or on the purchase answer.
- Feed: optional `actions {like, comment, share}` (strict) on `postCommunitySchema`; docblock now says it rides the post only when at least one is false.
- Stories: `openToBuyers` optional on the summary (curator read) and the patch (refine updated), `open_unavailable` appended last to `STORY_HIGHLIGHT_ISSUES` and pinned by a new issue-set test, `openHighlightCountsQuerySchema` (comma-separated, 1 to 50 uuids, deduplicated) and `openHighlightCountsSchema`.
- Communities: docblock of `communityActionsSchema` (the shape of `viewer.actions`) states the buyer union, staff all true and `post`/`story` false with `social` off; a test pins the five keys and strictness.
- Compatibility tests per module with a frozen previous-release fixture and a new-release fixture, plus an unknown-key refusal.
