# Phase 11.1 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Scope.** A store product says which communities its buyer sees (08.2) and now also what the buyer can do in each: like, comment, share, post in the community, post a story (D-880..D-892). This document contracts only what is new or changes on screen:

1. **The administrator's product form:** five checkboxes per linked community, their defaults, the `social`-off and no-open-highlight states, the helper copy, how the lock preview and the picker keep working, keyboard and screen-reader behaviour (UI-D-882..UI-D-885).
2. **The administrator's highlight screens:** the per-highlight switch "Aberto a compradores", its place, copy and states, and the staff-only mark in the manage list (UI-D-886, UI-D-887).
3. **The member side, where a control disappears** (D-888): the matrix per surface and per permission state, the post-card row, the comment sheet, the compose entries, the story "+" circle, the story destinations and the highlight picker, and the posts that show outside the community page (UI-D-888..UI-D-890).
4. **The buyer's story flow** (D-883..D-885): saving one's own story into an open highlight, the D-716 warning kept, the case with no open highlight, removing one's own story (UI-D-891).
5. **The buyer's post flow** (D-880..D-882): the composer and what the community page shows afterwards (UI-D-892).
6. **Live changes and refusals** (refund while a screen is open, the neutral 403), the state matrix, loading/empty/error, accessibility, strings and trust rules (UI-D-893..UI-D-898).

Everything else is **reused unchanged**: the product form's shell, picker and lock preview (08.2, 08.4), the highlight edit sheet and manage list (05.2), the member composer, story composer, checklist sheet and visibility notice (10), the post card, comment sheet, community page, locked sample (08.2) and Reels overlay.

**Design authority.** The design team's prototype (`reference/frontend-design/`) has **no per-product permission, no buyer role, no store product form and no highlight-management screen**: checked 2026-11-24 with `grep -rniE "checkbox|permiss|comprador|buyer|produto|aberto a" "reference/frontend-design/app/(app)/community" reference/frontend-design/lib/mock/community.ts reference/frontend-design/types/member.ts` (no match) and `ls reference/frontend-design/app/(app)` (no store, no settings for products). It does carry the post action row and the community's highlight circles, already ported (`PostActions`, `StoriesSurface`). Every new surface here is **[designed]** in the prototype's language (Manrope, light `#f5f7fb` ground, white cards, 12px radii, 44px touch targets, the tenant's single accent) from primitives that already ship. Nothing is **[proto]**.

**The design language, spacing scale, typography scale and colour model are inherited unchanged** from `02` to `12.5-UI-SPEC.md` and `11-UI-SPEC.md`. **Phase 11.1 adds no token, font, size, weight, glyph, library or dependency.**

## Motion & Accessibility

- **Nothing new animates.** A closing-up action row, a vanished control, a switch toggle and a pill appearing are instant (the shipped `Switch` transition only); sheets and dialogs keep their shipped springs under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Speech is polite, never assertive.** The toasts are the shipped polite ones; a control vanishing on a re-read announces nothing.
- **Focus.** After a community is removed, after a sheet closes and after a control vanishes, focus lands on the nearest remaining control, never on `body` (UI-D-885, UI-D-893).
- **Touch.** 44px everywhere; no exception is declared (the 40px exception of Phase 11 does not apply here).
- **Text safety.** Every string is a React text node; the product never shows a file name, URL, user id, product id or entitlement on a member screen.
