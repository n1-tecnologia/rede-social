# Phase 11.1 Plan 12: The product form permissions Summary

**Five native checkboxes per chosen community in the administrator's product form, saved in the same request as the links (communityLinks only when a flag differs from the defaults), with the always-on, social-off and no-open-highlight helpers, stored flags edited in place and a keyboard and screen-reader contract fixed by tests.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 25 min
- **Completed:** 2026-11-25T14:56:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 10 (4 created, 6 modified)

## Accomplishments

- `ProductCommunityPermissions` draws one fieldset per community with the legend "Quem comprar pode:" and an `sr-only` " em {community}" suffix, and five native checkbox rows (`min-h-11` labels, `accent-brand`, `min-w-0 break-words`) in the order Curtir, Comentar, Compartilhar, Postar na comunidade, Postar story. No card, no divider inside the group, no master tick-all.
- `ProductForm` holds the flags per community, builds the create and patch bodies with `toProductLinksBody` against `originalLinks`, and sends the patch links only when `linksChanged` says something changed: a price edit sends neither key, a default save still sends `communityIds` (older API compatible), a stored non-default link ticked back to the defaults is sent as `communityLinks`.
- Helper stack: the always-on line once while a community is chosen, one social-off line per form (boxes stay drawn and keep their values), a plain-text no-open-highlight line under a community only with story ticked and a known count of 0; the two lines are `aria-describedby` of the right boxes.
- The lock preview is untouched: a flags-only edit saves with no dialog, an added community still asks the shipped preview for that community only.
- `lib/stories-open-counts.ts` reads the staff-only route in chunks of 50 and answers a record or `null`; the two pages pass `socialOn`, the stored `communityPermissions` and the counts.
- After a community is removed focus lands on the next community's remove button, else the previous one, else "Escolher comunidades".
