# Phase 11.1: Permissões do comprador por produto numa comunidade - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-24
**Status:** Ready for planning (UI-SPEC and a new sketch are needed before the screen plans; the database, contract and API plans can run first, as in Phase 11)

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Today a store product (08.2) says only **which communities** its buyer can see: `store_product_communities` links a product to organization communities, and an active entitlement of any linked product unlocks the community. This phase makes the product also say **what the buyer can do in each community it unlocks**, five actions: like, comment, share, post in the community, post a story. The permissions live on the product-to-community link, add up when two products unlock the same community, end with the entitlement (refund, chargeback, revoke, cancel), and are edited only in the product form (D-363).

Existing links keep today's behavior (like, comment and share on; post and story off). A community with no linked product, the staff roles and the locked-community sample (D-356) do not change.

Out of scope here (new capabilities, see Deferred): a showcase that tells the buyer what a product unlocks, any server-issued share link, automatic hiding of a refunded buyer's content, permissions for member-created communities, a "story belongs to a community" concept.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Postar na comunidade (post in the community)
- **D-880:** The "post" permission exists only for **organization communities**. Member-created communities cannot be linked to products (D-713: the creator's form has no products field), so the permission never applies to them and the creator-only rule of D-706 is untouched there. This fills the third path that Phase 10 left open in the owner rule for an organization community with a member author (`resolveCommunityTarget` branch 3, `communityActionAllowed`). — **Reversibility:** costly — the rule lands in the post write seam, in RLS and in the permission composition, like D-706.
- **D-881:** A buyer's post is the **same post a member publishes** (D-709). It is stamped `visibility = 'community'`, never `'tenant'`: it appears **only on the community's page**, for whoever can read the community, and **notifies nobody** (D-702; community posts notify nobody). It does not go to Início or Explorar.
- **D-882:** "Post" **requires the `social` module effective** for the tenant. The route stays behind `feed.post.create` (members hold it only with `social` effective, D-707); no second publish path is created. With `social` off the buyer still reads the community and can still like and comment if the product allows (D-718: only writes that exist because of `social` stop). The database insert gate must carry its own `social_enabled()`, active-community, not-deleted and tenant terms: the reserved fourth clause in `feed_posts_social_insert_gate` has none of them today.

### Postar story (post a story)
- **D-883:** The "story" permission means the buyer can **save one of their own stories into a highlight of that community**. A story still has no community column; no "story of the community" concept is added. Saving it is the act of publishing it in the community: it is readable by everyone who can see the community, including after the 24 hours (D-716), and the publish and highlight screens keep the warning ("Todos os membros verão este story na comunidade").
- **D-884:** The buyer can use **only highlights the administrator marked as open to buyers**: a new per-highlight switch on highlights of organization communities (the admin sets it on the highlight screens of 05.2/05.1). Highlights that are not open stay administrator-only. A buyer cannot create a highlight and cannot edit or reorder one (D-713 is unchanged for creators). — **Reversibility:** costly — a new column on the highlight, only the item insert and delete gates (`ITEM_OWNER`, with a new buyer alternative) widen for it, because a buyer cannot create, edit or reorder a highlight (`HIGHLIGHT_OWNER` and `ITEM_STORY_AUTHOR` stay as they are; corrected after research on 2026-11-24), and the audience gate (`stories_audience_gate` branch 3) already makes saved stories readable to the community.
- **D-885:** A buyer adds and removes **only their own stories** in an open highlight. An administrator can remove any item from any highlight, as today. Which of the buyer's stories can be saved is the buyer's own (member story rules of Phase 10: member stories exist with `social` on).

### Duas compras e reembolso (two purchases and refunds)
- **D-886:** Permissions **add up**. A member's permissions in a community are the union of the five flags of every linked product that has an **active entitlement** for that member. This is how access already works (any active linked product unlocks; the gate is an OR in `app.community_locked_ids()`), now carrying the flags.
- **D-887:** Refund, chargeback, revoke or cancel ends the permission **at once, because the flags are read from active entitlements** (the gateway path already sets the entitlement to `revoked`). What the buyer already published (posts and stories saved in highlights) **stays**. Nothing is hidden automatically; the administrator removes content through the moderation that exists (D-711 log for admin actions on member content). If the buyer loses every product of the community, the locked-community behavior of 08.2 applies as today.

### O que o membro vê (what the member sees)
- **D-888:** A control the product does not allow **disappears**: no heart, no comment field, no post button, no "+" for a story, no disabled state, no notice. The server **refuses the same action regardless** of the screen (HTTP 403; the error code and body must not leak which product or which flag, the planner decides the code). Staff roles always have every action.
- **D-889:** "Share" is **only hidden**. It is a screen control with no server gate; a person who copies the link cannot read the community without access anyway. No server-issued share link is built. — **Reversibility:** reversible — adding a controlled share link later is additive.
- **D-890:** In the product form, linking a community shows **five checkboxes for that community**. A new link starts as today's products: like, comment and share ticked, post and story unticked. The migration fills every existing link with those values, so nothing changes for anyone until the administrator edits the product. Links are written only in the product form (D-363).
- **D-891:** The flags are stored **per link (product × community)**, not per product, so one product can allow different things in different communities. — **Reversibility:** costly — the flags are read by the like, comment, post and highlight gates, the viewer-actions contract and the product form; moving them later touches all of those.

### Unchanged (carried forward, not re-asked)
- **D-892:** A community with **no linked product** is open and behaves as today. Staff (`admin_tenant`, `support_tenant`) always hold every action. A locked community (member without an active entitlement) stays read-only sample access (D-356: the newest post, all-or-nothing, 403 `community_locked`); the permissions only split what an unlocked buyer may do.

### Taken while researching (2026-11-24, conservative defaults chosen by Claude; Igor overturns any with one line)
- **D-893:** The free sample of a locked community (D-356, `app.feed_sample_post_ids`) shows only a post that is visible to the whole tenant (`visibility = 'tenant'`). A buyer's post (`visibility = 'community'`, with the buyer's name and photo) is never shown to a member who has not bought. Without this the newest post could be a buyer's. — **Reversibility:** reversible — one `create or replace` of the sample function.
- **D-894:** A member who is locked out of a community cannot read a story that a buyer saved in one of its highlights, even if they know the story id: the audience gate (`stories_audience_gate` branch 3) gets the locked-community term. Locked stays locked (D-892).
- **D-895:** A buyer who no longer holds "story" (refund, or the administrator unticked it) cannot take their story out of an open highlight; removal exists only while holding the permission (D-885, UI-D-891). They can still delete the story itself. The administrator removes any item.
- **D-896:** A member post limiter applies to the buyer branch only (a post into an organization community): 30 attempts per hour, every attempt counted, before any read, the same figure as the story limiter. No limiter exists for feed posts today.

### Claude's Discretion
- Schema shape for the flags (columns on `store_product_communities` is the expected shape; there are no per-link columns today) and the migration/backfill details.
- The API contract for the per-link flags in the product body: today the body carries `communityIds: uuid[]` (`packages/modules/store/contracts/index.ts:385,407,439`); keep it compatible or replace it, as the planner finds safest. `replaceLinks` (`store/server/service.ts:1562`) uses `on conflict do nothing`, so a changed flag on an existing link needs an update path.
- The SQL function that answers "which actions does this member hold in this community" and how it replaces the constant-false stub `app.community_action_allowed` (`20261111143858_member_communities_functions.sql:69`); `230-member-communities.sql` §9 and `member-communities.test.ts:354` pin that stub and must be rewritten.
- How like/comment on posts that show outside the community page (Início, Explorar, Reels) get a per-post flag, since posts carry only `community {id,name,slug}` today; the server refusal is the guarantee, the hidden control is the screen's part.
- Where the "open to buyers" switch sits on the highlight screens, and its copy.
- The refusal error code and body, rate-limit reuse, test layout, plan order.

</decisions>

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- The access OR in `app.community_locked_ids()` already ranges over a member's active entitlements and their linked products: the union of flags is the same join with the flags aggregated.
- Every purchase path (simulated purchase, admin grant/revoke, gateway) writes `store_entitlements`; reading the flags from active entitlements revokes them with no new code in the refund path.
- `viewerOf` is the one site that builds `viewer.actions`; the web already reads it for `canPost` and the story circle.
- Member posts and member stories (Phase 10) and highlights (05.2) already exist; the buyer's post and story reuse them.

### Established Patterns
- Ownership and permission are tested in the statement or in RLS, never by comparing roles in the screen (D-706/D-707).
- Hidden control plus server refusal: a screen never is the guard.
- Migrations are expand-only; the product-to-community links are written only by the product form (D-363).

### Integration Points
- Store module (links, product form) -> communities module (`viewerOf`, `resolveCommunityTarget`) -> feed module (like/comment/post gates) -> stories module (highlight gates and the new "open" switch).
- The gates exist in RLS (`feed_likes_community_gate`, `feed_comments_community_gate`, `feed_posts_social_insert_gate`, the highlight policies) and in services; both layers must learn the flags.

</code_context>

- Igor's words on the screens: when the product does not allow an action, "o controle some". No gray state, no explanation.
- The open-highlight switch is the administrator's curation control: the administrator decides which circles accept stories from buyers.

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- A showcase or product page that tells the buyer what a product unlocks (which actions in which communities).
- A server-issued, controlled share link for communities (today "share" only hides a button).
- Automatic hiding of a refunded buyer's posts and stories (the administrator moderates by hand).
- Permissions for member-created communities (D-713 keeps products out of them).
- A "stories of the community" lane separate from highlights, and highlights created by buyers.
- Per-kind rules such as a limit of posts per buyer per day (reuse of the existing member rate limits is the planner's call).
