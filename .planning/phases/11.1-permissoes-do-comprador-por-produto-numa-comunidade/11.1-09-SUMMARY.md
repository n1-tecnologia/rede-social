# Phase 11.1 Plan 09: Stories service half of buyer permissions Summary

**A buyer holding the story permission saves, publishes into and removes only their own stories in highlights the administrator opened, through a new `contribute` place intent; the administrator opens and closes a highlight with one PATCH field that only curators read, the buyer's catalogue lists only open highlights, and the product form gets a staff-only open-counts read.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 14 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-25T12:57:00Z
- **Completed:** 2026-11-25T13:11:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 7 (service, routes, the new integration file, two stories unit scripts, the isolation inventory and its suite)

## Accomplishments

- `PlaceIntent` gained `'contribute'`: the place seam judges the curator first (an add behaves as `curate`, a removal as `takedown`), then the buyer rule: organization community, active, caller holds `stories.story.publish`, `social` on, the highlight open, and `app.community_action_allowed(community, 'story')`. Any other combination is the bare 403, the same bytes for a closed highlight, a missing flag and a missing grant.
- `addStoryToHighlight`, `removeStoryFromHighlight` and `publishStory` with a `highlightId` use it; `createHighlight`, `updateHighlight`, `deleteHighlight`, `reorderHighlights` and `newHighlight` do not, so a buyer is 403 on each. A member 42501 from the item write is mapped to the same bare 403.
- `updateHighlight` accepts `openToBuyers` through the existing `is distinct from` update; Inicio, member-community and archived-community highlights are refused `open_unavailable`.
- `toHighlight(row, curator)` emits `openToBuyers: true` only on curator reads; the story band, a member's list and detail, the community payloads and the buyer's catalogue never carry it.
- `listHighlightCatalog` adds the buyer branch; `GET /v1/stories/highlights/open-counts` (guard `stories.story.manage`) is declared and registered before `/{highlightId}` and passes the static-route guard.
- `memberStoryVisible` carries the locked-community term next to branch 3 of `stories_audience_gate`: a locked member gets 404 on a story a buyer saved, by id.

### Known limitation (accepted)

The chosen-cover pointer (`cover_story_id`) is cleared on a removal only when a curator removes the item. When a buyer removes the item that the administrator had chosen as the cover, the pointer stays (a buyer cannot write `story_highlights`); the read-time cover rule already ignores a pointer to a story that is not a member-visible item of the highlight, so nothing stale is shown. It could only resurrect if the same buyer re-added the same story to the same highlight.
