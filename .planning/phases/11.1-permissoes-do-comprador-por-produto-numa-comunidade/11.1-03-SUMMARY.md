# Phase 11.1 Plan 03: feed half of the buyer database gates Summary

**A buyer without `like` or `comment` is now refused by the database (restrictive like gate on posts and comment hearts, new restrictive comment insert gate), reading is unchanged for every flag set, and the free sample of a locked community can never be a buyer's post.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 8 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-25T11:47:57Z
- **Completed:** 2026-11-25T11:56:31Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 11 (three migrations, three drizzle snapshots, the journal, the feed schema, the kernel seam, 241 and the baseline)

## Accomplishments

- `feed_likes_community_gate` keeps its locked term and gains the denied term in both branches. The comment branch reaches the post through a LEFT join, so a story comment (no post) passes the policy and reaches its foreign key (23503) exactly as before: the 400 `story_comment_not_likeable` stays a 400.
- `feed_comments_community_insert_gate`: restrictive, insert only, covering root comments and replies; the select gate and every select policy are untouched (UI-D-890). A community with no linked product is never denied, staff get the empty set, a null role denies.
- `app.feed_sample_post_ids()` replaced with `create or replace`, same signature, attributes and grants, selecting only `visibility = 'tenant'` posts: a locked community whose newest post is a buyer's samples the organization's newest tenant post, and one with only buyer posts shows no sample.
- `241-buyer-permissions.sql` grew from 80 to 136 assertions (sections 9 to 13), every refusal beside a positive control, with a tenant B negative, a flag-flip pair, idempotency of refusal and an InitPlan pin.

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] deniedCommunityIds emitted a bind parameter in DDL**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (migration generation)

## Issues Encountered

- A post-time-shaped fixture: all rows of one pgTAP transaction share `now()`, so the buyer's newer post in the sample case carries an explicit `created_at` one minute ahead; without it ordering falls to a random uuid.
- Stored policy text is normalized by Postgres (`COALESCE(( SELECT ...::text) AS ...)`), so the static InitPlan idiom assertions use a regex, not a substring.
- Before the comment gate existed, a tenant B lane could insert a comment row naming tenant A's post (the inner post read is the new gate's cross-tenant refusal); with the gate it is refused 42501, pinned in section 10.
