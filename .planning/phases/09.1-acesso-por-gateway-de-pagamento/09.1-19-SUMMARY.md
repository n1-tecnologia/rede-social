## Findings from the browser

1. **Shared confirm dialog overflow (fixed, `915c03ad`).** A 120-character e-mail in the block dialog title ran 238 px past the left edge of a 320px screen: `overflow-wrap` does not shrink a flex item below its min-content width, so the title needs `max-w-full` to wrap inside the panel. Plan 14 had made the sheet title wrap (`break-all`) but the dialog title, the other half of UI-SPEC E06, was only unit-tested. The fix is three `max-w-full` classes in `packages/ui/src/overlays/ConfirmDialog.tsx` (title and both body paragraphs); no other dialog changes for text that has spaces; the 261 `packages/ui` tests pass; the e2e E06 case is its regression guard.
2. **Outline `sm` hitArea is 42 px, not 44 (not fixed, logged).** Measured in the browser on 'Liberar manualmente': the transparent `::after` is inset from the padding box, inside the 1px border, so a 36px outline button gets 34 + 8 = 42px (borderless variants get 44). The same primitive carries 'Liberar e-mail' and 'Adicionar produto de acesso'. It is a shared primitive with a pinned class test, so it is in `deferred-items.md` with the one-line fix, and the E07 assertion says 42 and why.
3. **F-6 (320px with the real font) is answered for Chromium.** The 320px walk asserts that the real Manrope face is loaded (`document.fonts`) and measures everything above with it: the webhook URL scroll and the 44px copy button, the product row (name truncated, pill and chevron in), the access row (e-mail truncated, pills and chevron in), the sheet and dialog titles wrapping, the Eventos row and its action under the text column below 360 px (F-4), and no horizontal scroll on any of the five screens. Nothing else was wrong at 320 px. This is Chromium with the iPhone preset, not a phone.
4. **F-2 followed.** The owned block is the shipped 'Comprado' / 'Você tem acesso a este produto.'; the plan's 'Você já tem este produto' does not exist in the app and was not pinned.
5. **Two older e2e cases were stale since plan 03/15 (fixed, `24b99c24`).** `login.spec.ts` and `signup.spec.ts` 'quick 261018-owq' (an unlisted e-mail is refused) assume a global allowlist; since Phase 09.1 an e-mail with no usable access is refused only on a tenant that requires a subscription, so on rede-demo (gate off) they landed on `/inicio` and `/verifique-seu-email`. Plan 15 edited their assertion text without running them. They now run under `withGateOn('rede-demo')`, which writes the gate flag into the tenant's `payments` row for their duration and restores the row.
