## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] The documented-example fixtures failed the guard the plan itself requires**
- **Found during:** Task 3 RED
- **Issue:** the plan says the guard must pass on the existing synthetic fixtures too, but they used `@example.com` e-mails and 13-digit phones, and the three instalment payloads and the unknown shape were not in the plan's file list.
- **Fix:** e-mails moved to `example.test`, phones zeroed, headers re-labelled `documented-example (synthetic)` with the reason each was not captured, on all eight remaining fixtures.
- **Files modified:** the eight uncaptured fixtures under `packages/core/tests/fixtures/payments/kiwify/`
- **Commit:** `1437a833`

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] ISO dates with their own zone were read as null**
- **Found during:** reconciliation against the six bodies
- **Issue:** the `Subscription` dates in the capture are ISO-8601 UTC (`...Z`); the old reader accepted only `YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm`, so an `access_until` written the same way would have silently read as null (no expiry).
- **Fix:** `vendorTime` places an explicit zone as written; no zone plus a fractional part, or an impossible offset, stays null. Small and evidence-driven; the optional read is still marked unconfirmed (no body carries `customer_access`).
- **Files modified:** `packages/core/server/payments/kiwify.ts`, `packages/core/tests/payments-kiwify.test.ts`
- **Commit:** `53b4395d` (test), `1437a833` (fix)

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug in the tests] Several existing cases assumed a one-off approval**
- **Issue:** the observed approval, refund and chargeback carry a `Subscription`, so they are recurring and keyed by the subscription id; cases that expected the order id as the reference, or that a refund and a chargeback share one reference, were wrong for real shapes.
- **Fix:** expectations read the ids from the fixtures; a one-off approval is derived by removing the `Subscription` (labelled as derived).
- **Commit:** `1437a833`
