- **09.1-03, found 2026-10-20 (out of scope):** `apps/api/tests/integration/stories.test.ts` cases 5-8 of "GET /v1/stories" fail with `SEEDED_ACTIVE = 3` against 2 live stories. A fresh `pnpm db:reset && pnpm db:seed` makes it green.

Not caused by the payments module: the payments routes alone build a valid document (checked with a scratch app mounting only `paymentsRoutes`, then removed). No test or client calls the route today. Fix belongs with those modules (`.openapi({ type: 'integer' })` on the three schemas, or replace `.catch` with a `.default` plus a refine).
