## Approval provenance (read this before trusting the gate)

The plan says the approval write is the user's edit and that an agent never writes its own gate approval. That is not literally what happened. Igor did not paste the lines by hand. He told the orchestrating agent, three times in writing across 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-21, to change the status, approve and continue, and the orchestrator wrote the five lines (`approved_by` says so in plain words). The approval is `approval_kind: provisional`, as the plan allows for a product-owner approval, but there is no evidence in the record that the six Task 3 checks were walked by Igor or by the design team. Treat it as "unblocked by instruction", not "reviewed". If the design team later disagrees, that is polish through F-1..F-6 below, not a reopened gate.
