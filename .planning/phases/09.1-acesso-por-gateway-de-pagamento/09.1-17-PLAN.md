<objective>
The store side of the gateway: what a member sees where they buy, what an admin types where they price a product, and the bell row that confirms a purchase. After this plan a member of a gateway tenant opens the tenant's payment page from the product, comes back to a product that has unlocked itself, and finds "Compra confirmada" in the bell; an admin registers the gateway product id and the checkout link next to the price, and is warned when a product cannot be bought.

Task 1 is the tracer: the product page in gateway mode with the live unlock, through the real `purchase` field of the product read. Task 2 is the product form block and its two-step save. Task 3 is the bell row, the manager pills and the catalog pins.

Decisions implemented: D-502 (the product form is the single writer of what a product pays through), D-505 (a revoked product returns to buyable), D-506 (Comprar opens the checkout link and nothing is faked), D-507 (the price is a storefront reference, the final value is on the payment page), D-508 (no link, no purchase), UI-D-524, UI-D-525, UI-D-526.

Purpose: a purchase that happens on another site must still feel like one flow, and the one silent, expensive failure of the phase (a product priced with no way to pay for it) must be visible to the person who can fix it.
Output: the gateway control and its live refresh, the Pagamento block and mapping client, the notification renderer and glyph, the manager pills, the catalogs and the tests.

<planning_decisions>
1. **The page decides the mode from the answer, once, on the server.** `productPageView` returns a `buyMode` of `simulated`, `gateway-link` or `gateway-unavailable` (only while the action is `buy`) and the link already validated as `https:`; the client component never sees a mode it must interpret, so a stale or tampered client state cannot reach the simulated control.
2. **The action zone persists.** In gateway mode the page wraps the action zone in one `aria-live="polite"` element that exists before and after the unlock, because a live region that is created together with its content announces nothing; in simulated mode no wrapper is added and the markup is unchanged.
3. **The live refresh is a refetch, never a state.** The signal only calls `router.refresh()`; the server decides `owned`. The refresh is throttled to one per 1500 ms with a trailing call, skips the first-join callback, and also runs when the tab becomes visible, which covers a device that dropped its socket while the person paid.
4. **The mapping is a second request, ordered after the store save.** The store and payments modules cannot share a transaction (MOD-02), so the form orders two calls and keeps its own state across a partial failure: a ref for the created id means a create is never sent twice, and the second request is idempotent (PUT replaces, DELETE of nothing is the same 204).
5. **Generic mapping failures reuse the shipped save toast.** No unapproved sentence is invented: the field errors are the approved ones, and anything else uses `store.form.errors.save`, with the typed values kept.
6. **The grid card pill is a third kind, not a new component.** `ProductCard` gains a `noLink` kind with the leading glyph; the host picks it by priority (archived, then owned, then noLink) and only for managers.
</planning_decisions>
