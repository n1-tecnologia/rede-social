<objective>
The two reactions to what the webhook does: when a member is cut, their devices are forgotten and their open app is told to look again; when a purchase is confirmed, the buyer gets exactly one in-app notice and one push. Everything else the webhook does stays silent on purpose (D-500).

Task 1 is the tracer: a canceled subscription through the real route, the apply job, the new subscriber, the deleted devices, the published signal and the member's next request. Task 2 adds the buyer's "Compra confirmada" notification source and its push copy, and the silence cases.

Decisions implemented: D-496 and D-497 (the cut and its screen, reached through the nudge), D-498 (a cut member receives no push), D-499 (the session is not revoked; the tail is cut by reusing the blocked-member chain), D-500 (no admin notification), D-506 (the in-app "Compra confirmada" notification).

Purpose: the API already refuses a cut member on the very next request; these two reactions only make the open app notice (so the person is not stuck on a stale screen) and tell a buyer their purchase worked.

<planning_decisions>
1. **A twin, not a refactor.** `membership-access-cut.ts` mirrors `membership-blocked.ts` line for line in structure (two independent best-effort transactions, ids-only logs) instead of generalising it: the block handler is shipped and tested, and the two events may diverge (a cut has a return, a block is manual).
2. **The nudge payload is `{ kind: 'membership' }`.** The shipped shell refetches counters on `notifications.changed` whatever the payload says; the refetch is what meets the 403, so no new Realtime event name is needed.
3. **Actor-less notice.** The buyer is the only person involved; the intent has no actor and the exclusion list is empty, which the row renders as the shipped glyph variant (UI-D-526).
4. **No archive retraction.** See the A-P30 truth: the buyer still owns an archived product.
5. **Copy server-side.** A push exists before any page loads, so the body is built here and only carried in the push hint, never stored in the row (the courses precedent).
</planning_decisions>
