# Phase 09.1 Plan 07: The apply job and the order-of-events policy Summary

**A recorded gateway delivery now actually changes what a person may do: `kernel.payment-event` opens the door for an e-mail before the account exists, cuts a member on a late, canceled, refunded or charged-back subscription with exactly one signal, returns them on a renewal with none, and unlocks or locks a store product at the amount paid, all through a pure order-of-events policy, a per-reference advisory lock and tenant-pinned statements.**

## Accomplishments

- **Tracer through the whole stack.** A signed `fake` delivery of an approved purchase for `ana@...` with no account yields one `access_grants` row keyed by the sale (`provider`, `external_ref`), `applied` / `access_granted`, `mapping_id` and `valid_until` set. After the person signs up, a gated rede-lab admits them on `GET /v1/me/bootstrap`. A `subscription_canceled` (and, in a table, `subscription_late`, a refund and a chargeback) turns the row `inactive`, the very next request answers `403 SUBSCRIPTION_PENDING`, a spy on `membership.access_cut` sees exactly one `{ tenantId, userId, membershipId }`, and the membership row and profile are byte-for-byte unchanged. A `subscription_renewed` (or an approved purchase) returns them: 200, no new event, no `notifications` row.
- **The brain, safe to run twice, out of order and in parallel.** `applyPaymentEvent` runs in ONE `withAdminTx`: `select … for update of e` (a state other than `received`/`failed` is an untouched no-op), `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended(tenant:provider:externalRef, 0))`, the mapping looked up with `tenant_id` pinned (a product another tenant mapped is `unknown_product`), then the access or store branch. Removing the advisory lock makes the refund-races-purchase case fail in two out of two runs, so the lock is load-bearing, not decorative.
- **Pure policy.** `decideAccessWrite` / `decideStoreWrite` carry the rule (one-off refund or chargeback terminal, recurring arrival order wins, older `occurredAt` stale, null never compares) with a docblock table and a unit table over every effect, recurrence, row and time relation.
- **Store branch.** A grant for a live member's e-mail calls `app.store_gateway_purchase(tenant, membership, target, provider, ref, amount, now())`; `purchased` records `product_granted` and emits one `payments.purchase_confirmed`; `already_active` (a renewal of a live sale, a re-sent body) is `no_effect`; a suspend or revoke calls `app.store_gateway_revoke`. An applied refund or chargeback of the same one-off reference makes a late approval `stale`; a recurring renewal after a revoke buys again as a NEW order (A-P11).
- **Everything ambiguous is visible, nothing is guessed.** No account, a plus-address (`ana+x@` against `ana@`), a blocked and a soft-deleted membership are `no_membership` with nothing written; an unmapped product, a USD grant and an event with no effect, e-mail, reference or amount each end in their own visible final state and never fail the delivery.
- **Failure and retry.** A thrown error rolls the transaction back, a separate transaction marks the event `failed` (conditional on `received`), the log carries ids and the message only, and the error is rethrown. The retry converges (proved with a temporary trigger that refuses one reference).

## Issues Encountered

- `tx.execute` through drizzle returns timestamps as text, so the job reads them with `to_char(... 'YYYY-MM-DD"T"HH24:MI:SS.MS"Z"')` and parses once; the stale comparison therefore has millisecond precision, which is the precision an adapter's `Date` has.
- A cancel and a renew racing each other can commit in either order; the concurrency case asserts what holds for both orders (both events applied, the row status and the member's request agree, exactly one cut) rather than a fixed final state.
