|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-12-28 | Tampering | the counters BFF or the shell following a location chosen by a response body | medium | mitigate | `location` is the `bootstrapRedirectPath` mapping of the code, never read from the body; `landOnFormFlow` requires the `/completar-perfil` prefix; protocol-relative and absolute values do not navigate |
</threat_model>
