### IN-02 side effect: Safari older than 16.4

The export route now accepts only `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin`. A browser that sends no fetch metadata (Safari before 16.4, released March 2023) gets an empty 403 on the export, and the dialog shows its failure state. Judged against PFORM-21's text: the requirement lists the roles, the row set, the streaming, the formula neutralisation, the audit row and the 403 for `support_tenant` and `member`; it names no browser support, so the requirement is not violated. It is also narrow: only a tenant admin, only the export, only on a browser over three years old, and every current iOS and Android browser sends the header. The cost of the opposite choice was an audit-row spam path (a state-changing GET accepted with neither header). My reading: acceptable for the requirement as written. It is still a product call about supported browsers, so it is listed as a human item rather than closed by me; if Igor wants old Safari covered, the fallback is to accept a same-origin `Origin` header when `Sec-Fetch-Site` is absent.
