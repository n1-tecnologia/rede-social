<planning_decisions>
1. **The floor is one module.** `PRIVACY_FLOOR`, `isBelowPrivacyFloor`, `BandGroup`, `mergeBandsBelowFloor` and `ENGAGEMENT_BAND_ORDER` move to `contracts/floor.ts` (pure, no React); `ui/metrics.ts` re-exports them so every web call site keeps its import; `server/floor.ts` adds `floorCount` and `floorBands` on top (D-462).
2. **What is floored.** Groups of people or orders whose members the admin cannot otherwise list: story reach by band, a post's reactivated members, RSVPs and orders associated with it, the vitrine purchases, and (plan 09) every money and order group by band, product buyers by band, ladder paths and cancellations. Interactors by band are not floored (the admin already lists who liked and commented), like the engagement bands (UI-D-486, R-A4).
3. **Associations are 7-day windows after the member's own interaction** with the item (first like or comment), counted per member once; `reactivated` = interactors whose previous counted action was 30 or more days before that interaction (or who had none).
4. **Lists read live, bounded by the snapshot** (plan 04's rule); the unanswered list is the one live exception whose 24-hour cut uses the read time, because it is a to-do list, not a number.
5. **Item ids are the raw uuid;** the service looks the id up among the tenant's live posts, then stories (uuids do not collide across tables), and answers the bare 404 otherwise.
</planning_decisions>
