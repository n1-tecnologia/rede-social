### CR-01: Privacy floor bypassable by subtraction for revoked orders and per-post product sales

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification
**Files modified:** `packages/modules/dashboard/server/finance.ts`, `packages/modules/dashboard/contracts/finance.ts`, `packages/modules/dashboard/server/routes.ts`, `packages/modules/dashboard/README.md`, `packages/modules/dashboard/tests/finance-floor.test.ts`, `apps/web/components/dashboard/OwnerFinanceOverview.tsx`, `apps/web/components/dashboard/OwnerFinanceProductDetail.tsx`, `apps/web/components/dashboard/OwnerFinance.test.tsx`, `apps/web/components/dashboard/OwnerFinanceDetail.test.tsx`
**Commit:** df9b0458
**Applied fix:**
- New `leaksBySubtraction` / `partToWithhold` in `applyFinanceFloor`. A split leaks when its parent is shown, a part is floored, and the remainder is not known to hold 5 or more people. When it leaks, one shown part or the parent is withheld, so the remainder always contains a group of 5 or more:
  - **A product's revoked orders vs the area's `revoked.current`:** the smallest shown product's `revoked` becomes `null`. The product detail then omits its "Cancelamentos" card, and the area number stays.
  - **A post's sales by product:** the smallest shown product leaves the post's `products`. That product's detail no longer lists the post.
  - **Posts and events vs their association totals:** these are near-splits (one order can tie to two posts), handled conservatively. `contentSales.total` and `eventSales.total` become `null`. The web keeps the column label and every row.
- Contracts: the product detail's `revoked` and the two association totals are now nullable.
- Unit tests subtract the shown parts from the parent and assert that no floored part's exact orders and cents come back. This covers both reported cases and the totals.
- **Human check:** the withheld representation (null, or left out of a list) needs a look. The UI omits the card rather than showing new copy, and that is a product/UI choice.
