# Phase 08.7 Plan 09: Financeiro on the API, floored by the server Summary

**The admin's API now answers Financeiro from the tenant's nightly build, behind the store gate. It covers primary and secondary revenue, revoked orders, revenue by product and by today's band, the 12 + 12 weekly trend, the summary at D and D-30, the purchase ladder by product, never-again, stalled products, the exclusive community's fading payers and the sales associated with a post or a check-in. A product detail and nine named buyers lists sit behind the numbers. Every group of 1-4 people leaves the API as `{ belowFloor: true }`, with no count and no cents.**

## Accomplishments

- **The index.** `store_orders_tenant_paid_idx (tenant_id, paid_at)` is in the store's drizzle schema and its generated migration, pinned by pgTAP `has_index`.
- **`app.dashboard_area_finance` and builder v6.** `payload.finance` holds ids and raw numbers only:
  - **Who counts.** Orders of active role members, paid before `untilAt` and not revoked before it (EP-41). A revoked order is never revenue and counts in `revoked`, dated by `revoked_at`.
  - **Primary and secondary.** Each member's first paid order by `paid_at, id` is primary; same-instant first orders resolve by id.
  - **Totals.** `totals`, `primary`, `secondary` and `revoked` for Window30 and Prev30.
  - **By product.** Every product created before `untilAt`, with the window numbers, entries and add-ons, the window before, the last sale, its revocations, its weekly cents and its buyers by band.
  - **By band and over time.** `byBand` by today's band, and `weekly` primary and secondary cents in 12 + 12 blocks.
  - **Summary and ladder.** `summary` at D and as of D-30. `ladder` runs from the first paid product to the second.
  - **Attention.** `neverAgain` by band, and `attention.stalled` (active products unsold for more than 60 days).
  - **VIP and associations.** The `vip` choice of UI-D-490, plus `contentSales` and `eventSales`.
  - **Ordering.** Every `jsonb_agg` carries an explicit `order by` (EP-40).
- **`GET /v1/dashboard/owner/finance`.**
  - **Gate.** `requireModule('store')` runs before `requireRole('admin_tenant')` (D-460).
  - **Floor.** One `applyFinanceFloor` pass runs before the answer.
  - **Live names.** Product, community and event names are joined live.
  - **VIP block.** `vip` is null when `communities` is off.
  - **Em breve.** `offers`, `paidEvents`, `attention.expiring` and `attention.fullEvents` are not measured (D-455, D-465).
- **`GET …/finance/products/{productId}`.** The card, its revoked orders, its weekly cents, its buyers by band (merged and floored) and its associated live posts (floored). A foreign, unknown or post-build id gets the bare 404.
- **`GET …/finance/buyers/{list}`.**
  - **The nine lists.** `band`, `never`, `primary`, `secondary`, `rebought`, `time-to-second`, `lifetime`, `vip-fading` and `product`.
  - **How they read.** They are read live up to `untilAt` with the build's rules. Each sorts by its own key (EP-39) and pages with plan 04's helper.
  - **Errors.** `expiring` and `offer` get the bare 404. A list without its `band` or `productId` gets 400.

## Decisions Made

Planning decisions 1-4 were followed as written (see `key-decisions`). These choices were made during execution:

- **Only counted members' orders count.** Active role members only, so the band split adds up to the totals and each list's total is its number.
- **"Paid" is judged as of an instant.** That gives EP-41 for free: a revoke after `untilAt` leaves that day's payload byte-identical. The D-30 summary is computed as of `from30At` the same way.
- **Money groups are floored on their buyers.** No buyer gives the value 0; 1-4 buyers give `{ belowFloor: true }`; 5 or more show orders, cents and buyers together. Totals and per-product numbers are not floored.
- **Bands: record in the area, merged in the detail.** The area's `byBand` is six individually floored bands, as the plan's truth says. Only the product detail merges bands.
- **`vip` has two additions.**
  - **`withPush`** counts the fading holders with push on, which matches the shipped note's `{push}`.
  - **`revenueCents`** feeds "{community}: receita e uso".
- **Unknown buyers lists get the bare 404.** This follows the plan's behavior, through a per-route hook. Other validation failures keep the 400 envelope.
