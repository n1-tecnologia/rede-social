# Phase 08.7 Plan 10: Financeiro on real data Summary

**The admin's Financeiro area now reads the tenant's nightly build, behind the store gate. It shows primary and secondary revenue, revenue by product and by today's band, the 12 + 12 trend, what needs attention, the sales after a publication or an event (worded as an association) and the exclusive community by its live name. A product opens its detail, with its buyers by band and a link to the store's buyers screen. Quem compra shows the ladder by product and who never bought again. Every number opens its named buyers, continued in place. Offers, expiring accesses and paid events read "Em breve". Every group of 1-4 people reads "menos de 5", and a group of 0 reads "nenhum pedido".**

## Decisions Made

Planning decisions 1-3 were followed as written (see `key-decisions`). These choices were made during execution:

- **Buyer rows name no product.** The API rows carry none, so every lead says when and how much.
- **The VIP fading rows** read "Pagou {value} desde que entrou" plus "Última atividade:". Their `at` is the last activity, not a payment.

- **Post titles** come from Conteúdo's cached answer. A post outside the window is named as such.
