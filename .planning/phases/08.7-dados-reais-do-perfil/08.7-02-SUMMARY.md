## Decisions Made
Planning decisions 1-6 were followed as written (see `key-decisions`). Three choices were made during execution:
- `previousEngagement` has the full bands shape. Its `newActivated` counts members who were new at D-30 and acted by D-30.
- A community's `exclusive` flag is true only when the store is on AND the community is linked to a product. This matches `app.community_viewer_ids`: with the store off, the base is every member.
- `ENGAGEMENT_BANDS` lives in the zod contract. A unit test pins it equal to `ui/metrics`' `ENGAGEMENT_BAND_ORDER`, so the client `ui` entry stays free of zod.

Interpretations to confirm:
- An RSVP or check-in counts even if its event was cancelled or deleted later. The stream does not filter on event status, so the action stays the member's.
- "Since joining" (`insights.communities` and `postTypes`) means the membership's whole history.
