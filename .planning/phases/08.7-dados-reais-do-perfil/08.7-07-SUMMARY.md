# Phase 08.7 Plan 07: Conteúdo on real data Summary

**The admin's Conteúdo area now reads the tenant's snapshot end to end. It shows the window's publications with their member likes and comments, the kinds, a 12-week trend with gaps for empty weeks, and a heatmap of full-day slots. "Em breve" appears only where nothing is measured. A publication opens its 48-hour curve, its floored associations and the people behind each number. The comments waiting for the team list oldest first, and "Responder" opens the post page. Every list continues in place.**

## Accomplishments

- **Module.**
  - `BarList` takes floored rows. Each one draws an empty track with the host's floor text, has no rate and no count-up, and sorts after every shown row.
  - `LineChart` takes `null` points and draws them as gaps. Its scale comes from the defined values only.
- **URL.** `parseOwnerContentView(params, flags)` is syntactic:
  - tabs, kinds, `curtidas` and `comentarios`, and the two measured numbers are slugs;
  - `post` and `membro` are uuids;
  - `responder=comentarios` opens the waiting comments;
  - a typed "Em breve" number or `quem` falls one level up with no notice;
  - with feed off, only Por tipo is left.
- **Loaders and continuation.**
  - Five React-`cache`d loaders read the plan 06 routes. A 404 on the detail or a people list is `notFound`, and the view falls one level up.
  - The server action has four new kinds with zod params: `content-posts` (offset), `content-people`, `content-interactions` and `content-unanswered`.
  - Their rows are built by `lib/owner-content-rows.ts`, for the first page and every appended page.
- **Overview.**
  - The area streams in its own `Suspense`, under the freshness line.
  - Publicações and Interações are linked KPIs. Membros que interagiram is measured and unlinked. Taxa, Visualizações and Alcance are unlinked "Em breve" tiles.
  - The trend divides the raw sums per block.
  - The heatmap has the four full-day slots and blank named cells.
  - Melhores publicações open `&post=`.
  - Quem interage shows exact counts by band.
  - Próximos passos: "Responder comentários" is linked. "Agendar publicação" and "Criar série" are disabled with the pill.
  - Empty blocks show their UI-D-481 sentences.
- **Por tipo and cards.**
  - Por tipo has one card per kind in the answer, with the line pill for every unmeasured metric. "% de comentários" is real.
  - A card shows its likes and comments and "salvamentos em breve · compartilhamentos em breve".
  - A story card shows its comment count only.
  - A card's meta names its community live, or "Início" for the tenant-wide feed. With communities off, the community part is dropped.
- **Detail.**
  - The 48-hour curve.
  - Reach by band: an "Em breve" block for a post; floored merged bars for a story.
  - Retention and story taps are "Em breve" blocks.
  - Reactivated members, RSVPs, orders (whole, or "menos de 5 pedidos") and the vitrine line, which names the community live.
  - The comments card, for posts only.
  - The recommendation: "até ontem", with its action disabled and the pill.
  - A video plays on `/post/[postId]`. A story's play stays disabled, with no pill.
- **Lists.**
  - Who liked and who commented: member cards to `&membro=`. A commenter the team has not answered shows "Sem resposta". A comment's text never shows here.
  - Every interaction opens its member.
  - The waiting comments show their text, and "Responder" opens `/post/[postId]`.
  - A member opened from any of these renders the real `OwnerMemberDetail` with Conteúdo's links.

## Decisions Made

Planning decisions 1-3 were followed as written (see `key-decisions`). These choices were made during execution:

- **"Membros que interagiram" opens nothing, and the bands only count.** Plan 06 built no list of interactors. The number stays measured and unlinked, the bands of "Quem interage" are exact counts, and `&numero=membros` and `&faixa=` fall to the overview.
- **The waiting comments are one screen for the tenant.** The API lists them for the whole tenant, oldest first. Every "Precisa de atenção" row and "Responder comentários" open it, and its "Voltar" lands on the attention block.
- **With feed off, only Por tipo is left.** Visão geral and Publicações are the feed's.
- **The publications list continues through a fourth kind, `content-posts`.** The area answer holds every item, so it pages by offset.
- **A post with no community reads "Início" in its meta** while communities is on (UI-D-475).
- **The RSVP card sums the events it can show.** A floored event is left out of the sum; its own line reads "menos de 5".
- **The push recommendation reads Membros' `atRiskWithPush`** from the cached members answer. It is absent with notifications off, and the series and reel advice is absent before any slot has a publication.
