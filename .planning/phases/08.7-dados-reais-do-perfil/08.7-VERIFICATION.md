### Gaps Summary

One blocker, with one root cause. The Financeiro privacy floor is applied group by group, but two floored partitions are shown next to an exact parent total of the same rows:
- **Revoked orders.** The area total sits beside each product's revoked group.
- **Publication-tied sales.** A post's sales total sits beside its per-product split, in the same JSON answer.

A floored group of 1-4 people therefore comes back exactly by subtraction. The verifier reproduced this against the real `applyFinanceFloor`. It contradicts D-462 ("every group total below 5 people or orders reads 'menos de 5' … applied by the API") and INSIGHT-14, which names "cancellations" and "sales tied to a publication". It is also the C-1 case the module's own `contracts/floor.ts` documents.
