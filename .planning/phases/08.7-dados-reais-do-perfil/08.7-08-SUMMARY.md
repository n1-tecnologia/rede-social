# Phase 08.7 Plan 08: Eventos on real data Summary

**The admin's Eventos area now reads the tenant's nightly build end to end, gated by the events module.**

- **The next events.** The next six events show their confirmed count and seats left.
- **Past events.** Each event of the last 30 days shows its check-ins and walk-ins.
- **In person against online.** The two formats are compared, with how many events filled up.
- **Who comes.** Presences are counted by the band each member was in on the day before the event.
- **"Em breve".** Ticket price, revenue and the waiting list read "Em breve".
- **Event rows.** Every event row opens the event's attendee list.
- **With events off.** The route answers 404 `MODULE_DISABLED` before the role check, and the panel renders nothing.

## Decisions Made

Planning decisions 1-3 were followed as written (see `key-decisions`). These choices were made during execution:

- **Per-event counts include everyone.** Confirmed, check-ins and walk-ins count every attendee row, the same count the capacity guard and the attendee list use. Only the band split is members-only.
- **Attendance percent: presences over everyone who was in.** Check-ins plus walk-ins, over confirmed plus walk-ins, through the shipped `attendancePercent`. This is UI-D-485's rule for members, applied to the owner. A walk-in is a presence in both terms, so the share never passes 100%.
- **The totals carry `walkIns`** beside `confirmed` and `present`, so Visão geral (plan 12) can use the same denominator.
- **An event cancelled or deleted since the build leaves the answer.**
- **The engaged share comes from Membros.** It reads the cached members answer. When that answer is not ready, the sentence is left out and the bars stay.
- **The time shows minutes only when needed.** "19h" or "20h30", through an ICU select.
