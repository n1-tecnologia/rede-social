# Phase 08.7 Plan 03: Member activity ("Sua atividade") Summary

**A member's "Sua atividade" on `/perfil` now shows their own numbers from the nightly snapshot. These are their likes and comments of the last 7 complete days with the change against the 7 before, the stories they saw, where they interact most ("Início" for the tenant feed), their presence at events of the last 30 days and their week streak. "Em breve" sits in the places that have no source yet, worded by the same family the owner panel uses. A new member gets a preparing card, never a sample.**

## Accomplishments
- Custom migration `20261018225848`:
  - `app.dashboard_member_activity(p_day)` is a tenant-pinned definer executable by no lane role. It reads 08.7-02's action stream for likes and live comments (Window7 vs Prev7), the per-community interactions and the Monday-first week streak. It reads `story_views` for the member's own views and `event_attendances` joined to active, live events for attendance.
  - Builder v3 (`v_version := 3`) writes every member row's `activity` right after inserting the rows, in the same transaction.
- `GET /v1/dashboard/activity` (`requireRole('member')`, no request schema, `no-store`):
  - It selects the row by the lane's own claims and answers preparing for a missing, null or invalid activity.
  - It reads module flags before the lane, so `byCommunity` and `attendance` can be omitted.
  - It joins community names live and drops unreadable ones.
  - It writes one log line with ids only.
