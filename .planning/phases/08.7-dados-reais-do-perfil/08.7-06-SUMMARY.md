# Phase 08.7 Plan 06: Conteúdo on the API, floored by the server Summary

**The admin's API now answers Conteúdo from the tenant's nightly build. It returns every live post and story of the 30 days with its kind, member-only likes and comments, the thread meta, story view totals, the KPIs with their previous values, the trend sums, a 7 × 4 heatmap of full-day slots, and per-community numbers. A publication's detail adds its 48-hour curve and its 7-day associations. The lists show who liked and who commented, every interaction of the window, and the comments waiting for the team. Every small group of people or orders is floored in the service before the answer leaves the API.**

## Decisions Made

Planning decisions 1-5 were followed as written (see `key-decisions`). These choices were made during execution:

- **Interactions are counted on the window's posts.** They are the member likes and live comments on posts published in the 30 days, up to `untilAt`. That makes the interactions list's total equal `kpis.interactions.current`, and lets the web divide interactions by posts per post. The previous window counts the D-59..D-30 posts up to that window's own end.
- **Everything posts-based is omitted with feed off.** That is the KPIs, interactors by band, the trend, the heatmap and the community rows. Stories have their own items and kind totals.
- **A story's views and reach are the same number.** `story_views` holds one row per member and story, so the measured total is its unique member viewers. Staff views count nowhere.
- **Fields beyond the fixture shapes:**
  - `kinds` (the kinds shown) and `byKind` (zeros for an empty kind, EP-29's "every kind with 0");
  - `kindMetrics`;
  - `commentMeta.unanswered`, because a comment count that includes members' replies cannot give it by subtraction;
  - live names on `byCommunity`.
- **Out-of-window ids are a 404.** A live publication outside the newest build's window, or one whose module is off, is the bare 404 on the detail and the people list.
- **Comments sort unanswered first on one keyset.** Their sort instant is shifted 1000 years forward, so the cursor stays one ISO instant.
- **Each post's weekly interactions count up to `untilAt`.** Older posts have simply had more time.
- **`flooredBandGroupSchema` lives in `shared.ts`, not `floor.ts`.** That keeps the client `ui` entry, which re-exports `floor.ts`, free of zod.
