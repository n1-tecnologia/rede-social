<planning_decisions>
1. **One turn definition.** A member turn starts at the first member message after the last staff message (or the conversation's first message); its response time is the minutes to the next staff message; unanswered turns count in `awaiting`, never in the medians.
2. **The change chip compares the last two weeks** (`weeklyMedianMinutes[7]` against `[6]`), computed by the web; a null side shows no chip.
3. **Staff, not members.** `answeredByStaff` lists only users holding a current `admin_tenant` or `support_tenant` membership; the answer carries no member identity of any kind (EP-43).
</planning_decisions>
