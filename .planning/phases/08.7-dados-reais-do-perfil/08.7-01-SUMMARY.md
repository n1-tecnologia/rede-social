# Phase 08.7 Plan 01: Snapshot tracer and daily dispatcher Summary

**The admin's Visão geral members card now shows the tenant's real active-member count and its change, read from a snapshot the worker builds once a day (after the tenant's local midnight) through a tenant-pinned definer. A kernel dispatcher queues that build for every tenant at boot and every hour.**

## Accomplishments
- Two snapshot tables: `dashboard_snapshots` is readable by `admin_tenant` only, and `dashboard_member_snapshots` by the member or the admin. Neither has a write policy or a lane write grant. Generated migration `20261018215328`.
- `app.dashboard_build_snapshot(date)` builds the snapshot and `app.dashboard_area_overview(date)` computes the Visão geral numbers (custom migration `20261018215342`). The builder accepts only the zero user, refuses a day that is not complete yet with 22023, and skips a day already built at the current payload version. Window bounds come from `AT TIME ZONE tenants.timezone` in SQL.
- The dashboard module now has `./contracts`, `./server` and `./db`. It adds the `dashboard.snapshot-build` job and `GET /v1/dashboard/owner/overview` (`requireAuth` + `requireRole('admin_tenant')`, `no-store`), wired into the app, the registry and the worker.
- `kernel.daily-tenant-dispatch` runs in one admin transaction. It reads each active tenant's local yesterday in SQL and skips suspended tenants and invalid timezones. It enqueues `<tenantId>:<day>` jobs and re-arms itself an hour later. The worker arms a boot run under a separate singleton key.
- Web: the `getOwnerOverview` loader never throws. `freshnessText` uses the tenant's zone and h23. `SnapshotFreshness` is the freshness line. `OwnerAreaState` covers preparing and error, and `OwnerAreaSkeleton` covers loading. Visão geral streams in its own `Suspense`.
- Tests: pgTAP 198 (46 assertions), plus new rows in 010, 020 (+12) and 160 (+5). Integration: the `08.7 tracer` (11 cases) and `08.7 dispatch` (5 cases) suites, the `phase 08.7 sweep: dashboard owner` case and its inventory entry. Unit tests cover the contracts, the job and the dispatcher seam. The e2e helper builds rede-demo's snapshot, and a new profile.spec test checks the real member count and the freshness line.
