# Phase 08.6: Code Review Report

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-17T00:20:54Z
**Depth:** standard
**Files Reviewed:** 88 (the 08.6 hunks only, in the pre-existing files the phase extended)

## Summary

I reviewed the six new migrations (SECURITY DEFINER posture, tenant pinning, grants, the insert policy), the server additions (member-area reads, reminder opt-in and job, lifecycle and course notification sources, lessons batch, reactivation, publish stamp and retraction), the pure member-area helpers, and the web member area (Início, Trilhas, Progresso, reminder toggle, live form reactivation, bell renderers).

What holds up:
- **Tenant isolation.** Every new definer has `search_path = ''`, fully qualified names, and pins `tenant_id = app.tenant_id()` in every statement. EXECUTE is revoked from PUBLIC. I found no path where one tenant's audience or opt-ins leak to another tenant. `app.live_locked_ids_for(user)` decides the staff exemption from the target user's membership, not from the worker's `support_tenant` claim, so the fire-time access re-check really drops revoked buyers.
- **Locked content.** The overview, tracks and progress answers carry titles only. The live projection (`app.course_live_cards`) carries no room link and no replay id. "Novas aulas" filters on `lockReason === null`.
- **Authorization.** Admin routes are behind `courses.manage`. `admin_tenant` preview writes are refused both in the service and by the RLS insert check.
- **RSC boundaries.** `ReminderToggle` and `HomeLiveChoice` are the only client components, and they get only serialisable props or JSX. The toggle is keyed on the server state, so it remounts on refresh.

The defects are in the notification pipeline's edge cases:
- the lessons batch's re-arm rule (WR-01);
- notices lost after the watermark commits (WR-02);
- republishing a course within 24 hours, which interacts badly with the retraction (WR-03);
- the write and privacy definers callable by any authenticated member lane (WR-04);
- a race where a lesson committed during a claim is never announced (WR-05).

No BLOCKER-class issue was found.

## Warnings

### WR-01: One stuck pending lesson disables the lessons-batch re-arm for every later lesson of the course

**File:** `packages/modules/courses/server/lessons-batch.ts:59-67` (with `supabase/migrations/20261016190717_courses_course_notice_functions.sql:110-112`)

**Issue:** `lessonsBatchPlan` decides `rearm` from `oldestPendingAt`, which is `min(created_at)` of all unannounced lessons whose video is not ready. Here is how it fails:
1. A lesson's video fails or stays `processing` for more than 24 hours. It is never claimed, so its `announced_at` stays NULL forever, and it stays the course's oldest pending lesson.
2. Any later lesson created with a still-processing video arms a batch.
3. The claim answers `pending >= 1` with the stuck lesson as `oldest_pending_at`, so `young` is false and the job does not re-arm.
4. When the new lesson's video turns `ready` minutes later, no job is waiting. The lesson is not announced until some unrelated future lesson is created, maybe never.

This breaks R-A5 / D-450 ("re-armed for 24 hours"), which is a per-lesson window, not a per-course one. The same stuck lesson is later announced inside an unrelated batch once its video becomes ready (see IN-04).

**Fix:** Decide the re-arm from the youngest pending lesson, or count the pending lessons still inside the window:
```sql
-- in app.course_lessons_claim_batch
select (select count(*) from flipped)::int,
       (select count(*) from waiting)::int,
       (select max(w.created_at) from waiting w)          -- newest_pending_at
-- or: (select count(*) from waiting w where w.created_at > now() - interval '24 hours') as young_pending
```
```ts
const newestMs = claim.newestPendingAt === null ? Number.NaN : Date.parse(claim.newestPendingAt);
const young = Number.isFinite(newestMs) && nowMs - newestMs < LESSONS_BATCH_MAX_AGE_HOURS * 3_600_000;
```

### WR-02: The lessons batch commits its watermark before the notice is enqueued; a failed enqueue loses the notice for good

**File:** `packages/modules/courses/server/lessons-batch.ts:125-164`, `packages/modules/courses/server/reminders.ts:170-179`, `packages/core/server/events/bus.ts:68-92`

**Issue:** `runLessonsBatch` commits `app.course_lessons_claim_batch` (which flips `announced_at`) in its own transaction, then calls `emit` and `flush` after the commit. `flush` never throws: it catches every handler error and only logs it (`domain_event.handler_failed`). So if the notification sink fails to enqueue the fan-out (pool exhaustion, a transient DB error), the job still returns `'emitted'` and pg-boss completes it. The lessons are marked announced, and nobody is ever notified. The docstring's safety claim ("a retry after a committed claim claims 0, and the fan-out's dedupe key keeps an emitted notice single") assumes a retry that cannot happen.

`runLiveReminder` has the same swallow, with lower impact (its job also completes without a retry, so the reminder is lost). Of the two, the batch is the harmful case because its watermark makes the loss permanent.

**Fix:** Write the fan-out enqueue in the same transaction as the claim, so the watermark and the notice commit or roll back together. One way is a transactional sink entry point (`enqueueInTx(tx, 'notifications.fanout', …)`). Another is to emit inside `withTenantTx` and have the sink use the transaction. If that is not possible, make the job fail when the handler fails: add a `flushStrict` that rethrows, and do not move the watermark until the enqueue has succeeded.

### WR-03: Republishing a course within 24 hours leaves members with "Este conteúdo foi removido." and silently swallows the lessons added while it was a draft

**File:** `packages/modules/courses/server/service.ts:1837-1879` (setCourseStatus), `packages/modules/courses/server/notifications.ts:586-591`

**Issue:** D-448's 24-hour stamp and D-454's retraction interact badly:
1. The admin publishes course C. `published_notified_at` is stamped and every member gets "Novo curso".
2. The admin unpublishes C. `course.unpublished` retracts every notice whose subject is C, so the course and lessons notices become `{"removed": true}`.
3. While C is a draft, the admin adds lessons. No batch is armed, because the course is not published.
4. The admin republishes C within 24 hours. The guarded stamp writes nothing, so no notice is sent. In the same transaction, every NULL `announced_at` lesson of C is stamped as announced, on the grounds that "the course notice covers them", even though no course notice went out.

**Result:** C is live, the members' bell shows only "Este conteúdo foi removido." for it, and the lessons added during the draft are never announced to anyone. The spec says only that a republish within 24 hours "does not notify again". It does not address the retraction having already erased the first notice.

**Fix:** Pick one of these:
- Stamp the lessons in the `draft → published` transition only when `notifiedAt !== null`. Otherwise arm `armLessonsBatch` so the draft-period lessons are announced through the batch.
- Undo the retraction, or always notify, when the course's previous notices were retracted. For example, clear `published_notified_at` in the unpublish transition, so a republish counts as a fresh publication.

```ts
if (course.status === 'draft' && body.status === 'published') {
  // ...stamp published_notified_at as today...
  if (notifiedAt !== null) {
    await tx.execute(sql`update course_lessons set announced_at = now()
      where tenant_id = ${ctx.tenantId}::uuid and course_id = ${courseId}::uuid and announced_at is null`);
  } else {
    await armLessonsBatch(tx, { tenantId: ctx.tenantId, courseId });
  }
}
```

### WR-04: The worker-only definers (one of them a write) are executable by every authenticated member lane, with no role guard

**File:** `supabase/migrations/20261016190717_courses_course_notice_functions.sql:63-116`, `supabase/migrations/20261016181946_courses_live_reminder_functions.sql:31-51`, `supabase/migrations/20261016184619_courses_live_notice_functions.sql:36-86`

**Issue:** `app.course_lessons_claim_batch(uuid)`, `app.course_live_reminder_user_ids(uuid, boolean)`, `app.course_notice_user_ids()`, `app.live_replay_audience_user_ids(uuid)` and `app.course_audience_user_ids(uuid)` are all `GRANT EXECUTE … TO authenticated`. None of them checks who is calling. They are documented as being for the notifications worker's zero-user lane only ("no route exposes it"). But any code path running a member's transaction (`set local role authenticated` with a member claim) can call them. That breaks two of the phase's own guarantees:
- `course_lessons_claim_batch` is a **write** (`volatile`). A member lane can flip `announced_at` on every claimable lesson of any course in its tenant, suppressing the D-450 announcement for everyone.
- `course_live_reminder_user_ids` returns exactly the list the schema calls PRIVATE ("no member and no support agent can list who opted into which paid live", PR-4, T-08.6-12). The own-row policies are bypassed by the definer for any caller.

The Data API is closed, so today these are reachable only through API code. Still, the project's defense-in-depth rule is that RLS and definers hold even when a route is wrong or SQL injection lands. The pgTAP posture block only asserts "authenticated yes, anon no".

**Fix:** Guard each definer to the worker lane, and add a pgTAP negative case for a member claim:
```sql
-- inside each function body (sql), as an extra predicate:
   and app.user_id() = '00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000'::uuid
   and app.tenant_role() = 'support_tenant'
-- or convert to plpgsql and raise when called from any other lane.
```
Alternatively, grant EXECUTE to a dedicated `courses_worker` role that only the worker's tenant transaction assumes.

### WR-05: A lesson that commits while a claim runs (or while the course is being published) is never announced unless another lesson follows

**File:** `packages/modules/courses/server/service.ts:1332-1342`, `packages/modules/courses/server/lessons-batch.ts:20-36`

**Issue:** `createLesson` arms the batch inside its own transaction. If a batch job for the course is still `created`, pg-boss drops the new enqueue under the `short` policy. That job may then move to `active` and run its claim before the lesson's transaction commits, so the claim cannot see the uncommitted row. The lesson stays `announced_at IS NULL`, and no job is waiting for it.

The `publish` race is the same: `createLesson` reads `status = 'draft'` without a lock while `setCourseStatus` publishes. The code comments and the docstring accept this ("it waits for the next lesson's batch"). On a course where that was the last lesson, though, "the next batch" never comes. The lesson is silently never announced, or it is announced weeks later inside an unrelated batch.

**Fix:** Keep the in-transaction arm, which is atomic with the lesson, and add a second arm after commit, from the service after `withTenantTx` resolves. Once the lesson is committed, either a `created` job exists that has not claimed yet (it will see the lesson, and the duplicate is dropped), or none does (a new job is created):
```ts
const view = await withTenantTx(ctx, async (tx) => { /* … existing arm … */ });
if (view.coursePublished) {
  await enqueue(COURSES_QUEUES.lessonsBatch, { tenantId: ctx.tenantId, courseId },
                { startAfter: new Date(Date.now() + LESSONS_BATCH_WINDOW_MINUTES * 60_000), singletonKey: courseId });
}
```
For the publish race, `setCourseStatus` could also arm a batch after it stamps the lessons, which picks up any lesson that committed in between.

## Info

### IN-01: The reminder insert policy does not check that the live belongs to the caller's tenant

**File:** `supabase/migrations/20261016171547_courses_member_area.sql:14,17`
**Issue:** `live_id` has a single-column FK to `course_lives.id`, and the insert check only tests `live_id <> all(app.live_locked_ids())`. Because that set is tenant-pinned, a foreign tenant's live id is never in it, so the insert passes. The policy also does not refuse cancelled or started lives, which only the route checks. A row like that is inert, because the fire-time definer pins both `r.tenant_id` and the live tenant. Still, the schema docstring presents this policy as the RLS backstop of the route.
**Fix:** Add `and exists (select 1 from public.course_lives v where v.id = live_id and v.tenant_id = app.tenant_id() and v.cancelled_at is null and v.starts_at > now())`, or a composite `(tenant_id, live_id)` FK.

### IN-02: The renderer comment promises uuid validation that the code does not do

**File:** `apps/web/lib/notification-renderers.tsx:123-136`
**Issue:** `courseHref` and `liveHref` say "anything else degrades to `/cursos`", but they accept any non-empty string (encoded with `encodeURIComponent`). This is safe, but a malformed id produces a 404 link rather than the promised fallback.
**Fix:** Test the id against a uuid regex before building the path.

### IN-03: A failed reactivation read tells the admin the live can no longer come back

**File:** `apps/web/lib/courses.ts:1023-1036`, `apps/web/app/(app)/cursos/lives/LiveForm.tsx:934-954`
**Issue:** `getLiveReactivation` answers `false` on any failure (transport, 5xx). The form then shows `live.form.reactivateLocked` ("can no longer be reactivated"), which is a definitive statement based on a failed read.
**Fix:** Return a tri-state (`true | false | 'unknown'`) and show a neutral note or retry when the read failed.

### IN-04: The claim has no age bound, so a lesson whose video becomes ready late is announced as "new" in an unrelated later batch

**File:** `supabase/migrations/20261016190717_courses_course_notice_functions.sql:66-87`
**Issue:** A lesson that passed the 24-hour re-arm window keeps `announced_at` NULL. If its video later becomes `ready`, the next batch for the course claims it and counts it in "N aulas novas", possibly weeks later. This is related to WR-01.
**Fix:** When the window lapses, stamp the expired pending lessons (or mark them skipped) inside the claim, for example `l.created_at > now() - interval '24 hours'` for video lessons. Alternatively, set `announced_at` on them once the job stops re-arming.

### IN-05: The "Live cancelada" notice is not retracted when the live is reactivated

**File:** `packages/modules/courses/server/notifications.ts:586-591`, `packages/modules/courses/server/service.ts:2723-2765`
**Issue:** After a reactivation, the members who opted in hold both "A live … foi cancelada." and "… reativou a live …". Only course subjects have a retraction. This is acceptable as a history, but the cancelled row still links to a live that is now active.
**Fix:** Optionally register a `course.live_scheduled` (`reactivated: true`) retraction matched on `kind = courses.live_cancelled` and subject `live`, or accept it as is and record that decision.

### IN-06: Reminder opt-in rows are never pruned

**File:** `supabase/migrations/20261016171547_courses_member_area.sql:3-9`
**Issue:** `course_live_reminders` rows survive after the live has ended and after a membership is removed or blocked. The definers filter them out, so this is correct, but the table grows without bound, and a removed member's private opt-in history is kept indefinitely.
**Fix:** Add a sweep function to the existing hourly sweeper (`sweepFunctions`) that deletes rows for lives whose end is in the past, or rows whose membership is soft-deleted.
