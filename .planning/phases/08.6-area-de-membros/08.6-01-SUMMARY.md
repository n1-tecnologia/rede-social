# Phase 08.6 Plan 01: Member-area tracer and schema Summary

**The school overview route (`GET /v1/courses/learn/overview`) now tells a member where to continue (começando, then retomando after a completion) and how long their study streak is. The answer comes from one tenant-pinned definer projection over the 08.5 evaluator, a SQL streak bucketed by `tenants.timezone`, and a pure `heroResume` run on the server. The plan also adds the own-row `course_live_reminders` table with its RLS backstop.**

## Accomplishments
- `app.course_member_lessons()`: one row per live lesson of every published course of the caller's tenant. It reuses `app.course_lesson_states` through a lateral join and adds the caller's own `completed_at`, a `touched_at` column, ready-video seconds and titles only. `app.course_member_streak()` counts consecutive local dates with a completion, anchored on today or yesterday, and answers 0 for the admin claim. Both functions are definers with `search_path = ''`, pinned to the tenant and executable by `authenticated` only.
- `contracts/member-area.ts`: pure `heroResume` (the D-437 cascade) and `courseProgressOf`, re-exported from the courses contracts. `learnOverviewSchema` and `learnOverviewHeroSchema` carry exactly the hero fields the UI-SPEC lists.
- `getLearnOverview` runs one `withTenantTx` and logs ids and the hero state only. `learnOverviewRoute` is registered at the head of the learn chain with `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- `courseLiveReminders` (generated migration): own-row select, insert and delete policies. The insert refuses the `admin_tenant` claim and any live in `app.live_locked_ids()`. There is no update policy.

## Decisions Made
The six planning decisions are listed under `key-decisions` and were applied as written. Two execution-level choices:
- The hero treats the order of its `courses` argument as the catalogue order instead of re-sorting. Re-sorting in TypeScript could disagree with Postgres's uuid ordering, and the service already reads `published_at desc, id desc`.
- pgTAP 160 asks the B-lane questions through the null-role (member-branch) lane. S is `admin_tenant` in B, so the explicit admin guard would answer 0 for the wrong reason. A-lane positive controls (streak 1, own opt-in visible, A's course listed) keep the B-lane zeros from being vacuous.
