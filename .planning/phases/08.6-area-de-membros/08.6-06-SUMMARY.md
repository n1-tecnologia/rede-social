# Phase 08.6 Plan 06: The live's lifecycle reaches members Summary

**Members now hear about a live's lifecycle. The first cancel tells the members who turned the reminder on ("Live cancelada"). A moved start re-arms the reminder silently. A new future live is announced to every member and support agent ("Nova live"). A replay is announced only to those who can watch it, through either the live door or the course door ("Gravação disponível"). The admin can reactivate a cancelled live while its start is still ahead (`POST …/reactivate`); this re-arms the surviving reminders and announces "Live reativada". A separate `GET …/reactivation` read tells the admin form whether the button may show, decided by the database clock.**
