## Claude's Discretion

These were offered with the "Pode escrever o contexto" answer and accepted:
- "Novas aulas da semana" means lessons created in the last 7 days in published courses.
- A replay notice reaches only members with access to the live or to the course.
- Início has no Premium block.

These carry stated defaults in CONTEXT.md:
- "Explore por categoria", "Próxima live", the greeting and the empty states.
- The course-by-course list, the certificates block being absent, and "Cursos concluídos" recomputation.
- The admin preview and `support_tenant`.
- Notification kinds, copy, dedupe and module state.
- Where the derivations run, and timezone math.
