# Phase 08.6 Plan 05: The live reminder end to end Summary

**A member can now turn on a live's reminder. `PUT /v1/courses/learn/lives/{liveId}/reminder` stores one row per membership and live and arms one delayed pg-boss job per live and start, set to fire 15 minutes before the live (when the join link opens). The API refuses the opt-in for a locked live (403 `live_locked`) and for a cancelled or started live (409 `reminder_closed`). When the job fires, it checks again that the live exists, is not cancelled, has not moved, that the school is on, that the live has not started and that the worker is at most 10 minutes late. The courses notification source then delivers one in-app notification and a Web Push. Recipients come from a tenant-pinned definer, which drops anyone who lost access.**

## Accomplishments
- **Opt-in routes.** `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE` on `/learn/lives/{liveId}/reminder` answer `{ on }` with `Cache-Control: no-store`. The service reads the live through `app.course_live_cards(liveId)`. `PUT` inserts `on conflict do nothing` and calls `armLiveReminder` in the same transaction. `DELETE` is never refused. The admin preview answers 204 and writes nothing. An unknown or foreign live gets the bare 404.
- **Arming.** One waiting job per live and start (singleton key `{liveId}:{epochSeconds}`, `short` queue policy). Two concurrent `PUT`s from one member, or opt-ins from two members, leave one job. A live five minutes away accepts the opt-in and arms no job.
- **Recipients definer.** `app.course_live_reminder_user_ids(uuid, boolean)` reads past the own-row policies (the worker's zero user owns no row) and returns opted-in live memberships of the caller's tenant only. pgTAP 197 adds 15 assertions, and the whole pgTAP suite passes (33 files, 1515 tests).
- **Job.** `runLiveReminder(raw, nowMs)` applies the skip table in the order not_found, cancelled, moved, module_off, started, late. If no check fails, it emits `course.live_reminder_due` and flushes before returning. The job runs in `coursesSystemCtx`, the zero user under the `support_tenant` claim.
- **Notification source and copy.** The source re-reads the live, takes its users from the definer, and returns one actor-less intent. The facts are exactly `liveId`, `previewAssetId`, `startsAt` and `title`. The push body reads "Em 15 minutos: {title} começa às {time}", with the time in the tenant's timezone. Tapping the push opens `/cursos/lives/{id}`.
- **Wiring.** The manifest declares `jobs` and `notificationSources`, and the README lists the emitted event, the consumed source, the four new kernel specifiers and the job. ## Decisions Made
The five planning decisions were applied as written and are listed under `key-decisions`.
- **Needs your confirmation: the jobs run as `support_tenant` (planning decision 1).** `coursesSystemCtx` uses the staff claim, so the restrictive live and lesson gates cannot hide product-linked rows from a job. Under `member`, every reminder for a live sold by a product would skip as `not_found`. The trade-off is that a course job's lane can read what staff can read. Today the job only reads ids, instants and titles, and it gets recipients only through the definer. If you prefer `member`, every job read has to go through definers instead.
- **Execution-level choices:** a null access flag on the definer means "check access" (fails closed). The school flag is read through the flag cache before the transaction, but `module_off` is reported after `moved` in the skip order.
