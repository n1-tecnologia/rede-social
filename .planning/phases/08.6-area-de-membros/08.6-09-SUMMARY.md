# Phase 08.6 Plan 09: The rest of Início Summary

**Início now shows four more sections under the hero, all from the one strict-parsed overview read. "Meus cursos" lists up to three courses in the server's cascade, with locked ones shown as a padlocked "Exclusivo" shop window. "Sua trilha" shows the server's automatic track with "Sugerido", "Etapa n de N" and a static dashed hook naming the last locked step. "Novas aulas da semana" is a 240px rail of up to four lessons the member can open. "Explore por categoria" shows 112px tiles that land on the filtered catalogue. A section the server leaves out renders nothing.**
