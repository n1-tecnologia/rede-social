## Decisions Made
The four planning decisions were applied as written. The execution-level readings are listed under `key-decisions`. Two of them call for the reviewer's attention:
- **Track percent is lesson-weighted.** The truth's `floor(done × 100 / total)` is read over the path's live lessons, as in the prototype's `trackProgress`. `total` in the view stays the path's course count, as the UI-SPEC asks.
- **`proximo_da_trilha` is decided before the hero exclusion.** If the hero is already the track's next course, the shelf shows no `proximo_da_trilha` row. This keeps the prototype's push order, and the unit case "the hero being the track's next course" pins it.
