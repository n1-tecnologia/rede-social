## Shared Patterns

- **Tenant tx:** every read/write in `withTenantTx(ctx, tx => …)`; jobs use `coursesSystemCtx(payload.tenantId)` (tenantId is data, RLS still pins).
- **Locks decided in SQL only:** read `lock_reason`/`locked` from definers (`live_locked_ids`, `course_locked_ids_for`); never compare entitlements in TS.
- **Events:** emit after commit with ids/instants only; jobs `flush(ctx)` before return; `enqueueInTx` inside the write tx with `singletonKey`.
- **Logging:** `moduleLogger('module-courses')`, `log.info({ event: 'courses.x', tenantId, userId, requestId, ... }, msg)`; never titles/URLs.
- **Errors:** `throw new ApiError(404, 'NOT_FOUND')` bare for misses/foreign ids.
- **Strings:** pt-BR in `apps/web/messages/pt-BR/*.json`; push bodies server-side in `notification-copy.ts`; `scripts/check-ui-literals.sh` green.
- **Isolation:** every new route → `isolation-inventory.ts` + phase sweep; every policy → pgTAP cross-tenant negative.
