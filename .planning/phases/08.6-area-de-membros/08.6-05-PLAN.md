The live reminder end to end. 1. **The jobs' lane is `support_tenant`, not `member`** (deviation from RESEARCH's `coursesSystemCtx` sketch): under the `member` claim the zero user holds no entitlement, so the restrictive live and lesson gates would hide every product-linked live and lesson and the reminder would skip `not_found`. 2. **Late tolerance 10 minutes** (R-A15), checked after `started` (R-Pitfall 7).

5. **Kinds declared together.** `COURSES_NOTIFICATION_KINDS` holds all six stored strings now (D-448 rates them costly), so plans 06, 07 and 12 extend sources and renderers, never the vocabulary.
