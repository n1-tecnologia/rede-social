# Phase 08.6 Plan 11: The live and its reminder in the member's hands Summary

**Início now shows the live the server chose. A live happening now is a neutral "Ao vivo agora" row right under the hero, with no join link and no reminder. Otherwise the next live is a 16:10 card with a server-decided "Hoje" / "Amanhã" / "Em n dias" pill and the long when-line. Under the card, an open live has the full-width `aria-pressed` reminder toggle, which flips optimistically, toasts, and reverts with the right words on a refusal. A locked live has the 08.5 buy path in outline instead. The same toggle sits under the live page's "Começa em …" card before the window. In the admin's "Ver como aluno" preview, the area page shows the banner between the row and the body, and the toggle is disabled with "Os lembretes não funcionam no modo aluno." on both surfaces. It sends nothing.**

## Accomplishments
- **Views.** `relativeDayLabel` compares tenant-local calendar dates of the start and of `serverNow`. A start at 01:00Z the next UTC day is still "Hoje" at 21:00 in São Paulo. `homeLiveView` words the server's choice. Nothing reads a clock, and no href other than the live page is ever built.
- **Início.** `MemberHomeTab` renders `HomeLive` in slot 3 (live now) or slot 6 (next live). For a locked next live only, and only with the store on, it reads `getLiveAccess` and builds the body. One product gives the outline link to `/loja/{id}?live={liveId}`. Several give the outline button opening the shipped `ProductChoiceSheet`. None for sale gives the 08.5 line. A failed read gives "Ver a live". In preview, the body is the disabled toggle.
- **Toggle.** `ReminderToggle` is a client component used on both surfaces. It guards against a second tap with a ref, sets `aria-busy`, and calls `setLiveReminderAction` (`PUT` on, `DELETE` off). On `live_locked` and `reminder_closed` it toasts and refreshes. On `gone` it toasts and goes to `/cursos?aba=lives`. Any other failure reverts with the on / off failure toast. Its initial state is always the server's.
- **Live page.** `MemberLivePage` reads the new `GET …/reminder` only in the API's `before` state, so `learnLiveSchema` is unchanged (R-A12). `LiveStateZone` draws the toggle under the `CalendarClock` card (`mt-3`).
- **Preview.** `PreviewBanner` gains `backHref` and `className`. The area page shows it with `backHref="/cursos"`. `/cursos/lives/[liveId]?ver=aluno` for an admin renders the member page with the disabled toggle and no banner.
- **E2E.** A new describe, `member area: the live and its reminder`, runs on its own throwaway tenant (`e2e-ml-<project>`, store on) and covers 7 cases plus 2 backstops.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] The live page had no preview branch**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** UI-D-459 and the plan's truth want the live page's toggle disabled in preview. But `page.tsx` sent every manager to the live form, whatever `?ver=` said (the 08.5 rule). So the admin could never reach the member live page, and the preview links on Início and the Lives tab opened the form.
- **Fix:** `page.tsx` reads `searchParams` and, when `isPreview(bootstrap, ver)` holds, renders `MemberLivePage` with `preview`. Without the flag the form is unchanged. A member's or support user's `?ver=` changes nothing.
- **Files modified:** `apps/web/app/(app)/cursos/lives/[liveId]/page.tsx` (not in `files_modified`)
- **Verification:** The e2e admin preview case on both projects, including "without the flag the manager keeps the live form". `courses-admin.spec.ts` stays green.
- **Committed in:** `3d1d28d9`

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The several-products button needs a client file**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** `HomeLive` is a server component, and the sheet's open state needs a client boundary. No listed file could host it cleanly.
- **Fix:** A new `HomeLiveChoice.tsx` (`'use client'`) composes the store's `ProductChoiceSheet` in the app tier (MOD-02).
- **Files modified:** `apps/web/app/(app)/cursos/HomeLiveChoice.tsx` (new, not in `files_modified`)

- **Committed in:** `f828e509`

### Minor execution-level adjustments (no behaviour beyond the plan)
- The live page reads the reminder AFTER the live read, not in parallel. The zone that decides whether to read comes from that same read.
- The read and write readers also return `redirect`, so a session refusal navigates the way every other learn action does.
- In the e2e, the disabled toggle gets a dispatched click. A forced pointer click cannot land on a `pointer-events-none` button on the phone project. The no-write proof also checks that the admin has no DB row.
- The support case turns the tenant's member into `support_tenant` (the role is read at sign-in) instead of adding a fixture helper.
