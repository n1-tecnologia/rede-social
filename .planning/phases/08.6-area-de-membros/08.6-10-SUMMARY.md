# Phase 08.6 Plan 10: Trilhas and Meu progresso in the web Summary

**The member area now has its five tabs: Início, Cursos, Trilhas, Lives and Progresso. Trilhas shows one track at a time, picked by `?trilha=` from the server's own tracks. Each track has a 16:10 hero with "Sua trilha" or "Sugerido", "Etapa n de N", the line and one CTA. "O caminho" lists every course with its state in words and in a 28px disc, and every step is a link, a locked one landing on its buy path. "Outras trilhas" swaps the hero by link. Progresso shows the 88px ring with the server's percent, an encouraging start card when nothing is done, four ported `StatBlock` numbers ("1,5 h", "1 dia") and the started courses. Every number is the server's.**

## Accomplishments
- **Tabs.** `COURSE_MEMBER_TABS` now reads `inicio, cursos, trilhas, lives, progresso`. `MemberCatalogue` sends each new key to its body, each in its own keyed `Suspense` with its own skeleton. `?trilha=` goes from `page.tsx` through a new prop, and the manager's hub ignores it.
- **Views.** `selectedTrackId` matches the param only against the answer's ids, so a foreign, unknown or repeated value falls back to the default (T-08.6-20). `trackTabView` builds the pill, the eyebrow, the bar, the line and the CTA, with complete keyed on `coursesDone`. `trackStepView` gives the two-digit step and the feminine state words (A-UI-11), and a locked step still gets `/cursos/{id}`. `otherTrackView` builds the `?aba=trilhas&trilha={id}` link. `formatWatched` and `progressTabView` only format. No view counts a day, a lesson or a second.
- **Components.** `MemberTracksTab` has the hero, `TrackTimeline` and "Outras trilhas", plus the empty and error cards. `TrackTimeline` has no animation (UI-D-463). `MemberProgressTab` has the ring card, the empty card, the `<dl>` of four `StatBlock`s and "Curso a curso". Nothing else from the prototype is ported, and no placeholder stands in for it.
- **Ports.** `StatBlock` puts a `<dt>` row (neutral 16px glyph, 12px truncating label) before the `<dd>` value (24/700, one line). `ProgressRing` gains `label` and `tone`, and without them it renders exactly as before.
- **E2E.** A new `member area: Trilhas and Progresso` describe runs on its own throwaway tenant (`e2e-mt-<project>`, store on). It covers the five-tab order and bodies, the Progresso empty card, the suggested track turning into "Sua trilha" after a completion, the locked step reaching "Comprar para liberar", "Outras trilhas" and an unknown `?trilha=`, the full numbers after two completions, and the three 320px backstops.
