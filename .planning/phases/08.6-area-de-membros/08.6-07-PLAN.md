<planning_decisions>
1. **A watermark, not a time window** (R-Pitfall 6): `course_lessons.announced_at` makes each lesson announced exactly once; a lesson whose enqueue was absorbed while its transaction straddled the claim is caught by the next batch.
2. **Ready videos only, re-armed for 24 hours** (R-A5, adopted): "5 aulas novas" never counts a lesson whose video still processes; after 24 hours an unready lesson is left unannounced (its course page still lists it).
3. **An exact 24-hour stamp** (`published_notified_at`), not a day-bucketed dedupe key (R-Alternatives): `published_at` cannot serve because it is kept through unpublish.
4. **One retraction by subject** covers both kinds whose subject is the course; live kinds use subject `live` and are untouched.
5. **Accepted residual (D-454 × 24-hour dedupe):** unpublish then republish within 24 hours leaves the earlier notice marked removed and sends no new one; the course is reachable from the catalogue. Surfaced in `<flagged_assumptions>`.
</planning_decisions>
