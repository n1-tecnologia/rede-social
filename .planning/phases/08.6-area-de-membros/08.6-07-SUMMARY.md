## Decisions Made
- Planning decisions 1-5 were applied as written (see `key-decisions`).
- **No course lock in `createLesson`.** It reads the course status with a plain select. A `for share` lock would take the course after the module, while `reorderModules` locks the course and then updates modules, which is a deadlock cycle. A lesson that races a publish is caught by the next lesson's batch, which is the watermark residual the plan accepts.
- **No `for share` in the course sources.** The zero-user `support_tenant` lane has no update policy on `courses`, and a row-locking clause applies update policies, so the lock would hide the course. The publication fan-out runs before the unpublish retraction because the status is re-read and the fan-out queue is serial (`localConcurrency` 1, one worker). This is documented in the source's docblock.
- **Dedupe keys use epoch seconds**, as the plan's `notifiedAtEpoch` / `batchAtEpoch` say. Two integration cases compress a day or a window into milliseconds, so they wait until the previous stamp's second has passed.
