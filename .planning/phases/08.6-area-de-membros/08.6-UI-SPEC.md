# Phase 08.6 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Scope.** This phase turns the member side of the school (08.5) into a member area. It adds these member surfaces:
- the **area row** of `/cursos` with five items: **Início · Cursos · Trilhas · Lives · Progresso**;
- **Início** (bare `/cursos`): greeting and study streak, the hero ("Continue de onde parou" / "Comece por aqui" / "Você está em dia"), "Meus cursos", the member's track, the live happening now or the next live with its reminder, "Novas aulas da semana" and "Explore por categoria";
- **Trilhas** (`?aba=trilhas`): one track at a time with its path of courses and their states, plus the other tracks;
- **Progresso** (`?aba=progresso`): the overall ring, the four numbers, and progress course by course;
- the **reminder toggle** on the 08.5 live page;
- **six notification kinds** in the bell, plus their Web Push banners.

It also changes these admin surfaces:
- the admin preview (`?ver=aluno`) now lands on Início, with the preview banner on the area page;
- the live form's cancelled state gains **"Reativar live"** (D-452), and the cancel dialog's body is reworded, because opted-in members are now notified (D-447).

**Design authority.** `reference/frontend-design/` draws three of these screens, and they are ported, not redrawn (PROTOTYPE.md: components are ported into module packages, never refactored in place):
- Início, from `app/membros/page.tsx`;
- Trilhas, from `app/membros/trilhas/page.tsx`;
- Meu progresso, from `app/membros/progresso/page.tsx`.

The area row follows `components/layout/AreaTabs.tsx` and `lib/nav.ts` `getAreaTabs` (five items, this order, hidden on detail routes). `StatBlock` is ported from `components/members/StatBlock.tsx`. The derivations follow `lib/members-progress.ts` (`heroResume`, `courseShelf`, `activeTrack`, `trackProgress`, `overallStats`), and the server is the authority (D-440, CONTEXT Discretion "Data"). The web renders and never derives.

**Porting deltas.** These prototype habits are replaced by decisions that are already locked or carried from 08.5:
- **"Premium" becomes the store's padlock.** "PREMIUM" in the track step, "Premium · N aulas" on the shelf, the grayscale covers and `pill-gold` all become the 08.2 / 08.5 padlock language: "Exclusivo" in words, `Lock` 12, and the cover in colour (D-357, D-421).
- **No red "Ao vivo".** The prototype's red strip and its pulsing dot become a neutral row with the over-media style pill and a `Radio` glyph. Red is reserved for destructive actions (08.5 UI-D-434, sketch 010 question (g)).
- **No gold and no `btn-gold`.** Every CTA is `buttonClasses`, and every tint is `bg-brand/10` (PROTOTYPE.md §2, token rename, `check-ui-literals.sh`).
- **Not ported:**
  - the attendee counts ("6.300 alunos assistindo agora", "alunos confirmados"), because there is no data;
  - the category icons, because categories carry no icon;
  - the track "audience" pill, because there is no column;
  - the track finish card and the Progresso certificates block, because certificates are deferred;
  - "Conquistas";
  - "Simular assinatura Premium" and "Zerar progresso", which are prototype-only.

**Prototype-less surfaces.** These go through the sketch gate (D-365, D-417), `.planning/sketches/011-phase-08.6-designed-screens`. Each is named so the planner can schedule it:
1. the six notification rows (glyph, leading, sentence, deep link) and the six push banners;
2. Início's **empty school** and the **hidden-section** behaviour (a section with no data is absent, title included);
3. the **live-now row** on Início (the prototype's red strip, redrawn neutral);
4. the **locked variant of the next-live card** ("Comprar para liberar" instead of the toggle, plus the not-for-sale and store-failure lines);
5. the **reminder toggle on the 08.5 live page** (state zone "before the window");
6. the **admin-preview disabled toggle** and its hint, plus the **preview banner on the area page**;
7. **Trilhas with several tracks** (`?trilha=`, "Outras trilhas") and the **"bloqueada" step**;
8. **Progresso** without the certificates, "Conquistas" and demo blocks, and its **empty** card;
9. the **"Reativar live"** admin control, its past-start note and its dialog, plus the reworded cancel dialog;
10. the **loading and error states** of the three new tabs;
11. the **area-row style choice** (UI-D-446): one comparison frame, underline versus pills, so the user can overturn it at the gate.

§ Sketch gate lists the design question and the frames.

**Inherited design language.** The design language, spacing scale, type scale and colour model are **inherited unchanged** from the approved `02`–`08.5-UI-SPEC.md`. This document restates them for the checker and records only the Phase 08.6 deltas.

**Upstream decisions.**
- `08.6-CONTEXT.md` decisions are cited as **D-435..D-454** and its Claude's Discretion items by name. The roadmap criteria are **SC 1..6**. None is re-litigated.
- `08.6-RESEARCH.md` is cited as **R-Pattern n** / **R-Pitfall n** / **R-A n** / **R-OQ n**. Its draft requirements are **COURSE-28..COURSE-52**.
- Earlier visual decisions keep their numbers: UI-D-203, UI-D-211, UI-D-214, UI-D-251, UI-D-266, UI-D-288, UI-D-386, UI-D-390..UI-D-392, UI-D-403..UI-D-405, UI-D-409, UI-D-410, and 08.5's UI-D-423..UI-D-445.
- The UI questions this phase opens are decided below as **UI-D-446..UI-D-465**. They continue after 08.5's UI-D-445, the highest id in any UI-SPEC (checked 2026-10-16 with `grep -rhoE "UI-D-[0-9]+" .planning | sort -t- -k3 -n -u | tail -1` → `UI-D-445`).

**Research open questions settled here.**
1. **R-OQ 3, the area-row style.** The member row stays the shipped underline `Tabs` (through `HubTabs`) with five items, in a compact density. It does not become the prototype's pill `AreaTabs` (UI-D-446). The rationale is in the decision row. The sketch carries a comparison frame, so the user can overturn it with one line at the gate.
2. **R-OQ 5, the overview payload (the UI side).** Início renders from **one** overview read, inside **one** `Suspense` with one skeleton (UI-D-447). The page does not stream section by section. A per-section split stays a planner option only if it keeps one skeleton.
3. **R-A10, the member track detail.** There is no member `/cursos/trilhas/[trackId]`. The Trilhas tab shows one track at a time, chosen by `?trilha=` (UI-D-456).
4. **R-A11, the Progresso ring.** The prototype's overall ring stays (percent of the live lessons of every published course, locked ones included). "Conquistas" and the certificates are dropped with no placeholder (UI-D-457).
5. **R-Pattern 9, the course page's back link.** It stays `/cursos` "Voltar para Cursos", which now lands on Início, the area's home. That is the same place the BottomNav "Cursos" tab opens (UI-D-446).

**Assumptions made in this session.** This is a non-interactive session. Each assumption is stated here instead of being asked, and the planner may overturn any of them with one line:
- **A-UI-8.** On Início, the locked next live's "Comprar para liberar" is `outline`, not brand. Início's one brand fill is the hero CTA (the one-brand-fill rule). The live page keeps its brand buy section.
- **A-UI-9.** The reminder toggle is the prototype's full-width toggle **button** (`aria-pressed`), not a `Switch` row. The prototype draws it, it is a 44px target with words, and it matches the live page's full-width state zone.
- **A-UI-10.** Notices from a person carry the actor: course published, and live scheduled or reactivated (the events precedent, "**{actor}** criou o evento"). The other four are actor-less (the reminder precedent, UI-D-251).
- **A-UI-11.** The track step's state word agrees with "etapa" (feminine): "Concluída", "Em andamento", "A seguir", "Bloqueada" (D-439's four words). The course inside a bloqueada step still says "Exclusivo" (masculine, D-421).
- **A-UI-12.** "Horas assistidas" renders "{m} min" under one hour and "{h} h" or "{h},{d} h" (one decimal, pt-BR comma) from one hour up. That keeps the value inside a 2-column cell at 320px.
- **A-UI-13.** The publish dialog is not reworded. Only the cancel-live dialog changes, because only that body became false ("Ninguém é avisado automaticamente."). The republish 24-hour dedupe makes any promise in the publish dialog inexact.

**Interaction truths outside the 8-category state taxonomy.** The planner lifts these as plain truths, and they are listed here so they are not lost:
- **E02, E11:** the streak and every number come from the server's answer. The web never counts days, lessons or seconds, and no request parameter influences them (D-440).
- **E03–E09:** the web never decides a lock, a hero state, a shelf kind or a track. It renders the server's fields (R-Pattern 2). No locked lesson title appears in "Novas aulas" (COURSE-33).
- **E06, E07:** Início never contains a live's `externalUrl`. The toggle's initial state is the server's, and the web keeps no local reminder store.
- **E06:** the relative-day pill ("Hoje", "Amanhã", "Em n dias") is decided on the server in the tenant timezone. There is no `Date.now()` in render (UI-D-288).
- **E12:** no sentence or push names a lesson, and no notice to a member without access carries more than the course or live title (D-449).
- **E01:** every member link to the catalogue carries `aba=cursos`, every preview link keeps `ver=aluno`, and bare `/cursos` means Início.

**Contract requirements this document places on the API** (for the planner; each is a projection of R-Pattern 1–3 and COURSE-29..40, not a new rule):
- **Overview:**
  - `streakDays`;
  - `hero { state, courseId, courseTitle, courseTagline, coverAssetId, lessonId \| null, lessonTitle, lessonDurationSeconds, moduleNumber, moduleTitle, lessonNumber, moduleLessonsLeft, isFree, percent, done, total, lessonCount, durationSeconds }`;
  - `shelf[] { courseId, title, coverAssetId, kind, percent, done, total, lessonCount, durationSeconds }`;
  - `track { id, title, coverAssetId, suggested, percent, total, coursesDone, currentIndex, currentCourseTitle, resumeCourseId, lastLockedCourseTitle \| null }`;
  - `live { id, title, coverAssetId, startsAt, durationMin, status: 'live' \| 'scheduled', locked, reminderOn }` plus `serverNow`;
  - `newLessons[] { lessonId, courseId, courseTitle, title, durationSeconds, coverAssetId, done }`;
  - `categories[] { id, name, count, coverAssetId }`.
- **Tracks:** `tracks[] { id, title, coverAssetId, percent, coursesDone, total, currentIndex, lessonCount, durationSeconds, resumeCourseId, mine, suggested, courses[] { id, title, coverAssetId, state, locked, lessonCount, durationSeconds, done, percent } }`.
- **Progress:** `{ streakDays, lessonsDone, lessonsTotal, secondsWatched, coursesDone, courses[] { id, title, coverAssetId, percent, done, total, locked } }`. **`lessonsTotal`** (the live lessons of every published course, locked included) is a field the ring needs beyond the COURSE-38 draft.
- **Reminder:** `{ on }` on GET, PUT and DELETE (COURSE-40).
- **Live-scheduled facts** may carry `reactivated: true` for the reactivation sentence (UI-D-460).
- **Live form read** (admin): whether a cancelled live's start is still ahead, decided on the server, for UI-D-462.

If the planner drops a field, the surface omits that piece rather than estimating it.
