**The owner's Atendimento builder now counts support cases instead of conversations and turns: an active-case queue, one first-response sample per case, cases opened by category over 30 days (`bySubject`), payload version 9, with the overview alert and the member's support line on the same rules.**

- `app.dashboard_area_support` (final definition in `20261117092952_dashboard_cases_first_response.sql`): `awaiting` counts `open` and `in_progress` cases whose last message is the member's and unread; the first-response median holds one sample per case (the first staff message after the case's first member message, bucketed by the week of that message); `weeklyConversations` counts cases opened per week; the new `bySubject` has the five exact keys over Window30 (`from30_at` to the end of D, tenant timezone), counts only.
