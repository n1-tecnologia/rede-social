# Phase 12.4 manual proofs (P1 to P22)

This file is the ledger of the proofs only the owner can run: the first real phone walk of a support case (open,
answer, take, resolve with the bell row and a real push, reopen by reply, close, open a new case), the read-only
counts and the release-day view on a hosted project, the 7-day close observed on homolog, the browser run of
`chat-cases.spec.ts`, the held-out visual checks, the screen-reader walk, the homolog deploy and every production
step, the full `pnpm verify` (it resets and seeds the local database, so it is his), and the reading of the
assumptions, defaults and wording the plans left to him. Everything an agent could build is built and proved on the
local stack (plans 01 to 17, 19 and 20: pgTAP, integration, unit and component tests, and a Playwright spec that is
written and listed but was never run); what is left here cannot be faked, so nothing in this file is ever recorded
as done by an agent.

**The rule.** Every row ships `pending`. A row becomes `passed` only when Igor pastes the evidence of its steps and
the date, in the row, himself. An agent never edits a Status line and never writes evidence it did not see. A phase
whose ledger has a `pending` row closes `human_needed`, never green. (Project memory: a blanket UAT pass is checked
against `docs/DEPLOY.md` before it is recorded, and a phone cannot reach `*.localhost`, so a row that needs a phone
needs a homolog or production host.)

**The evidence rule.** Paste shapes, counts, statuses, HTTP codes, exit codes and the words the screens show.
**Never** a key, a password, a token, a connection string, a webhook token, an e-mail address of a member or a
project ref. Replace a ref by `<ref>` and a host by `<host>` in what you paste; name a tenant "tenant 1", "tenant 2".

**The procedures** are in `docs/DEPLOY.md`, "Phase 12.4 release (support cases)".

**The design approval is yours.** Sketch `023` (the design gate) carries `approved: true`, kind **provisional**,
recorded on your word "aprovado" of 2026-11-16 in the session, not by an executor, with no line-by-line answers. It was
read as "keep every default as drawn": the four CONTEXT defaults A-UI-1 to A-UI-4 and the contract's own A-UI-5 to
A-UI-28. It stays provisional until you walk the built screens (rows P6 to P8 and P11).

## P1 - The first real phone walk of a case (CASE-28)

- **Proves:** that a case lives end to end on a real device against a hosted tenant: the opener, the case bar with its
  number and pill, the team's take and reply, the resolve with the bell row and a REAL push on the phone, the reopen by
  the member's reply, the close, and the next message opening the next number. Every push of the repository was proved
  with the fake sink; the real browser push is what is unproven. The browser run of the same path
  (`chat-cases.spec.ts`, row P12) cannot reach `*.localhost` from a phone, so this row is the only real-device proof.
  CASE-28 had no probe category (EP-096): a reviewer should confirm none is missing.
- **Needs:** a homolog or production tenant with the `chat` module on, a member account that installed the app and
  allowed notifications, an `admin_tenant` account and a `support_tenant` account.
- **Steps:**
  1. As the member, open `/suporte` with no open case: the opener shows five chips (Outro selected) and the optional
     subject. Pick a chip, type a subject and a message, send. The bar shows `Chamado #N`, the pill and the subject, and
     the one-time row "Chamado #N aberto." shows.
  2. As the support account, open the inbox: the case is under Em aberto with the member's name, the subject and the
     category. Take it: the pill becomes Em atendimento. Reply. As the member, read the reply live and the push on the phone.
  3. Resolve the case as the team. As the member: the bell row for the resolved case, the push, the notice strip above
     the composer, still enabled.
  4. As the member, reply to the resolved case: it reopens (Aberto, no owner), the strip leaves, a toast says the case
     was reopened. The team gets a push (every live staff member, since the owner was cleared).
  5. Close the case as the team. As the member: the read-only strip with Novo chamado. Tap it: the opener. Send a
     message: the next number opens.
- **Evidence to paste:** the device, the browser, the words the screens showed at each step, the number of the first
  and of the next case, whether the push arrived and how long it took.
- **Status:** pending

## P3 - The per-state counts and the number range per tenant

- **Proves:** that the backfill numbered every support conversation 1..N per tenant and gave every one a state.
- **Steps:** after the migration, on every project, the read-only SQL of "After the migration: read-only counts"
  in `docs/DEPLOY.md`. The per-state counts are read in the order open, in_progress, resolved, closed. Right after the
  migration in_progress and resolved are 0 on every project. For each tenant `max(case_number)` equals the number of
  support conversations (the pair is printed for each tenant). One off in either direction is a defect, and the
  query F prints 0. A project or tenant with no support conversation records 0 and passes; nothing is counted for a
  tenant with chat off. A tenant whose members were all answered ends with every migrated case Fechado and none Aberto:
  that is expected, not a defect.
- **Evidence to paste:** per project, the four counts in that order and, per tenant labelled "tenant 1", "tenant 2",
  the pair (max number, count). Never a tenant id.
- **Status:** pending

## P18 - The planner assumptions and wording to confirm

- **A-12.4-WINDOW:** in the seconds between the functions file and the activation file, a conversation inserted by the
  previous API is numbered by the new trigger before the backfill numbers the older ones; harmless, a cosmetic ordering
  of one row.
- **A-12.4-ACTOR-GONE:** the history sentence for an event whose actor left reads "Alguém da equipe"; the roster failure line reads
  "Não foi possível carregar a equipe." The two history hints (the member did it, the actor is gone) come from
  heuristics over the API row, which cannot tell a manual close of a resolved case by a departed person from the
  automatic close, nor a team member who shares the member's display name; their wording is yours to overturn.

  opening the awaiting view. You may overturn any of these.

- **D-841:** a case the member reopens loses its owner, so the team notice goes to every live staff member; the team's
