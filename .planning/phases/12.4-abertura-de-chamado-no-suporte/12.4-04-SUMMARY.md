## Decisions Made

- The close goes through `app.chat_case_signal` (three topics: conversation, support inbox, member) rather than the two `realtime.send` calls the research sketch spelled. The plan names two topics; the helper is a superset, keeps all `chat.case` topics defined in one place, and lets the member's other devices refetch counters. It still publishes only ids and writes no notification, so D-808 holds.
- Residue in the local database (an old resolved case of an earlier run) would be closed by any sweep call and skew counts, so pgTAP section 12 first moves such rows to `now()` inside the rolled-back transaction, and the integration race block drains once before it asserts a return of 0, which is what the hourly job does anyway.
