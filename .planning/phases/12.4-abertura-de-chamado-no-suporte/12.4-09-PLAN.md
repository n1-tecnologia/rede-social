## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| API emit to worker | ids-only events cross the sink; the worker re-reads through the tenant lane |
| notice recipients | the member only, the team only, or the assignee only |
| Realtime topic subscription | the authoriser admits a participant or staff of the same tenant |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| T-12.4-41 | Information disclosure | a bell row or push leaks a subject, a message text or a staff name | high | mitigate | facts are `{ caseId, number }`; the member push body is a fixed sentence; asserted with `toEqual` |
| T-12.4-42 | Information disclosure | a notice reaches another tenant's user | high | mitigate | audience queries pin `tenant_id` in the worker lane; the cross-tenant test with a positive control |
| T-12.4-43 | Tampering | a stale or duplicate notice after a reopen | medium | mitigate | the resolver compares the case's current `resolved_at` with the event's; dedupe key carries it |
| T-12.4-44 | Information disclosure | an old case's Realtime topic readable by another member | high | mitigate | the authoriser is unchanged (participant or staff); integration cases for cross-member and cross-tenant subscribes |
| T-12.4-45 | Denial of service | a flood of team pushes for one case | low | accept | one device tag per case replaces older banners; the shipped renotify and TTL rules apply; no new fan-out beyond the shipped one |
</threat_model>
