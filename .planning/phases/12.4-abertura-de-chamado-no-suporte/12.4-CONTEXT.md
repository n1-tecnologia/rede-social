# Phase 12.4: Abertura de chamado no suporte - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-16
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A member's message to the tenant's support opens a case (a "chamado") for the team, who take it, answer it, resolve it and close it. Today the support chat is one conversation per member, forever, with no status, no owner and no closing (D-221 and D-225 of Phase 7). This phase reverses that on purpose for `kind = 'support'` only, and delivers the database, the API and the screens: a numbered case with a state, an owner, a subject, a category and a priority; an inbox for the team with tabs by state and the case actions; the member's case number and state; the notices; the existing conversations turned into cases; and the owner's "Atendimento" panel read per case.

It reuses what Phase 7 built: the conversation, the messages with their gapless `seq`, Realtime topics, the unread badge and the push to the support staff. Direct messages between members (Phase 11, `kind = 'direct'`) are not touched.

Chosen by Igor on 2026-11-16 as the next delivery of the "Cronograma Social e App" (its "Chamados no suporte" section), ahead of Phase 11 and the buyer-permissions delivery. It runs before Phase 13.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The case model (the biggest change)
- **D-800:** This phase formally reverses D-221 (no resolve/close status, one thread per member forever) and the "nothing is assigned" half of D-225 for the support kind. The other half of D-225 stays: any staff member may reply to any case, whoever owns it. Phase 7 itself listed both as "revisit if a pilot tenant's support volume demands it"; Igor chose to do it now. `kind = 'direct'` and `kind = 'group'` conversations keep every rule they have today.
- **D-801:** A member has several cases over time. Each case has its own number, its own beginning and end and its own list of messages. When the member writes after their case is `Fechado`, a new case opens; the old one stays as history. — **Reversibility:** one-way — once a member has two cases the model cannot go back to one conversation per member without merging message sequences, and the database loses the unique index per member; the new partial unique index of D-802 replaces it.
- **D-802:** One active case per member at a time. "Active" means not `Fechado`. Every message from the member goes to that case. The database guarantees it with a partial unique index (tenant, member) where the kind is support and the state is not `Fechado`, in place of `chat_conversations_one_support_per_member`. The lazy creation keeps the `insert … on conflict do nothing` shape of `sendSupportMessage` so the 20-way concurrency test still holds, now against the active-case index.
- **D-803:** What the member sees. The chat bubble in the top bar opens the active case directly, or, when there is none, an empty composer that creates the case on the first message. There is no "Ajuda" hub (D-220 stays). The member's earlier cases are reached through a "Meus chamados" link inside the conversation, newest first, each opening that case read-only when it is `Fechado`.
- **D-804:** The case number is sequential per tenant, starting at 1, shown as `#42` ("Chamado #42"). A per-tenant counter is bumped safely when two cases open together. The member sees only their own numbers. — **Reversibility:** costly — the number is shown to members and staff and quoted in notices, so renumbering later breaks references.

### States, owner and reopening
- **D-805:** Four states, with these labels in pt-BR: `Aberto` (nobody took it), `Em atendimento` (a staff member took or was given it), `Resolvido` (the team thinks it is over, the member can still answer) and `Fechado` (over; the next message from the member opens another case). A new case is born `Aberto`. Resolving works from `Aberto` and from `Em atendimento`. Closing works from any state. A member message on a `Resolvido` case moves it back to `Aberto` in the same case and tells the team (D-809). A member message on a `Fechado` case never touches it (D-801).
- **D-806:** Any `support_tenant` or `admin_tenant` can take a case for themselves ("Assumir"). Only `admin_tenant` assigns a case to another person. Anyone on the staff can reply regardless of who owns the case. A case is owned by at most one person; a case with no owner is `Aberto`.
- **D-807:** Closing. The team has a "Fechar" action. A background job also closes, by itself, every case that has stayed `Resolvido` for 7 days without a message from the member. The 7 days are fixed in code in this phase, not a tenant setting. The job runs in the existing worker queue (`apps/api/src/worker.ts`, pg-boss), idempotent, and writes the same history row as a manual close with no actor.
- **D-808:** The member's notice. Only when a case becomes `Resolvido`: a push and a row in the bell ("Seu chamado #N foi resolvido"). Closing, by hand or by the job, is silent; the conversation shows the state. This is the first chat entry in the bell, so it needs a renderer in `apps/web/lib/notification-renderers.tsx`, a catalog entry and a kind in `CHAT_NOTIFICATION_KINDS`; today chat kinds are push-only.
- **D-809:** The team's notices. A new case, a case assigned to you and a reopened case notify the team as the cronograma describes. When the member writes in a case that has an owner, only the owner gets the push; with no owner, every active staff member does, as today. The numeric badge of the team (D-238) keeps counting all cases that await the staff (last message from the member, unread by the staff under the shared read state), whoever owns them.
- **D-810:** History. Every step (opened, taken, assigned, resolved, closed, reopened, a field changed) is a row in a table of case events of its own, with who did it, when and from which state to which. It is append-only and tenant-scoped.

### Fields
- **D-811:** A case has a subject (short text), a category and a priority, besides its number, state and owner. The categories are a fixed list in code, from the design prototype: `Check-in`, `Eventos`, `Pagamento`, `Conta`, `Outro`. The priority is `Baixa`, `Normal` or `Alta`. No attachments in this phase.
- **D-812:** Who fills them. Above the composer of the first message of a new case, the member taps a category (`Outro` is preselected) and may type a subject. With no subject, the case takes the start of the first message as its subject (one line, trimmed, the length is the planner's call). The priority is born `Normal` and only the staff change it. The staff can also correct the category and the subject. The member cannot change any field after the case exists, and the picker is not shown again for replies.
- **D-813:** The category is what feeds the owner's "Atendimentos por assunto" card, which is "Em breve" today (`bySubject: { measured: false, reason: 'needs_support_subject' }` in `packages/modules/dashboard/contracts/support.ts`).

### Existing conversations and the owner's panel
- **D-814:** Migration of what exists. Each existing support conversation becomes the case of its member, keeping all its messages and its id. Cases are numbered per tenant by the date of the first message (ties broken by id). A case is `Aberto` when its last message is from the member and the staff has not read it, and `Fechado` otherwise. Category `Outro`, priority `Normal`, no subject (the list falls back to the preview of the first message). The e2e seed conversation id `1d000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000001` must stay a valid case. The release is expand-only, like the previous phases' migrations. — **Reversibility:** costly — the backfill renumbers nothing that was shown before, but the states it assigns drive the first day's inbox and counters.
- **D-815:** The owner's "Atendimento" panel keeps its numbers, now counted per case. "Awaiting a reply" counts active cases whose last message is from the member and unread by the team. First-response time is per case (the first staff message after the case's first member message), replacing today's per-turn rule in `app.dashboard_area_support`. Cases per week and cases answered per staff member (names only) stay. "Em aberto" and the overview alert read the state (`Aberto` plus `Em atendimento`). "Atendimentos por assunto" leaves "Em breve" and counts by category. New numbers (cases by state, time to resolve) are deferred.

### Carried forward from earlier phases (informational)
- **D-220:** [informational] The member's chat bubble opens the thread directly; no "Ajuda" hub. Kept by D-803.
- **D-222:** [informational] Attribution on the member side: tenant name and logo in the header, the staff member's first name on each staff bubble, no staff avatars.
- **D-223:** [informational] `admin_tenant` also answers support through `chat.support`; staff are never offered a support case of their own (`staff_has_inbox`).
- **D-224:** [informational] Staff reach the inbox from the same top-bar slot; list on mobile, list plus thread on the desktop rail; inbox row with name, avatar, preview, relative time and unread indicator; staff thread header links to the member profile; no side panel.
- **D-237:** [informational] The member's chat badge is a dot (a staff message above the member's read mark).
- **D-238:** [informational] The staff chat badge is a number, kept by D-809.
- **D-465:** [informational] The `subject` column on conversations was never written; this phase gives it meaning (to be confirmed by the researcher against `chat_conversations.subject`).
- **Message body rules of Phase 7:** [informational] plain text, 1 to 2000 characters after trim, line breaks kept, URLs auto-linked, no markdown, no link preview. Unchanged.

### Claude's Discretion
- The shape of the data: whether a case is the conversation row itself with a state, an owner, a number and the fields added to it, or a case table that owns a conversation. The constraint is that each case keeps its own gapless `seq`, its own read marks and its own Realtime topic, so the team's shared read mark (`staff_last_read_seq`) and the member's mark stay per case.
- The requirement prefix and numbering for this phase (`CASE-01..NN` is suggested), and the next D-NN / UI-D-NN numbers after D-815.
- The tabs and filters of the team's inbox (for example `Abertos`, `Meus`, `Resolvidos`, `Fechados`, a default tab), the pt-BR copy of every state and notice, the length of the derived subject, and the layout of "Meus chamados". The design is settled in the UI-SPEC and the sketch gate.
- Realtime events for a state change (a new `chat.*` event on the existing topics or a refetch trigger), the worker job name of the auto-close, the case-events table's column names, the counters' implementation.
- The API surface (routes, error codes in `CHAT_ISSUES`, the closed Zod contracts) and the exact column grants and UPDATE policies for the staff-writable columns (state, owner, category, subject, priority), which `20261001004638_chat_rls_grants.sql` does not allow today.

### Defaults assumed, not asked (confirm or overturn at the UI-SPEC gate)
*Confirmed by Igor on 2026-11-16 at the sketch 023 gate (his word "aprovado", kind provisional): all four stand as written.*
- A staff reply to an `Aberto` case that has no owner takes the case for the replier and moves it to `Em atendimento`, so a case does not stay "Aberto" after being answered.
- "Reabrir" by the team works on `Resolvido` only; `Fechado` is final, as D-801 says.
- Everything stays under the existing `chat` module key. No new module toggle; with `chat` off, the cases, the notices and the owner's areas are off as today.
- Staff cannot be the member of a case; a blocked or removed member keeps getting `member_blocked` and `member_removed` from the reply route.

## Existing Code Insights

The repo has no `packages/db` and no `apps/worker`: the chat schema is `packages/modules/chat/db/schema.ts` and the worker entry is `apps/api/src/worker.ts`. The web app does not use React Query: it uses server components, server actions, plain `fetch` BFF routes and the `useRealtimeTopic` hook.

### Reusable Assets
- `sendSupportMessage` already creates the conversation lazily in one transaction with `on conflict do nothing` and then inserts the participant and the message; the case creation extends it.
- The gapless per-conversation `seq`, `last_staff_seq`, `staff_last_read_seq` and `last_message_side` give the awaiting count and the read marks per conversation; a case that owns its conversation keeps them.
- `packages/modules/chat/server/notifications.ts` and the sink to the worker's `notifications.fanout` and `notifications.push-send` carry the push; the new notices are new sources on the same path.
- `packages/core/ui/realtime/useRealtimeTopic.ts` and the `support-inbox` and conversation topics already refresh the inbox and the thread; `LiveShell.tsx` refetches the counters on chat events.
- `apps/web/components/dashboard/OwnerSupport.tsx` already has a `ComingSoonBlock` for "Atendimentos por assunto" to replace with data.
- pg-boss in `apps/api/src/worker.ts` for the auto-close job.

### Established Patterns
- Permissions are checked by permission, never by role: `chat.support` for the staff, `chat.support.contact` for the member (`bootstrap.permissions.includes(...)`).
- Staff are inlined as `app.tenant_role() in ('admin_tenant','support_tenant')` in four policies and the Realtime function, pinned by a drift test; a new policy or function must keep the literal identical.
- Messages are append-only and nobody can delete (`deleted_at` is read but never written).
- Every contract is `.strict()`; refusal codes are a closed list (`CHAT_ISSUES`).
- Migrations are expand-only with release notes; every new table has RLS, a tenant-isolation pgTAP and an `isolation-inventory.ts` entry; the web ships a route inventory and an e2e affected-map entry.
- The sketch gate: a web plan carries `grep -q '^approved: true'` on the phase's sketch README, and only Igor writes the approval.

### Integration Points
- `chat_conversations` and its triggers (state, owner and number live here or in a case table that owns the conversation).
- `listInbox` and `chatCounters` (filters by state and owner, the awaiting count, the member's active case).
- `app.dashboard_area_support` and the dashboard builder (the per-case rules of D-815) and `OwnerSupport.tsx`.
- `apps/web/lib/notification-renderers.tsx` and `CHAT_NOTIFICATION_KINDS` for the first chat bell row.
- `apps/api/src/worker.ts` for the 7-day auto-close.
- The e2e seed (`scripts/seed.ts:1701`) and `chat-admin.ts` that hard-code the seed conversation id.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- "Seu chamado #42 foi resolvido" as the bell and push copy; the case shown as "Chamado #42" in both sides.
- The prototype's "Suas conversas" and "Concluídas" split is the model for "Meus chamados"; its "Marcar como resolvido" confirm dialog is the model for the team's "Resolver" and "Fechar".
- The number must be easy to say aloud ("chamado 42"), which is why it is sequential per tenant and not a random code.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- Image attachments in a case (up to 4 in the prototype): a new capability for the chat (size, type, privacy, rendering), its own phase.
- Categories editable per tenant (the list is fixed in code here).
- Priority chosen by the member (the prototype lets the member pick; here only the staff set it).
- The owner's new numbers: cases by state and time to resolve.
- A tenant setting for the auto-close period (fixed at 7 days here).
- The member reopening a `Fechado` case, and the staff reopening a `Fechado` case.
- Linking a case to an event (the prototype shows an event picker for the category "Eventos").
- Satisfaction rating, SLA targets and breach alerts, e-mail or WhatsApp notices for cases (NOTIF-04 adapters).
- Merging two cases of the same member.
