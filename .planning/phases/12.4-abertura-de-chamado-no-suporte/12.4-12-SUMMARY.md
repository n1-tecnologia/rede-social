# Phase 12.4 Plan 12: The member's thread with cases Summary

**A member now opens a support case from the thread with a category and an optional subject, sees the case bar with its real number and state follow the server live (Resolvido strip, reopening reply, Fechado read-only strip) with polite announcements and a stable scroll, and reads any of their own past cases at /suporte/{id}.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 14 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-17T11:09Z
- **Completed:** 2026-11-17T11:24Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 15 (9 created, 6 modified)

## Accomplishments

- The opener (UI-D-819) lives inside the scroller under the greeting: the caption "Sobre o que é?", the five chips in a named group (`Outro` preselected, always exactly one pressed, wrapping, never sideways), the "Assunto (opcional)" field (16px, 80 characters, no counter). The composer reads "Abrir chamado" and "Conte o que você precisa" for this one state through its existing props. A failed first send keeps the draft, the chip and the subject; the optimistic "Enviando…" bubble shows while the opener stays.
- The first send (tracer) calls `sendSupportMessageAction(body, category, subject)`, swaps in the bar with the case number from the server's answer, shows "Chamado #N aberto." on the creating client only (a status, no timer) and keeps focus in the composer; a later reply sends the body only.
- The member's bar (UI-D-818) shows number, pill and the server's subject label (never omitted), the "Meus chamados" link wrapping to its own right-aligned line when it does not fit, the slim bar for a member with only past cases, nothing for no case; a test feeds it a staff-shaped payload and asserts no priority, owner, category or action text renders.
- The lifecycle (UI-D-820, UI-D-833): the `Resolvido` strip above an enabled composer, the `Fechado` strip replacing it with the outline "Novo chamado" to `/suporte` (full width below 360 px), a blocked or removed notice winning over it, a reply that reopens toasting "Chamado #N reaberto." once and moving the pill back to `Aberto`, a live close silent with no focus move.
- Live behaviour (UI-D-830, T-12.4-59): the header is re-read on every `chat.message` and `chat.case` signal (a replayed seq included), every (re)subscribe and every refocus to visible, coalesced to one in flight and one queued, no polling; one persistent visually hidden polite region speaks the resolved, closed and reopened sentences only for a change that arrives while the page is open.
- `useBarHeightAnchor` keeps the reader in place when the bar's height changes (stay at the bottom, else `scrollTop` moves by the bar's delta), proven with stubbed metrics and a hand-driven ResizeObserver.
- `/suporte/{id}` (UI-D-821, T-12.4-56): the first gate is a three-way split by permission; the member's closed case renders read-only, an active case redirects to `/suporte`, every other id is the one not-found screen.

- **Fix:** the chips are a `<fieldset aria-labelledby>` (role and name unchanged: `getByRole('group', { name: 'Sobre o que é?' })` passes); the bar's reading unit is a visually hidden "Chamado #N, {state}" sentence with the visible title and pill `aria-hidden`.
- **Files modified:** `CaseOpener.tsx`, `CaseBar.tsx`, their tests
- **Committed in:** `8ccc60bf`

- **Found during:** Task 1

- **Fix:** the bar swaps in with the typed subject (else the message the member sent, one truncated line) and `refreshCase()` runs immediately, replacing it with the server's finished label. The pane builds no label itself.
- **Files modified:** `ThreadPane.tsx`
- **Committed in:** `8ccc60bf`, `4c4fcecc`
