# Phase 12.4 Plan 07: Staff case read API Summary

**The team's inbox now lists cases by open, resolved or closed tab with owner, priority and awaiting filters, every case carries its server-decided list of legal actions, and the history, the member's earlier cases and the live roster are one staff-only read away.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 13 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-17T10:02:00Z
- **Completed:** 2026-11-17T10:15:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 9 (plus this summary)

## Accomplishments

- The tracer: staff reads the default inbox and finds the member's case with its number, `open` state, `subjectLabel`, category and priority normal, the shipped row keys unchanged; once the admin lane closes it the default tab no longer lists it and `tab=closed` does.
- The three tab statements are complete literal predicates chosen in TypeScript; the open one restates `state in ('open','in_progress')` so `chat_conversations_open_inbox_idx` serves it, which pgTAP 152 now asserts by name beside the closed tab on `chat_conversations_inbox_idx`.
- `actionsFor` computes `take`, `assign`, `resolve`, `close`, `reopen`, `edit` from the state, the owner and the viewer's permissions; the support lane never gets `assign`, a closed case lists none, and the staff `case` rides on the detail route.
- An owner who left the staff (demoted, blocked or removed) keeps the stored `ownerUserId`, reads `ownerName` null and a take-over stays offered; no name of a departed actor ever appears in the history.
- `GET /case/history` answers the events oldest first plus at most five earlier cases of the same member and `hasMoreEarlier` from a sixth-row probe; `GET /staff` answers only `{ userId, name }` of live staff.
- A thread with no message sorts first and its cursor carries the literal `none`, so a `limit=1` walk goes past it; EP-050..EP-053 are asserted in the new suite.

## Decisions Made

- The label of an inbox row reuses plan 06's `firstMessageJoin` (first visible message by seq) rather than a literal `seq = 1` lateral, so the member card and the team row can never disagree.
- Null-instant cursor: `none` in the instant slot; the page after it reads `(last_message_at is null and id < cursor id) or last_message_at is not null`. The shipped `nextCursor` rule skipped such rows (a null instant gave no cursor).
- The inbox inventory row now points at `chat staff cases`; the old `phase 7 sweep` case keeps running untouched.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] A thread with no message answered `awaiting: null`**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (EP-053 fixture)
- **Issue:** `last_message_side = 'member' and ...` is null when the side is null, so the row broke `awaiting: boolean`.
- **Fix:** `coalesce(..., false)` in the inbox statement.
- **Files modified:** `packages/modules/chat/server/service.ts`
- **Verification:** EP-053 asserts `awaiting: false` on the no-message case.
- **Committed in:** `f1647a3f`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] The shipped cursor rule would have stopped a walk at a no-message thread**
- **Found during:** Task 1 design (EP-053 requires walking past it)
- **Issue:** `nextCursor` was withheld when the last row's instant was null, which dropped every later row from a `limit=1` walk.
- **Fix:** the `none` cursor described above.
- **Files modified:** `packages/modules/chat/server/service.ts`
- **Verification:** `case staff edges` EP-053 walks at limit 1 and equals the whole page.
- **Committed in:** `32045a71`

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] pgTAP 152 fact 7 was sensitive to autovacuum timing**
- **Found during:** Task 3 gate (the new open and closed probes failed twice right after integration runs, then passed on rerun; the failing plan was a sequential scan of a freshly vacuumed small heap)
- **Fix:** `set local enable_seqscan = off` for the two probes; the by-name assertions stay, and the two "not a sequential scan" lines became "does not fall back to the other index" lines.
- **Files modified:** `supabase/tests/152-chat.sql`
- **Verification:** five consecutive passes plus three more each after an isolation run.
- **Committed in:** `f1647a3f`
