# Phase 12.4 Plan 13: Meus chamados and the resolved-case bell row Summary

**The first chat row of the bell ("Seu chamado #N foi resolvido", a CircleCheck disc linking to its case) and the member's "Meus chamados" page at /suporte/chamados, with counted Chamado atual and Anteriores sections, keyset paging, pull-to-refresh that replaces the whole page, and empty, error and skeleton states.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 9 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-17T11:24:00Z (approximate; no start stamp was captured before the first read)
- **Completed:** 2026-11-17T11:33:00Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 11 (6 created, 5 modified)

## Accomplishments
- A `chat.case_resolved` notification renders in the bell with the 40px CircleCheck disc, the bold "Seu chamado #42" lead and a link to `/suporte/<id>` (encoded, else `/suporte`); only `caseId` and `number` are ever read, proven by a hostile-facts test.
- `/suporte/chamados` is a member page gated by permission (staff and chat-off callers get not-found), with the shipped `PageHeader` back to `/suporte`, a counted "Chamado atual" (only when active) and "Anteriores" (only when closed cases exist), and a card that is ONE anchor with number, pill, two-line subject, one-line prefixed preview and category, dot and time. A closed card carries no fade, only a neutral pill and the secondary subject colour.
- Paging through `InfiniteScroll` (a failed page keeps its rows and shows the inline retry line), `PullToRefresh` that calls `loadMoreMyCasesAction(null)` and replaces the current case, the list and the counts, and a generation guard that drops a page that was in flight during a refresh.
- Empty state ("Você ainda não abriu nenhum chamado" with "Falar com a equipe" to `/suporte`, no redirect), first-read error card with "Tentar novamente", and `loading.tsx` with three card skeletons at the card geometry.
