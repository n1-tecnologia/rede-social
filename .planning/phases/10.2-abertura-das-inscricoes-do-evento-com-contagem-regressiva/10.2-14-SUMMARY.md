## Accomplishments

- `EventPoster` and `EventHero` (module UI, props-only) gain `tagIcon` (`ticket` by default, so every shipped caller is byte-identical, asserted; `clock` draws the Clock on the same over-media tag) and a `ReactNode` `note` / `overline`. No import from the app tier, the framework or the catalog.
- `EventLiveNote` is the host island: it renders `LiveCountdown` in the `text` variant from a card, hero or holder descriptor (price first on the opening, `{until} · {when}` on the hero, the `reine` scope on Meus eventos) and falls back to the finished static note string with no descriptor or no seed, arming no timer.
- Tracer: the gallery card of a paid event with a future opening draws the `Em breve` tag with the Clock, `R$ 80,00 · Abre em 2 d 4 h 13 min` live, inside ONE link whose accessible name is the static string (absolute opening date and time, no count unit), built from the REAL `eventCardView` and the real catalog.
- The event page: `Em breve` with the Clock and the shipped overline for a viewer who is not in (no competing counter); a paid holder gets the live overline `Faltam 12 d 4 h 13 min · ter., 13 de out.` with exactly one `role="timer"` and no `aria-live` ancestor, still counting 30 min before the start (`hoje, 13:13`), no milestone sentence; at the start the shipped `Acontecendo agora`; a free event keeps `Faltam N dias` and `É hoje!`; a holder with no seed keeps the shipped overline.
- `Meus eventos` and the profile rail draw the holder's live count through the island; free events and no-seed answers keep today's lines. `TenantAppPreview.tsx` and the server action payload are unchanged (descriptors stay plain data).
- `GalleryBoundaryRefresh` renders nothing and hands every `Em breve` opening within 24 h of the seed to ONE `useBoundaryRefresh` with a key of the page's `id:tag` list: one refresh just after the soonest opening, one bounded retry chain (five refreshes at most), re-armed when a card turns `Pago`, no timer with no such card, correct under a device clock a day fast.
