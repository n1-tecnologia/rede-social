## Accomplishments

- Groups 1 to 3 (17 frames): card with and without price, under a minute, Abrindo agora, back to Pago, holder count, 320px; hero for a non-holder and for the holder; the zone with price, without, online hint, long time-zone label, 320px, Abrindo agora, the swap to the buy zone and the unavailable-before-opening precedence row.
