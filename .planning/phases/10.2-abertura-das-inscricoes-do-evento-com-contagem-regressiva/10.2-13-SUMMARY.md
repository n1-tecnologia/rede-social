# Phase 10.2 Plan 13: The Em breve zone and the live count Summary

**A paid event before its opening now shows a seeded, hydration-safe, silent count in three cells with the exact opening, the zone and the price and nothing to click, asks the server just after the opening, and swaps by itself to Comprar ingresso with one announcement.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 12 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-14T11:51Z
- **Completed:** 2026-11-14T12:03Z
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 8 (4 created, 4 modified), +1187 / -27 lines

## Accomplishments

- `LiveCountdown` (new client component): `blocks` draws one `flex-1` tertiary cell per non-zero unit with a 24px tabular number and a 12px label, the last minute as one `Menos de 1 min` cell and zero as one `Abrindo agora` cell; `text` draws the card, hero and `Meus eventos` short count with the exact keys of the server view (price first, singular `Falta`, hero `{until} · {when}`, the live word at zero). The first render is `target minus seed` from props alone, then one seeded timeout through `useCountdownClock`. With no seed it draws nothing (or a static fallback) and never asks the clock for the value.
- `SoonZone` is the zone of UI-D-784 inside the shipped `Zone` shell: Clock heading, the count, `Abertura: 20 nov, 10:00`, the tenant's long zone line (`break-words`, no truncation class), the price row only when filled, the hint, and the Lock hint for an online event. No button, anchor, href or brand fill whatever props arrive, no skeleton.
- `PaidEventZone` gained the `soon` branch: the zone is a sibling of the persistent polite wrapper; the wrapper keeps `useLiveRefresh` mounted through `soon` and holds only a hidden status sentence (`ticket.soon.milestone.day|hour|minute` at 24 h, 1 h and 1 min, none at 24 h exactly or at zero) and, once, `As inscrições abriram.` when this client saw `soon` become `buy`. The count's `role="timer"` is outside the wrapper and changes silently.
- `EventActions` passes `[checkinOpensAt, startsAt, endsAt, opening]`, the key `{phase}:{zone}` and the server seed to `useBoundaryRefresh`: one refresh lands just after the opening on the server clock, the shipped retry chain (2, 5, 15, 30 s, five refreshes in total, proved bounded) keeps trying while the server still answers `soon`, the zone keeps `Abrindo agora` with no toast and no error copy, and the swap to buy, sold out or unavailable re-arms and cancels the chain. An early payer's presence swaps to the confirmed zone inside the same wrapper.
- Plan gate, every command run once, in the plan's order: the four component test files (140 passed in the final component run together with `EventActions`), `pnpm --filter @rede-social/web test` (380 files, 9173 tests, all passed, exit 0), `TURBO_CACHE=local:r pnpm turbo typecheck --filter=@rede-social/web --concurrency=4` (exit 0, 3 tasks, includes the web build so no server import leaks into the client bundle), `bash scripts/check-ui-literals.sh` (exit 0), `pnpm boundaries` (exit 0, 2521 files, no issues). Biome check on the touched folders is clean. `apps/web/.next/cache` and `.turbo/cache` were removed afterwards.
