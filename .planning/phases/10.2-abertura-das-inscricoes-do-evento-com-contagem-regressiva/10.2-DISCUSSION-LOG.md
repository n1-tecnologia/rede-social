## Claude's Discretion

- The date must be before the event's start; a date already in the past is accepted (it means open).
- Precedence of states in the buy zone and for a presence holder.
- Tick rate (once a minute), column and function names, issue codes, copy keys and strings.
- How the server time seeds the live counter, hydration, accessibility and reduced motion.

- Notification to the admin on an early payment.
- "Me avise" button on an `Em breve` event.
- Opening date for free events.
- Scheduling the sale inside Kiwify.
