# Phase 10.2 Plan 05: The grant half of the opening rules Summary

**An early approved payment confirms the presence on every write path and stamps `ticket_confirmed_at`; the attendance guard keeps members from forging it; a service-only definer answers "was this delivery paid before the opening".**

## Accomplishments

- `app.event_gateway_grant` redefined as a byte copy of the replay-first body (diff against `20261113144645` shows only the three write statements and one comment line) with `ticket_confirmed_at = now()` on the insert, the `not_going` update and the upgrade update. No check of the opening anywhere (D-782): proved with an opening an hour ahead, 50 days ahead and in the past, and by a catalogue probe that the function definition never mentions the opening column.
- `app.event_attendance_guard()` redefined as a byte copy of the bookkeeping-lock body: `ticket_confirmed_at` added to step 1a (member lane refused 42501, constraint `event_attendances_bookkeeping_locked`, message `bookkeeping_locked`, probed for SQLSTATE, constraint and message) and removed from both sides of the step 1b comparison. The trigger is untouched.
- `app.event_ticket_before_opening(uuid, uuid, text, text)`: `service_role` only, empty `search_path`, tenant pinned; false for null or malformed arguments, an unknown reference, a reference of another event, another tenant's reference, and a row with a null instant or no opening.
- pgTAP 234 grew from 102 to 176 tests: section 6 (the grant on five write shapes plus the manual release), section 7 (member lane and positive controls), section 8 (derived mark at its boundaries, refund removes the mark, no index on the column, equal instants keep `(responded_at, id)`), section 9 (definer matrix, both cross-tenant directions with a shared reference, catalogue hardening, trigger kept, lock key text).
