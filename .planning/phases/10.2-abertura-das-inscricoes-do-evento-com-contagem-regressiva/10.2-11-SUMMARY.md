# Phase 10.2 Plan 11: Countdown timing hooks Summary

**Server-seeded countdown timing: a single device-clock reader, a one-timeout hook that sleeps in a hidden tab and re-seeds after 60 s, and a boundary refresh that follows the server clock and arms for a page opened days earlier.**

## Accomplishments

- `serverClock.ts` is the only file of the stack that spells the device-clock call; `useCountdownClock` and `useBoundaryRefresh` both go through it (grep: exactly 1 of 3 files).
- `useCountdownClock` first renders `target minus seed` from props (a `renderToString` test proves no effect and no timer are needed), arms one `setTimeout` at `msToNextMinuteChange(left) + 10 ms`, never an interval, returns `null` with no seed, and gives identical values with the device clock a day fast, a day slow and in 2099.
- Hidden tab: the timer is cleared, the value is recomputed at once on return, one `router.refresh()` after more than 60 s hidden (none at exactly 60 s), the same on `pageshow` persisted, one refresh per return, listeners and timer gone on unmount.
- `useBoundaryRefresh` takes an optional `serverNowIso`; waits are capped at 24 h and re-evaluated, so a target three days away refreshes exactly once at the boundary; the 2/5/15/30 s retry chain survives the re-render the refresh itself causes.

## Decisions Made

- Margin of 10 ms on top of the shipped `msToNextMinuteChange` (which returns `(remaining % 60000) + 1` and 0 at nothing to count, treated as "arm no timer"); every fire recomputes from the offset clock.
- A back-forward restore commonly fires both `visibilitychange` and `pageshow`; a 1 s same-return window on the device clock keeps it to one refresh.
- Retry chain lives in a ref keyed by `phase#boundaries`. Without it, adding the seed to the effect dependencies would have cancelled the chain on the very re-render the first refresh causes (the new seed). A re-render with the same key restarts only the pending delay; once the chain is over the next boundary is armed, which also arms later boundaries when the phase string does not change.
