**Phase Goal:** A paid event (one with a linked Kiwify product) gets an optional registration-opening date set by `admin_tenant` in the event form. Before it, members see the event with an "Em breve" tag and a live countdown in days, hours and minutes to the opening, and cannot buy (the API does not return the purchase link); after it, a member who holds a presence sees a countdown to the event. A payment that arrives before the opening, through a direct link, still confirms the presence and is marked for the admin. Free events and events with no opening date behave exactly as today.
