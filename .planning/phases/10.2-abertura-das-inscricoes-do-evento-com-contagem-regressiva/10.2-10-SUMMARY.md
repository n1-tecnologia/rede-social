# Phase 10.2 Plan 10: Decision layer of the opening screens Summary

**A paid event with a future opening is now a finished `soon` zone, card tag and holder countdown decided from payload strings alone (exact to the microsecond, no clock), plus the neutral early-payment mark as data for Participantes and Eventos recentes.**

## Performance

- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto), all committed before the interruption
- **Files modified:** 4 (two view models, two test files), +1019 / -44 lines
- **Interruption:** the machine went to sleep after the third task commit and a green build, before this SUMMARY; the resume re-ran every check (see Deviations)

## Accomplishments

- `eventSaleState` gained the `soon` kind with one first-match-wins precedence: not paid, cancelled or ended is `none`; under way is `salesClosed`; `saleReady === false` is `unavailable`; then `soon`; then sold out; then no safe link (`unavailable`); then `buy`. The opening and the seed are compared as plain strings, so the comparison is chronological and exact to the microsecond. With an opening and no seed, `soon` holds only while the server withheld the link. The function reads no clock (grep gate prints nothing).
- `formatEventOpening` and the `soon` block of `EventActionState` (`EventSoonView`: opening and seed ISO, `dateLabel`, `timeLabel`, `zoneLabel`, reference price) give the zone its exact opening as tenant-zone finished strings, with the year only when it differs from the request's tenant-local year.
- The card shows the `Em breve` tag instead of `Pago` (only for a viewer who is not in, never cancelled or ended), the price-first note with the short count, no spots note, and an accessible name carrying the absolute opening date and time with no count; `EventCardView.countdown` and `EventDetailView.hero.overlineCountdown` are plain descriptors for the client island.
- A paid event's holder gets the count to the event on the card, the `Meus eventos` note (`eventCountdown`, new sibling `eventHolderCountdown` returning text plus descriptor) and the hero overline, running through the check-in window until `starts_at` and handing over to `Acontecendo agora` and `Aconteceu em`; free events and no-seed answers keep today's lines byte for byte.
- `AttendeeMark.kind` gained `beforeOpening` (neutral, after `Pago`, before `Reembolsado` and `Acima do limite`), and `EventRowView.marker` carries the delivery label for an item with `beforeOpening` true.
- `events-view.ts` re-exports the pure countdown module of plan 09.
