# Phase 10.2 manual proofs (P1 to P17)

This file is the ledger of the proofs only the owner can run: the first real purchase made before an opening date
and its Antes da abertura mark, a real phone with a wrong clock, how VoiceOver and TalkBack speak the counter, the
held-out visual checks at 320 px, the homolog deploy and every production step, the full `pnpm verify` (it resets and
seeds the local database, so it is his), and the reading of the assumptions, defaults and wording the plans left to
him. Everything an agent could build is built and proved on the local stack (plans 01 to 15: unit, component, pgTAP,
integration, and the Playwright journey of plan 16); what is left here cannot be faked, so nothing in this file is
ever recorded as done by an agent.

**The rule.** Every row ships `pending`. A row becomes `passed` only when Igor pastes the evidence of its steps and
the date, in the row, himself. An agent never edits a Status line and never writes evidence it did not see. A phase
whose ledger has a `pending` row closes `human_needed`, never green. (Project memory: a blanket UAT pass is checked
against `docs/DEPLOY.md` before it is recorded, and a phone cannot reach `*.localhost`, so a row that needs a phone
needs a homolog or production host.)

**The evidence rule.** Paste shapes, counts, statuses, HTTP codes, exit codes and the words the screens show.
**Never** a key, a password, a token, a connection string, a webhook token, an e-mail address of a member or a
project ref. Replace a ref by `<ref>` and a host by `<host>` in what you paste.

**The procedures** are in `docs/DEPLOY.md`, "Phase 10.2 release (opening of the event sale)".

**The design approval is yours.** Sketch `022` (the design gate, D-33, D-365) carries `approved: true`; the
approval was recorded on your word in the session, not by an executor, and it is provisional until you walk the
built screens (rows P4 and P12).

## P1 - The real early purchase (OPEN-26)

- **Proves:** that a purchase made in the real gateway BEFORE the opening still confirms the presence (D-782: the grant
  never checks the opening), that the member's open page swaps to Presença confirmada by itself, and that
  Participantes and Pagamentos, Eventos recentes mark it Antes da abertura. Every early purchase of this repository was
  made with the `fake` adapter; the real product id and order id formats are what is unproven. OPEN-26 had no probe
  category for its real half; a reviewer should confirm none is missing.
- **Needs:** a tenant with the `payments` module on and the real gateway connected, one test event with a real gateway
  product, an opening date a few hours ahead, a member account, a real card, and the gateway panel.
- **Steps:**
  1. In the event form, turn Cobrar ingresso on, type the real product's id, its purchase link and a price, set
     Abertura das inscrições a few hours ahead, and save. A member's page shows Em breve, the count, the exact opening
     and no Comprar ingresso.
  2. As that member, keep the event page open. Buy the real product with the member's e-mail from the gateway's own
     page (open the link the gateway gave you by hand: the app will not show it yet).
  3. See the presence appear by itself: the open page becomes Presença confirmada with the count to the event; the
     bell lists the row; Participantes marks the member Pago and Antes da abertura; Pagamentos, Eventos recentes shows
     the same mark.
  4. Open the event form: the opening is read-only with the sold line.
- **Evidence to paste:** the words the screens showed at each step and whether the product id and order id were
  accepted as they came.
- **Status:** pending

## P2 - The real-phone walk with a wrong device clock (OPEN-27)

- **Proves:** that a phone whose clock is wrong (a day ahead, then a day behind) shows the same count and swaps at the
  same moment as a phone with a right clock, because the count is seeded by the server; and that the screens read
  right at 320 and 390 px. The local spec proves it on an emulated phone with a clock a day ahead
  (`events-opening.spec.ts` test 3, `page.clock`); it cannot prove a real device. EP-61 (unclassified): OPEN-27 had no
  probe category; a reviewer should confirm none is missing.
- **Steps:** on a phone, set the clock a day ahead (turn off automatic date and time), open an event whose opening is a
  few minutes ahead, and compare the count with a second phone with the right clock; wait for the opening and see the
  zone become Comprar ingresso with no reload. Repeat a day behind. Put the phone in the background for more than a
  minute during the count and bring it back: the count is right at once.
- **Evidence to paste:** the device, the browser, the width, the two clocks, the count read on each phone, and whether
  the swap happened by itself.
- **Status:** pending

## P3 - How a screen reader speaks the counter (EP-42, unresolved UI consideration)

- **Proves:** how VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android) speak the visually hidden `role="timer"` and the three polite
  sentences ("As inscrições abrem em menos de 1 dia / 1 hora / 1 minuto") of the Em breve zone. This is the one UI
  consideration no automated check can settle. The planner's assumption: the pattern may be adjusted by what you hear,
  but never the rule that nothing is announced per minute (UI-D-787).
- **Steps:** with the screen reader on, open an Em breve zone; swipe to the count; wait across a minute change and
  across the day, hour and minute thresholds; at the opening, listen for the swap to Comprar ingresso.
- **Evidence to paste:** the device, the screen reader, what was spoken at each step, and whether anything was spoken
  per minute.
- **Status:** pending

## P12 - The planner assumptions to confirm

- A-10.2-CONF: the early-payment mark is derived from a dedicated confirmation instant, not from `responded_at`,
  because the upgrade of an existing Vou or courtesy never touches `responded_at`.
- A-10.2-SALE-READY: the detail carries one extra optional boolean so Venda indisponível can sit before Em breve.
- A-10.2-SERVER-NOW: the countdown seed is the database instant of the transaction that decided the link.
- A-10.2-OMIT: the form omits the opening key unless the administrator typed or cleared it, so a web ahead of the API
  still saves events.
- A-10.2-REFUND-LIFT: a fully refunded sale lifts the lock on the date; a refunded checked-in ticket keeps it.
- A-10.2-STALE-DATE: a date stored on an event whose product link failed is invisible and never sold.
- A-10.2-NATIVE: plan 13-18 does not cover the Comprar ingresso zone (see P13); nothing native is built here.
- A-UI-15 and A-UI-3: see P5.
- **Status:** pending
