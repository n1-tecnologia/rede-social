## Phase Goal

**As a** person invited by an organisation that runs a TRIA community (a tenant), **I want to** sign up through that organisation's public link, log in with e-mail and password, stay logged in, recover my password and log out, **so that** I reach my organisation's private community on one URL and the API only ever serves my organisation's data.

<objective>
Close the two remaining steps of the story that the tracer left open: password recovery by e-mail link (`/esqueci-senha` → e-mail → `/auth/confirm` → `/redefinir-senha`, D-10) and the blocked-member contract (403 `MEMBERSHIP_BLOCKED` on the very next request, session cleared, "acesso suspenso" screen, D-09), plus the orphan-identity safety net (`/sem-comunidade`). Local e-mail goes through Mailpit; real delivery through Resend is wired in 01-11 (D-13) and verified manually per VALIDATION.md.

Purpose: AUTH-03 (recovery), AUTH-06 (blocked cut off immediately; forged/expired tokens rejected).
Output: recovery pages/actions/route, template + local config, blocked/no-membership screens and routing, auth-middleware integration test, two e2e specs.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
