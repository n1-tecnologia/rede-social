- **Local acceptance is vacuous:** the local seed holds 0 `store_products`, 0 `store_categories` and 0 `store_front` rows after the reset and seed, so "every tenant that had products now has eight category rows" is not exercised locally (the load seeds only tenants that have products). Plan 10's pgTAP fixture is what proves the load; the production fan-out is Igor's release step.
- The files are expand-only and fan out to every tenant project; central-clean stays clean because nothing is driven by `tenant_modules` or a trigger.
