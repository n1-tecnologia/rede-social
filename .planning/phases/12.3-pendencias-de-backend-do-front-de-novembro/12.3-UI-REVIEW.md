## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (3/4)
- Provisional markers are removed. A grep of `app components lib messages` for "neste aparelho", "vale só" and "front only" finds nothing in the phase's surfaces. The only "neste aparelho" hits are the unrelated push-notification strings (`notifications.json:70-81`). Remaining "FRONT ONLY" and "TEMPORARY LOCAL VIEW" comments (`MemberHomeTab.tsx:320`, `lib/resume-spot-local-view.ts`, `CatalogueSearch.tsx`, `HomeGreeting.tsx`) are the "Continue de onde parou" example and the search, declared out of scope.
- Contract strings found: `store.setup.featured.scope` and `categories.scope` (`store.json:392,419`), `admin.menu.scope` (`admin.json:144`), "Menu salvo." (`admin.json:143`), "Destaque da loja salvo." (`courses.member.json:331`), top-cover add/change/remove/preview/helper/fallback/invalid (`courses.json:634-641`), poster helper and refusal (`events.json:246,348`), category sentences (`store.json:424-426`), product-form `categoryInvalid` (`store.json:270`), menu `rules` and `generic` (`admin.json:146-147`).
- Deviation: the `promo_invalid` sentence differs from the contract (see fix 2).
- I did not confirm every contract key exists (for example `too_many_categories` or the Destaque-da-loja scope line wording inside a rendered sheet); I read the catalogue only by grep.
- Bare "Salvar"/"Cancelar" kept; acceptable under the CONTEXT lock, but still a generic label.

### Pillar 2: Visuals (3/4)
- `CoverField.tsx` implements the top block as its own picker: the `data-{hook}-top-cover-preview|local|fallback|error|remove` hook family (lines 53-57, 288), its own `disabled={topUploading}` (623, 643), its own `aria-live="polite"` and `role="alert"` (513, 519).
- The event poster has its own upload state (`EventForm.tsx:383-420`), its error is placed under its own block (687-688), and `busy` includes `posterUploading`.
- Not verifiable by code: the visual anchor per screen, the 4:1 cropped fallback legibility under long titles, the 4:5 poster composition. needs_human_review.

### Pillar 3: Color (4/4)
- The added lines in `app` and `components` contain no hex or `rgb(`. Errors use `text-danger`, helpers `text-text-tertiary`, selection `ring-brand`/`bg-brand`. No new brand use found. Dark mode and tenant brand are token-driven; actual contrast needs_human_review.

### Pillar 4: Typography (3/4)
- Contract: sizes 12/14/16/24, weights 400/700. Added lines include 2 `font-semibold` (600) and no `text-[Npx]`. Helpers use `text-xs font-normal` (12px/400, conforming); errors mostly `text-sm font-normal text-danger` (14px/400, conforming), but `StoreSetupPanel.tsx:748` uses `text-xs` for an alert line.

### Pillar 5: Spacing (3/4)
- No arbitrary `[..px]`/`[..rem]` values added. One added `gap-2.5` (10px) is not in the 4-multiple scale; the shipped 6/10/20/28/36 exceptions are declared, new ones are not. Controls keep 44px (`min-h-16` rows, `h-9` placement buttons at `PhoneMenuEditor.tsx:381` are a shipped 36px that undercut the 44px target; the contract lists `size-9` as kept as shipped).

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (3/4)
- Save pattern (UI-D-686) is implemented consistently: `pending` state, `disabled` on actions and rows, `onClose={pending ? NO_CLOSE : ...}` (`StoreSetupPanel.tsx:303,579`; `HomePromoChoice.tsx:204`), "Salvando…" label, `router.refresh()` on success, `SaveError` with `role="alert"` above Save, draft kept on failure, catch -> generic code.
- Stale handling: `featured_invalid` and `category_invalid` set `awaitingRefresh` and refresh (`StoreSetupPanel.tsx:293-297,569-573`); `promo_invalid` does the same (`HomePromoChoice.tsx:163-166`).
- Menu editor: `pending` disables Restore and Save, `role="alert"` footer line, `aria-pressed`, sr-only live region (`PhoneMenuEditor.tsx:93-429`).
- Product form: `category_invalid` maps to a field-level alert and keeps other values (`ProductForm.tsx:476-477,901`); category omitted when the store has none (436-437).
- Storefront: `StoreBrowseScreen` takes `front = null` and degrades to products only (lines 50-66), keeps the shipped error card for a product-list failure (167-170).
- Gaps: partial-answer behaviour, 320px overflow and real-phone behaviour of the saved menu are unproven here (needs_human_review). The registry audit was skipped (no `components.json`). A 4 would need rendered evidence for the backstop rows.
