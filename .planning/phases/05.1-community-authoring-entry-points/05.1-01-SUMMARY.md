# Phase 05.1 Plan 01: A story born attached to a community Summary

**`POST /v1/stories` takes an optional `communityId`. The community check, the story insert and the pin insert all happen in one `withTenantTx`, using the same pin statement and archived refusal as `pinStory`. The body also needs `stories.story.manage` when it names a community, and `story.pinned` fires after commit. Integration tests no longer read `VIDEO_PROVIDER=mux` from `.env.local`: it is pinned to `fake`.**

## Accomplishments
- A story published with a community is written pinned to it. The story and its `story_community_pins` row are saved together or not at all, and the response's existing `pinnedCommunityCount` reads 1. No response field was added.
- Every refusal writes nothing and answers exactly what `pinStory` answers: archived gives `400 { pin: 'archived' }`; an unknown, removed or other-tenant community gives one bare 404 with byte-identical bodies. The cross-tenant case is covered by isolation case b6.
- A caller who can publish but not manage stories cannot pin through publish (403), and can still publish without a community. This closes a gap V2 would otherwise open, where members can publish.
- A story pinned at publish and a story pinned afterwards produce the same stored state, and a test asserts this. A community story stays in the tenant-wide `/inicio` strip (D-96).
- Wave 0: `VIDEO_PROVIDER: 'fake'` is the last `test.env` entry, and case 05.1-0 asserts it.
- The full story, isolation and pgTAP suites pass on a freshly reset and seeded database: 29 integration files / 490 tests, 93 stories unit tests, 285 pgTAP tests.
