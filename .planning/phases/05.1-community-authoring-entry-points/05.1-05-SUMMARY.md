# Phase 05.1 Plan 05: Reativar from the archived page and the Destaques + Summary

**An archived community's own page now gives managers a "Reativar comunidade" outline button under the note. It opens an explained brand confirmation and then refreshes the page into the active state. Every active community's Destaques row now leads with the admin's "Seu story" `+`, which opens the composer already addressed to that community. Browser replays of the three Phase 5 UAT reports pass on phone and desktop, and the full phase gate is green.**

## Accomplishments
- **ROADMAP criterion 2, reactivation half (D-90, UI-D-52).** `ReactivateCommunity` is a client island placed in `CommunityHeader`'s existing `note` slot, for `canManage` only. A tap opens a `ConfirmDialog tone="brand"` ("Reativar comunidade?", explaining that the community returns to the list). Confirming calls `reactivateCommunityAction`, shows "Comunidade reativada." and calls `router.refresh()`, so the pill, note and button disappear in place and the `+` appears. Any refusal, including `not_found`, shows "Não foi possível reativar a comunidade. Tente novamente." and leaves the button for a retry. A member's note is unchanged, and the edit-form control is still available as a second route.
- **ROADMAP criterion 3's door (D-92, D-93, UI-D-53).** `canPublishHere = !archived && stories.story.publish && stories.story.manage`. This is the pair the API requires to attach a story and the pair the composer row checks. When it holds, `StoriesSurface` gets the `own` circle linking to `/stories/publicar?comunidade={id}`. A pin-less community shows the section with the `+` alone to an admin. A member, or anyone on an archived community, gets no section when there are no pins.
- **Criteria 3-5 end to end.** In the browser, a photo story published from a community's `+` returns to that community with its circle in Destaques. The same story then heads `/inicio` once the worker readies it: one publish, with no second editorial step.
- **Catalog.** The six new keys are in the UI-SPEC's words. The phase's 16 keys, their 4 placeholders and 3 reused words are pinned in `messages.test.ts`.
