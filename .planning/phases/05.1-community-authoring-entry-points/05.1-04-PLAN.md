<objective>
Put the community choice INSIDE the story composer — ROADMAP criterion 5 — and make it the same
selector a community page pre-fills through `?comunidade=` (criterion 3's half that lives here). The
two are one mechanism, as the ROADMAP Notes require.

`/stories/publicar` today takes no parameters and posts `{ mediaAssetId, mediaKind, caption }`. This
plan resolves `?comunidade=` on the server exactly as `/criar` does (D-93), adds the D-97
`Publicar em` row to the post-pick frame (UI-D-54), opens the shipped `CommunityPickerSheet` with a
host-built `Nenhuma comunidade` row shared with the post composer (D-95, D-98, UI-D-55), sends the
optional `communityId` 05.1-01 added (D-99), lands where the story went (D-94, UI-D-57), and handles
the archived-while-composing race legibly (UI-D-58). With no communities or no attach permission the
composer is exactly today's.

Purpose: the one selector behind criteria 3 and 5.
Output: the shared default row, the refusal mapping, the revalidation, the server resolution, the
composer row and sheet, the catalog keys and the unit evidence.
</objective>
